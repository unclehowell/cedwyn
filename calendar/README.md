# Cedwyn Calendar (v0.0.2)

The Great House Farm chronology on a calendar: https://cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/calendar/ (Cloudflare Worker `cedwyn-calendar`, branch `calendar`).

- **Address (0.0.2):** a path on the main site, not a subdomain. Worker routes on the zone: `cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/calendar*` (the app; anything else the pattern catches, e.g. `/calendars`, is passed to the site) and `cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/_cedwyn/graph/calendar.json*` (the shared header graph asks `/_cedwyn/graph/<area>.json?v=…`; the `*` is needed for the query string). `calendar.bucklerfamily.estate` (still the Worker's custom domain) answers 301 to `https://cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/calendar/` + the same path and query; browsers keep the `#view/date` hash. Shared header files load straight from the site's own `/_cedwyn/`. `/calendar/` was a 404 on the site before the route (checked live 31 Oct 2026).
- **Missing evidence (0.0.2):** every date or period an entry of the missing-records register (`/research/missing-records/`, MR-0001–0055) points to is bright pink (#ff1493) in every view: pink cells (day cells for exact days; month cells for month dates and month ranges; year cells for years and year ranges), a "Missing evidence pointing here" strip listing every entry that touches the period, a pink count in the coverage line, hover titles with MR number, title and status, and a modal linking to `/research/missing-records/mr-NNNN/`. Several dates in one entry ("1938; 1969; 1975", "1959 and 1965") each get a mark. Pink is never counted in coverage. The register is read at request time (5-minute cache; new entries' "Date of the record" from their own pages) with the snapshot as fallback. MR-0032 has no date of the record ("not stated") and is not drawn.
- **Two MR series:** the register's MR-#### (missing records) and the evidence index's MR-#### (newspaper and online records) share numbers. 0.0.1 let the catalogue's register rows hide index rows MR-0001–0049; 0.0.2 keys them apart, so those newspaper records now appear.

- **Views:** Day, Week, Month, Year, Compare (4–32 years side by side) and Decades (century tabs, 10×10 year grid). Swipe to move; pinch, +/− or the tabs to zoom; tap the title to jump to a year. The URL hash keeps the view (`#month/1988-12`, `#year/1988`, `#compare/1983-8`, `#decades/1900`).
- **Coverage:** a day counts when a record is dated to it; a month when a record is dated to it or a day in it; a year when any record is dated within it. Month-only and year-only records sit in "Sometime in …" bands, never on an invented day; ranges and decades are bands and never count. Missing evidence (missing-records register) is bright pink and never counts.
- **Data (architecture rule 30 Oct 2026):** central db = the evidence catalogue (`core_items`, D1 `cedwyn-apps`), read at request time with a 5-minute cache. The calendar's own db is derived in `worker/derive.js` from the catalogue, the public evidence index (`/research/evidence/`), the Master Timeline (`/research/timeline/`) and the missing-records register. `tools/build_snapshot.mjs` writes `worker/snapshot.json` (the index and timeline rows, and the catalogue as fallback); it is over 100 KB, so it ships with the Worker deploy and is not committed here. No new D1 database; the app writes nothing.
- **Front end:** near-A2UI. `worker/ui.js` renders one A2UI v0.9 surface on the server (`/_ui/index.a2ui.json`, catalog `/_ui/catalog.json`); the Calendar component is a mount drawn by `worker/client.txt` from `/api/events`. Shared Cedwyn header via `/_cedwyn/*` pass-through; the header graph (`/_cedwyn/graph/*.json`) plots only this app's own events. Dark only.
- **Scope rules:** undated records, the Llandough Farm series (a different property, listed under Kissed Frogs, KF-35: the Ideal Homes drawings EV-0133/0135, the 1855 and 1881 timeline lines and the 1881 "Land Doc Home Farm" census reading EV-0223; noted in the ⓘ panel) and Bute-estate papers dated before 1824 are left out; banned public words are swapped ("not checked"), and a verbatim quote carrying one is withheld with a pointer to its evidence page, never altered.
- **Deploy:** `node tools/build_snapshot.mjs && TAB_TASK_ID=<task> python3 deploy.py "<message>"`; routes: `python3 deploy.py --routes` (ids in `state.json` routes). Rollback: `python3 deploy.py "rollback" --promote <previous version id>` (ids in `state.json` history on the box).

Rollback of 0.0.2: `python3 deploy.py "rollback" --promote 86f800bf-b10e-4ed8-9a9d-8812e816bba2` and delete the two routes (ids in `state.json`), which restores calendar.bucklerfamily.estate as the address.
