How the score works

Each lead is scored on the four limbs of the test for setting aside a judgment obtained by dishonesty or concealment, the Takhar threshold (Takhar v Gracefield Developments Ltd [2019] UKSC 13, adopting RBS v Highland Financial Partners LP [2013] EWCA Civ 328).

  1. Dishonesty or concealment shown (0–50)
  2. An operative cause of the 1987 outcome (0–50)
  3. Evidence not available then with reasonable diligence (0–50)
  4. Evidence quality and admissibility (0–50)

The four add up to 200. 100 means the Takhar threshold is plausibly met; 200 means proved beyond doubt on every limb. A lead is ready for counsel only with at least 40 on every limb.

Mapping: our reopen research scores each limb 0–5 (0 nothing; 1 inference or gap; 2 several documented facts; 3 a primary official record shows the anomaly; 4 a primary document proves the limb; 5 admissible originals ready for counsel). Each point is 10 here. Ties are ordered by limb 2. Leads marked as seed scores have not yet been tested.

Quarantined items (kissed frogs, under scrutiny) are never scored here. Data: leaderboard.json v1.0.0.
