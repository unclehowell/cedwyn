Sections

Every section: what it is, its branch, where its data lives, how it is sourced.

  • How a section is sourced
  • All sections
  • Sections with a live store of their own

The section registry is themes.json on main. Its order is the order of the header's section selector (left and right arrows) and of the directory. Each section is a separate web app on its own branch and appears in the core catalogue under its id (locations.theme).

How a section is sourced

KindMeaningCount
branchStatic output copied from the section's own branch (branch + dir; optional build command).29
snapshotA section with a live origin, frozen into section/<id> and pinned by sections.lock.7
crawlStill copied by a read-only crawl of its live origin on each build (moving to snapshot).4
mainGenerated on main from shared data.1

Sections with a live store of their own

  • FCG: a Worker with a D1 database for live writes; the static snapshot is pinned on section/fcg.
  • Ask the Archive and The Case Office: Workers at their own origins; snapshots on section/chat and section/app.
  • The Post Office: the email Worker answers full-text search; the static viewer reads an index file.
  • Guest book: generated from the repository's commit history on every build.
  • Site editor: a key-value store for drafts; it refuses a change that spans more than one section.
