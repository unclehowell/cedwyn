# Flywheel: Cedwyn film — daily scene

**Cadence:** daily 06:00 UK  
**Goal prefix (duplicate check):** `CEDWYN MOVIE RUN`  
**Runner:** `work/cedwyn-movie/RUNNER.md` (on the project workspace)

## Purpose
Make one two-minute scene a day from the chronology, in Welsh, English and Spanish, and open the next door on /movie/.

## Inputs
- the editable prompt in the film repository
- the bible (cast, voices, sets)
- the chronology

## Outputs
- one clip under 25 MB
- the door opened
- scripts ten frames ahead

## Metric
one quality clip per day, chronology-correct

## Report rules
One bubble: "Door N is open".

Built to the [flywheel schema](SCHEMA.md).
