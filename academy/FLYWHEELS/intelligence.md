# Flywheel: Intelligence flywheel

**Cadence:** daily 04:15 Europe/London  
**Goal prefix (duplicate check):** `INTEL`  
**Runner:** `work/intel-flywheel/RUNNER.md` (on the project workspace)

## Purpose
Normalise events and actors, test patterns honestly, map who answered and who stayed silent, and publish missing-record (MR-) pages.

## Inputs
- the research catalogue
- research cycles
- FOI correspondence

## Outputs
- events and actor tables
- analyses and charts
- missing-record pages
- a ranked "what most likely occurred" note marked as interpretation

## Metric
new events, new missing records, analyses re-run

## Report rules
Per its runner.

Built to the [flywheel schema](SCHEMA.md).
