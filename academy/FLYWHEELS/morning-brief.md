# Flywheel: Morning brief

**Cadence:** daily 08:00 UK  
**Goal prefix (duplicate check):** `(inline)`  
**Runner:** `schedule instruction` (on the project workspace)

## Purpose
Tell the project lead what finished overnight, mail that needs him, today's calendar and deadlines due soon.

## Inputs
- finished work
- mail
- calendar
- deadlines

## Outputs
- at most three bubbles, or nothing

## Metric
nothing repeated; nothing missed

## Report rules
Sent only if something is new.

Built to the [flywheel schema](SCHEMA.md).
