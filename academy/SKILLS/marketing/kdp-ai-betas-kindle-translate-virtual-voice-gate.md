# Amazon KDP AI betas (Kindle Translate and Virtual Voice): what they would do for Cedwyn, and the gate (verdict: not open to the current books)

Harvested 28 Oct 2026 (skills harvest run). Builds on `amazon-kdp-2026-price-band-select-vs-direct.md` (KDP itself is still a PROPOSAL needing the project lead's tax, bank and ID setup). Index check: 'kindle translate' 0 hits, 'virtual voice' 0 hits.

## 1. What they are (KDP Help pages, read 28 Oct 2026)
**Kindle Translate** (launched 6 Nov 2025, expanded by July 2026)
- **Access:** invite-only beta on kdp.amazon.com. Free to beta authors.
- **Language pairs:** English → French (France), German, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), **Spanish (Spain)**. Those five → English (US).
- **Quality gate:** a translation only appears for preview and publishing if it passes Amazon's "7-step quality assurance threshold". Checks include mistranslations, named entities, terminology consistency, formal/informal address and gender consistency.
- **Money:** the same 35%/70% royalty plans as the source book, with its own list price. KDP Select enrolment is separate for each translation.
- **Label:** translations carry a visible "Kindle Translate" label in the store.

**Audiobooks with Virtual Voice**
- **Access:** invite-only beta. **"Only available for books to be distributed in the US marketplace at this time."**
- **Voices:** 80+ virtual voices. The book's primary language must be English, Spanish, Italian or French.
- **Money:** list price $3.99–$14.99, 40% royalty for the beta. Amazon may discount the price, or sell it as a cheaper add-on to the e-book.
- **Rule:** an Audible audiobook can't already exist for that edition.

## 2. Eligibility, the part that decides it for us (read verbatim on the eligibility pages)
To be translated, a Kindle Translate e-book must:
- **"be reflowable (not a fixed format)"**
- **"not be in the public domain"**. This also covers books that "primarily consist of public domain material, even where that material has been translated, annotated, or illustrated". Books mainly of the author's own writing, including retellings, *are* eligible.
- **not already be a translation**
- not be a pre-order
- have a cover and a primary author

**Content that won't carry over:** text inside images, "language excerpts that aren't in your book's primary language", subtitles, and books with tables or many images.

**Content Amazon states translates well:** narrative non-fiction and standard prose. **Content it states may not:** "extensive cultural references".

## 3. Practitioner read (thin, labelled)

## 5. When it would become worth it (the trigger, recorded so a later run can recheck)
All three must be true:
1. Cedwyn publishes an **original reflowable narrative** book written mainly by us, for example a short "story of the house" EPUB from the Sphinx sources (`epub-editions-sphinx-epubcheck.md`), not a records compilation.
2. the project lead has decided to list on KDP (`amazon-kdp-2026-price-band-select-vs-direct.md`).
3. KDP shows "Translate your eBook" on the Bookshelf. That's the only invite signal.

Then the plan is:
- **Translate EN→ES** as a draft only.
- **Native Argentine-Spanish review before publishing.** Rioplatense usage differs from Spain Spanish, and Welsh names must survive as named entities.
- **Banned-word sweep on the Spanish text too.** the project lead's public-text rule covers the Spanish equivalents of the banned words: "según", "afirma", "presunto", "supuesto", "fraude", "robado", "no verificado", "rumor". AI translation can reintroduce them even when the English source avoided them.
- **Price** the ES edition per the KDP skill's band. Leave Select off unless the category test states otherwise.
- **Measure:** ES units in the first 90 days against the EN edition. If under 5 units, unpublish and keep the effort on our own /ebooks/ shop.
