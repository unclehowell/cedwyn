## Evidence (with how strong it is)
- **Elise McDowell (practitioner, Substack, read the free part):** a $7 "tiny offer" sold by **two emails to a 1,442-person list added $1,385 in 24 h**; new-customer names appeared in the sales feed; she credits "a simple upsell tweak" with **+17% revenue** (detail paywalled, not read); 60% repeat-customer rate among people already in her world (her number, method not shown). Derived, not stated: at $7 each, $1,385 is at most ~198 orders (~13.7% of list) - fewer if the upsell is counted.
- **Peel Insights citing Optimove (read; Optimove study itself not read):** new customers repeat more when the first-purchase discount is in the **5-20%** range; **negligible difference in repurchase/LTV between 10% and 20% off**; bigger first discounts raise order count but cut AOV and lifetime value. So: if any first-order incentive is used, small beats deep.
- **Vendor/agency figures (not checked, do not quote):** CartFlows 2026 "$7-$27" range, humanswith.ai "6-18% tripwire conversion" - no data shown.
- Repeat-purchase ("20-30% more likely to buy again") is McDowell's claim without a source - treat as not checked.

## The method
1. **The offer is a smaller, complete thing, not a discount** on the main book: one chapter, one record with its story, a map pack, a short "guide to the case in 10 documents". It must be finished and useful alone.
2. **Price low enough to remove the decision**: practitioners use $5-$9 / £3-£7. Below ~£3 card fees eat it (Stripe UK standard cards ~1.5% + 20p, from memory - not re-read today).
3. **Sell it where intent already exists**: thank-you page after a free signup, the end of a free sample chapter, the welcome email E1-E2. Two emails (announcement + last call) is McDowell's whole campaign.
4. **Next step built in**: the tiny product ends with a link to the full e-book; the thank-you page offers the full title (post-purchase-cross-sell-bundle-upgrade.md) - that is where the +17%-style lift comes from.
5. **Measure the first-buyer count, not revenue**: success = new paying customers and how many go on to the full book within 30 days.
6. **Never** a fake countdown or invented scarcity (CMA/DMCC 2024 rules; see existing pricing skills).
