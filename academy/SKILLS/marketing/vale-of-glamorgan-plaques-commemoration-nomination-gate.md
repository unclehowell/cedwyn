# Vale of Glamorgan plaques: what exists (GATE note)

Harvested 29 Oct 2026. This closes the "Vale scheme not checked" gap left in blue-plaque-schemes-penarth-civic-society-further-reading-gate.md. Result: **the Vale has no council heritage blue-plaque scheme.** Three adjacent routes exist, and none is a sales channel for the farm.

## Facts (council and tourism pages, read 29 Oct 2026)
1. **Recognition and Commemoration review** (valeofglamorgan.gov.uk). Started after 2020 under the Strategic Equality Plan. It covers statues, monuments, buildings and street names, and future celebration of "the diverse communities within the Vale".
   - There are two online forms: *Submit a Commemoration for Review* (location + reason) and *Suggest a Person/Event to be Recognised or Commemorated* (name + reason).
   - No criteria, timetable, panel or count of awards is published on the page. Not checked further.
2. **Memorial plaques on benches** (Dedications and Memorials page). These are bereavement dedications, not heritage plaques.
   - Price: £322.50 per plaque on a shared bench, or £800 for an exclusive bench. A new bench costs from £1,475.
   - Not allowed at Porthkerry or Cosmeston Country Parks or on Penarth Pier, which have their own routes.
   - The council may alter or refuse inscriptions, and the plaque becomes council property.
- Penarth: Penarth Civic Society Heritage Trail (see the Penarth gate skill). Llandough sits outside every town trail.

## Judgement
- A plaque for the farm, the Williams family or the case = **NO**. It is private, contested, a non-town site with no family holdings, and the bench plaques are memorial and paid.
- The *Suggest a Person/Event* form is open to anyone. A defensible use is a **public, long-dead figure or documented event** that touches Llandough/Penarth and has its own sourced Cedwyn page. That lets the council's research find the Cedwyn page as further reading.
  - Candidates to test against sources: the May 1897 Lavernock–Flat Holm wireless trials. These belong to Lavernock, not Llandough, unless the parcel-B worker finds a primary source for lodging at Great House.
  - Never put a hypothesis on a council form.
