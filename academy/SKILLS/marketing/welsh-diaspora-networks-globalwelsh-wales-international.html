Welsh diaspora networks (GlobalWelsh Connect, Wales International): what is free, what is paid, and how a small heritage shop uses them

Written 28 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). There was no prior skill on GlobalWelsh or Wales International; the Wales–Argentina Society is already in welsh-patagonian-societies-chubut-outreach.md.

GlobalWelsh Connect (read: globalwelsh.com and the Business Information Pack v4, Dec 2022)

  • A not-for-profit online community "with Wales at its heart". Its own figures: members in 47+ countries, and an estimated 3 million people with a connection to Wales living abroad.
  • Size assertions vary with date and source. A Hivebrite case study states "nearly 3,000 active members" (snippet; the page is Cloudflare-blocked). An Instagram post from 2023 states "70+ countries and 7000+ individuals" (snippet). Treat both as GlobalWelsh's own marketing.
  • Free tier (everyone starts here; membership is approved by hand; over-18s only):
  • a profile
  • browse the business directory and jobs
  • post news and updates on the Live Feed
  • reply to community Callouts
  • event tickets
  • Paid features:
  • promoting your events to the community, submitting Callouts and joining City Hubs need Pathfinder (£35 a year, ages 18–27 only) or Pioneer.
  • a Business Directory profile needs GlobalWelsh for Business: £295 a year for under 10 employees (+£150 per extra seat), £995 for 10–49, £1,495 for 50+. It's private-sector only, and charities and public bodies must email [address kept in the project workspace]
  • The useful free move: a member's Live Feed post on a Welsh heritage topic, plus answering Callouts where heritage knowledge is asked for. That's goodwill, not sales.

Wales International / Cymru a'r Byd (read: walesinternational.cymru home + membership)

  • A volunteer-run Welsh community network with no outside funding. Its president is Aran Jones.
  • For members: a monthly newsletter, the bilingual Enfys magazine by post three times a year, members' events and free refreshments at the National Eisteddfod. Membership is £15 (students), £25 (individuals) or £40 (organisations and societies).
  • Business partner route: individual members get "10% off every time you shop or book online with our business partners". Its corporate partners include Wales Week in London, Visit Cardiff and Graffeg, a Welsh publisher.
  • Its blog, "Meet the World Wide Welsh", features Welsh people abroad. A Welsh-interest story could be offered to it, but that's an outward pitch.

What practitioners report

  • There are no published sales numbers for heritage businesses on either network. GlobalWelsh's own business pitch is the "buy Welsh" mindset and promotion "via our social channels" for paying business members (information pack). Plan for reach and goodwill. Don't plan for sales.
  • The diaspora fit is real for Cedwyn: Wales International's members are older, story-driven and read a print magazine. That matches the Home & Heritage and diaspora gift buyers in cultural-audience-segments-spectrum-culture-segments.md.

How to do it (small heritage shop, zero spend)

  1. Read before joining: GlobalWelsh's free-tier rules and Wales International's partner terms (ask by email what a business partner gives and gets; HubSpot draft only).
  2. If joining GlobalWelsh free: complete the profile, then post one heritage story (not an advert) every 4–6 weeks with a link to a free page. Reply to relevant Callouts.
  3. Wales International partner offer: that's a member discount, i.e. a price change, so it's a decision for the owner.
  4. Track it: tag every link utm_source=globalwelsh or utm_source=walesintl. Review after 90 days.
