# Komoot Highlights + a public route for a heritage walk (walker discovery, zero spend)

Skill date: 26 Oct 2026. Topic: getting a walking/heritage tour found by walkers inside route apps (komoot first; OS Maps and AllTrails as seconds).
Why it matters: walkers plan inside route apps, not on heritage websites. A public route plus community "Highlights" on the stops puts the walk where planners already look, before any paid listing.

## The evidence
- komoot newsroom, 20 Jan 2025 (read 26 Oct 2026): "over 40 million users"; its native ad format is Sponsored Collections, and "more than 4,300 Collections have already been promoted"; the partner awards were judged first by the community on click-through rate and interaction rate. Takeaway: komoot discovery runs on Collections (curated route sets) and Highlights, and editorial value is what earns clicks.
- komoot help, "Introducing Highlights" (komoot.com, read 26 Oct 2026): Highlights are community-recommended places or segments shown as red dots on the map; "points of interest" qualify; the best ones have "a descriptive name and clear photos" and "helpful insider tips"; you create one once you have visited the place yourself.
- komoot support (pages returned 403 to the box; search snippets only, not checked): Collections "can be created by komoot editors, partners, community groups, and komoot users"; "every partner can create and publish Collections for free as part of their Business Partner Profile"; one video states personal Collections need Premium (not checked).
- Pricing changes after the March 2025 Bending Spoons acquisition: new users charged to sync routes to a device, partly reverted Sep 2025 for a free region (dcrainmaker, Reddit snippets: not checked). Browsing public routes and Highlights stays free on the web (komoot.com home: "With a free komoot account, you can easily find, customize, and navigate" — snippet, not checked).
- Seconds: OS Maps routes can be created, shared and exported as GPX (App Store listing snippet, not checked); AllTrails reports 100 million members and offers custom routes (press page 403: not checked; custom-route paywall changes in 2025 per Reddit: not checked).

## The how-to
1. Walk it first (Highlights require a real visit). Record the GPS track on the day of a pilot walk.
2. Publish ONE public route: clear name ("Llandough heritage walk: church, cross, Roman villa site"), distance/time, start at a bus stop, accessibility notes, 3-6 photos you own.
3. Add Highlights only for publicly accessible points of interest, each with a factual one-line tip (what to look at, where to stand, opening of the church if known). No sales links in tips; tips are recommendations, not adverts (community norms; spam is reported, per r/komoot thread snippet, not checked).
4. Bundle route + Highlights into a Collection (free if the account type allows; check in-app; never buy a Sponsored Collection).
5. Put the same route on OS Maps (GPX) for UK walkers. Keep one master GPX so all copies match.
6. Measure monthly: route views/saves/completions on komoot, Highlight recommendations, and referral visits to the tour page.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn

The programme-office route facts (walking times, land-access status) are the source; anything marked not checked there stays out.
- Never place a Highlight on a private home or garden, and describe the house stop only as the site of a demolished house seen from the public road, with no family names, holdings or addresses.
