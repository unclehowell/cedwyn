Quora answers (English + es.quora.com) for niche Welsh local history

Skill harvested 26 Oct 2026. Topic: answering existing Quora questions on Welsh local history, Vale of Glamorgan farmhouses, Welsh Patagonia and land records, with a disclosed link to the evidence page or the free first edition. Different from the Reddit/forum file (communities with mods) and the AI-answer citations file (our own pages).

What the practitioner evidence shows

  • Luca (divbyzero.com, ex-AdEspresso marketer), "Quora Marketing" guide, 2023, read in full: a few answers written over several years earned 376,000 views, 1,000+ upvotes and "thousands of clicks every month"; answers keep sending traffic for years ("still receiving traffic from answers I wrote 3 years ago"). One of his most-viewed answers pitched a free tool and still sent meaningful traffic two years later. He recommends lead magnets such as e-books: give part of the material in the answer, offer the rest behind the link.
  • Same guide, question selection: check the question's view log, not the lifetime total. His example: 1,238 total views but under 50 views a week = not worth answering. Avoid questions that already have very many answers; ~30 answers can still be worth a try if yours is clearly better.
  • Same guide: Quora runs language sites in many countries, so the same work can be done in Spanish on es.quora.com.
  • Quora Help, affiliation policy (snippet only, not checked; page behind a bot check): "A user should disclose any relevant affiliations explicitly in an answer or in the credential that appears with the answer." Answers with links judged promotional get removed as spam (user reports, not checked).
  • No heritage-book case study with sales numbers found. Expect slow, compounding referral traffic, not launch spikes.

How to run it

  1. Account + credentials: a real profile; a topic credential such as "Researches the records of a lost Vale of Glamorgan farmhouse". The credential is where the affiliation is disclosed every time.
  2. Find questions (30 min): search Quora for: Vale of Glamorgan history, Llandough, Penarth history, Welsh farmhouses, Welsh land records, tithe maps, Welsh Patagonia/Chubut, and in Spanish: "galeses en Patagonia", "colonia galesa Chubut". Open each question's log/stats; keep ones with steady recent views and under ~30 answers.
  3. Answer shape: answer the question fully in the first 2-3 sentences; then one concrete record (date, document, archive) and an image (a map extract or cutting); then ONE link to the most specific page that goes deeper, with plain disclosure ("I help run the site that publishes these records").
  4. Rhythm: 2-3 answers a week, never the same paragraph twice, never a link in an answer that is not genuinely useful without it.
  5. Never: ask your own questions to answer them, use sock-puppet upvotes, or drop bare links.
  6. Measure: utm_source=quora&utm_medium=answer&utm_content=<question-slug> on every link; monthly look at Quora's views per answer and the site's Quora sessions.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Status: PROPOSAL. No Quora account exists yet (the project workspace lists Quora as "account needed"). The owner decides whether to open one; Tab drafts the answers in a file first and posts nothing until GO.
  • Target questions (examples to search for): "What are the oldest farmhouses in the Vale of Glamorgan?", "How can I trace the history of a Welsh farm?", "Where can I find old Welsh land surveys?", "¿Por qué emigraron los galeses a la Patagonia?".
  • Example answer core (English): "Estate surveys are often the earliest place a farm appears by name. For one lost Vale farmhouse, Tŷ Mawr, the 1824 David Stewart survey is the first record mention found; local newspapers from 1974 and an ITV News report from 1988 record that the family acquired it in 1667." Then one image and the evidence page link with UTM.
  • Example (Spanish): the same facts in Spanish, linking to the Spanish Cedwyn pages.
  • Lead-magnet link: for "how to research" questions, point to the free first edition on /ebooks/; for specific-place questions, point to the evidence page. No price talk in answers.
  • Report: answers posted, views and Quora sessions into the Sunday growth notes.
