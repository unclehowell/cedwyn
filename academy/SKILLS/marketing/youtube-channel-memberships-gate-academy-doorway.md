# YouTube channel memberships: gate, then a members level that feeds the Academy

Harvested 2026-10-31 (skills harvest run). Topic check before writing: 'channel membership', 'youtube membership' and 'join button' had 0 hits in INDEX.md. The YPP 500-subscriber threshold appears only in passing, inside other skills.

## The gate (primary sources)
- **How to qualify.** Memberships come with the **expanded YouTube Partner Programme**, which is available in the **United Kingdom** (YouTube Help 13429240, read). Apply on either route:
  - (a) **500 subscribers, plus 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, plus 3,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months**; or
  - (b) **500 subscribers, plus 3 public uploads in the last 90 days, plus 3 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days**.
  - Studio > Earn > "Get notified" emails you when you qualify.
- **Further rules** (YouTube Help 7636690, read): the channel must not be set as Made for Kids, must not have a significant number of ineligible videos ("Made for Kids and videos with music assertions"), and must follow the fan-funding policies.
- **Money** (YouTube Help 7491256 and youtube.com, snippets): creators keep **70% of membership revenue after applicable taxes and fees**. YouTube currently covers transaction costs, including card fees.
- **Levels and trials** (YouTube Help 7544492, read):
  - **Up to six levels** at different prices. Higher levels automatically include the perks of the levels below.
  - Perks can be badges, custom emoji and members-only videos, Shorts, live streams and posts.
  - Custom emoji slots grow with the number of members: 4 at 0 members, up to 54 at 5,000+.
  - **Membership trials:** YouTube automatically offers eligible viewers **one free month**, once per viewer across all of YouTube. Creators can opt out in Studio > Memberships.

## What practitioners report (numbers)
- **Rachana Ranade** (YouTube Blog, Muli Salem, membership PM, 16 Dec 2020, read): a chartered accountant teaching financial literacy. She made **discounts on her courses** a members perk, and memberships became "the majority of her YouTube revenue", **over $100,000**. This is the closest precedent to ours: an education channel using memberships as the doorway to paid courses.
- **Anna Yakimenko** (same post): offers merch discounts, member live streams and tailored advice, and her YouTube earnings **doubled**. The same post states creators earned **4x** more from memberships in 2020 than in 2019. That's YouTube's own figure, from 2020.
- **Agency benchmark** (AIR Media-Tech, air.io, snippet; their own client data, not checked): **0.5–3% of subscribers become members**, and **5–10% of members cancel each month**. A 2026 trend piece (milx.app, snippet) states to estimate from 0.5–2% of *monthly viewers*, not subscribers. Use the viewer base: it's the stricter one.
- **YouTube creators page** (snippet): "Betty" saw total members triple, and membership became 40% of her YouTube earnings. It's a vendor example, so take it lightly.

## Arithmetic before building anything
- **Monthly net** = members × level price × 0.70, before tax.
  - Example: 1,000 monthly viewers × 1% = 10 members at £2.99, which is about **£21/month**.
  - The same 10 people at a level that includes an Academy code are worth far more if even 1 of them upgrades to the £49.99/month Academy (our own Stripe, with our fees, not YouTube's).
- **Churn of 5–10% a month** means a members level needs fresh posts every week. Plan perks we already produce, such as Story Time episodes early or source scans with commentary, not new work streams.

## How to do it once eligible
1. **Start with one level.** Add a second only when 20+ members ask for more. Six levels is a ceiling, not a target.
2. **Use perks we already make:**
   - members-first Story Time episodes, released 48 h before public;
   - a monthly members-only post with one primary source and how to read it;
   - a members badge;
   - **a code or discount for the Academy** (the Rachana model). Check the channel-memberships policy before offering an off-YouTube perk; that check isn't done yet.
3. **Leave the free-month trial on at first.** It costs us nothing and fills the base. Look at trial-to-paid after 60 days.
4. **Make one clear ask** at the end of the Story Time episode ("Join" button + one line), not a pitch in every video.
5. **Members-only posts** go in the Posts tab. Members-only Shorts tend to read as teasers, so keep them short.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **GATE: not eligible now.** The channel had about 8 subscribers in the latest growth notes (youtube-backfill/growth notes), against the 500 needed. It's far from 3,000 watch hours or 3M Shorts views. **Nothing to switch on; zero spend; nothing changed.**
- **What to track meanwhile** (Sunday growth report, one line): subscribers / 500, public uploads in 90 days / 3 (already met by the daily Shorts), watch hours in 12 months / 3,000, Shorts views in 90 days / 3M. Whichever route gets closer is the one to push.
- **Design now, build later (PROPOSAL):** one level, price chosen by the owner (no price is set here). Perks: members-first Story Time, a monthly source post, and an **Academy access code or a first-month discount** delivered through the same ACD-code route the Academy worker is building. Memberships are then the low-price doorway and the £49.99/month Academy (questionnaire grades) the main product. The e-books at £4.99 stay on /ebooks/.
- **Watch-outs:**
  - Keep the channel **not Made for Kids**.
  - Avoid music assertions on Story Time beds: use our own or licence-clear audio. Claimed videos count as ineligible.
  - Public text follows the editorial rules (no owner name, banned words, "our case, prospective, not a finding" labels).
  - The AI likeness/voice intro needs YouTube's altered-content disclosure (existing skill).
