Wales official tourist guides (WOTGA Blue/Green Badge) as partners for the Llandough tour and the e-books

Harvested 2026-10-30 00:28Z. Checked first: 'wotga' 0, 'tourist guide' 0, 'cpd' 0 hits in INDEX.md. This is a different route from wales-travel-trade-gto-coach-dmc-net-rates.md, which covers the BUYERS (group organisers, coach firms, DMCs). This file covers the qualified GUIDES, who choose what goes into a South Wales itinerary and recommend reading to their clients.

What the sources say (read 2026-10-30)

  • Who they are. The Wales Official Tourist Guides Association (WOTGA, walesbestguides.com) was formed after devolution. Its Blue Badge is mapped to the European standard EN15565. It has about 50 working guides and no head office; enquiries go to the chair, secretary or treasurer. It now trains its own regional Green Badge guides (South East Wales, South West Wales, North Wales), with an all-Wales Blue Badge top-up to follow. (Source: FEG "10 things to know about WOTGA".)
  • They must collect CPD hours. CPD became compulsory "from 2020": at least 20 hours a year, with 40 expected. It is "well supported by courses and lectures led by Cadw, Visit Wales and the National Museum of Wales" (same FEG page). This is the opening: a well-sourced local-history talk or walk counts toward hours guides have to find anyway.
  • What they earn. WOTGA's guide fees for 2026 (walesbestguides.com, read in full; a guide, open to negotiation):
  • walking guide: half day (4 h, starting 09:00 or 13:00) £208; full day (up to 9 h) £343;
  • in another language: £245 half day, £391 full day;
  • driver-guide, half day: £489 for 1–3 passengers, £522 for 4–6, £558 for 7–8;
  • driver-guide, full day: £717 to £790;
  • multi-day: £811 a day;
  • bank holidays +50%, Christmas Day +100%.

A client paying £208 for a half day is a high-intent history visitor, and a £4.99–£9.99 e-book is a trivial add-on for them. - Visit Cardiff already curates them. visitcardiff.com lists 15 official guides, chosen with the association. Each entry shows the guide's interests (e.g. "History and Prehistory", "social history", "Countryside"), region, languages and whether they drive. That page is the shortlist to approach. - Why this route works. VoiceMap's case studies (docs.voicemap.me, read) show the same pattern for self-guided content: guides and podcasters whose own audience buys more than one product (Annie Sargent: half her listeners bought more than one of her five tours). Treat that as analogous evidence only, not checked for Wales.

The playbook

  1. Shortlist 5–6 guides from the Visit Cardiff list and WOTGA's find-a-guide filter. Pick those who list history or prehistory, social history or countryside and cover South East Wales. Prefer guides who speak Welsh or Spanish, since we have cy/es material.
  2. Offer CPD, not a sales pitch. One free 60–90 minute session (online, or a walk on public paths), sourced from the records, with a one-page handout and source list. Ask WOTGA whether it can be logged as CPD (not checked whether WOTGA accredits outside providers).
  3. Give guides something to use on the day. A printable 1-page "Llandough in 10 records" sheet with a QR code to /ebooks/?utm_source=guide&utm_campaign=<guide-slug>. Each guide gets their own campaign tag so sales show which guide sent them. Offer an affiliate code if Gumroad affiliates are available (see gumroad-discover-affiliates.md).
  4. Make it easy to include us. Give guides a list of 3–4 stops that are on public land, with a 2-minute talking point for each and the record it comes from.
  5. Run the numbers before paying for a pilot. A paid pilot half-day walk costs about £208 at WOTGA's guide rate. At ~£4.99 an e-book, net of fees, it is not paid back by e-book sales alone. Only run a paid pilot if it also produces content (photos, a short clip for social media, testimonials) or a booking channel for the guided-tour objective.
  6. Measure. Count UTM/QR visits and sales per guide each month. Drop guides with nothing after 90 days. Ask the ones who are sending sales for a quote we can use.

Rules that apply

  • Approaching a guide is outward contact. Draft as a HubSpot task only, and send only on the project lead's GO. No DMs to strangers.
  • Public handouts and talking points follow the editorial file:
  • never name the project lead;
  • none of the banned words;
  • no insignia;
  • no family holdings or addresses: route stops are village/public-path places only, never the farm or its house;
  • say "former manorial house" on the Wales side.
  • Zero spend unless the project lead approves a pilot.
