Local & Community History Month (May, Historical Association): register a free event, ride the yearly theme

Harvested 2026-10-28 (skills harvest). Topic: seasonal campaign / events / community. Index had 0 hits for "local history month".

What the sources show

  • What it is (Historical Association, read): May is Local and Community History Month across the UK. Aim: raise awareness of local history and get the community involved; typical activity = trips, library exhibitions, local lectures. Organisations register free and add events to the HA's searchable events database; the HA supplies a free poster template; details appear in The Historian from about April; contact [address kept in the project workspace] HA branches run speaker programmes and are a second route for a talk.
  • Themes (HA, "Local History Month 2026", published 30 Apr 2026, read): 2026 was linked to the National Year of Reading - "writers and local records" bringing a place to life (the HA explicitly states writers "don't need to be famous - just published"). 2027 is announced already: art, artists and local history, tied to the 300th anniversary of Thomas Gainsborough's birth (May 2027) - painters who used their local area (Lowry, Judith Ackland). Groups may also ignore the theme and just celebrate their local history.
  • Practitioner use (search snippets, not opened): Newport City Council (Wales) marked the month with a display for its 90th year; UK libraries post "two events for adults and one for children" each May; a US bookshop hosts society talks. These show the pattern (a display or talk, announced 2-4 weeks ahead) - no attendance or sales numbers were found; treat any uplift as unmeasured until we measure it ourselves.
  • Overlap check: free online talk listings (free-online-talk-listings-eventbrite-meetup.md), society talks (cowbridge-history-society-talk-route.md, civic-local-history-society-talks-penarth.md) and Event markup are already covered; this file adds the national May hook and its listing route only.

How to do it

  1. January-February: read the HA theme page; pick one free event that fits the theme (talk, online talk, library display, walk-and-talk). One event, not a programme.
  2. March: register on the HA events database (free account) and, if online, also list on the existing Eventbrite/LinkedIn routes; put Event structured data on the event page.
  3. April: fill the HA poster template; give copies to the venue/library/local shop; one email to the list and one post per channel, each with a UTM-tagged link to the event page.
  4. May: hold the event; collect sign-ups at the door/online (consent stated); the e-book is mentioned once at the end, never the subject of the talk.
  5. Within 7 days: follow-up email with the promised resource; record attendance, new sign-ups, tracked sales.
  6. Keep only if the event beats our cost (time) - kill rule below.

How we apply it to Cedwyn (proposal; needs the project lead's GO for any public event in the company's name)

  • May 2027 fits the art theme well: a free online talk or display "The farm at Llandough in maps, drawings and photographs" using our own public-domain/licensed images from the evidence catalogue (no family likenesses, no insignia, no accusations, never name the project lead, banned words apply).
  • Timeline: decision by 28 Feb 2027; HA registration and Eventbrite listing in March; poster + one email + one post per channel in April (UTM utm_campaign=lhm2027); talk in the week of 10-21 May 2027.
  • Pair with an existing HA branch or a Vale/Cardiff society (see the society talk files) rather than a standalone event, to borrow their audience.
  • Measure: registrations, attendees, Mailchimp sign-ups tagged lhm2027, tracked e-book sales in 14 days. Kill rule: under 10 registrations by 1 May 2027 -> run it as a recorded short video for the site instead, and skip 2028.
