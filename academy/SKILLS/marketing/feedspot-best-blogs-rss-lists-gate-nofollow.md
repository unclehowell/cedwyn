## Verdict
Low value, low cost. A listing gives a credibility line and a place in a feed reader; it gives no SEO link (nofollow) and near-zero referral traffic (5K lifetime views on a 28-entry national list; testimony of 0 visits). The real cost is privacy and spam: listing pages show the site's contact email and the database sells it.

## The skill (if we do it)
1. Only the free 'Submit Your Blog' form; never 'Sponsor this list', never a paid 'featured' upgrade, never buy the database.
2. Submit the feed, not a person: the Cedwyn Atom/RSS feed URL and a role inbox (a Tab mail address or a site address), never a personal Gmail or a family member's name.
3. Target lists where we genuinely fit: UK History blogs, Wales RSS feeds, Genealogy/Family history blogs. Posting frequency matters to their ranking, so only submit once the feed has a steady weekly cadence.
4. Measure honestly: referrers containing 'feedspot' in the beacon/Cloudflare logs for 30 days; record 0 as 0. Drop the channel if under 5 visits in 60 days.
5. Expect inbound pitches to the listed address ('guest post', 'sponsored post' offers) - ignore; no replies, no payment.
