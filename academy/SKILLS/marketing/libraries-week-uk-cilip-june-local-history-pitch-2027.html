Libraries Week (UK, CILIP, early June) as a pitch window for a local-history talk or library e-book

Skill harvested 2026-10-29. Verdict: 2026 week has passed (1-7 Jun); plan for June 2027, start the ask in March. Nothing to install. Companion to library-venue-talk-vale-libraries-booking.md (how to pitch/book) and library-elending-overdrive-libby-via-kobo.md (getting the e-books into library catalogues).

The facts

  • CILIP runs Libraries Week: Libraries Change Lives across all UK library sectors. Dates move: 2025 = Mon 2-Sun 8 June (theme: sustaining economic growth); 2026 = Mon 1-Sun 7 June (theme: literacy and reading, tied to the National Year of Reading). Older years ran in October (Sutton Libraries post: 9-14 Oct), so always re-check the year's dates.
  • Not the same as Green Libraries Week (CILIP, 21-28 Oct 2026, climate theme): poor fit for us.
  • What libraries do in the week (DCMS Libraries blog, CILIP post, 4 Jun 2025, read in full): host activities, share local-impact stories, invite policy-makers on visits; CILIP collects impact case studies on an interactive map (almost 200 stories by Jun 2025), open to every UK library.
  • Librarian testimony (library-venue-talk skill): individual unknown local authors are rarely given a slot; showcases, panels and series work better. So the pitch is a ready-made, free, local programme item that serves the year's theme, not "host my book".

How to do it

  1. January-February: find the year's dates and theme (CILIP news, #LibrariesWeek). Write a one-paragraph offer that maps directly to the theme (2026's "literacy and reading" -> a reading-aloud session of a 19th-century local record, bilingual).
  2. March (libraries plan the week ~2-3 months out; timing is our estimate, not checked): send the offer to the local service's events/local-studies lead as a HubSpot draft for the owner to send. Offer: free 45-minute talk or reading, a printed one-page record handout, a QR to the free evidence pages, and a library-only free e-book code for the week.
  3. Make it easy to say yes: no fee, our own slides, fits a 35-seat room, can join a local-authors showcase instead.
  4. During the week: post the session from our channels tagged with the library and #LibrariesWeek; never hard-sell inside the library session.
  5. After: offer the library a short impact note (attendance, questions asked) it can submit to CILIP's case-study map; that is their win, and it earns a repeat invitation.
  6. In parallel, check whether the e-books are borrowable through the library e-lending route; a talk + "borrow it free at your library" line is the cleanest library pitch.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Targets: Vale of Glamorgan Libraries (Penarth, Barry) and Cardiff Libraries local-studies, the libraries nearest Llandough. Draft goes to HubSpot only, on the project lead's GO; nothing sent by agents.
  • Content: a record-led, bilingual (cy/en) reading of a dated farm/parish record; handout with QR ?ref=librariesweek-2027 to a Cedwyn evidence page; /ebooks/ named once at the end.
  • Add PROPOSAL to the project workspace: "Libraries Week June 2027: free talk/reading offer to Vale + Cardiff libraries; re-check dates Jan 2027; draft by 1 Mar 2027."
  • Rules: public text never names the project lead; banned words; no accusations about named people; never the community council; zero spend.
