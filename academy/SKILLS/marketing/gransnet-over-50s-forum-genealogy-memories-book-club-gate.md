# Gransnet and older-reader forums: earn a place, don't post links

Skill for: reaching readers aged 50+ (the core local-history and family-history audience) on Gransnet, the over-50s sister site of Mumsnet. Covers how the forums treat links, the free book club route, and what older readers say about how they are marketed to. Written 31 Oct 2026, harvest run.

## What the sources say (read 31 Oct 2026 unless marked)

- **Links:** Gransnet's Netiquette page (read): "We have no problem with people posting the odd link to other sites that other posters might find useful or helpful. But we will delete anyone's attempts to 'spam' the boards with links, as a way of promoting their own site or product or blog, as it annoys our members." It sends would-be promoters to its paid advertising page instead.
- **Affiliate links:** Gransnet Terms of Use (read): "You may not post affiliate links without the prior permission of Gransnet." Gransnet may also reuse anything posted "for any purpose whatsoever, commercial or otherwise, without payment".
- **A member asking the same question** (Gransnet "Self-promotion" thread, 2013, read): "What are the rules about promoting one's own books/craft/cakes on gransnet?" Answer: "I think you have to ask HQ." Lesson: there is no free self-promotion lane. Ask the site team first, or just be a useful member.
- **Where history people are:** the forum section is **Genealogy/Memories** (`/forums/genealogy_memories/`). A member wrote in Jan 2022 (read): "My favourite hobby is Family History so mine is Genealogy/Memories but it doesn't get a lot of postings." So it is quiet, which means one good thread stands out, but don't expect volume. Typical threads (search results, not all opened): "Which genealogy site do you recommend?", "Is one Family History site any better than another?"
- **Book club** (Gransnet "Book club - how it works", read): "Each month, we choose a book, give you the chance to win a free copy, and get the author in for a webchat." Books are announced mid-month, and members get 3–4 weeks to read. Picks are "page-turning reads (both fiction and non-fiction)". There is no open submission form. Picks come from Gransnet/publishers, so this is a **gate**: only a pitch to the books editor, and only once a print or e-book edition is ready.
- **What over-50s say about being marketed to** (Gransnet + Mumsnet survey of **1,028** users aged over 50, read; date on page not checked): 78% agree advertising "misrepresents and under-represents my age group", and 81% say it misrepresents them. Headline: "nearly two-thirds of over 50s think advertisers are too young to understand them." An earlier Gransnet/Mumsnet survey (search snippet only, not opened): "Only 12% believe agencies creating ads really understand the 50+ age group."
- **Are they online?** Ofcom Adults' Media Use and Attitudes 2025 (search snippet of the PDF, not opened): "Three quarters of internet users aged 65+ use social media apps (75%)". The 2026 report (snippet, not opened): 6% of adults have no home internet, and of those, 66% are aged 75+. So most 65–74s can be reached online, but many 75+ readers need print, talks or a family member.
- **Scale:** Mumsnet's own media pack (Oct 2025, snippet) gives 8m UK unique users a month *across the portfolio*. There's no separate Gransnet figure; treat Gransnet as a minority of that (not checked).

## How to do it

1. **Be a member first.** Read Genealogy/Memories for a week. Answer 3–5 real questions (where to find Welsh tithe maps, how to read a census entry, Glamorgan Archives opening routines) with no link.
2. **One useful link, only when asked.** When a thread asks about Llandough, Penarth, Leckwith or Glamorgan farms, give the answer in the post. Then add at most one link to the specific free page (an evidence page or free sample), not the shop. Say plainly that it's your own project ("I run this site").
3. **Never post affiliate or shop links.** They are banned without permission and annoy members.
4. **Start one thread of your own** about something to share, not sell: e.g. "What did Llandough look like before the hospital? Photos and records from 1840–1950". Reply to everyone for 48 hours.
5. **Book club: gate.** Pitch the books editor only when (a) a finished e-book (ideally print too) exists, (b) the author can do a one-hour webchat, and (c) there are 5–10 free copies. Expect a no; it's publisher-led.
6. **Copy for this audience:** write as one adult to another. Use real dates and places. No "nostalgia" clichés, no stock photos of grey-haired models (the survey's 78%). Use plain, larger type on the landing page.
7. **Measure:** a `?utm_source=gransnet&utm_medium=forum` link and a Mailchimp tag `gransnet`. Judge after 30 days on sign-ups, not clicks. If there are fewer than 3 sign-ups, park it and keep answering occasionally.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn

- **Who posts:** there is no Gransnet login on file. Posting in a forum as the project lead is his choice, so this is a **proposal**. If he states GO, the first job is registering a project account (not his name) and answering questions with no links.
- **What to link:** only internal Cedwyn pages that are already public and neutral: the Llandough place pages, free sample chapters, the house-history guide if built. Never case-dispute pages, family holdings or anything naming living people.
- **Editorial rules apply on the forum too:** no banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken), no accusations about named people, no DMs to strangers.
- **Book club:** add to the e-book launch checklist as a gate item ("Gransnet book club pitch: only with print + webchat + 10 copies; the project lead's GO").
- **Offline twin:** the 75+ readers who aren't online are reached via U3A, WI, talks and care-home reminiscence packs (separate skill files). This skill is for the online 50–74s.
