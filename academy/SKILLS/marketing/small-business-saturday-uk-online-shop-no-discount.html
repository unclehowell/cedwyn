Small Business Saturday UK (Sat 5 Dec 2026): join in free as an online shop, with no discount

What it is. Small Business Saturday UK is a free, "grassroots, non-commercial" campaign run by Small Business Britain, with American Express as principal supporter. It falls on the first Saturday in December, which in 2026 is Saturday 5 December. On 28 Oct the site countdown showed 38 days to go. Online businesses are named as eligible: "family business, local shop, online business, wholesaler…" (smallbusinesssaturdayuk.com, read 28 Oct 2026). Black Friday (27 Nov) and Cyber Monday (30 Nov) come a week earlier, so this is a separate, later hook. It matches the Cedwyn story of a small Welsh family enterprise.

Evidence (read unless marked)

  • SBS UK home, About, Marketing Pack and Media pages (read 28 Oct 2026):
  • The campaign covers "5.7 million small businesses" and is in its 13th–14th UK year, with "billions spent". No current-year figure is published on the site.
  • Free 2026 Marketing Pack: logos dated and undated (JPEG/PNG/SVG), social graphics with suggested posts, a print-ready A3 poster PDF, brand guidelines and a one-page guide. Downloading needs a tick box for the pack's terms.
  • Hashtags #SmallBizSatUK and #SmallBusinessSaturday "trend throughout the day". Free "Out And About" town visits and a webinar series. Press contact: [address kept in the project workspace]
  • Nation's Favourite Businesses (formerly the #SmallBiz100):
  • The 2026 list is already published, so we can't join it this year.
  • In 2025, applications closed at midnight on 30 June (SBS Facebook snippet, not read). Plan for the 2027 window to open in spring.
  • Benefits named on the site: social and PR features, plus receptions at Downing Street, the House of Lords and the Treasury.
  • One past member wrote that it "raised our profile and exposure, expanded networks" (Facebook group snippet; no sales figure given).
  • Size of the day in the UK:
  • £717m spent with small businesses in 2016, up 15% on 2015 (Telegraph, 8 Dec 2016).
  • £748m in 2017 (Retail Gazette, 6 Dec 2017).
  • Both figures come via Wikipedia's citations; the original articles were not opened. These are consumer-survey estimates, not till data.
  • US for comparison (not the UK):
  • American Express reports $100bn in consumer-reported small-business spending from 2021 to 2025 (Amex newsroom headline).
  • Forbes, 3 Dec 2025 (snippet, page blocked): spending fell 18% in 2025, while online small-business shoppers rose from 54M to 63M.
  • What owners actually report (r/smallbusiness "How did everyone do on Small Business Saturday?", 2022, read via RSS):
  • "I did horribly… one sale all day."
  • "I've never had a good day on small business Saturday in 7 years."
  • "Always the best day of the year (retail gift store) but this year we were down 27%."
  • "Best day in over a month (out-of-season retail)."
  • From a new business: "many don't know of me."
  • A support provider's summary: restaurants did well, retail was mixed, services were business as usual.
  • Lesson: the day brings no strangers to an unknown shop. It works as a reason to remind your own audience, and as a hook for local roundups that already list small firms.

How to run it (small online shop)

  1. By 13 Nov: download the free pack once and keep the undated logo for future years. Choose one offer, which must not be a price cut. The Black Friday skill's stance (B), a bonus rather than a discount, applies here too. Under the UK DMCC Act, any "limited time" wording must be true.
  2. Week of 30 Nov: draft 3 posts using the pack graphic plus our own photo: - Thursday: "who we are" - Saturday morning: the offer and a coded link - Saturday evening: thank-you Add one line to the newsletter only if it's going out anyway; don't send a special email.
  3. On the day: post with both hashtags. Like and reply to the official accounts' posts; don't tag them in every post. Reshare one or two other Welsh small firms, because reciprocity is the culture of the day.
  4. Local roundups: Welsh public bodies post on the day: - Business Wales on X, 5 Dec 2025 - Development Bank of Wales on Facebook, 29 Nov 2025 - TrawsCymru's "hidden gems to discover this Small Business Saturday" page Add these to the press list, and treat any pitch as press.
  5. Measure: use ?utm_source=smallbizsat&utm_campaign=sbs2026 on every link. If the day brings fewer than 5 coded visits and no sale, write "skip in 2027 except the free logo post".
  6. Spring 2027: decide whether to apply to Nation's Favourite Businesses. This needs the project lead's GO, because it features the business owner publicly.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Proposal only, zero spend, no outreach without the project lead's press GO. Post on our own channels only (Facebook Great House Farm II, Instagram @cedwynestate, YouTube Posts) on Sat 5 Dec 2026. Use the undated pack logo and a heritage photo, and include one Welsh line (Helo Blod can check it free).
  • Offer: a bonus, not a discount, at listed prices. For example, buy any e-book that weekend and get the preview PDF pack of the second book. Run it Sat 5 to Sun 6 Dec only and say exactly that.
  • Print copy: if the local-shop consignment from the consignment skill is live, Saturday is that shop's day. Draft one post naming the shop and tagging it with its permission.
  • Public text: never name the project lead. No banned words, nothing about family holdings, Cedwyn links only (/ebooks/?utm_source=smallbizsat&utm_campaign=sbs2026).
  • Pitches: possible TrawsCymru and Business Wales roundup pitches go into HubSpot as drafts, held until press GO.
  • Calendar: draft posts in week 48. The auto-social flywheel posts at most one item per channel per 4 h, so queue Saturday's two posts at least 4 h apart.
