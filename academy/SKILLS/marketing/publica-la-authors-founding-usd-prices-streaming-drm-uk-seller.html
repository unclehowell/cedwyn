publica.la Authors / publica.now terms for a UK seller: USD prices (not ARS), monthly USD payouts, streaming-only DRM, Founding Author 20% + $0.25

Skills harvest, 31 Oct 2026. Follows bajalibros-runs-on-publica-la-marketplace-route-no-direct-signup.md, whose sign-up terms (UK eligibility, payouts, ARS pricing) were blocked by a bot check. The public Authors page (publica.la/es/solutions/authors) and its FAQ answer most of them, so no browser was needed.

What the primary source states (read 31 Oct 2026)

  • Plan. "Plan Founding Author", free for life: "$0 alta de servicio, $0 suscripción mensual … Solo cobramos cuando vendes."
  • Founding authors pay 20% + USD 0.25 per sale, locked for life. The standard rate is 25% + USD 0.50, and "cuando abramos al público, los términos pueden cambiar — los tuyos quedan fijos".
  • Access is by application ("Solicitar Acceso Anticipado"), for "un grupo reducido de autores".
  • Conflict to resolve: the earlier skill quoted publica.now's KDP comparison page at 15% + USD 0.30. That page now returns 403 from the box. Treat 20% + $0.25 as the current written offer and 15% + $0.30 as not checked. On a USD 6.50 sale, 20% + $0.25 leaves $4.95; 15% + $0.30 would leave $5.23.
  • Countries. "Más de 50 países en América Latina, América del Norte, Europa y más." publica.la handles invoicing, taxes and payment methods ("facturación, impuestos y pagos en todo el mundo").
  • The UK is not named as a seller country, so UK seller eligibility is still not checked. It is the first question to put in the application.
  • Currency. "Los precios se manejan en USD." There is no ARS or EUR list price for authors. The earlier lead "price La visita in ARS on publica.la" fails on this plan.
  • Payouts. "Pagos mensuales en USD por transferencia bancaria, PayPal o métodos de pago locales según tu país," with a real-time transaction panel.
  • ISBN is optional: "si lo tienes, lo registramos".
  • Rights. No exclusivity and no rights capture. KDP and your own site are fine alongside it.
  • Delivery is streaming-only with proprietary DRM: "El lector puede leer; no puede descargar ni compartir el archivo … No hay EPUB ni PDF circulando." Readers use the web reader or apps (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows), with sync and offline reading.
  • Readers who expect an EPUB for Kindle or Kobo cannot get one here.
  • Network. Titles are auto-listed on ebooksdepository.com ("555.000+ títulos · 50+ librerías") and sold "a través de" the network's stores, Bajalibros among them. Store pick-up is still the store's choice (earlier skill).
  • Marketing tools included: discount coupons, gift codes for reviewers or press, a free first chapter as a lead magnet, UTM tracking, a QR code "listo para imprimir en eventos … ferias", and the buyer's email and country ("Tienes el email y país de cada lector") with nurture sequences.

How to do it (any small publisher)

  1. Apply while the founding rate is open. The 20% + $0.25 rate is locked "al ingresar"; the public rate later is 25% + $0.50.
  2. In the application, ask three things in writing: Can a UK-registered seller join? Can a country be excluded from sale? Can you set a fixed local price per country, or is it USD only?
  3. Set the USD price as the anchor, and test what an Argentine buyer pays at checkout before promoting it there.
  4. Use the QR code and coupon for events (fairs, talks). Use UTM links so each channel's sales show up.
  5. Keep your DRM-free EPUB sales on your own shop. Use publica.la for network reach and buyer data.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Fit: partial. The buyer data is strong, but three conflicts need answers before the project lead's GO: 1. Spain fixed price. A USD list price shows Spanish buyers a euro amount that moves with the exchange rate. That cuts against one fixed €5.99 under Ley 10/2007 (our reading, not legal advice). Unless Spain can be excluded, or a fixed EUR price set, keep publica.la off for Spain. Our own shop and Kobo hold that price. 2. ARS. USD-only means no ARS shelf price, so the Argentine price stays at the USD equivalent. Argentina's card surcharge on foreign USD purchases still applies (see argentina-card-surcharges-30pct-ebook-checkout-es.md). That favours Bajalibros' own peso shelf, if it ever picks the title up, over a direct publica.la link. 3. Streaming-only DRM conflicts with our open-EPUB promise and with device read-aloud. Use it as an extra channel, never the main one.
  • Proposal (the project lead's GO, owner-only sign-up, no account opened by us):
  • Apply for Founding Author with La visita (es) only, at USD 6.50: about €5.99 equivalent, $4.95 net at 20% + $0.25.
  • Ask the three questions above in the application text. We can draft it as a HubSpot task for the project lead to send.
  • If the UK is not eligible, or Spain cannot be excluded, close this lead.
  • If live: use its QR code and a coupon for the April–May 2027 fair window (see dia-del-libro-filba-2027-spain-guest-promo-plan-welsh-patagonia.md). Tag every link utm_source=publica&utm_campaign=filba27.
  • Not checked: UK eligibility, country exclusion, the 15% vs 20% conflict, and payout fees on PayPal or bank transfer.
