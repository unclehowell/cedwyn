Heneb HER (Archwilio) and Coflein: contribution and reuse rules

Harvested 29 Oct 2026. A discovery-and-credibility route, not an advert. The Welsh HER record is what councils, planners, researchers and AI crawlers read about a site. A sourced, credited addition there is a lasting, citable presence. Our farm record is GGAT02038s (Llandough; Heneb added source-file PDFs after its 14 Oct 2026 email: findings F055).

Facts (primary sources, read 29 Oct 2026)

  • Every Archwilio record page ends with "Do you have information to add to the record? Contribute here!" It links to the Heneb HER Information Submission Form (Jotform) with the record's PRN pre-filled: heneb.jotform.com. Seen on record GAT48603 at 22:25Z.
  • The form's fields, in order:
  • funded project? (yes routes you to email [address kept in the project workspace]);
  • new site or existing site;
  • PRN, site name, county (Vale of Glamorgan is listed), whether you're suggesting a new location;
  • NGR and how you took it, plus a what3words square;
  • Site Type (one term, from heritagedata.org scheme 10);
  • Description;
  • Evidence (one term, scheme 19, e.g. "Documentary Evidence");
  • Period (POST MEDIEVAL / MODERN …);
  • Condition (DESTROYED, NEAR DESTROYED, N/A for place-name evidence …), condition description, threats;
  • file upload (photos, drawings, sources);
  • name, email and consent, under the Data Submission Agreement.
  • Guidance for the Submission of Data to Welsh HERs (v2, 2022), §2.1: "individuals, volunteers and community groups can submit information for individual historic assets informally". Larger volumes should use a database structure (template on request). "Private researchers" count as non-funded contributors.
  • §6: "The HERs will not transfer any rights and all rights remain with the original rights holder(s)". A third party whose data is imported "will be acknowledged in the core HER record". So credit to Cedwyn (as a source title, never a person) is the norm.
  • Reuse cuts the other way. Each Archwilio page states that the data is "for information and research only and is not for use as part of a commercial project". Publication "in printed or multimedia form, or to compile resources for commercial use" needs prior written permission. Required credit line: "Derived from information held by the Heneb HER Charitable Trust copyright."
  • Coflein (RCAHMW, NMRW): 120,000+ sites and ~125,000 images. Images are Crown copyright (licensed by RCAHMW, "sometimes for a fee") or third-party copyright, and can't be used commercially without permission. Coflein has no public contribution form that we found (coflein.gov.uk timed out from the box). The route is RCAHMW's enquiry service or Donation/Deposit (rcahmw.gov.uk menu). RCAHMW has a tender out for a "Next Generation Heritage Platform", so contribution features may change.
  • Practitioner evidence: the guidance itself names incidental public submissions as a standing source. A count of informal submissions per year has not been checked.

How to do it

  1. Prepare one fact per submission, with a primary citation: catalogue reference, date, page. Example: a dated documentary reference that names the site, not an interpretation.
  2. Fill the form offline in a HubSpot draft task: - PRN GGAT02038s, existing site; - Evidence = "Documentary Evidence"; - Condition as the record already has it; - Description written in neutral catalogue voice; - "Any additional information": the citation, plus "summary page: " as a reference, not a promotion.
  3. Contributor identity: the project lead's name and email (it's his research and his open Heneb thread). This is an outward, irreversible step, so it waits for the project lead's GO. Never submit mid-FOI with Heneb unless he states so.
  4. After acceptance: cite "Heneb HER PRN GGAT02038s" on the Cedwyn evidence page (a reference only, no copying), and log the date in the change-watch.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • E-books are commercial. Do NOT reproduce Archwilio record text or Coflein images in paid e-books or the shop without written permission. Cite the PRN and catalogue refs only. Check this in the next e-book build (the v0.0.2 PDF on sale): a PROPOSAL for the e-book pipeline is to grep for verbatim HER text and Coflein images.
  • Free wiki/research pages: short quotes with the exact credit line are covered by "information and research". Keep them short, and add the credit line wherever HER data appears.
  • Change-watch: Heneb updated GGAT02038s after 14 Oct. Add the record URL plus a monthly check of its "Sources" list (an absence watch for the 1938 conveyance and the 1926 Cardiff Corporation reference).
  • Permission request for e-book reuse (one paragraph to [address kept in the project workspace], naming the PRN and the exact text) = PROPOSAL: HubSpot draft, the project lead's GO.
  • Kill rule: no acceptance or reply within 12 weeks of a submission means stop submitting and keep citing only.
