Subscription shelves for the Spanish edition: Kobo Plus (yes, one toggle) vs Nextory (no direct route for us)

Harvested 31 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). Kobo Plus's 60% value-per-minute rate is already one line in free-epub-storefronts-kobo-itch-books2read.md, and Everand has everand-subscription-reads-distributor-gate.md. Nextory was "not checked" in both. This file is the subscription decision for La visita (Spanish), with the territory list, the toggle, the payout arithmetic and Nextory's own eligibility rule. Proposals only; nothing was listed.

What the sources show (read 31 Oct 2026)

Kobo Plus (KWL Help Centre articles, read through its public feed): - Territories ("What is Kobo Plus?", updated 29 Oct 2026) include Argentina, Mexico, Spain, Chile, Colombia, Peru, the United Kingdom, the US and about 25 more. - Prices (Kobo's launch news, per its search snippet): Kobo Plus Read costs MXN 140 a month in Mexico and USD 6.99 in Argentina. - Pay ("How does Kobo Plus pay?"): each month Kobo pools the subscription revenue and divides it by all minutes read, giving a value per minute. KWL authors get 60% of it. Every minute counts, re-reads included, even under 10% of the book. Reports come in blocks of 300 minutes, in a separate "SUBS" report. - Kobo's own worked example (illustrative, not a real rate): 0.0027 per minute, so a 3-hour book earns about 0.29. - No exclusivity is needed. The switch is per book: "Maximize your Sales" > slide the Kobo Plus toggle. By default it covers every eligible country, including future ones; "Edit Eligible Countries" narrows it ("How do I add or remove my books", updated 18 Sep 2026). - Effect on single-copy sales (Kobo's own claim, not independent): opted-in authors' à la carte sales "have remained steady (and for some, have increased)", reads are extra income, and backlist titles get a boost. Opting out "will in no way impede" à la carte sales. - Practitioner data (PublishDrive, Kinga Jentetics, 2 Apr 2026, from its 2026 market report): - Across its network in 2025: Kobo Plus royalties +158% in value, +98% in volume. Average royalty per Kobo Plus e-book read was $2.20, against $3.10 per Kobo retail sale. - Kobo growth Mexico +85%, Spain +68%, UK +46%. - Caveat: PublishDrive averages are dominated by full-length genre fiction. A short guide earns far less per read (see below).

Nextory (its support article "Publishing books on Nextory as a small publisher or author", updated 28 Oct 2026, read): - "We do not currently support direct uploads or agreements with smaller or independent publishers with only a few titles." - The route is a large distribution partner: Publit or Publizon (both Nordic). - StreetLib's blog (27 Feb 2025) lists Nextory among its Pro-plan channels (Pro is $99, a spend). It states Nextory pays a fixed amount per hour read, with a strong presence in the Nordics, Spain and France. - Bonnier Books agreed to buy Nextory (announced 1 Sep 2026; LinkedIn snippet, not read in full). - Context from the existing Spanish-stores file (De Marque 2025): subscriptions are 5.6% of the Spanish-language e-book market. Bookwire 2026 states small publishers "find their readers" on Nextory and Kobo Plus without losing unit sales.

The arithmetic for a short guide (our estimate, labelled)

  • La visita's EPUB is about 160 KB. Assume about 60 minutes of reading (not measured).
  • At Kobo's example rate: 60 × 0.0027 × 0.6 ≈ 0.10 per complete read. A La visita sale at the €5–7.99 band earns roughly €3.50–5.60 at 70%.
  • So one sale equals roughly 35–55 full reads. Kobo Plus is reach and discovery for us, not income. It's worth having because it's free and non-exclusive.

How to run it

  1. Price La visita on KWL above the 70% floors first. MXN 39.99 and €1.99 are KWL's thresholds (from "What will my earnings be?"). KWL has no ARS storefront: Argentine buyers pay in USD, so the USD 2.99 floor applies.
  2. At upload, leave the Kobo Plus toggle on, all territories. Only narrow it if a store data point later shows cannibalisation.
  3. After 60 days, compare the SUBS report's minutes by country (map view) with à la carte sales by country. If Kobo Plus minutes in AR/MX are greater than zero while sales there are zero, Kobo Plus is finding readers our checkout doesn't reach. That's the result to keep.
  4. Nextory: don't chase it now. There's no direct door, and the routes are a Nordic aggregator contract or a $99 StreetLib Pro plan. Revisit only if the owner approves StreetLib Pro for other reasons, and then Nextory comes free with it.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Proposal 1 (no spend; owner's go to list): when La visita goes on KWL (already proposed in the Spanish-stores file), keep Kobo Plus on in all territories. This puts it in front of Kobo Plus readers in Argentina (Chubut families), Mexico and Spain at no cost and without exclusivity.
  • Proposal 2: apply the same toggle to the English titles when they go on KWL. UK Kobo Plus readers are local-history browsers, which suits the backlist boost Kobo describes.
  • Proposal 3: add a "Kobo Plus minutes by country" row to the Sunday growth report once there's a SUBS report.
  • Not proposed: Nextory (no route without spend or a Nordic contract), and Kindle Unlimited (KDP Select exclusivity would pull the book off Kobo, Google and our own shop; see the Mexico file).
  • Status: nothing listed, nothing sent.
