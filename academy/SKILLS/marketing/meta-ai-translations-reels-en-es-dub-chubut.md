## What it is (primary source: Meta Newsroom, read in full 30 Oct 2026)
- It's free. Meta AI translates the speech in a Reel, dubs it in a clone of the creator's own voice and tone, and can lip-sync the mouth movement if you switch that on.
- Languages:
  - Oct 2025: English, Spanish, Hindi and Portuguese, in both directions.
  - Jan 2026: added Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Kannada.
  - 14 Jul 2026: Instagram added French, German, Italian, Japanese and Korean.
  - 4 Jun 2026: Facebook "soon" adds Arabic, Indonesian, French, Thai and Vietnamese.
  - **Welsh is not supported, as a source or a target.**
- Who can use it:
  - Facebook: creators with a Page or professional mode **and at least 1,000 followers**.
  - Instagram: **all public accounts**, in countries where Meta AI is available.
- Viewers see the translated version in their preferred language, labelled "Translated with Meta AI". Each viewer can choose "Don't translate" in the three-dot menu, under Audio & language.
- Note: Meta's Facebook help page (business/help/1090261056577580) and the creators blog both returned an error page when fetched today. The eligibility line above comes from Meta Newsroom plus the help-page search snippet.

## How to switch it on (Metricool news piece 17 Jul 2026, read; it describes Meta's own flow)
1. On the Reel's share screen, tap "Translate your voice with Meta AI". Turn on translations, and lip-sync if you want it.
2. **Turn on "Review translation"** before sharing. You get a notification (or a Professional Dashboard item) to approve or reject the dub. Rejecting it doesn't touch the original Reel.
3. You can turn translations off or remove them at any time after posting.
4. Insights split views by language, so you can see Spanish views on their own. This comes from Meta's 2024 launch, as reported by Medium/CherryZhou and a creator post. Not checked in Meta's own docs.
5. **Facebook only:** you can upload up to **20 of your own dubbed audio tracks** per Reel. It's in Meta Business Suite > the Reel > "Closed captions and translations" > "Upload your own translated audio tracks", and works before or after publishing. Meta announced this in Aug 2025 (Nairametrics, Digital Information World, Metricool). Not checked whether Meta accepts Welsh (cy) as a language label.

## Best practice (Meta's tips, as Metricool relays them)
- The speaker faces the camera with lips visible and nothing covering the mouth.
- Facebook handles **two speakers at most**. Don't let them talk over each other.
- Keep music and background noise low under speech. Loud beds hurt accuracy.
- Lip-sync can look "slightly off" even when the audio is right, so review before it goes live.
- Be consistent. A new-language audience builds over weeks.

## The numbers
- No independent study gives lift figures. Meta publishes none for creators, and the vendor pieces only repeat Meta's assertions.
- So treat it as a free experiment and measure it ourselves with the per-language Insights split.

## Risks
- Machine translation can change neutral wording, for example turning a careful "the record shows" into a Spanish verb that reads as an accusation. **Review is mandatory for us.**
- Some viewers dislike auto-dubs and turn them off (there are creator complaint threads). Whether a viewer sees the original is their choice.
- The "Translated with Meta AI" label covers Meta's dub. It doesn't replace our own disclosure for AI-made scenes (see the lip-sync disclosure skill).

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
1. **Instagram @cedwynestate is eligible now** (public account; the UK and Argentina aren't on the reported exclusion list of South Korea, Australia, Turkey, South Africa, Nigeria, Texas and Illinois). **Facebook (Great House Farm II) needs 1,000 followers.** Check the follower count before planning a Facebook run.
2. Use it only on Reels with **clear English speech, one or two voices, low music**: a short spoken explainer about a record, or a talking clip. It does nothing for:
   - music-and-text Reels, like the 15-pin map video (no speech; on-screen text isn't translated);
   - documentary scenes in Welsh, since Welsh isn't supported.
3. **Documentary scenes:** we already render cy/en/es tracks.
   - Facebook: once eligible, upload our own **es** track (and cy if Meta accepts the label) through "Upload your own translated audio tracks".
   - Instagram: post the clip with Welsh audio and burned-in or caption subtitles. Don't use Meta's dub, because the AI dub would start from the wrong language.
4. **Review gate before approving any dub:** read the Spanish transcript or listen through, and check it for:
   - Spanish equivalents of our banned words: "afirma/asegura" (assertions), "según" (per), "presunto/supuesto" (reported), "fraude", "robado";
   - Llandough Farm vs Great House Farm being kept apart;
   - no family holdings or addresses;
   - a correct parcel name, or "parcela no identificada".
   If it fails, reject the dub. The original Reel stays as it is.
5. **Measure:** log Spanish views per translated Reel in the project workspace After 4 translated Reels, compare the Spanish share against the Chubut-facing Spanish captions already in use, and keep or drop it on that number.
6. No Spanish link change is needed. Purchases still route through /ebooks/ from the bio link. A Spanish caption line pointing to the Spanish shop page is a PROPOSAL only.
7. Zero spend, nothing sent. Approving each dub is a click inside the posting flow. The posting worker does it only when it owns uploads (it doesn't today; another worker holds video uploads).
