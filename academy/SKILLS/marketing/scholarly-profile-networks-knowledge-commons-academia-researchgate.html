Scholarly profile networks for independent heritage research: Knowledge Commons yes, Academia.edu as a mirror, ResearchGate no

Harvested 2026-10-30 09:15Z (skills harvest). Topic: SEO/AI visibility + an academic audience for free research reports. Complements zenodo-doi-scholar-argentine-repositories.md (DOIs, Google Scholar); this file covers the profile networks where researchers browse and follow.

What the evidence shows (read 30 Oct 2026 unless marked)

  • Academia.edu citation study is vendor-funded. Niyazov et al., PLOS ONE 2016 (31,216 papers, matching + regression): a paper in a median impact-factor journal posted to Academia.edu got 16% more citations after 1 year, 51% after 3, 69% after 5, versus similar papers not online; 58% more than papers posted only to personal/department pages after 5 years; a paper posted ONLY to Academia.edu got 15% fewer citations than one on another site in year 1, then +19% (3 y) and +35% (5 y). Competing-interests statement (read): Academia.edu paid its employees, contractors and a consultancy (Polynumeral) to do the study; 7 of 9 authors were Academia.edu staff, incl. the CEO. Treat as the platform's own number.
  • The general open-access citation advantage is mixed. Langham-Putrow, Bakker & Riegelman, PLOS ONE 2021 systematic review (134 studies from 5,744 items, protocol on OSF): 64 (47.8%) found an advantage, 37 (27.6%) found none, 32 (23.9%) only in subsets, 1 inconclusive. So: being findable helps more often than not, but there is no guaranteed lift.
  • Knowledge Commons is open to anyone, free, non-profit. About page (read): "open to anyone, regardless of field, language, institutional affiliation, or form of employment"; "full-featured accounts are and will remain free for individual users"; hosted by Michigan State University (MESH Research) since Nov 2020; renamed from Humanities Commons in May 2024. About.hcommons.org (read): every work in its open repository (KC Works) is issued a DOI; groups and a WordPress site are included.
  • ResearchGate is effectively closed to us. Help centre (read): sign-up asks for an institutional email; without one, accounts are considered "in rare circumstances" on request with evidence of recent peer-reviewed publications or commercial R&D. An independent heritage project does not meet that bar; do not try workarounds.
  • Academia.edu sign-up is open but heavily upsold (homepage snippet: 65M papers). Practitioner testimony from a non-academic user (academia.edu, snippet only, page not read): posting content regularly "does seem to be effective in generating interest". No numbers we can stand on beyond that.

How-to

  1. One canonical DOI per work. If a report already has a Zenodo DOI, do NOT deposit it again in KC Works (two DOIs split citations and confuse Google Scholar). Use KC for works that have no DOI yet, or link the Zenodo DOI from the KC profile instead.
  2. Profile first, then works. KC profile: plain description of the research area (place history, archives, land records), link to the evidence catalogue home only, interests tagged (Welsh history, local history, archives, genealogy). Join 2-3 relevant groups (history, digital humanities, archives) and read before posting.
  3. Deposit the free research reports, not the paid e-books. Each deposit: title as on the site, abstract (answer-first, 120-200 words), keywords, CC BY licence only if the text is ours to license, the canonical site URL in "related works". Never upload third-party scans or private correspondence.
  4. Measure: KC Works shows views/downloads per work; record monthly in the growth sheet next to Zenodo views and referral visits from hcommons.org / academia.edu (Cloudflare referrers). Kill rule: if a mirror sends fewer than 5 referral visits in 90 days, stop adding to it (leave existing items).
  5. AI visibility side-effect: repository records are clean, structured, crawlable metadata pages that answer engines cite; keep titles and abstracts consistent with the site so the entity matches.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Status: PROPOSAL. Opening a KC or Academia.edu account means an account in a real person's name (individual accounts only), which is the project lead's decision; nothing created, nothing posted.
  • Candidates: the free research PDFs (missing-records register summary, place-names notes, the chronology), never the £4.99 e-books.
  • Public text rules apply to abstracts: never name the project lead, the requester or private people; banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken); no family holdings or addresses; author shown as the editorial team where the platform allows, otherwise as the account holder the project lead approves.
  • If approved: KC first (free, non-profit, DOI); Academia.edu mirror only for works already DOI'd; ResearchGate skipped.
