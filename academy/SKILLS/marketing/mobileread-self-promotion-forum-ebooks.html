MobileRead self-promotion forum: rules, realistic reach, and a post for Cedwyn's e-books

Harvested 2026-10-28 02:12Z. Status for Cedwyn: PROPOSAL (needs a brand account; nothing posted).

What it is

MobileRead (mobileread.com) is a long-running, international e-reader forum (Kindle/Kobo/calibre crowd). Self-promotion is allowed in exactly ONE place: "Self-Promotions by Authors and Publishers" (forum f=226, 5,368 threads on 28 Oct 2026). Everywhere else it is deleted and can get the account banned.

The rules (moderators' "Promotion Posting Guidelines", thread t=90052, last edited 17 Oct 2025, read)

  • Self-promotion ONLY in f=226. Outside it: deleted, possible ban.
  • Authors: ONE thread per e-book. Duplicate threads = spam.
  • Publishers (books not written by the poster): ONE thread for the whole house, titled " - Featured Titles ", and ONE post in it per 7 days.
  • Post a small blurb plus discreet links to where it can be bought. The book must be on sale at the time of posting.
  • External sites named in full. NO URL shorteners (bit.ly, TinyURL). Hyperlinked text is fine if the link underneath is not shortened. NO affiliate links.
  • Cover image max 300 px high x 600 px wide; one image and one video, post #1 only (oversize images are "often the culprit" in silent deletions).
  • A purely promotional bump at most once every 7 days. Replies to members' questions: any time.
  • No adult content. Violating posts are deleted without a PM.
  • Spirit: "We welcome promoting authors and publishers who participate in the forum discussions... Using the community solely as a promotion platform ... could be easily misunderstood as spam."
  • Also stickies: "The Shameless Promotion and Endless Plug-Your-Novel Thread" (3,173 replies, 5.38M views) and "Shamelessly (& Endlessly) Promote Your Blog/Website" (1,834 replies, 1.09M views).

Realistic reach (measured from the forum listing, 28 Oct 2026 ~02:12Z)

Newest 25 non-sticky threads: most had 0 replies and 250-1,000 views in their first 1-7 days (e.g. 255, 271, 322, 402, 602, 703, 733, 831, 878, 891, 948, 953, 985). Outliers: a thread a moderator kept bumping reached 46,759 views / 20 replies; a few tools 1,500-3,000. Views include bots and guests, so treat them as an upper bound on humans. Practitioner testimony (2011 thread "Isn't this Author Self-Promotions forum a total waste of time?", t=139341, read): one member "bought several books because of posts in this forum"; an author's view that "posts across multiple forums ... soon add up". Old and anecdotal: no sales figures were posted. Expect a handful of clicks, not a sales spike.

How to do it well (smallest effective version)

  1. Register a brand account (never a personal name). Fill the profile and a plain signature per general guidelines.
  2. Before posting, take part for real: 3-5 genuine replies in reading/history threads over a week (the guidelines reward participation, and it avoids the spam reflex).
  3. ONE thread per book, or because one house publishes several titles, ONE " - Featured Titles 2026" thread. Title format seen working: Title - one-line genre hook (e.g. "Last Shift in Flint Hollow (Laura Beckett Mysteries #1) - Appalachian mystery").
  4. Post #1: cover <=300x600, 80-120 word blurb, format/DRM line, price, full un-shortened buy URL carrying UTM tags (?utm_source=mobileread&utm_medium=forum&utm_campaign=<book>) - UTM is allowed because it is a full URL, not a shortener.
  5. Bump only with news (new edition, free chapter, format added), at most every 7 days; answer every question quickly.
  6. Measure: Cloudflare/analytics referrals from mobileread.com + Stripe/Gumroad sales tagged mobileread, 14 days after posting. Stop if <20 visits.

Fit notes

  • This audience prizes DRM-free and EPUB. A PDF-only offer is a weaker fit; state the format honestly. If an EPUB edition exists or is cheap to produce, lead with it.
  • Free/discount days get the most attention here ("[FREE]" titles reached ~700-900 views). A free sample chapter is the natural hook.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • PROPOSAL only, logged for the sales-fix run / BIZDEV plan: one publisher thread "Cedwyn - Featured Titles 2026" for the Great House Farm e-books, posted from a Cedwyn brand account (never the project lead's name), linking to cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/ebooks/ with UTM tags, cover 300 px high, prices exactly as listed (v0.0.1 free, v0.0.2 £4.99, bundle £19.96; no "was"/saving assertions).
  • Blurb must pass the editorial rules: no banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken), no insignia, no accusations about named people, nothing about family holdings.
  • Lead with the free v0.0.1 edition: it suits the forum's free-day pattern and feeds the Mailchimp list.
  • Account creation needs a brand email ([address kept in the project workspace] exists) - check that the sales-fix rules allow a new public account before acting; a no-DMs rule applies (never PM members).
  • Success bar: >=20 referral visits or 1 sale within 14 days; otherwise one news bump at day 7, then leave it.
