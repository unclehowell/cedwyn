Instagram reach collapse: triage before changing content (Account Status, originality, watermarks, 5 hashtags)

Harvested 2026-10-27 22:30Z for @cedwynestate (tonight's sales run: last 6 posts reached 1-36 accounts, ~5x down on 24-25 Oct).

What the platform itself states (primary, read 27 Oct 2026)

  • Account Status is the first check, not the last. Professional accounts can see in Settings > Account > Account Status whether their content "may be eligible to be recommended to people who don't follow you" (Explore, Search, Suggested Accounts, Reels, Feed recommendations), see a sample of the post or profile element (photo, bio) that breaks the Recommendations Guidelines, edit/delete it, and request a review (help.instagram.com, read).
  • Whole-account switch: "If your account is not eligible to be recommended, none of your content will be recommended regardless..." Followers still see posts. Repeated guideline breaks, or something in the profile photo/bio, can make the entire account ineligible "for a period of time" (Creators blog, recommendations-eligibility tips, read). With few followers, ineligible = reach of 1-36, which matches tonight's numbers.
  • Guideline categories named by Meta: violence (e.g. people fighting), regulated products, sexually suggestive. For a history archive the risk is old images of violence/executions/injury, or graphic court material - check any post that shows these.
  • Originality (Creators blog, 30 Apr 2024, read): every eligible piece of content is first shown to a small non-follower test audience, then widened in steps if it performs. "Eligible" = original, within Community Guidelines, no visible watermarks, within Recommendation Guidelines. When two identical items exist, only the original is recommended; copies get a label linking to the original; aggregators that repeatedly share unoriginal content they didn't enhance are removed from recommendations. Material edits (new voiceover, meme, reaction) are not treated as copies.
  • Hashtags: platform-enforced maximum of 5 per post/Reel since Dec 2025 (Later guide, upd. 21 Apr 2026, read; Instagram's own announcement post seen as snippet only). Hashtags go in the caption, not comments, to be found in Search (Creators blog, read).
  • Insights: Accounts Reached splits followers vs non-followers. Non-follower reach near 0 while follower reach holds = recommendation problem, not content quality.

Practitioner testimony (weaker, flagged)

  • Reddit r/Instagram (2026 thread, snippet only, not read in full): account "suddenly not eligible to be recommended to non-followers", which also disabled Trial Reels. Trial Reels being greyed out is a cheap tell.
  • Reddit r/InstagramMarketing (snippet): "delete and repost" to test - we do not do this (copies of our own post can be matched as duplicates; and it wipes any engagement).
  • Agency posts (Wheeler, Hootsuite 2026; snippets) list first-30-minute engagement - not checked.

Triage, in order (stop at the first hit)

  1. Account Status (app only, human or browser session): Eligible / Not eligible; note the sample post/profile element it names. If Not eligible: edit or remove the named item, request review, log date. Do nothing else for 7 days except normal posting - changing everything at once hides which fix worked.
  2. Insights split: last 6 posts, followers vs non-followers reached. Non-followers ~0 on all = step 1 or 3; mixed = content.
  3. Originality/watermark sweep: any image carrying another site's watermark or logo (archive stamps, stock watermarks, TikTok/YouTube marks on re-used clips), any clip identical to one already posted by us or by an archive account, any quote card that is the same template + same image as an earlier post. Replace with our own crop/redraw, add our own narration/voice to re-used clips.
  4. Duplicate cadence: the same picture or video posted to IG twice (e.g. once as carousel, once as Reel) - the second is a copy; pick one.
  5. Caption check: <=5 hashtags, keyword in caption, no link-bait text. Links in captions are not clickable on IG anyway; the URL text is fine but doesn't drive clicks (bio link / Stories link sticker do - see instagram-bio-link-conversion-no-link-yet.md).
  6. Only then: format (Reels vs carousel), posting time, hooks (instagram-reels-carousels-heritage.md, instagram-new-account-90-days-trial-reels-gate.md).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Checked tonight: the project workspace caption uses exactly 5 hashtags (#TyMawr #Llandough #WelshHistory #Heritage #Wales) and puts them in the caption - hashtag count is not the cause of the drop. Keep at 5; never add more.
  • Not checked (needs the app): Account Status for @cedwynestate. This is the single highest-value check; the API does not expose it. Route: a browser session on the saved Instagram login if one exists, otherwise it is an item for the PROBLEMS queue (the project workspace), not a message to the project lead.
  • Likely suspects to sweep (from the archive-heavy feed): archival photos carrying an archive's watermark/stamp; the quote-card template repeated with near-identical layout; any Short re-posted from YouTube with a visible YouTube mark; court/violence imagery. Fix by re-cropping, re-typesetting, adding narration.
  • Log: add a row to the project workspace per triage (date, eligible Y/N, non-follower reach of last 6, fix applied). Re-read reach 7 days after a fix, not sooner.
  • Editorial rules unchanged: no banned words, never name the project lead, no insignia; captions link only to cedwyn pages.
