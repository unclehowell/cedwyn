Print-on-demand paperback for talks and gifts (KDP Print maths, PROPOSAL for the project lead)

Read 27 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). Our likeliest buyers (local history societies, U3A, WI, Probus, Rotary audiences, Christmas gift buyers) handle paper. Every talk-circuit skill in this folder ends at a QR code to an e-book; a paperback on the table and on Amazon is the format many of those people actually buy. Print-on-demand means no stock, no upfront print run.

The numbers (KDP help pages, read 27 Oct 2026)

  • Royalty (Paperback Royalty, G201834330): Amazon.co.uk pays 60% of list price at GBP 7.99 or above, 50% at GBP 7.98 or below; printing cost is then subtracted. Expanded Distribution (shops, libraries) pays 40% minus printing.
  • Printing cost (G201834340), Amazon.co.uk, black ink, regular trim (up to 6.12 x 9 in):
  • 24-110 pages: GBP 1.93 flat per book (GBP 2.15 large trim).
  • 110-828 pages: GBP 0.85 + GBP 0.010 per page (large trim GBP 0.85 + 0.012).
  • Premium colour costs far more (GBP 0.85 + 0.0435/page) - keep interiors black and white, colour only on the cover.
  • Formula: royalty = (rate x list price) - printing cost. Max list price GBP 250.
  • Author copies are sold at printing cost plus shipping (KDP help "How much do proof or author copies cost?" - page index read, figures not checked); that is the stock for the talk table.

Worked examples for our page counts (regular trim, amazon.co.uk, 60% band)

| Book (pages v0.02) | Print cost | List | Royalty per sale | | Ty Mawr Deaths (69) | 1.93 | 7.99 | 0.60x7.99-1.93 = 2.86 | | Ty Mawr Deaths (69) | 1.93 | 9.99 | 5.99-1.93 = 4.06 | | Projects dossier (187) | 0.85+1.87 = 2.72 | 12.99 | 7.79-2.72 = 5.07 | | Main casebook (221) | 0.85+2.21 = 3.06 | 12.99 | 7.79-3.06 = 4.73 | | Dartboard (404) | 0.85+4.04 = 4.89 | 16.99 | 10.19-4.89 = 5.30 | (Page counts are e-book pages; a 6x9 print layout will differ - re-run the KDP calculator on the real PDF.) At the talk table, a copy bought at author-copy cost (about the print cost plus postage) and sold for GBP 10 clears roughly GBP 6-7 each.

How to do it (zero spend; KDP charges nothing to publish)

  1. Pick ONE title first: the shortest with the widest appeal (Ty Mawr Deaths). 6 x 9 in, black interior, matte cover.
  2. Interior PDF: reuse the e-book source; set trim 6x9, margins per KDP's template, embed fonts, images at 300 dpi greyscale. Cover: KDP Cover Creator or a full-wrap PDF sized by KDP's cover calculator (spine width depends on page count).
  3. ISBN: KDP's free ISBN is fine for Amazon; it lists KDP as imprint. A legal deposit copy of a UK print book is due to the British Library and on request to the National Library of Wales (legal-deposit-ebooks-uk-web-archive-nomination.md).
  4. Order a proof, check it physically, then publish. Link the paperback and e-book editions so they share one Amazon page.
  5. Order 10-20 author copies for talks only after the first booked talk.
  6. Sell it on Cedwyn /ebooks/ as "Paperback - on Amazon" beside the direct e-book (direct e-book stays the primary, higher-margin route), and as the gift option in the Christmas gift path (ebook-gifting-christmas-gift-path-guide-email.md).
  7. Track: KDP reports (paperback sales by marketplace), talk-table sales in a simple log, and whether e-book sales dip (cannibalisation) or rise.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • This is a NEW public channel and a new product listing, so it is a PROPOSAL: append to the project workspace; nothing is created on KDP without the project lead's GO, and it needs his Amazon/KDP account (we hold no KDP login).
  • Titles on Amazon are public: the main casebook's current title contains a banned public word, so a print title must be reworded (e.g. "Concealment and Government Involvement: the Great House Farm records") and the author line must not be the project lead's name unless he chooses it; the cover, blurb and keywords follow the public text rules (no insignia, no accusations about named people, no family holdings or addresses).
  • Paperback price must stay at or above GBP 7.99 to keep the 60% band.
