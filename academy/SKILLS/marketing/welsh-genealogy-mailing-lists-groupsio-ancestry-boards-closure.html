Welsh genealogy mailing lists (groups.io) and the Ancestry message-board shutdown, Oct 2026

Harvested 30 Oct 2026 (skills harvest, a worker). Topic: zero-budget first real visitors from family/local-history communities that still talk by email. Read live today; numbers are what the pages showed at 16:10Z.

What is true now (primary sources, read)

  • Ancestry message boards are closing to new posts. Banner on ancestry.co.uk and ancestry.com pages: "This page will soon be archived. Users will no longer be able to post on these message boards, but we will preserve them so they can continue to be perused and explored." First reported on Wales-Gen 27 Oct 2026 (message 1130), same banner quoted on the Cheshire list. Exact shutdown date: not checked (no Ancestry announcement found).
  • Ancestry's replacement is a new community site, ancestrycommunity.com ("Ask The Community", events, support hub), toured by Genealogy TV on 16 Oct 2026. List members say it "won't help with information and contacts on the closed boards" (Wales-Gen msg 1135, 29 Oct).
  • RootsWeb mailing lists stopped sending on 2 Mar 2020 and their archives went to the Internet Archive in late 2023 (RootsWeb support page, snippet; the page itself answers 403 to us). Most county lists re-formed on groups.io.

Why it matters for a tiny site

  • Mailing-list readers are the opposite of drive-by social traffic: they read every message in a digest, click to records, and reply. A 348-member county list is small, but it is exactly the Glamorgan family-history audience, and Ancestry's board closure is pushing displaced posters toward lists like these right now.
  • What gets replies on these lists (observed in the RSS): a specific question or a specific find (a record, a probate fee change, a TNA project). Promotional posts are not what gets answered; nothing in either feed is a shop link.

Playbook

  1. Monitor first (free, no posting). Pull /rss for GLAMORGAN and Wales-Gen once a day. Flag any message mentioning Llandough, Leckwith, Penarth, Cogan, Tŷ Mawr / Great House, Cydfin, Williams of Llandough, or the 1987 case. Each hit is a person already looking for what we hold.
  2. Mine the archived Ancestry boards (read-only, still browsable) for Glamorgan / Llandough / Williams threads. Old posters asked questions there that our evidence pages answer. Record them in a lead list; do not cold-contact.
  3. Answer, don't announce. If the project lead gives the GO to join under his own name, one reply per relevant question: the answer in the email body, the record reference, one link to the specific evidence page (not /ebooks/). Members follow the link when the answer is good. One "introduce yourself" post on joining, as the lists' own new members do.
  4. Never post the same text to several lists (the people overlap; it reads as spam). Never put prices in a list message.
  5. Measure: give each list its own short redirect (e.g. /r/glam-list) to the page answered, and count real-phone/desktop clicks the same way the sales check does (strip link-checker bots).

Rules that bind us (Cedwyn)

  • Outreach is on HOLD and the auto-social rule is "never post to Reddit/communities, never DM". So joining or posting to these lists is the project lead's call and the project lead's own account; we prepare drafts only. No DMs to members.
  • Public text: neutral, no banned words, never name the project lead; Llandough Farm is not Great House Farm; nothing about family holdings or addresses.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Now (no GO needed, read-only): add the two RSS feeds to the online gap finder / email-ingest watch so Llandough mentions surface as leads; one line per hit in the project workspace
  • Proposal for the project lead (one line): "Want me to draft a short introduction for the Glamorgan family-history mailing list (348 members) and answers to any Llandough questions that come up there? You'd post them from your own email."
  • Page fit: the pages most likely to answer list questions are the evidence catalogue entries and the names page ("Enwau: the names of Tŷ Mawr"), so those, not the shop, are the link targets. The e-book link already sits on those pages.
  • Expected size: honest guess, single-digit visitors per good answer; but they are the right visitors, and today we have about 25 real visitors a day.
