# Welsh-language and learner podcasts: guest spots via Y Pod

Harvested 30 Oct 2026. Topic: PR and audio guesting for a Welsh heritage e-book. Before this, 'podlediad', 'Y Pod', 'Clera' and 'Pod Cymraeg' had 0 hits in the skills folder; podcasts came up only in general terms.

## The evidence
- **Y Pod (ypod.cymru, read).** A directory of Welsh-language podcasts, with an app on Android and as a web app, an "Ychwanegu eich podlediad" (add your podcast) form, a newsletter, a learner category ("Addas i ddysgwyr") and the Gwobrau Podlediadau Cymraeg (Welsh podcast awards).
  - **Shows on the homepage today:** history (Hanes Mawr Cymru), farming (Amser mewn Amaeth, episode #133), culture (Clera, celebrating its 10th year in October 2026), Siarad Siop, Beti a'i Phobol.
  - **Learner shows:** Y Newyddion i Ddysgwyr, Pigion: Uchafbwyntiau Radio Cymru i Ddysgwyr, Learn Welsh with Siân Davies, Cymraeg Bob Dydd, Pod Cylchgrawn Cip.
- **Y Pod's own history (Clwstwr case study, read).** The founder counted 38 Welsh-language podcasts in 2018. Promoting them on one social account helped their number "almost double in 2019". Clwstwr then funded a listening hub with £31,500. A later blog snippet states there are "over 130" (not checked).
- **Learners (Learn Welsh news, 17 Mar 2026, read).** 20,445 people completed National Centre courses in 2024–25 (+12% on the year, +61% since 2017–18). 4,110 of them were aged 16–24, up 56%. The Centre's resource library tags podcasts as a category, including Pigion y Dysgwyr and Pont (snippet).
- **What works in podcast publicity (Jane Friedman, 1 Jul 2026, US Book Show panel, read).**
  - "The niche shows outperform the larger shows" (Cobello).
  - Shows with around 10,000 followers can be the best partners because their audiences reply in the comments (Davidson).
  - Start outreach 4–6 months before a launch, and ask hosts to hold the episode until launch week.
  - Listen to the show first and engage before you pitch; don't blast the same pitch to everyone.
  - A host in the comments added that they book guests who will post the interview on their own site with a link back, and who will share it.

## The playbook
1. **Shortlist from Y Pod, not from Apple or Spotify charts.** Those charts can't filter by language. Pick 5 shows in three groups: history (Hanes Mawr Cymru), rural or farming (Amser mewn Amaeth), and learner or culture (Pigion, Clera, Pod Cylchgrawn Cip). Listen to two episodes of each and note what the host likes.
2. **Pitch an angle, not the book.** For example: "how a farm's records survive (and don't) across 150 years, told for a Welsh audience". Offer a learner-friendly 5-minute version for the learner shows. Lead with a primary record and an image the host can post.
3. **Give before asking.** Follow the show and leave one genuine review or comment first. Add a show-notes page on Cedwyn (`/press/audio/`) carrying the episode embed and a link back to the show. This is the reciprocity hosts say they want.
4. **Timing.** Hosts book months ahead. A St David's Day 2027 slot means pitching in November or December 2026; an e-book launch means pitching 4–6 months before it.
5. **Have audio ready.** Provide a 30-second sample of the Welsh speaker, 3 suggested questions, a short bilingual bio with no personal names, and one consistent pronunciation of place names (Llandochau, Tŷ Mawr).
6. **Measure.** Use a per-show link such as `utm_source=<show>&utm_medium=podcast&utm_campaign=cy-audio` on the show-notes page. Our own rule: if a show brings fewer than 15 visits within 30 days of the episode, don't repeat it. Also count email signups from the show-notes page.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Spokesperson.** A guest spot needs a fluent Welsh speaker who is willing to talk. Who that is, is a decision for the owner (PROPOSAL). Public text never names the owner, and the guest is introduced by role only.
- **Pitches.** Two HubSpot drafts on the owner's GO: Hanes Mawr Cymru (history angle) and Amser mewn Amaeth (one Vale farm's records and land-use history). Pitches use neutral wording about the 1987 case, never call the family "tenant farmers", and never use the banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken).
- **Learner shows.** Pair the pitch with the Sylfaen pack "Diwrnod yn Llandochau" (heritage-learner-packs skill) as a free download in the show notes.
- **Our own podcast.** If the documentary scenes ever get an audio-only Welsh edition, list it on Y Pod through "add your podcast" (free). That is a PROPOSAL, because a feed needs hosting.
- **No paid podcast placement** and no Y Pod ads ("Hysbysebu" is a paid page): zero spend.
