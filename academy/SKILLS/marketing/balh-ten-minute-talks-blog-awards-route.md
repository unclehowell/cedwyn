## What it is (read today)
The British Association for Local History (BALH) is the national charity for local history in Great Britain. Three of its doors cost nothing and need no membership:

1. **Ten-Minute Talks** (balh.org.uk, read in full). "Record a presentation of about 10 minutes... using PowerPoint, or any other method you prefer. Send us the finished video." BALH publishes it on its YouTube channel and the Ten-Minute Talks page. Its Digital Team offers help with preparing and recording. Contact: [address kept in the project workspace] (subject "Ten-Minute Talk Enquiry"). A Facebook post from BALH (snippet): "We're always looking for new stories of local history research!"
2. **BALH blog** guest posts. Recent posts are by outside researchers (Ben Robinson, 30 Oct 2026; Tom Trafford, 12 Oct 2026, read on the awards page). The guidelines page ("Would you like to contribute to the BALH Blog?", 2020) showed a bot check; only its search snippet was seen: "Choose a topic relating to local history". Not checked in full.
3. **Annual awards** (balh.org.uk, read in full):
   - Research and publication certificates go to "a published article presenting sound original research", which "must appear in a journal, newsletter or similar publication produced by a local voluntary body" and be sent to the Reviews Editor (Dr Heather Falvey, postal address on the page). Everything sent to her is considered automatically. **We are not a voluntary body, so a self-published e-book does not qualify. An article printed in a society journal or newsletter would.**
   - Newsletter award: for a society's own members' newsletter. Not us.
   - Outstanding Individual Contributions: anyone may nominate, with two 300-500-word references, between 1 July and 31 December. Up to six a year. Not a self-promotion route.

## How to do it (once a GO is given)
1. Pick one tight, record-led story that stands on its own in 10 minutes, e.g. "What the 1841 tithe apportionment shows at Great House, Llandough". Use one record per slide and the source line on screen.
2. Build 8-10 slides in the site's dark style and record a voiceover in PowerPoint (BALH's own guide: balh.org.uk). Keep it under 11 minutes. Print the notes, as BALH asks.
3. End slide: one line plus the site URL (cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/learn/ or /research/), and no sales pitch. BALH is a charity audience.
4. Email the Digital Team with a 3-line pitch and the file link, as a HubSpot draft for review. Nothing is sent without a GO.
5. Alongside it, offer a 600-900-word blog post on the same record, or keep it for later. Read the guidelines page in full first (open it in a browser; requests hit a bot check).
6. For the article certificate: when a society journal (Morgannwg, a Glamorgan FHS journal, a Penarth society newsletter) prints one of our articles, ask its editor to send the issue to the BALH Reviews Editor. It is then considered automatically.
