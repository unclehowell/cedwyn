Museum-guide apps: Smartify free plan + Bloomberg Connects via a partner museum

What it is: two free arts-and-culture apps that hold self-guided audio and map tours. Smartify (1,200+ cultural organisations) lets a small organisation publish one tour for free. Bloomberg Connects (1,500+ partners) is free too, but "with rare exceptions" it takes only non-profit or civic organisations. For a commercial heritage project, then, the route in is a partner museum's guide, not a guide of our own.

Facts read (30 Oct 2026)

  • Smartify pricing page (read):
  • Free plan: one tour, a venue page and a map.
  • Starter £1,800/yr: 3 audio tours, up to 500 objects, games and quizzes.
  • Branded £3,500/yr: custom web app, AI translations, 7 tours.
  • Premium £9,500/yr.
  • Smartify free-plan page (read): the free plan includes multilingual uploads (sign language and audio-described too), map integration, and dynamic QR codes with built-in analytics that "update the link anytime, no need to reprint signage". When you're ready, tours can be "turn[ed] into paid content".
  • Bloomberg Connects For Partners (read):
  • free for institutions and visitors;
  • best fits named: museums, gardens, historic houses and heritage sites, with a physical space that is open to the public;
  • it strongly encourages audio and supplies training and audio kits;
  • its marketing team builds an onsite promotion plan with the partner;
  • non-profit or civic organisations only, with rare exceptions;
  • first step: an interest form, then an info session.
  • Museum of Cardiff is already a partner (cardiffmuseum.com, read):
  • its guide is in Welsh, English and 50+ other languages;
  • BSL tours with Welsh and English subtitles;
  • it can be viewed in a browser with no download.
  • Contact: [address kept in the project workspace]
  • Practitioner testimony (AIM Bulletin interview, republished on the Bloomberg Connects blog, read):
  • Ryan Ellis (Gurkha Museum): the app's reporting shows which content is popular. A volunteer's talk on the Brunei revolt was "number one on the app for six months". Bloomberg's team gives training on wording, photos and marketing.
  • Tom Short (Dulwich Picture Gallery): "no need to put every video you have ever made on there". Use it for take-home extra content "to keep them in your ecosystem". Auto-translate shows which nationalities visit.
  • Not checked:
  • whether Smartify's free plan accepts a commercial, non-venue heritage project (its page states "small organisations");
  • a "1 million users" figure for Bloomberg Connects, which appears only in a third party's Facebook post.

How to do it (steps)

  1. Smartify free tour (own route). - Apply for the free plan as a historic place. Upload one 6–10 stop tour of the public, street-visible history: place-names, the Iron Age to Romano-British landscape, documented events. Stay on public land and use no private addresses. - Make it bilingual (Welsh first, then English), and add the map. - Generate a dynamic QR code and put it on cards, e-book back pages and stalls. The QR analytics show scans by placement.
  2. Bloomberg Connects (partner route). Don't apply as Cedwyn. Offer a partner museum's guide (Museum of Cardiff, or a local-studies service) a free, ready-made 2–3 minute audio stop, with images we hold rights to. Their guide team decides; we supply the content plus a credit line pointing to Cedwyn. A HubSpot draft only, and the project lead sends it.
  3. Measure: QR scans per placement (Smartify dashboard); visits to /ebooks/ from links tagged with ?utm_source=smartify (or bloomberg).
  4. Keep it short, per Dulwich: one strong tour, with the deeper material held back as the paid e-book.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • This week (zero spend):
  • Draft the Smartify tour script from the existing free documentary Scene 2 and the timeline: 8 stops, Welsh plus English audio, already rendered or TTS-free narration from our own clips.
  • Free tier only; never the £1,800 Starter without the project lead's GO.
  • Archive images: follow the archive-image permission skill. Use our own reconstructions and photos only; don't put NLW or Glamorgan scans on a third-party app without a licence for it.
  • The QR code: link it to /documentary/ (free scenes), then the /ebooks/ strip. It's the same printed code for every stall, card and bookmark, and it can be repointed later without a reprint.
  • Museum of Cardiff pitch: write it as a HubSpot draft task (hive format, the project lead's sign-off), offering one free audio stop about Llandough/Leckwith. Never send it. Respect the editorial rules (no banned words, no names of private people).
  • Success bar:
  • 50+ QR scans in the first month;
  • at least 1 tagged /ebooks/ visit per 20 scans;
  • otherwise drop it after 60 days.
