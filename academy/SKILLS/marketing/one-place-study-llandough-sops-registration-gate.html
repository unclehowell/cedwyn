One-place study registration: Llandough-juxta-Penarth (Society for One-Place Studies)

Date: 2026-10-29 · Status: GATE. Membership is paid (£10/yr), so nothing happens without the project lead's GO. Zero spend until he states go.

What it is

A one-place study (OPS) is family history and local history for one real place: a parish, village, street or single building. The person running it sets the boundaries. The Society for One-Place Studies (one-place-studies.org) keeps a public Registered Studies directory. Each study gets its own page with these fields: Country, Region, Website, Contact, Photo credit, Study description, Timeframe, Population, Social media links, In-depth report. Findmypast's own guide to one-place studies points readers to that directory as the place for examples (findmypast.com).

Facts read 29 Oct 2026 (society's own pages)

  • Cost: membership is £10 a year (about US$15.50). It's free for students under 21. Members register studies free, through the Members' Area ("Study Registration") (/join/).
  • PayPal payments auto-renew every year. Bank transfer and cheque payments don't (/membership/).
  • Directory size: about 458 "One-Place Study" entries on /all-studies/. Wales has about 17.
  • Glamorgan has 3: Barry, Llangiwg and Llwydcoed. Cardiff has 1: Ton-yr-ywen Estate, Birchgrove.
  • No Llandough, Penarth, Leckwith or Cogan study is registered. The place is open.
  • Example of how thin some entries are: the Llwydcoed page has no website, no population figure and no social links. A one-line description is enough to register.
  • Events:
  • One-Place Wednesday Live: an online member meet-up.
  • Annual Conference and AGM on Zoom, Sat 21 Nov 2026, theme "Food".
  • The society invites anyone "interested in speaking at a future event" to get in touch.
  • It contributes talks to the Society of Genealogists' All About That Place (7–16 Oct 2026). That route is already covered in heritage-talks-circuit-aatp-branches.md.
  • Practitioner model: the Sticklepath OPS (sticklepathhistory.family/about) runs as a WordPress site.
  • Its explicit call: "contact the project … to add your stories, photos or research … collaborative project".
  • It points to Janet Few's OPS course (Pharos) and her book Ten Steps to a One-Place Study.
  • That is the shape the society recommends: open-ended, a community record, inviting contributions.

What the number tells us

This is a small, specialist audience: hundreds of studies worldwide and 17 in Wales. It won't bring traffic on its own. What it is worth: 1. A topical listing in the one directory one-placers and Findmypast readers browse. It carries a Website field. 2. A credible frame: "one-place study of Llandough-juxta-Penarth, 1841–1939". People with Llandough ancestors look for exactly that. 3. A way into member webinars and the speaker route. 4. Being "the Llandough study". When someone searches the place, descendants who hold photos or letters can find a contact point. That serves the research as much as the shop.

How to do it (when GO'd)

  1. Boundaries and timeframe: the ecclesiastical parish of Llandough-juxta-Penarth (Llandochau Fach), 1841–1939 (from the tithe to the 1939 Register). Great House Farm is one household within it, not the subject.
  2. Study page home: a Cedwyn page, e.g. /research/llandough-one-place-study/. It should hold: - a household-by-census table (1841–1921, built from free FreeCEN/FreeBMD lines); - a sources list; - a "share a memory or photo" form (consent wording from reader-memories-call-ugc-consent-local-history.md). - No /ebooks/ strip above the fold. The shop footer only.
  3. Register: pay the £10 (the project lead's GO and the project lead's card; Tab has no card on file). Then use Members' Area → Study Registration: - description of 2 sentences or fewer, neutral and dated; - Website = the Cedwyn study page with ?utm_source=one-place-studies&utm_medium=directory&utm_campaign=ops-llandough; - Contact = the [address kept in the project workspace] address, never the project lead's name.
  4. Add an In-Depth Study Report once the census table covers 4 or more censuses. Members can attach one to the study page.
  5. Members' Interests: add the surnames Williams (Llandough) and Rees (Llandough Farm).
  6. After 3 months: offer one "shortie" talk to the society's events contact ("1871 census: telling two Llandough farms apart"). Free records only, no selling in the talk.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Fit: strong. Tonight's 1871 FreeCEN entry that separates Great House Farm (John Williams, 110 acres) from Llandough Farm (Thomas Rees, 160 acres) is classic one-place work. Our parish-wide census reading is already happening.
  • Gate: the £10/yr membership is a spend, and the registration is outward (a public listing). Both need the project lead's GO. Propose it in QUEUE-after-framing.md as one line.
  • Rules:
  • Public text never names the project lead.
  • Banned words list applies; write "not checked".
  • Nothing about family holdings or addresses.
  • Living people never listed (family-tree-page-living-privacy-proof-standard.md).
  • No accusations about named people in the study description.
  • Measure: directory visits by UTM, monthly. Contributions received through the form. Kill rule: under 10 visits and 0 contributions in 6 months means don't renew.
  • Not checked: the registration form's own fields and rules. They sit behind the members' login; /studies/ states the study registration page is in the Members' Area.
