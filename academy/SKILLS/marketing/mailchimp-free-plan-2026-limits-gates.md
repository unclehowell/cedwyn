# Mailchimp Free plan in 2026: what still works at 250 contacts, and the gates before paying

Date: 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 21:23). Type: email ops / zero-spend. Status for Cedwyn: read-only plan check now; any plan change or new journey = PROPOSAL.

## The limits (Mailchimp help page, read 26 Oct 2026)
- Free: **250 contacts, 500 sends a month, 250 sends a day**, 1 audience, 1 seat, limited templates, basic reporting, landing pages and forms.
- Over 250 contacts: **sending (live and test) is put on hold** until you upgrade or cut the total; forms keep collecting.
- Not on Free (they are listed under paid tiers): **scheduled emails, A/B testing, automation flows** (Essentials: 'single email automations' and 'flows with up to 4 steps'; Standard: expanded flows, advanced segmentation). Whether Free keeps a one-email welcome automation: **not checked** (not listed on the help page; one review site writes 'no automation flows').
- Send caps on paid tiers: Essentials 10x contacts a month, Standard 12x.

## History and hidden counts (EmailOctopus, a competitor, Jan-Apr 2026; read)
- Jan 2026 cut: contacts 500 -> 250, sends 1,000 -> 500, new 250/day cap; existing free accounts moved on **17 Feb 2026** (Mailchimp notice quoted on Reddit, snippet). In 2019 Free was 2,000 contacts / 12,000 sends.
- Since Apr 2024 **unsubscribed contacts count** towards the limit unless archived; the **same address in two audiences counts twice**.
- Conflict to note: EmailOctopus writes Essentials is single-step only; Mailchimp's own page lists flows up to 4 steps. Trust the Mailchimp page; re-check before buying.

## Playbook for a tiny list on Free
1. Monthly hygiene: archive unsubscribed and cleaned contacts (Audience > All contacts > filter > Archive) so they stop counting.
2. One audience only; use **tags** (source, language en/cy/es, buyer) instead of extra audiences.
3. Budget sends: list size x sends per month must stay under 500 (eg 100 contacts = max 5 campaigns a month; 250 contacts = 2). Keep 10% spare for tests.
4. No scheduling on Free: send at the chosen time by hand (see email-send-time-frequency-tiny-list.md) or plan a send window in the task list.
5. Welcome: if no automation, the signup form's **final welcome email** (form settings) carries the free chapter link; check it is on and edited.
6. No A/B tests on Free; at our size tests are underpowered anyway (ab-testing-small-lists.md).
7. Upgrade gate (owner decision, spend): only when 2 of 3 are true: list > 200, a multi-email welcome is proven to sell (manual sends converting), or monthly sends hit 400. Before paying, compare at least two other providers' free tiers (figures not checked here).
