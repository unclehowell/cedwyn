Instagram search SEO: profile, captions, 5 hashtags, and showing up on Google

Harvested 2026-10-26 (skills harvest 19:52). Fits: Instagram growth + SEO. Not covered by instagram-reels-carousels-heritage.md (formats/signals) or instagram-bio-link-conversion-no-link-yet.md (the link); this is about being found by search, inside Instagram and on Google.

What the evidence shows

  • Google indexes public posts from professional accounts since 10 Jul 2025, when the account is public, adult, professional, and the setting that lets public photos and videos appear in search engines is on (Settings -> Account privacy) (Visilay 2026, read; Evolare 2025, read).
  • Visilay test, 3 Oct 2026 (practitioner, own extraction via DataForSEO, read): 24 Italian mobile searches; instagram.com in organic results for 6, anywhere on the page for 13. Five of six organic hits were profile pages, mostly for brand-name searches; average position 4; no photo post (/p/) ranked organically. Reels appeared in Google's "Short videos" carousel in 7 of 24 searches (6 of 10 for a local restaurant query, 4 of 10 for "what to see in Matera"). AI Overviews cited Instagram in 2 of 5, both reels. UK check, 10 Oct 2026, 7 searches: @ottolenghi_restaurants 2nd for "ottolenghi"; a location page 7th for "dishoom". Small sample, one day: treat as direction, not law.
  • A profile whose username matched the query reached 10th even though the shop was in another town: Google weights the name text heavily (Visilay).
  • Keyword captions vs hashtags: Hootsuite test (Jul 2022, cited by Visilay): 5 keyword-caption posts got ~30% more reach than 5 hashtag-only posts (e.g. 379 vs 216 accounts). Old and small; a hint.
  • Hashtags capped at 5 per post/reel since 19 Dec 2025 (Instagram @creators on Threads, via Visilay). Guides that still say 30 are out of date.
  • Keywords belong in the caption, not the comments (Instagram's 2021 search post, via Visilay).
  • Alt text: Instagram auto-writes generic alt text; you can replace it (Advanced settings). Write one plain sentence for screen readers; no keyword lists.
  • Location tag links the post to a location page Instagram uses in search.
  • Measurement: Search Console platform properties now report Google clicks/impressions/queries for an Instagram account (see search-console-platform-properties-social.md).

How to do it

  1. Settings check (2 min): professional account, public, search-engine visibility on.
  2. Username keeps the brand; Name field (separate, searchable in Instagram) = brand + what it is, e.g. " | Welsh farm history archive". No keyword strings.
  3. Bio in the reader's words: topic + place + the free thing ("Vale of Glamorgan farm records, 1824 on. Free first edition below").
  4. Every caption, first line = plain searchable phrase with proper names and a date: "Ty Mawr farmhouse, Vale of Glamorgan, 1824 survey". Then the story.
  5. Reels: on-screen title in the first seconds + burned-in or auto captions (both readable by ranking systems).
  6. Hashtags: max 5 - one topic (#WelshHistory), one place (#ValeOfGlamorgan), one format/series (#FromTheRecords), one community (#LocalHistory / #Genealogy), one Welsh-language tag where it fits (#HanesCymru). Skip #reels/#explore.
  7. Alt text on every image post: one sentence describing the record or photo.
  8. Location on posts about a real place (parish or village page, not a private address).
  9. Review monthly: Instagram search for our 5 core phrases (logged-out browser) + Google search for the brand name; note where we appear.

Trial Reels (checked, parked)

Trial Reels show a reel to non-followers first for 72 h, then auto-share to followers if it clears a relative threshold (Storrito, read). One creator logged 9 consecutive Trial Reels -> 2.3M impressions in 14 days (niredonahue.com, Dec 2025, read). Access is reported as professional accounts with 1,000+ followers from early 2026 (Storrito; not checked against Instagram Help, which our fetcher could not open). Our account has 5 followers -> parked until 1,000; re-check the toggle in the app then.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Do now (owned channel, no GO needed): a bizdev run checks the search-engine setting on the Instagram professional account, rewrites the Name field and bio to topic + place, and from the next post onward uses the caption/5-hashtag/alt-text/location recipe above. These are edits to an existing channel, not a new one.
  • Respect the existing posting cap (IG 6/day today, reached) - the recipe changes how, not how often.
  • Public text rules apply in captions: never name the owner; none of the banned words; follow the Bute-dates rule in the editorial memory note; 1824 David Stewart survey as the first record mention of Ty Mawr.
  • Track: Google clicks/impressions to the Instagram account (platform property) + Instagram search reach monthly in marketing/weekly.csv notes. Bar: brand-name Google search shows the Instagram profile on page 1 within 8 weeks.
