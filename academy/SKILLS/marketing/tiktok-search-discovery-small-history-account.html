TikTok search and discovery for a small history account (2026), plus the bio-link gate

Harvested 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 20:07). Status for Cedwyn: PARKED. The TikTok sign-in is blocked (the project workspace, 21 Oct), so only the account owner can unblock it. Held: don't ask today.

Facts read 26 Oct 2026

  • Bio link: "You can add a link to a website on your TikTok profile if you have at least 1,000 followers, or if you have a Verified Business Account" (TikTok Support). There is one URL field. Below that, write a plain-text URL in the bio and pin a video that names the site.
  • Metricool 2026 TikTok Study (via its TikTok SEO guide, read):
  • Content volume was up about 80% year on year (+72% videos, +140% photo/carousel posts), and average views fell about 31%. There's more competition per post.
  • The For You feed drives 7 of 10 views, and it's the only source that held steady. Direct search is about 4% of impressions and shrinking, but it matters more as an account grows.
  • Small accounts get more of their reach from hashtags. Search becomes bigger past about 100K followers. Weight early effort toward hashtags plus clear topic signals.
  • A post gets 96% of its reach and about 98% of its interactions within 10 days, so a post has a short life.
  • Follower count is not a ranking signal. 16.47% of accounts moved up a follower tier in a year, and 44% of accounts under 100K gained.
  • TikTok videos are indexed by Google.
  • Keyword in the first line of the caption, natural wording (digitalapplied.com 2026, snippet only, not checked). "Keywords boost visibility 20-40%" (riseatseven, snippet only, not checked).
  • Brand-launch scale (not our scale): the HISTORY Channel TikTok launch reached 50K+ followers, 21M reach and 12.2M views with paid creators (NeoReach case study). This shows history content travels on TikTok. The paid creator route is out for us (zero spend).

Playbook (zero spend)

  1. Profile: the name field carries topic + place ("Great House Farm | Vale of Glamorgan history"). Bio in reader words, plus a plain-text shop URL until 1,000 followers. Check whether a free Business Account plus verification would unlock the link sooner (eligibility for a sole-trader shop not checked).
  2. Each video: the hook in the first 2 s names a known name, date or "first" (Marconi 1897, the 1824 survey map). Say the keyword out loud and put it on screen too. Caption line 1 = the search phrase people type ("Vale of Glamorgan history", "Welsh farm history", "Lavernock Marconi"). Use 3-5 hashtags: topic, place, series, community (#walesHistory, #localhistory, #history), Welsh.
  3. Search the phrase on TikTok first. If strong recent videos exist, it's a live topic; if none, people may not search for it there.
  4. Cadence: 3-5 posts a week for 30 days before judging. Reuse the Instagram/Facebook reels without other apps' watermarks.
  5. Measure per video at day 10 (when ~96% of reach is in): views, average watch time, % from search vs For You, follows. Add TikTok to the Search Console platform-properties weekly row (see search-console-platform-properties-social.md).
  6. Stop or continue at day 30: continue if median views beat Instagram's and any video brings site visits (UTM or referrer).
