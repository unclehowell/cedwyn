# Vale of Glamorgan Walking Festival (Valeways): getting a Llandough heritage walk into the programme

Harvested 2026-10-29 19:45Z (skills harvest). Topic: local community / events / heritage-walk demand.

## What the evidence states
- **Valeways** is an independent, volunteer-run charity in the Vale of Glamorgan. Its aims are walking for health, safe countryside access, and guided and self-guided walks for all abilities.
  - It publishes a **quarterly guided-walk programme**. The Oct-Dec 2026 programme is live, and walks are graded by boot icon (about 1.5 h, about 2-2.5 h, or 3-4 h).
  - It runs a Welsh-language walking strand (*Cerdded Cymraeg*) and a Millennium Heritage Trail. Source: valeways.org.uk, read 29 Oct 2026.
- **The festival in numbers** (all are the organiser's or local press figures; not checked independently):
  - **2026:** 5-10 May, themed on the Vale's **churches**. It ran **27 guided walks** "to showcase their history, architecture and stories", plus **5 Stroller** short walks and **2 specialist walks**: Llantrithyd Deer Park (historic landscape) and an expert-led geology walk (visitthevale.com, read 29 Oct).
  - **2024:** 3-8 Sept, **30 routes**. Tickets were **£3 per adult per walk** via TicketSource, children free, booking required, with a named walk leader at each meeting point (walkingfestivals.org listing, read).
  - **2022:** 6-11 Sept, **350 people from all over Wales, 30 walks**. It included **history walks and a Welsh-language walk**, and Gileston Manor opened its gardens with tea and a tour (Penarth Times, read).
  - **Partners named:** Vale of Glamorgan Council Rights of Way, Tourism and Park Rangers; Vale of Glamorgan and Penarth Ramblers; Vale Tourism Association.
- **Lesson for us:** the festival already sells heritage and "stories of a place" to local walkers who are willing to pay a little. Volunteer **walk leaders and the charity run the walk**. That means a host with a story can contribute content without building their own accredited tour business first.
- **Nearby festivals listed for 2026, from search snippets only (not read):** Gower Walking Festival 5-13 Sep; a national "Festival of Walking" 12 Sep-11 Oct with free guided social walks across England and Wales. These are fallbacks if the Vale festival declines.

## How to do it
1. **Watch for the call.** The 2027 dates and theme aren't announced yet. 2026 ran in May; 2022 and 2024 ran in September. Check valeofglamorganwalkingfestival.org.uk and Valeways news each month from Jan 2027, and note the theme once it's announced.
2. **Fit the theme.** Valeways themes the programme, so pitch a walk that fits the year's theme. Churches (2026) would have fitted St Dochdwy's, Llandough. Each walk should carry one story, not a lecture.
3. **Offer content, not liability.** Propose a route and a story sheet, from approved Cedwyn text, for a Valeways walk leader to lead, with a Cedwyn volunteer as guest speaker if wanted. The charity's leaders and insurance cover the walk. Cedwyn supplies:
   - a one-page story sheet;
   - 4-6 stops with the record behind each (catalogue IDs);
   - a QR code to a /wales/ page.
4. **Bilingual helps.** The festival has run a Welsh-language walk before, so offer the story sheet in Cymraeg and English.
5. **After the walk:**
   - Put the story sheet online on Cedwyn, internal links only.
   - Invite walkers to the newsletter with a QR code to the signup.
   - Count signups via a `?ref=valeways` path.
   - Thank the charity publicly.

## Limits
- The farm itself is not a stop, and nothing is said about the land or the case. The walk tells the public history of Llandough: the Roman villa, church, manor and village. Editorial rules apply in full. Don't make any accusation, and don't name family holdings or addresses.
- Any approach to Valeways is outreach: a **HubSpot draft only, sent on the project lead's GO**. No direct messages, and no pitch made before the 2027 call is known.
- This doesn't replace the Visit Wales listing or accreditation gate for Cedwyn's *own* paid walk. It is the low-risk step before it.
