YouTube Posts (formerly the Community tab) for a small history channel

Skill date: 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 18:22). Status for Cedwyn: PROPOSAL. Posting is done in Studio or the app, and nothing has been posted yet.

What Posts are (YouTube Help, "Learn about posts", read in full 26 Oct 2026)

  • Formats: text, images, GIFs, polls, quizzes, music and video.
  • Where they show: on the channel's Posts shelf (Home tab) and Posts tab. They may also appear on viewers' homepage and Subscriptions feed, and image posts may also show in the Shorts feed. Subscribers may get a notification.
  • Not available on channels set as "Made for Kids" (their posts are hidden from viewers) or on supervised accounts. Posts are not available in Germany.
  • Discovery: engaging with other creators' posts may help your viewers see those posts across YouTube.
  • Settings: Studio > Customization > Home lets you move the Posts shelf up (it sits at the bottom by default). Studio > Content > Posts lists posts and lets you schedule them. Comment settings follow the channel default.

Eligibility: third-party write-ups put the removal of the old subscriber minimum in 2023, and YouTube opened "Communities" (viewer posting) to all creators with Posts access on 17 Jun 2025. Both are search snippets, not checked. Check the Create > Post button on the channel itself.

Practitioner guidance

vidIQ "How do you use the YouTube Community Tab?" (updated 20 May 2026, read in full): - One idea, one question, one next step per post. - Polls that expire in 24 hours create urgency to vote. Follow up with a second post that shares the result and what you chose. - Use polls as an idea engine for next videos and titles. - Rotate formats: poll, behind-the-scenes image, question. - Reply to comments. - At most 1-2 posts a day: posting too often can reduce feed showing and may bring a temporary restriction. - Their engagement table (polls and video clips "high", text "low-medium") is qualitative, with no numbers.

Counter-evidence: a 10k-subscriber channel on r/PartneredYoutube reported that all its growth came from long videos and Shorts and that its Community tab was "a ghost" (search snippet, not checked). A marketing agency figure of "15-25% higher video engagement" for consistent posters is unsourced, not checked. - What we take from it: Posts keep existing viewers warm and generate ideas. They are not a growth engine on their own at 7 subscribers. Expect little until Shorts bring an audience.

Playbook

  1. Move the Posts shelf up on the Home tab so channel visitors see recent posts.
  2. Post 2-3 times a week, never more than one a day. Rotate: - an image post (a record scan with a one-line question). This is the format that can reach the Shorts feed. - a 24-hour poll ("Which year is this map from?"), then the answer post linking to the Short or video that explains it. - a "which next?" poll. Make the winner, and say so.
  3. Pair each new Short with a post the day after: one image from it, the question it answers, and the video attached.
  4. Reply to every comment within a day, and turn good questions into Shorts.
  5. Measure monthly in Studio > Content > Posts: votes, likes, comments, and any lift in views of the attached video. Drop formats that get no response after 4 weeks.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Proposal: a weekly rhythm for the Cedwyn channel (7 subscribers; 17 Shorts waiting to upload once the quota resets at 07:00 UTC on 27 Oct):
  • Monday: an image post from the archive (for example a plan extract from the 1824 David Stewart survey, first record mention found), asking "What do you think this field name means?".
  • Wednesday: a 24-hour poll, "Guess the year the family acquired Tŷ Mawr". Thursday: the answer, "1667 (1974 newspapers; ITV News 1988)", with the timeline Short attached.
  • Friday: a "which story next?" poll with 3 options from the queued Shorts.
  • Spanish: one post a fortnight in Spanish for Patagonia viewers, with the Spanish Short attached.
  • Wording:
  • nobody in the family is named personally;
  • shop links only to cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/ebooks/ with UTM utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=post. Check in Studio whether a text link is clickable on a small channel; if it isn't, write "link in channel header".
  • Do it inside the existing upload runs. This needs Studio or app access, so it goes into the existing YouTube upload worker's checklist. Log each post in the project workspace
