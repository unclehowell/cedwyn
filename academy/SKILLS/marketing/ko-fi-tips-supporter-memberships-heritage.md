# Skill: Ko-fi tips and supporter memberships for a free-records heritage project (2026)
Written 26 Oct 2026 by the project lead's Tab agent (skills harvest). Topic: a zero-cost way for readers who will not buy a £9.99 e-book to still back the archive work, without splitting book sales away from /ebooks/.

## What the evidence shows
- **Fees, from Ko-fi's own pricing page (ko-fi.com, read 26 Oct 2026):** account is free, no monthly charge. Three fee modes: **"Ko-fi free" = 0% on tips, 5% on memberships, shop and commissions, no advanced features**; **"Standard" (the default for new creators) = 5% on everything including tips**, with supporter-only content and scheduled posts; **Gold = $12/month, 0% service fee**. Payment processor fees (Stripe/PayPal) always apply. Ko-fi never holds the money: it pays straight into your own Stripe or PayPal.
- **Breakeven:** Gold only pays for itself above about $240/month of shop or membership sales (Built by Foundry review, May 2026, updated Jul 2026). For us: stay on the free mode.
- **Scale (secondary, platform-reported):** 1.5M+ creators and $200M+ paid out (Built by Foundry, 2026); an earlier Ko-fi performance review reported $45.2M earned by ~168,000 creators in a year with an average payment of $19.5 (cited by Behind Rankings, Jan 2026).
- **Practitioner testimony:** Victoria Kurichenko (writer, Behind Rankings, Jan 2026) sells her e-books on Gumroad and uses Ko-fi only to collect tips from Medium readers: "money is instantly transferred to my PayPal". Lesson: keep products on the shop, use Ko-fi as the tip jar.
- **Critique worth heeding:** Built by Foundry rates Ko-fi 3.4/5: best-in-class for starting (setup 5/5) but weak membership tools (2.5/5) and no mobile app; "the right tool for tips and one-off sales, the wrong tool if recurring revenue from a serious audience is the goal".

## How-to
1. **Choose the mode on day one:** switch from the Standard default to "Ko-fi free" so tips carry 0% service fee. Do not buy Gold (zero-spend rule).
2. **Connect the existing Stripe account** (already used for the buy buttons) so all money lands in one place; no new processor.
3. **Tips first, membership later:** launch a single tip button with a goal that names a concrete, finished piece of work (e.g. "scan and publish the next 20 pages of the 1974 newspaper file"). Add one membership tier only when 10+ people have tipped.
4. **One membership tier, if any, and keep it simple:** e.g. £3/month, benefit = early access to each new record page + a monthly "from the archive" post. No exclusive evidence: the records stay free for everyone.
5. **Do not open a Ko-fi Shop** for the e-books: it would split sales tracking from Stripe/Gumroad and add a 5% fee. Link "Buy the e-books" to /ebooks/ instead.
6. **Place the button where free readers finish:** the /research/ and record pages, and the monthly email; NOT the free e-book end page, which keeps its one next step (ebook-back-matter-one-next-step.md), Tag every link with UTM (`utm_source=site&utm_medium=tipjar`).
7. **Thank every supporter by name within 24 h** (Ko-fi supports messages to supporters) and log totals weekly in the project workspace as "support", separate from e-book sales.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Owner step:** a Ko-fi page is opened in the project lead's name or the project's name and linked to his Stripe account: the project lead's decision. Runs may only prepare the page text and goal in HubSpot as a draft task.
- **Framing guard:** supporter money funds publishing and scanning work. It must never be described as paying anyone for disclosure, and the never-pay-for-disclosure policy is never mentioned publicly.
- **Measure:** if the tip jar earns nothing in 60 days after being placed, remove it rather than add more asks.
