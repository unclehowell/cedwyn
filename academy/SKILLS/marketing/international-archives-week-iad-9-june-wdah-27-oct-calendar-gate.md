# International Archives Day (9 June) / International Archives Week + UNESCO World Day for Audiovisual Heritage (27 Oct): two archive-calendar hooks, sized honestly

Written 31 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). Before this, 'archives week', 'archives day', 'audiovisual heritage' and '#IAW' were in 0 skill files (Explore Your Archive, #AskAnArchivist, the ACA hashtag party and #MuseumWeek already have their own files).

## The facts (primary pages, read 31 Oct 2026)
- ICA "About International Archives Week" (read): ICA's 2007 AGM set **9 June** as International Archives Day (the day ICA was founded in 1948 under UNESCO); first celebrated 2008; widened to a **full week in 2019**. Objectives include "showcase the unique, extraordinary and rare documents preserved in archival institutions". Organisations share their own events alongside ICA.
- ICA event page (read): IAW 2026 ran **8-12 June 2026**; the theme was chosen by a global vote with **483 responses**. ICA search result title: 2026 theme **#ArchivesForJustice: Rights, Memory & Futures** (hashtags #IAW2026, #InternationalArchivesDay).
- Same ICA page (read): UNESCO's 2005 General Conference set **27 October** as the **World Day for Audiovisual Heritage** (WDAH): film, sound and video records.
- 2027 dates are not yet published. Pattern: the week holding 9 June (Wed 9 June 2027, so expect Mon 7 - Fri 11 June 2027; confirm on ica.org in April).

It will not sell e-books directly. The value is (1) a dated reason to post a record image that archivists reshare, (2) being visible to the record offices we already write to, (3) a theme that matches our material, since the 2026 theme was literally archives and rights/justice.

## How to do it (any small publisher)
1. Put both dates in the content calendar: 9 June (+ the IAW week) and 27 October.
2. Two weeks before, read the ICA IAW page for the year's theme, hashtags and any poster kit. Use the theme hashtag first, #IAW<year> second, at most 3 hashtags.
3. One post per day of the week at most, each built on ONE catalogued record image with its archive reference and holding institution named in the text (tagging the institution's account where it has one). No sales line in the post; the link goes to the record's own page.
4. Prefer the theme tag over the generic tag (higher p90 above).
5. 27 Oct: post one restored or catalogued film/sound clip with its source and date (audiovisual only).
6. Measure: likes/reposts per post vs our normal median, follows gained that week, and visits tagged utm_campaign=iaw<year> or wdah<year>. Gate below.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **27 Oct 2026 has passed**; first usable date is **IAW 2027 (expected 7-11 June 2027)**, then WDAH 27 Oct 2027.
- IAW 2027 plan (draft for the June content calendar): five posts, Mon-Fri, each one record already on the evidence catalogue: a tithe/estate map, a 1974/1988 survey photo, a parish register entry, a deed page, a newspaper cutting. Caption = what it is, date, holding archive, catalogue reference; link to its /evidence/ page with utm_source=bluesky&utm_campaign=iaw2027 (or own /r/ short link). Same five on Instagram/Facebook as a carousel on the Friday.
- If the 2027 theme touches rights, access or memory, the first post is the record-access story told neutrally (dates and what was requested and supplied), no accusations about named people.
- WDAH 2027: one documentary clip that uses archive film/photos only (no AI scenes), with source line.
- Public text rules apply: never name the owner, banned words list, no family holdings or addresses, Cedwyn internal links only.
- **Gate:** keep it in the calendar if any IAW 2027 post beats 2x our median likes or brings 1 new follower from an archive/library account; otherwise drop to a single 9 June post in 2028.
- Status: calendar item, no account or spend needed; no contact with anyone (tags only).
