# X (Twitter) growth after Communities closed: replies, threads, XChat (2026)

## Facts (read at source, 26 Oct 2026)
- X Communities are gone. TechCrunch 23 Apr 2026 (read): head of product Nikita Bier wrote that Communities "were used by less than 0.4% of users - yet contributed to 80% of spam reports, financial scams, and malware on X". Shutdown first set for 6 May, extended to 30 May 2026. Members were pointed to XChat group chats with public "joinable" links that can be shared and pinned; X also launched custom (topic) timelines.
- Postory (23 May 2026, read, vendor): XChat groups "a few hundred members" with plans toward 1,000 (not checked); migrate audiences to Lists + XChat; growth now from strategic replies, tight-niche threads, a weekly routine.
- Graham Mann (Feb 2026, read, one creator's own data): engagement in the first 30 minutes is the biggest ranking factor (his read, not checked); a post with 20 replies beats 100 likes and no conversation; one genuine question reply got 12K impressions and 7 profile visits; on days with 40-60 posts+replies combined, profile visits rose to 30-35/day and new follows to 7-8/day; a fortnight off gave engagement -24%, likes -32%, profile visits -26% and net unfollows. Content mix he sees working: 40% stories, 30% teaching, 20% milestones, 10% selling. Native video ~10x text engagement (not checked).

## How to do it (zero spend)
1. Profile: name + plain topic, bio line with what you post and one link (the shop/library page), pinned post = best evidence thread.
2. Daily, 20 minutes: find 10 recent posts in the niche (search "(keyword) min_faves:50", sort Latest) and leave 5-10 replies that add a fact, a source or a real question. No links in replies; no self-promotion.
3. Original posts: 1-2 a day, a 3-6 post thread twice a week (one record, one image, one source line). Reply to every reply in the first 30 minutes.
4. Video: post short clips natively (no YouTube links) with burned-in captions.
5. Lists instead of Communities: build a public list of local-history/heritage accounts and work it daily.
6. XChat joinable group: only once there are 20+ engaged followers who ask for it; it is a new channel, so it needs an owner decision.
7. Weekly in X analytics: profile visits, follows, replies received. Keep the routine 4 weeks before judging (his data shows gaps cost more than slow weeks).
