# Skill: Welsh-language media for a Cardiff/Vale heritage story (2026)
Written 26 Oct 2026 by the project lead's Cedwyn editors (skills harvest). Topic: getting the Tŷ Mawr / Cedwyn records in front of Welsh-speaking readers through the outlets that actually take community stories. Press contact is on the project lead's GO only; runs draft, never send.

## What the evidence shows (checked 26 Oct 2026 on each outlet's own page)
- **Pitching in general (Muck Rack, State of Journalism 2026, 1,000+ journalists, summarised by Univ. of Minnesota Marcom, 13 Apr 2026):** 86% say some of their work began with a PR pitch; 43% say relevant pitches are rare; what they value most is clear relevance to their beat (70%), access to a source (58%), original data or research (40%) and high-resolution images (37%); 62% prefer a 1:1 email; 78% prefer it before noon; 69% want it under 200 words.
- **Y Dinesydd** (dinesydd.cymru): the papur bro for **Cardiff and the Vale of Glamorgan**, first published April 1973, Wales' oldest papur bro; 10 issues a year (none in January or August); the site invites readers to contribute a story through its Cysylltu page; sold at Griffin Books, Penarth among other shops; £15 a year for the PDF edition. This is the closest Welsh-language outlet to Llandough.
- **Papurau Bro network** (papuraubro.cymru, run by Mentrau Iaith Cymru): a volunteer-made paper in almost every area of Wales (58, Wikipedia "Papur Bro"); content includes local histories. Network contact [address kept in the project workspace]
- **Golwg360 / Golwg magazine** (golwg.360.cymru): national Welsh-language news site, funded with Welsh Government and Books Council support. Press releases and story ideas go to **[address kept in the project workspace]** (general enquiries [address kept in the project workspace]; Golwg Cyf, Llanbedr Pont Steffan SA48 8LT; [number kept in the project workspace]). It runs Llyfrau (books) and Cymru news sections.
- **Bro360 hyperlocal sites** (bro.360.cymru): residents create an account, write a story with photos/audio, and a local editor checks it before publishing (3 steps). Seven areas covered about 91,000 people in 2021 (Arsyllfa, Feb 2021). **No Bro360 site covers Cardiff or the Vale** (the sites are in Ceredigion, Arfon, Môn, Wrexham etc.; "BroCardi360" is Aberteifi/Cardigan, not Cardiff). So for us Bro360 is a model, not a channel.
- **S4C Heno** (s4c.cymru, nightly magazine, Mon-Fri 19:00): runs a **calendar** of events on Monday and Friday evenings. Send a 15-20 second Welsh-language video, filmed landscape, with time, cost and venue, to [address kept in the project workspace]; for Monday the clip must arrive by midday the Friday before, for Friday by midday Thursday. Only useful when there is a real public event (a talk, a walk, a launch).

## How-to
1. **One story, one outlet, one angle.** Lead with a dated record and a picture, not the shop: e.g. "Tŷ Mawr, Llandough: the 1824 David Stewart survey is the first record mention found" with the map image and its archive reference.
2. **Write it in Welsh first**, under 200 words, plain register (papurau bro welcome learners' Welsh but an editor still edits). Get a fluent check before the project lead sends; never machine-translate unseen.
3. **Order of approach:** Y Dinesydd (local, monthly, needs copy ~4 weeks ahead) -> Golwg360 datganiadau@ (news-led: only with a genuine new record or event) -> Heno calendar (only for a real event, with a 15-20 s clip).
4. **Give, don't sell:** offer free use of a scan or photo with a credit line and one link to the specific Cedwyn page (not /ebooks/). The paper's readers find the shop from there.
5. **Track:** HubSpot task per outlet (draft only), UTM `utm_source=dinesydd|golwg360&utm_medium=press&utm_campaign=cymraeg-2026`, outcome logged to the project workspace

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- A Heno calendar clip only when a public event exists (e.g. a heritage walk or talk from heritage-talks-circuit-aatp-branches.md); the clip must be in Welsh, so it needs a Welsh speaker on camera: the project lead's decision.
- Do not post to Bro360 sites (none cover our area; posting outside your own bro breaks the spirit of the scheme).
- Spend: zero. A £15 Y Dinesydd PDF subscription would let us read before pitching; that is the project lead's call, not ours.
