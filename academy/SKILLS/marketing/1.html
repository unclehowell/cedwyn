Skill #1 — Email, CRM & follow-ups for a niche heritage e-book business (2026)

Written 26 Oct 2026 from practitioner testimony; refresh when evidence changes.

What practitioners report works (sources)

  • The welcome flow is the highest-performing automation: Omnisend May 2026 benchmarks put automated welcome emails at ~35.5% open, 3.9% click, 2.1% conversion; a 3-email series drives ~90% more orders than a single email; the first 48 h after signup are the highest-engagement window and set sender reputation. (digitalapplied.com, "The Welcome Email Sequence: a 2026 onboarding framework", 4 Jun 2026)
  • Consistency beats optimisation for small lists: one newsletter a week, same time, short and conversational; growth came from content found elsewhere (LinkedIn posts, Substack Notes) pointing back to the list. (Greg, The Introverted Networker, 2,500 subs without ads, 30 May 2025)
  • Zero-to-4,000 subscribers in 12 months with no prior platform came from steady, high-quality posting plus cross-promotion; no shortcut. (Jeannine Ouellette, Writing in the Dark, Dec 2023)
  • Aggressive list-building (frequent sends, swaps, big freebies) brings unsubscribes; a relevant reader magnet + a gentle cadence keeps the list warm. (r/selfpublish, "I tried aggressively growing my author newsletter for 30 days")

How-to (Cedwyn)

  1. Every contact in HubSpot carries a source (outreach/heritage-2026, signup, buyer, prospect) and lifecycle stage; institutions are companies, people are contacts. Never import outreach targets into the reader list — only opt-ins join it.
  2. Welcome sequence (day 0 / 3 / 7): deliver the free first edition → one striking record + a specific Cedwyn page → soft mention of the £9.99 latest edition / bundle. One link per email, UTM-tagged.
  3. Weekly reader email, max once a week, same day/time: story first, one e-book featured, never a book the reader already owns (check the buyers ledger).
  4. Follow-ups: reply to every reply within a day; positive reply → thank, offer the free edition, ask them to share with their members/followers.
  5. Log every touch as a HubSpot note; opt-outs go on the do-not-contact list at once.
  6. Measure: signups/day, welcome open & click, reads (soft conversion), sales (strong conversion), replies.
