## Evidence
- **Warwick "Through the Shop Window" trail (Warwickshire County Council news, 16 Jul 2026, read).** Heritage and Culture Warwickshire ran a free summer family trail: **window displays in town-centre shops, a map and QR codes** telling the stories of businesses that once served the town and the castle (a baker who supplied bread for a castle elephant, a butcher's sausages for the Earl's breakfast), drawn from the castle archives. Free maps at the museum, the TIC **and participating shops**; finishers get their map **stamped** at the TIC; ran about six weeks (to 29 Aug). Supported by the town council and the Friends of the County Record Office. No visitor numbers published (none found).
- **Stockton-on-Tees "Window to the Past" heritage trail (council and library Facebook posts, snippets only, not read in full).** A town-centre heritage trail across shopfronts "to find out more about the area's history and reveal the changing buildings, people and places", with a linked trail "in 12 shops" on the High Street. Count from a snippet, not checked.
- **Historic England High Streets Heritage Action Zones (context, secondhand).** £103m programme, 2020-24, 67-69 high streets, a four-year cultural programme alongside building repair (Nottingham-hosted evaluation PDF snippet; Independent report, read): shopkeepers reported more footfall and "more people stopping and looking in". That footfall is attributed to restored shopfronts, not to window displays; do not cite it as proof that a window trail lifts trade.
- **What is missing:** no measured visitor or sales figure for a window trail was found in this run. Treat it as a low-cost visibility and goodwill format, measured by our own counts.

## Why it works (mechanism, from the cases)
- The archive story belongs to the shop's own building or trade, so the shop gets a free story about itself, not an advert.
- The stamp at the end turns a display into a route and gives a natural finish point (a café or the shop that hosts the leaflets).
- It needs no permission from the public realm (no signs on walls or posts): only the shopkeeper's window, for a fixed number of weeks.

## The method
1. **Pick 6-12 windows** on one walkable line where an archive image or record matches the building, street or trade.
2. **One A3 or A4 panel per window**: the image, a 40-60 word caption with its record reference, a stop number, a QR (own coded redirect per window).
3. **Fixed run** (4-8 weeks), with a start and end date agreed in writing by each shopkeeper; we fit and remove the panels ourselves.
4. **Map with stamp boxes** at the start (library, community centre or café) and in each window shop; stamp at the finish shop.
5. **Count**: maps taken, stamps at the finish, scans per window. Publish the totals afterwards on the walk page; never estimate.
6. **Hand-over**: each shop keeps its panel as a print; the images and captions go into the site so the trail outlives the windows.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- Llandough itself has few shopfronts, so the realistic line is a Penarth or Llandough-edge cluster (village shop, café, pub, garden centre) **plus** the walk route. Only images and records already in the evidence catalogue with a clear source and reuse right; captions name the record and the holding institution.
- Strongest panels: then-and-now pairs of Tŷ Mawr / Great House Farm and the Llandough streets (demolished 1988 shown as absence, never as a generated image); the old parish names from /nomenclature/ as a "what this place was called" panel.
- Each panel's QR goes to that parcel's page on cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate (the "NN of 59" parcel modal), the walk page, and from there /ebooks/. No price on any panel.
- Neutral wording only: no case argument in shop windows, nothing that reads as an accusation about named people or institutions, no banned words, never name the project lead, no family holdings or addresses, no insignia. Llandough Farm is not Great House Farm.
- **Gates:** shop approaches are HubSpot hive draft tasks only, sent by nobody until the project lead states GO; zero spend from us (panels printed by a sponsor per the sponsor-a-stop skill, or deferred); run dates avoid any period the project lead freezes.
