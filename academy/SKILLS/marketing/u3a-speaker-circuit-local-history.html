u3a speaker circuit for a local-history talk (South Wales)

Date: 2026-10-26 (skills harvest 16:37). Zero spend. Nothing here is sent to anyone; outreach goes to HubSpot as drafts only, on owner GO.

What it is

u3a groups (retired and semi-retired learners) run monthly general meetings with a booked speaker, plus local-history interest groups. Each u3a has a Speakers Secretary who books the year ahead and shares ratings with neighbouring u3as. Two doors exist, and they work differently:

  1. Outside speaker (paid or expenses): the Speakers Secretary books from approaches and from regional directories. Ems Valley u3a's role description (read in full): book for monthly meetings "bearing in mind price (including any travel expenses) and the variety of subjects"; "Max budget for any speaker is £150"; no speaker in May (AGM), August or December; the secretary sends the talk blurb to the webmaster and newsletter editor; and attends a yearly Speakers Secretaries Conference (normally October) with "a list of our speakers and their presentations from the last year ... and an evaluation of that presentation for distribution amongst other local u3as". One good talk therefore travels to other u3as through that evaluation list.
  2. Member-to-member network: the u3a Wales Region "South Wales u3a Network - Speakers for Your u3a" page (read in full) lists u3a members who offer talks to other u3as; "They cannot charge for their talks to another u3a but can request travel expenses." Only a u3a member can be listed there.

Regional directories exist elsewhere too: the YAHR (Yorkshire and Humber) Speaker Directory, updated 12 Jun 2026, adds speakers only on a u3a's recommendation and with the speaker's consent (read in full). So the route in is a first good talk, then a recommendation.

The national u3a site has a Local History subject page with a link to "history talks and speakers" (search snippet; the page sat behind a browser check, not checked).

How to do it (steps)

  1. One talk, one title, one paragraph. 40-45 min plus questions. Title names a place and a question, not a product. Have a 3-sentence bio and an equipment line (laptop/HDMI, or no slides) ready; the secretary forwards these as-is.
  2. Find the Speakers Secretary. Each u3a site (u3asite.uk pattern) lists a contact or a speakers page. Target nearest first (Penarth, Barry, Cowbridge/Vale, Cardiff, then the South Wales network area). Not checked today which Vale u3as exist; look up via the u3a "Find a u3a" tool before drafting.
  3. Approach email (draft only): subject = talk title; body = blurb, length, fee position ("free, travel expenses only" for a first talk is the realistic ask at £0-150 budgets), dates you cannot do, and an offer of a 10-minute call. No attachments on first contact.
  4. On the day: no sales table push. One closing slide with a QR code to the free edition on our own site (utm_source=u3a&utm_campaign=), plus a printed half-sheet with the same link. Sign-up sheet for the newsletter only with explicit tick-box consent.
  5. After: thank-you email within 48 h; ask if they will add the talk to their list for the Speakers Secretaries Conference. Log result in HubSpot (attendees, QR scans, sign-ups, sales).
  6. Scale: after 2 good talks, ask a host u3a to recommend the talk to the Wales Region network or a regional directory.

Numbers and honesty check

  • £150 cap per speaker at Ems Valley u3a (read in full; one u3a, not a national rate).
  • South Wales network: members cannot charge, expenses only (read in full).
  • No source found giving book or e-book sales per u3a talk. A WI/u3a speaker author (pigpen.page, read in full) wrote: "I am yet to sell a book after an event". Treat a talk as list-building and word of mouth, not a till.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Speaker identity: whoever gives the talk is the owner's choice; public blurbs never name the owner unless he decides to present in his own name.
  • Call to action: the free edition at cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/ebooks/ with UTM tags; paid volumes (£9.99, bundle £31.99) are mentioned once, on the last slide only.
  • Drafts: one HubSpot draft per nearest u3a Speakers Secretary, owner GO before any send. Repeat bar: 15 attendees, 5 sign-ups or 1 sale per talk; below that twice, stop.
  • Pair with library-venue-talk-vale-libraries-booking.md (same talk, different host) and event-structured-data-in-person-talk-page.md (mark up only a confirmed public venue talk; u3a meetings are members-only, so no event markup).
