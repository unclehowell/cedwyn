New Instagram account: first 90 days + the Trial Reels gate (Cedwyn)

For a brand-new professional account (the Cedwyn account was being set up on 27 Oct 2026, separate from the personal one). Researched 27 Oct 2026.

What the sources actually show

  • Trial Reels (Meta, first published 10 Dec 2024, updated 26 Jun 2025). You switch on "Trial" before sharing a reel. It goes to non-followers first and does not appear in followers' Feed or Reels tab. After about 24 h you see views, likes, comments and shares, compared with your earlier trials. You can tap "share with everyone", or set it to share with followers automatically if it does well on views in the first 72 h. Leaks: followers can still meet a trial through a DM share or audio/location/filter pages, so treat it as low-visibility, not invisible.
  • Eligibility gate (PostFast 2026 guide; not checked against Meta's help page): accounts under 1,000 followers do not get Trial Reels. No collaborators on a trial. Duplicate detection: changing only the text overlay does not count as a new trial. About 20 trials a day via the API. So a new Cedwyn account cannot use Trial Reels until 1k followers. Plan for that.
  • Practitioner case (Erin Donahue, niredonahue.com, updated 16 Dec 2025): after a reach slump on a large account with many "ghost" followers, she reposted casual Story clips as Trial Reels. 9 consecutive trials made 2.3M+ impressions in 14 days, with reach spread across several posts instead of one spike. Follow-ups that pointed back to the breakout post performed well too. Lesson: trials pay off most on an account whose existing followers are disengaged. A brand-new account has no such problem.
  • Ranking signals (Mosseri, Jan 2025, as reported by dataslayer.ai and creatorflow.so; secondary sources): watch time, likes per reach, and sends (DM shares) per reach are the three that matter most. Reposting someone else's video gets less reach than the original.
  • Small accounts: improvado.io (2026) puts Reels at about 1.5x the reach of static posts for accounts under 10k. That is a vendor figure, not checked.

How to do it: a 90-day plan for a new account

  1. Days 0-7: profile first, posting second. Name field with search words ("Cedwyn Estate · Welsh history & records"), a link in bio (UTM-tagged per utm-discipline-ga4-funnel.md), a non-human profile image, a professional account with a category. Then 3 pinned posts: what this is, the best record, the e-book. Nobody follows an empty grid.
  2. Borrow the existing audience. Do not wait for strangers. Use Collab posts with the established personal account (instagram-collab-posts-heritage-partners.md): one post shows on both grids and shares engagement. This is the fastest legitimate route to the first few hundred followers. Also share the new handle once on the FB page, in the newsletter footer and in the Story of the personal account.
  3. Cadence until 1k: 3-4 Reels a week (7-30 s, the hook in the first 1-2 s, captions on, original footage or archive images we own), plus 1-2 carousels a week for saves. Judge each post on sends per reach and average watch time, not likes.
  4. Write for sends. Make posts people forward: "send this to someone from Penarth", a then-and-now of a place name, a one-record mystery. A send is the strongest signal a small account can earn.
  5. At 1,000 followers, turn on Trial Reels as an A/B lab. Put 2 different hooks for the same story as 2 trials (different first frame and audio, not just new text). Leave auto-share on, so a trial that does well on 72-h views goes to followers on its own. Log each trial in a sheet: hook, 24-h views, 72-h views, shares, shared-to-followers yes/no. Compare trials with trials, never with regular posts.
  6. Never: buy followers, follow and unfollow strangers, mass-DM, or post the same video twice with a new caption (duplicate detection).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • New Cedwyn account: week 1 = profile + 3 pinned posts + one Collab carousel with the personal account (both grids). The bio link points to /ebooks/ with utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=bio.
  • Content pillars from what we already hold: place-name origins (Wales), Welsh Patagonia threads (kept as a separate narrative; never mixed in one post), "one record" carousels from the evidence catalogue, and short clips from the daily movie scenes (our own footage, so originality is safe).
  • Measure weekly in the Sunday growth report: followers, reach from non-followers, sends per reach, and bio link taps. Trial Reels go into the plan only when the account passes 1k. Until then, Collab posts and sends are the growth engine.
  • Public text rules apply: no name of the account owner, none of the banned words, no insignia, no people's faces from the archive.
