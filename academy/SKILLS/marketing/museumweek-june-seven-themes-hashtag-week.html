MuseumWeek (7 days, 7 themes, 7 hashtags, early June) for a small heritage archive

Checked 30 Oct 2026 (harvest run). '#MuseumWeek' had 0 hits in any skill file before this.

What it is (primary pages, read)

  • Organiser: Culture For Causes Network, a Paris non-profit. "The official international week of museums, organized with the support of UNESCO" (museum-week.org, read). Launched 2014 on Twitter; "thousands of institutions from over 100 countries". Content on X, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.
  • Format: "7 days, 7 themes, 7 hashtags" (MuseumWeek Facebook post, search snippet). Each day has its own theme hashtag beside #MuseumWeek. The yearly Social Kit gathers all multilingual hashtags in one visual (Downloads page, snippet).
  • Dates: 2025 = 2-8 June; 2026 = 1-7 June, theme "Museums, AI and Futures" / "Intelligences, Museums, Futures" (MuseumWeek Magazine posts, Dec 2025 and Jul 2026; bodies paywalled, headers read). An earlier edition ran 11-17 May (#HeroesMW). 2027 dates not announced yet; plan for late May / early June and confirm on museum-week.org around March.
  • Scale, past (search snippets, not checked against the originals): 2015 = 2,207 museums from 64 countries (Bright x Twitter case study; Twitter blog states "more than 2,200 ... over 64 countries"); Guardian 23 Mar 2015 counted 800+ UK/Europe/Americas/Asia institutions in the first wave.
  • Who can join: the 2026 rankings have separate tables for GLAM, Institutions & networks, Individuals, Medias and Others, so non-museum accounts take part (ranking pages, read).

Measured: what a small UK account can get (ranking2026-glam, read 30 Oct)

  • "83 accounts ... out of more than 700 GLAM institutions registered". Score = logarithmic engagement relative to the top account (Granaderos a Caballo = 100). Lowest listed GLAM score 14.4.
  • UK entries: Westminster Abbey 66.1 (#13), The Royal Welsh Museum (Brecon regimental museum) 57.3 (#38), Museum of Enfield 49.2, Bow Street Museum 44.5, Historic Croydon Airport 34.5. Small local museums rank beside the Abbey, so the week rewards consistent daily posting more than size.
  • Instagram is not measured: "platform API restrictions ... since 2021 prevent us from accounting for it." Rankings count X/Facebook-type activity only.
  • No Wales/Cymru account in the Individuals or Networks tables. The Welsh slot is open.

How to do it (practitioner method)

  1. Register (free form on museum-week.org) about 4-6 weeks out, when the year's themes and kit appear. Registration puts you on the list the organisers repost from.
  2. Map all 7 themes to 7 records in one sitting. One post per day, each with #MuseumWeek + that day's hashtag + one place tag. Don't stack other hashtags.
  3. Post early in your own morning (UK) and again in the Americas afternoon (~16:00 UK) for the day's theme. The hashtag is global, and the Spanish/Latin American accounts dominate the top of the 2026 tables.
  4. Reply and repost within the hashtag all day. The ranking is engagement-relative, and the replies are where peer institutions find you.
  5. Bilingual helps: the kit is multilingual; a short Welsh line plus English fits the format (see bilingual-welsh-english-layout-posts-cards-email.md).
  6. Afterwards, check the ranking tables (published by late July) and note your score. The rankings are a free, dated third-party mention.

Limits

  • No traffic numbers published for small participants; the 2026 wrap-up article is paywalled. Expect re-shares and follows from peer heritage accounts, not sales.
  • Instagram activity is invisible to the ranking, but the hashtag still surfaces posts on IG.
