# Threads for a heritage archive: profile, communities, cadence and Instagram cross-posting

Harvested 2026-10-26. Threads is the text network inside Meta; a profile is made from an existing Instagram account, and posts can be recommended to Instagram and Facebook users.

## The numbers (primary or first-hand sources)
- **Meta, 16 Jun 2026**: Threads reached **500 million monthly active users**; **communities left beta** (custom icons, "champion" recognition for active members) and a "Your Algo" feed setting launched.
- **Meta, Mar/May 2025**: eligible Threads posts **can be recommended on Instagram and Facebook**; profiles take **up to five bio links**, and Insights now show **clicks per link**, follower cities/countries and where posts were discovered.
- **Buffer 2026 format study**: on Threads, **video posts lead at 5.55% median engagement, nearly double text posts (2.79%)**. Buffer's earlier comparison: Threads median engagement **6.25% vs X 3.6%**.
- **Digiday (2025)**: one publisher's Threads referral traffic rose **677% month over month**; it then posted far more often and Threads overtook Reddit and MSN as a referrer for it. Chartbeat data in the same piece: Threads referrals to publishers on average doubled. These are news publishers posting at high volume; our scale is far smaller.

## Playbook
1. **Profile**: create it from the Instagram account the archive already runs, so followers can follow in one tap. Same name and photo; bio in one line with Welsh and Spanish words; the five links = /ebooks/, the free first chapter signup, /movie/, the Welsh-Argentina page, the evidence index (each with UTM `utm_source=threads&utm_medium=social`).
2. **Join, don't spam, communities**: find history, Welsh, genealogy and local-history communities; read their rules; reply under other people's posts with one dated record from our catalogue before ever posting a link.
3. **Post shape**: lead with the record (an image or a short clip of the document, since video and images outperform text), one sentence of context with the date and source, then the link in a reply or the bio, not the first line. One topic tag (Threads groups posts by tag; check in the app how many a post takes).
4. **Cadence**: 1 post a day on weekdays plus replies; a second post only when it carries a new record. Re-use the anniversary calendar dates and tag @exploreyourarchive during Explore Your Archive (10 Nov; focus week 21-29 Nov 2026), whose team reposts from Threads.
5. **Cross-post from Instagram** with care: the "share to Threads" toggle copies a post; rewrite the first line for a text-first feed rather than repeating the caption.
6. **Measure weekly** in Threads Insights: link clicks per bio link, followers by country (Wales, England, Argentina), which posts were discovered on Instagram/Facebook. Stop or change any post type that gets no replies after four weeks.
7. Optional: Threads can share posts to the fediverse (Mastodon); if switched on, follow the Mastodon instance rules skill. Not checked in the app this run.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Threads is not among the connected apps** (Instagram and Facebook are). Making the profile is an in-app step on the archive's Instagram account; it needs the account holder's go-ahead and phone, so it is listed as a step only the account owner can take. Nothing was created or posted by this run.
- Framing per the editorial file: the Williams family as a family of standing with a long-held claim; outside narratives are tested, not repeated. No banned words, no insignia, never name the family member who runs the archive, no accusations against named people or companies.
- Every link goes to Cedwyn's own pages; purchases only via /ebooks/. No DMs to strangers.
