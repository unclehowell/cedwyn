# The talks circuit: society branch meetings and All About That Place
Harvested 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). Scope: short talks to family-history and local-history audiences as a free channel for a heritage archive and its e-books. Etiquette, deadlines and who may speak.

## What the sources show (read 26 Oct 2026)
- **All About That Place (AATP)**, Society of Genealogists with the Society for One-Place Studies: a FREE 10-day virtual event on family and local history (2026: 7-16 Oct, recordings available to 31 Oct, then in SoG's public Learning Hub). Earlier editions left "140+ FREE bite-sized talks".
  - Talks are **8-12 minutes, pre-recorded**; slides and showing your face are optional (face helps lip-readers).
  - **Self-promotion is invited**: "Please do use your talk as a chance to appropriately plug your services, books, websites" with links and logos on screen; **three links in the speaker bio**.
  - **£30 per talk** on request (invoice with bank details) — income, not spend.
  - 2026 timetable: ideas by 30 Jun; acceptance 8 Jul; title/bio/photo 31 Jul; video by 31 Aug. Daily themes (2026: Getting Started, Health and Medicine, Tools, Military and War, Work and Fun, Collections and Archives, Changing Places, Innovation, Politics and Rights...). Speakers may submit surnames and places mentioned.
  - SoG's own tip: tell local history societies, family history groups and social media groups for the places in your talk that their place features.
- **Glamorgan FHS branch meetings** (glamfhs.org.uk): six branches. **Cardiff & Vale Branch meets at 2 pm on the first Tuesday every other month at Glamorgan Archives** (Clos Parc Morgannwg, CF11 8AW; [address kept in the project workspace]); Swansea also meets online. Guest speakers are part of every branch programme.
- **Precedent**: Richard Morgan, author of "Place-Names of Glamorgan" (historical evidence and meanings of 1,100+ place-names), was billed to speak to Glamorgan FHS in Pontypridd in autumn 2026 (Pontypridd Historical Society Facebook post). A place-names book author on the society circuit is normal and welcome: the exact lane of our Enwau names book.

## The method
1. **One talk, many venues**: write one 10-minute talk with one strong story and 3-5 record images, record it once (AATP format), then offer the same talk live or online to branches.
2. **Pitch the society, not a person**: branch contact addresses (cardiffmeetings@...) are role addresses of a registered charity = institution. HubSpot draft only; the project lead signs off.
3. **What a branch secretary needs**: title, 50-word summary, length (30-45 min live; 8-12 min for AATP), speaker bio, AV needs, fee expectation (we offer free).
4. **Sell softly**: last slide = the free records site, with one line on the e-books; offer a printed QR to the free Enwau download (email capture there). No hard sell; family-history audiences are volunteers and buy from people who give.
5. **Measure**: unique UTM per venue (utm_source=glamfhs_cardiffvale, utm_medium=talk); downloads and sales within 14 days.
