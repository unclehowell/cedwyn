Historypin: an ordered "Tour" of archive pins on the world history map

Skill date: 26 Oct 2026. Topic: community/heritage discovery platform (map + timeline) as a free shelf for archive images and a virtual walking tour; reaches local-history users worldwide, including Patagonia. Why it matters: people browse Historypin by place and date. A Tour (ordered pins) is a ready-made virtual version of a walking tour, and it is free to create.

The evidence (opened 26 Oct 2026)

  • About Historypin: "427,983 stories pinned across 27,844 projects and tours – from 2,600 cities", built by "more than 100,000 storytellers, archivists, and citizen historians, including nearly 4,000 cultural heritage organizations". Free tutorials; paid extras exist (Historypin Plus custom look, Accelerator bulk upload, GIS historic map overlays, paid help sessions). Run by the non-profit Shift Collective; created 2009 (historypin.org home).
  • Historypin home: "Get started with a free account"; featured collections show scale, e.g. Green Book Travelers 2,366 pins.
  • Historypin API docs: every pin is geotagged and dated (at least roughly); "Tours ... are a special kind of Collections in which pins have been put in a particular order, like a walking tour".
  • Terms of use: text you post (stories, comments) is licensed CC BY-SA 3.0, so others may reuse it with attribution; for media (photos, video, audio) "you can choose from a range of licenses" (the exact option list was not read: not checked). You must hold the rights to all you upload.
  • Older third-party FAQ copy: free to browse; you can build collections or tours from your own and others' pins (abouthistorypinarchive.jumpslide.com snippet: not checked).

The how-to

  1. Free account under the project name (organisation profile), plain bio, one link to the site's tour page.
  2. Pin only what we hold copyright in (own photos of the sites today, our maps/diagrams); for each pin: precise location, date or date range, a 2-3 sentence factual caption with its record reference, and the media licence chosen deliberately (prefer the most restrictive available for our own photos; text is CC BY-SA regardless, so keep captions short and let the e-books hold the depth).
  3. Build a Tour: pins in walking order, matching the planned guided-tour stops, so a remote visitor can "walk" it.
  4. Add a then-and-now pair where we own both images; this is what Historypin users engage with most (inference, not checked).
  5. Join or comment on existing local collections only with genuine additions; no sales copy.
  6. Log monthly: pin views if shown, comments, follows, referral visits.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Two Tours: (a) Llandough and Cardiff stops of the planned guided tour, as a virtual preview with a "book the guided version" line on the tour page (not in pins); (b) a Welsh Patagonia Tour later, with Chubut places from published records, pairing with the Argentina project and the Spanish-language audience.
  • Complements People's Collection Wales (already a skill): PCW is Wales-only and Welsh-institution-facing; Historypin is the global map, useful for diaspora and Patagonia readers. Do not duplicate long text on both; use short captions and keep depth on Cedwyn.
  • PROPOSAL only: a Historypin account is a new public channel; prep the pin sheet (place, date, caption, reference, image we own, licence) in the project workspace and wait for approval. No paid Historypin services.
