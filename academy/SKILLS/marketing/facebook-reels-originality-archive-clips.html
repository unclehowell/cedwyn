Facebook Reels for archive clips under Meta's 2026 originality rules

Skill date: 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 18:22). Status for Cedwyn: applies to clips already being posted. Any change to the posting plan is a PROPOSAL.

What Meta changed (Meta Newsroom, "Rewarding Original Creators on Facebook", 12-13 Mar 2026, read in full)

  • Results: views and time spent watching original Reels roughly doubled in H2 2025 compared with H2 2024, as Meta pushed original content and demoted unoriginal content. Meta removed 20M+ accounts impersonating large creators in 2025.
  • Original: content filmed or produced by the Page or profile owner. A Reel that uses third-party material is original only when the focus is a creator presenting something genuinely new (fresh information or analysis).
  • Unoriginal (deprioritised):
  • watching along, reacting, stitching clips together, or narrating what is already on screen without adding anything;
  • re-uploads of posts the Page did not make;
  • small edits to others' content (borders, captions, speed changes).
  • Penalty: a Page that keeps posting mostly unoriginal content can become non-recommendable and demonetised. Appeals exist.
  • Protection: the content protection tool flags copies of your original Reels. Check access in the professional dashboard.

Format data (Buffer, "Best content format on social platforms in 2026", 45M+ posts, published 19 Mar 2026, read in full)

  • Facebook median engagement: images 5.20%, video 4.84%, text 4.76%, link posts lowest at 4.43%. Facebook is the most format-neutral platform in the set.
  • Reach vs engagement: across platforms, video and Reels win reach while images and carousels win engagement. Buffer's Facebook-specific Reels reach multiple was only in a search snippet, not checked.
  • What this means for us: use Reels to reach new people, image posts to engage the people we already have, and keep links out of the post body.

Playbook

  1. Make every Reel ours. Use our own narration (voice or on-screen text that adds the record's meaning), our own scans and photos, and our own renders. Never re-post third-party footage or a news clip, even with captions added: under Meta's rules that is unoriginal, and it is a copyright risk too.
  2. Use third-party material only as cited stills inside our own analysis. The new information is the point: what the record shows and why it matters. The source goes in the caption.
  3. Keep it vertical, 15-60 s, with a hook in the first 2 s. That hook is the record image plus a question. Burned-in captions are fine, because they are our own production.
  4. Keep links out of the caption. Put the shop link in the first comment, since link posts score lowest. Use UTM utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=reel.
  5. Alternate formats: a Reel for reach, then an image post (a record scan with a question) for engagement. Stay inside the 6-posts-a-day cap the bizdev runs already observe.
  6. Turn on content protection if the dashboard offers it, and check monthly for copies.
  7. Measure monthly in Meta Business Suite: Reel plays, 3-second views, average watch time, follows from the Reel, and link clicks (first comment, by UTM). If watch time is under 30% of length, shorten the next one.
