# Launching a new e-book with a discounted (or free) backlist title

Skill type: e-book launch and pricing. Read 26 Oct 2026 by the skills harvest.

## Evidence (BookBub's own partner data; BookBub Insights posts, read; post dates not shown, not checked)
From "How to Use Price Promotions to Market a New Book Release" (insights.bookbub.com):
- **89%** of BookBub partners who used a price promotion on an older book to market a new release sold more of the new book afterwards.
- **77%** of bargain readers also buy full-priced books.
- Series: discounting **book 1** gives a **5x** higher lift in sales of the other books than discounting any other book; making book 1 **free** gives **8x** higher sales of the rest than discounting it to $0.99+.
- Free promotions get **10x** the downloads of $0.99 promotions.
- Back matter: **3x** higher lift in sales of other books when the discounted book links to them in its back matter.
- Books with **150+** Amazon/Goodreads reviews going into a featured promotion see **15%** more sales.
- Steps it gives: pick the backlist book (book 1 of a series, or the most-reviewed standalone) -> pick the price -> update its back matter with the new book's cover, link and an excerpt -> line up emails, social and promotion-site applications before the date -> discount on every store at once -> raise the price back afterwards.
From "What's the Best Price for Your Next Ebook Promotion?" (insights.bookbub.com, read):
- Serious nonfiction tolerates higher discount prices (**$2.99-$4.99**) than fiction.
- Box-set deals: **20%** higher average click-through and **29%** higher purchase rate than single-title deals.
- (BookBub Featured Deals themselves are paid; a Reddit thread puts them at $500-$3,000 - not checked, and out of scope under zero spend.)

## Steps (zero-spend version)
1. Choose the "door" book: the one most likely to make a reader want the next (in a set, the first in reading order).
2. Decide the launch window: 5-7 days around the new book's release.
3. Door book price for the window: free (most readers) or a lower price (more revenue). Nonfiction can hold a smaller discount.
4. Back matter of the door book: one page for the new book - cover, two-line pitch, a short excerpt, one link to its page on our own shop (see ebook-back-matter-one-next-step).
5. Announce once to the email list and once on each social channel: "New: <title>. Start with <door book>, free/£x this week." Keep the end date real and keep to it (CMA false-urgency rules, black-friday skill).
6. Restore the price on the stated date. Measure: door-book downloads, new-book sales within 30 days, email signups, against the week before.
