YouTube Shows (serial) + Shorts series for a door-a-day documentary

Topic: package the Cedwyn documentary on YouTube as an official serial Show (seasons + episode numbers) and its 30-60 s cuts as a Shorts series, so each new scene feeds "Continue watching" and pulls viewers into the next one. Distinct from upload cadence (youtube-upload-cadence-6-hourly-scenes-frequency-vs-quality.md), chapters/captions, premieres and multi-language audio files, which are already indexed.

What the primary sources say (read 30 Oct 2026)

  • YouTube Help 16590581 "Create shows from your videos" (read): a Show is "a collection of high-quality episodes with a clear connection across episodes" (same characters or one long story, same intro and thumbnail style, good picture and sound; "videos with no visuals likely won't be considered a show"). Shows "may be eligible to appear in search as well as under 'Recommended shows' and 'Continue watching.'"
  • Serial vs non-serial: non-serial (default when you convert a playlist) lists newest first; serial = "intended to be watched in sequential order", listed oldest first. Choose serial for a chronology.
  • Studio > Content > Playlists > Options > Add show features. All videos land in Season 1; episode numbers follow the manual playlist order, else publish date. You must set a season number when adding videos. Edit numbers in Content > Shows > Episodes.
  • Artwork: two 16:9 images, JPG/PNG, max 10 MB, 1080p minimum (4K preferred): a banner/poster WITH the stylised title, and a backdrop WITHOUT title.
  • Shorts series: playlist of Shorts only, portrait or square, max 180 s each; viewers get a "watch series" button into a swipe feed of next/previous episodes; channel page shows the series as one thumbnail.
  • Feature a show on the channel Home: Customisation > Home > Add section > Single playlist.
  • YouTube Help 6084043 "Series playlists" (read): an official series playlist makes other videos in it "featured and recommended when someone is viewing a video in the series". Needs a verified account; a video can be in only one series playlist; only your own videos.
  • YouTube Help 3529123 playlist analytics (read): per playlist Overview/Content/Audience tabs plus "views from playlist", "playlist watch time", "playlist average duration"; Groups (up to 500 videos) for ad-hoc sets.
  • Mashable, 10 Jul 2026: launch of playlists-into-shows, at launch described as for YouTube Partner Program members. The current Help page does not repeat a YPP condition, so whether a non-YPP channel sees "Add show features" is not checked; look in Studio.
  • Business Insider, 23 Sep 2026 (Made on YouTube): Shorts series launched for any Shorts creator; YouTube's own figures: micro-drama format passed 6.5 billion views in H1 2026, watch time +50% YoY, TV views +90% YoY. YouTube's suggested freemium pattern: a few episodes free, the rest behind channel memberships (memberships need YPP).
  • Practitioner numbers on Show uplift for small channels: none found; treat any lift as not checked and measure it (below).

How to do it (checklist)

  1. One master playlist per language, never mixed: a video can sit in only one series playlist. With cy/en/es audio tracks on ONE upload, a single Show is enough; with three separate uploads, make three Shows ("Cedwyn" / "Cedwyn (English)" / "Cedwyn (Español)").
  2. Set the playlist to manual order = scene date order, then Add show features, choose Serial, Season 1 = scenes 1-x (seasons can map to eras later; renumbering is automatic).
  3. Upload both 16:9 artworks (title poster + clean backdrop) at 3840x2160, under 10 MB.
  4. Each episode: same 3-5 s ident, same thumbnail template with the door number, description first line = "Door N of 147 · " + link back to the scene page on the site.
  5. Shorts series: vertical 9:16 cut of each scene (max 180 s, ideally 30-60 s), playlist containing Shorts only, the same order; end card text "full scene: Door N" (Shorts carry one related-video link: point it at the full episode).
  6. Channel Home: Single-playlist shelf for the Show at the top.
  7. Measure weekly in playlist analytics: views from playlist, playlist average duration, and the share of episode-2+ views arriving from the playlist. Decide after 4 weeks; no claim of lift before that.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • The site already runs "one door a day" with 2 of 147 scenes out; the YouTube twin is a serial Show "Cedwyn", Season 1 = the scenes released so far, episode number = door number. Serial matters: the site's chronology is the story.
  • Doors are 720p landscape webm: upload an mp4 per door (720p is fine, Show artwork is the only 1080p+ requirement). The Shorts series needs separate 9:16 cuts; reuse the 30 s teaser cuts (documentary-teaser-cutdowns skill) re-framed vertical.
  • If the lip-synced cy/en/es versions become three separate files, either attach them as audio tracks on one upload (one Show) or make three language Shows; never put one video in two series.
  • Freemium mirror: scenes 1-2 are free on the site; on YouTube keep every episode public for reach (no YPP, so no memberships wall), and let the site's Academy/e-books be the paid layer. Revisit only if the channel reaches YPP.
  • AI disclosure still applies to every episode from scene 3 (ai-lip-sync-dubbed-documentary-disclosure-youtube-meta.md): Studio "Altered or synthetic content" = Yes.
  • Owner-side check: "Add show features" visibility and channel verification must be read in Studio on the connected YouTube account before promising it; record the answer in INDEX.md.
