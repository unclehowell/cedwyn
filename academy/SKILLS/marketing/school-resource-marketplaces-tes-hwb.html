School resource marketplaces: Tes and Hwb (Curriculum for Wales)

Skill date: 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 18:22). Status for Cedwyn: PROPOSAL only, nothing uploaded, nothing submitted.

Why this channel

Teachers look for ready-made local-history packs, and both places put a resource in front of them where they already search: - Tes (tes.com): the UK's big teacher marketplace. Free or paid packs, a public author shop, reviews. - Hwb (hwb.gov.wales): the Welsh Government's learning platform for every school in Wales. Resources are quality-assured by Adnodd before they go live. Free only, but it is the official shelf for the Curriculum for Wales.

A school pack is a free sample of the books in a form a teacher can use in class. It builds reach and credibility more than direct sales.

What the sources show

Tes Selling Resources FAQ (tes.com, read in full 26 Oct 2026): - Anyone 18+ with a Tes account can be an author. Most are teachers, but companies and Tes's own team publish too. - Royalty tiers on a rolling 12-month sales total, applied to the VAT-exclusive price: Bronze £0-£999.99 = 60%; Silver £1,000-£5,999.99 = 70%; Gold £6,000+ = 80%. Resources priced under £3 also pay a 20p transaction fee. - Resources must download from Tes itself, not send people to an outside host. Links inside the file are allowed only if they need no login, fit the lesson, and do not promote commercial or personal gain (a Creative Commons credit is the exception). - Don't split one resource into several uploads that make no sense alone. Give a clear title, learning objective and how-to-use note. - A free resource cannot later be switched to paid: it has to be deleted and re-uploaded, and its reviews are lost. - Max 200 MB per file; PDF, PowerPoint, Word, JPG and video are all accepted. Tes is the seller and charges VAT; the author handles their own VAT position on royalty income.

Tes author testimony, "Rocket Sheets" guest post on The Ginger Teacher (read in full): a £1 sale to a UK buyer leaves the author 30p at Bronze (17p VAT, 60% of 83p = 50p, minus the 20p fee). The author's fix: bundle so the price is at least £3, which removes the fee. Payout from £10.

Hard truth on income: a TeachingUK Reddit poster reported about £2 in six months of Tes uploads (search snippet, not checked). Lesson Planned (a rival marketplace, 2022 post, read) gives "top sellers over £2 million" and "thousands over £200 a month" with no source, so treat those as marketing, not checked.

Hwb guidance, "Resources to support the Curriculum for Wales" (hwb.gov.wales, first published 27 Jan 2026, read in full): - Resources sit under the six Areas of Learning and Experience. Local history goes under Humanities. - Every resource is reviewed and quality-assured by Adnodd before it is published. Check it against Hwb's publishing criteria, then use the "check and submit your resource" page. - Contacts: [address kept in the project workspace] (curriculum); [address kept in the project workspace] (resources and Adnodd's remit). Bilingual (Welsh and English) is Adnodd's stated aim.

How to do it (playbook)

  1. Build one strong free pack first. Tes reviews build up on free items, and free packs are the only route on Hwb. Contents: - a 4-6 page teacher sheet: objective, timings, curriculum link; - 3-5 primary-source cards (image plus transcription); - 5 enquiry questions; - a one-page timeline; - an answer sheet.
  2. Make it stand alone. No "buy the book to finish this". Tes forbids promotional links in files. Put the credit line on the cover only as plain text: the site name, with no sales wording.
  3. Paid items come later, priced at £3 or more (this avoids the 20p fee). For example, a 6-lesson unit as one bundle. Keep any free item free for good, because switching it to paid wipes its reviews.
  4. Write for teacher search. Use titles teachers type, like "Local history enquiry: a Welsh farm through 300 years of records (KS2/KS3, Humanities)". Fill in the age range, subject and objective fields.
  5. For Hwb: make a bilingual version (cy/en), check it against the publishing criteria, submit through the Hwb page, then wait for Adnodd's review. If it isn't a fit, email Adnodd first and ask.
  6. Measure: Tes downloads, reviews and author-shop views each month. UTM links don't work inside Tes files, so count teacher-led visits on the site through a short memorable URL printed in the pack (for example /schools/).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Proposal: a free pack, "Tŷ Mawr through the records" (KS2/KS3 Humanities).
  • Source cards: the 1824 David Stewart survey (first record mention found), a census page, a newspaper notice, and a field-name map.
  • Enquiry questions in neutral wording, with none of the banned words.
  • Proposal: Spanish and Welsh editions later. A Welsh edition is needed for Hwb. A Spanish one suits Welsh-Patagonia schools.
  • The account is the owner's decision. A Tes author account and the Hwb/Adnodd submission are made in the owner's name, or a company's. The public author name must not be the owner's personal name. Nothing is submitted until he gives the go-ahead.
  • Paid units come only after the free pack has reviews. Price at £3 or more as a proposal; current e-book prices stay unchanged.
  • Before any upload: a copyright check on every image (our own scans, or archive images whose licence allows reuse).
