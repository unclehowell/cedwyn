UTM discipline + GA4 funnel exploration for a small e-book site (2026)

Harvested 2026-10-26. Goal: know which email variant, post or page produced a signup, a read or a sale, without corrupting the data.

Rules that matter (with sources)

  1. Never put UTMs on internal links. Analytics Mania (Julius Fedorovicius): "Internal links (e.g. on your menu links) should never be tagged with UTMs … it can mess up your attribution." Optimize Smart: they override the original source/medium. A visitor who came from our outreach email and then clicks a UTM-tagged header strip gets re-credited to the strip, which destroys the A/B read. - For internal clicks, use a GA4 event instead: gtag('event','upsell_click',{placement:'header_strip',book:'<slug>'}).
  2. Lowercase everything, with one fixed vocabulary. GA4 is case-sensitive: Facebook and facebook become two rows.
  3. Medium decides the channel. From GA4's default channel group rules (support.google.com): - Email: source OR medium = email|e-mail|e_mail|e mail. (Our outreach links use utm_source=email, so they land in Email even with medium=outreach. Good.) - Organic Social: source in GA4's social-site list OR medium in social|social-network|social-media|sm|social network|social media. - Referral: medium referral|app|link. - Anything else becomes Unassigned. Example: utm_source=hubspot&utm_medium=newsletter is Unassigned, because neither value matches.
  4. Always send source + medium + campaign. Content = the variant (A/B), term = rarely.
  5. Keep a master sheet of every tagged URL (Analytics Mania). Ours is the project workspace plus the sendlog.

GA4 funnel exploration (once the property is live)

Explore → Funnel exploration. Up to 10 steps, built from events. Use an open funnel (people can enter at any step, since our emails deep-link mid-story). Breakdown: Session source/medium, then Session manual ad content (the variant). 1. page_view on a story or evidence page (big net) 2. upsell_click OR page_view where path contains /ebooks/ 3. sign_up (Mailchimp submit) / generate_lead 4. ebook_read (reader opened, free chapter) 5. begin_checkout (buy click → Stripe/Gumroad) 6. purchase (from the Stripe/Gumroad thank-you, or logged server-side in the project workspace) Mark sign_up, ebook_read and purchase as key events. Use "elapsed time" to see how long people take from story to signup. With about 17 UK visits a day the funnel will be thin: read it weekly, not daily, and expect GA4 thresholding to hide small rows.

Tool

  • tools/utm.py (standard library only, written 2026-10-26):
  • build <url> source medium campaign [content] lowercases the values, keeps existing query strings, and refuses internal-to-internal use when given --from-site cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate.
  • check <url…> prints the GA4 default channel each URL will land in, and flags uppercase, spaces, missing campaign, and UTMs on internal links (anything in the header/drawer HTML pointing at cedwyn with utm_*).
  • scan <html files…> finds internal links carrying UTMs. Run it on built site pages only: email templates link in from outside and should carry UTMs (a test run over the outreach templates correctly lists their tracked links, which is expected).
  • Note: the outreach engine writes utm_content in mixed case (e.g. T3b, as in ab.csv). Consistency is what matters for GA4, so keep the engine's case for that field. build lowercases values, so don't use it to rebuild engine links.
