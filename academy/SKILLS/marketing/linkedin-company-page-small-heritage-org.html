LinkedIn Page for a small heritage org: what a Page can and cannot do in 2026

LinkedIn so far has meant the personal profile (linkedin-requests-posts-heritage.md, linkedin-newsletter-one-tap-subscribers.md). This skill covers a Cedwyn LinkedIn Page: whether to make one, and how to run it cheaply. Researched 27 Oct 2026.

What the sources actually show

  • Invite credits cut. LinkedIn's help page (a547492): Pages get monthly invitation credits, shared across all admins and renewed on the 1st of each month. A change for free and Premium Pages is "gradually being made available". The Scott Partnership (2026) puts the free limit at 50 a month, down from 250 (-80%), with credits returned when an invite is accepted. Inviting followers of similar pages, or people who engaged, needs a Premium Company Page (paid, so not for us).
  • Pages are a small share of the feed. DSMN8 found company pages were 5.37% of the posts people actually saw. No organic Page post appeared in a feed without someone in the viewer's own network engaging with it first.
  • People beat Pages. Metricool 2026 (673,658 posts, 63,108 accounts): personal profiles get 63% higher engagement than company pages (via meet-lea.com, Apr 2026). DSMN8 H1 2026 (517,374 posts): original posts by employees got 9.2x the engagement of unedited reshares, and editing even a word or two gave 3x. In one engineering firm, the CEO's 5,000 followers matched the engagement of the company page's 300,000. van der Blom's Algorithm InSights 2025 (1.8M posts, via meet-lea): comments in the first 60-120 minutes drive distribution.
  • Cleverly (via meet-lea): personal 3.85% vs Page 2.1% engagement rate. A small-sample Refine Labs claim (7 profiles, "561% reach") is too small to quote as a fact.

How to do it

  1. Make the Page for legitimacy, not reach. A Page gives institutions, journalists and companies something to check, a logo for press, and something for people to list as their workplace. Fill in everything: tagline, about with search words, website with a UTM, location (Wales), and an industry such as "Museums, Historical Sites, and Zoos" or "Book and Periodical Publishing".
  2. Spend the 50 credits on purpose each month, on the 1st. Invite the first-degree connections most likely to accept: local-history people, archivists, Welsh-heritage contacts. Accepted invites return their credits, so invite in small batches of 10-15 and top up as they are accepted.
  3. Post on the Page once or twice a week. Reach comes from the person. The owner's profile writes its own post with a personal line and links the Page (@mention). It does not do a bare reshare (9.2x vs reshare; 3x even for a light edit).
  4. The first 2 hours matter. Post when the owner can reply to comments within 60-120 minutes. Ask a real question at the end, such as "does anyone hold the 1936 sale plan?"
  5. Use document posts (PDF carousels) for records. A 6-10 page PDF of one record's story, with source lines on each page and the e-book on the last page, works on both Page and profile.
  6. Showcase Pages, Premium Page, ads and paid invites: skip (zero spend; a Page this small gains nothing from them).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Gate: LinkedIn is not a connected app and there is no saved LinkedIn login, so the Page needs the owner to create it (or to add a login) and to be its first admin. Propose it once, as a decision: "worth a Cedwyn LinkedIn Page for credibility with institutions? 15 min of your time to create; I'll fill it in." Do not create it without his yes.
  • If yes: Page name "Cedwyn Estate", the about text from the site's own words (no owner name, none of the banned words), website link /?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=page.
  • Weekly loop: one Page post (a record PDF carousel or a site update), plus one personal-profile original post for the owner as a ready-to-paste draft. Never auto-post as him. Track Page followers and post impressions in the Sunday growth report.
  • B2B pitches to UK companies (b2b-pitch-uk-companies.md) can show the Page as proof of a real project. That is its main job for us.
