Podcast guesting for a heritage history (pitching hosts, 2026)

Skill harvested 26 Oct 2026. Outward step: every pitch is a HubSpot draft for the project lead's OK. Nothing is sent by an agent (same rule as press: only on the project lead's GO).

What the 2026 numbers show

  • Booking rates are low, and fit roughly doubles them. Podseeker measured 5,012 pitches sent 27 Feb–28 Jun 2026: 165 bookings, so 3.3% overall. For high-fit shows (85%+ fit score) it was 55 bookings from 894 pitches, 6.2%. Teams sending 200+ pitches a month got a median of 8 bookings a month. That is a vendor's own customer data, but the methodology is published.
  • Podchaser's 2026 pitching stats put the median acceptance rate at about 5%, roughly 1 in 20. The article blocked our fetch, so this figure comes from its search snippet.
  • publishing.co.uk (Robert Prime, UK indie-author service, updated 20 Sep 2026): 10–30% acceptance is normal for well-targeted pitches. It is strongest for non-fiction with expertise or a strong hook. It also notes that UK indie authors underuse podcasts compared with US authors, and that several writing shows have shut or closed to pitches (status checked 20 Sep 2026).
  • US Book Show panel, written up by Jane Friedman on 1 Jul 2026: start 4–6 months before a launch, because shows record weeks ahead. Pick niche shows. A host on the panel (Northwest Book Talk) books guests who will post the episode on their own site with a link back and share it on social media.

The pitch (Podseeker's five parts, about 100–150 words)

  1. Subject line names the conversation, e.g. "The farmhouse demolished in 1988: what the records show". Never "Podcast guest request".
  2. One real connection to the show: a specific recent episode or a recurring topic. Don't pretend to be a longtime listener.
  3. One focused episode idea the guest can carry for 30–45 minutes.
  4. One proof point: a dataset, a document or a credential, not a full biography.
  5. A low-pressure close: make it easy to say yes, no or "not now". Offer a one-sheet link instead of attaching files.

Also: follow each show's published guest guidelines exactly. Many reject promotional angles outright.

Process

  1. Build a shortlist of 20–30 shows: Welsh history, local and British history, archaeology and heritage, family history and genealogy, and Spanish-language Patagonia/Welsh-Argentine shows. Sources: Apple Podcasts category charts, Spotify search, and each show's own site. For each show, record whether it takes guests, how to pitch (form or email), the date of its last episode, and the host's name.
  2. Drop shows with no episode in 60 days, shows that never have guests, and shows closed to pitches.
  3. Score fit from 1 to 5. Only shows scoring 4–5 get a pitch, because high fit is what doubles the rate.
  4. Write one HubSpot draft task per show in the hive format. the project lead's sign-off is needed before anything is sent.
  5. At 3% to 6%, expect about 1 booking per 20–30 good pitches. Plan the effort for that.
  6. After recording, ask for the episode link and post it on Cedwyn: an "As heard on" line on /ebooks/ and the relevant story page. Use a UTM'd link to /ebooks/ in the show notes, e.g. utm_source=podcast&utm_medium=audio&utm_campaign=.
  7. Measure sessions arriving from each show's UTM, plus Stripe sales in the 14 days after each episode is released.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Bilingual reach: a Welsh-language pitch to Welsh-medium history shows, and a Spanish-language pitch to Welsh-Patagonian community shows (Wales/Argentina halves). Use the same evidence and translated subject lines.
  • Voice on air: the guest should present the Williams as a family of standing and cite documents. Outside parties' stature is tested, not assumed. No accusations against named people or companies. Avoid the banned public words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken).
  • Never name the project lead in pitch copy. The guest is whoever the project lead chooses. The HubSpot drafts leave the guest name as a placeholder for him.
  • Offer to listeners: the free Enwau names e-book, plus the £9.99 title and the £31.99 bundle at /ebooks/. Use one link in the show notes, not three.
  • KPI: 2 or more bookings from the first 30 pitches. Below 1 in 30, rewrite the angle before sending more.
