YouTube Hype: eligibility gate and first-week plan (2026)

What it is: viewers in eligible countries (UK included) can "hype" up to 3 new long-form videos a week, free. Each hype gives points; YouTube adds bonus points the smaller the channel. The most-hyped videos of the last 7 days sit on a non-personalised, per-country leaderboard. Hype refreshes Mondays 00:00 local time.

Facts (read at source, 26 Oct 2026)

  • Eligibility (YouTube Help 15333273, read): only long-form videos, uploaded in the last 7 days, from creators IN the YouTube Partner Program with 500 to 500,000 subscribers. Shorts cannot be hyped.
  • UK is on the availability list (read). Paid "Jewels" hype recognition only in Brazil, Japan, Korea, Taiwan.
  • Viewers who hype can share a post to their own Posts tab; top hypers get a monthly "Hype Star" badge (read).
  • OutlierKit (Apr 2026, read, vendor blog): launched in 39 countries Aug 2025; median channel under 35K subs across 112,562 channels in 20 niches (their dataset, not checked); paid hype tested in Brazil/Turkey (not checked).
  • No small-channel before/after numbers found that could be read: the Reddit threads (r/PartneredYoutube "Has the hype feature impacted your views") are blocked from this box; snippets only, so no figure is used here.

How to do it (once eligible)

  1. Gate: YPP accepted AND subs >= 500. Below that, Hype does not exist for the channel: do nothing, no "hype us" asks (viewers will not see the button).
  2. Plan uploads so the 7-day window starts on a Monday or Tuesday (fans get fresh hypes at Monday 00:00; a Monday upload gets a full week of fresh hypes).
  3. One clear ask per long-form video, in the first 48 h: end of video + pinned comment + community/Posts tab + email list: "If this helped, the Hype button (under the video, this week only) helps small channels most."
  4. Ask your most engaged people first (email list, commenters); hype is limited to 3 per person per week so a small warm list matters more than reach.
  5. Measure in Studio: traffic source "Browse features" and impressions for days 1-7 vs the previous 3 long-form uploads. Keep if browse impressions rise; otherwise drop the ask (it costs viewer goodwill).
  6. Never buy or swap hypes; leaderboard is per-country and any manipulation falls under the Terms/Community Guidelines.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • The channel is about 7 subscribers (index, 26 Oct) and not in YPP: Hype is NOT available. Status: parked. Re-check at 500 subs + YPP (monthly line in the growth report).
  • Until then the levers already in the index apply (end screens/playlists, Posts tab, test & compare titles, chapters/captions).
  • When eligible, PROPOSAL (needs the owner's GO, as it adds an email ask): one "hype this week" line in the newsletter for flagship long-form uploads only, e.g. the 1824 David Stewart survey film (first record mention found) or the 1667 film (1974 newspapers; ITV News 1988). Upload on Mondays.
  • Wording rules: no banned words, never name the owner.
