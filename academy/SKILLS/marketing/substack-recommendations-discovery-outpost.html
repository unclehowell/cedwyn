Substack as a discovery outpost (Recommendations network) alongside Mailchimp

Skill for: newsletter growth through a platform network rather than our own traffic. Read 27 Oct 2026. Data is dated 2022; newer figures not found in this run.

Evidence (primary, read)

  • Substack, "Substack's most powerful feature is growth" (29 Sep 2022, read): the network drove 40%+ of all subscriptions on the platform and 12% of paid.
  • Mechanisms: Recommendations (writers suggest other writers in the subscribe flow, on homepages and in automated emails), referrals, and post-page subscribe prompts.
  • Real testimony quoted there:
  • Lenny Rachitsky (31 Aug 2022): Substack drove 72% of his free and 10% of his paid subscriber growth.
  • Gergely Orosz (1 Sep 2022): network brings ~70% of free and ~10% of paid new subs.
  • Both are large publications. Small-list results are not checked.
  • Cost: free to publish free posts. Substack takes 10% only of paid subscriptions (Orosz's tweet, read). Subscriber export as CSV is a known Substack feature (not checked today).

How to do it (practitioner pattern)

  1. Create a free Substack under the brand (not a person's name). Same topic promise as the Mailchimp newsletter.
  2. Post a short excerpt version of each story, ending with the full piece on our own page. - Our site stays canonical: if a full post is mirrored, set Substack's canonical URL to our page where the setting exists (see syndication-canonical-noindex-medium.md). Not checked whether Substack offers it today.
  3. Recommendations are the engine: recommend 3-5 genuinely related history newsletters (Welsh history, local history, Patagonia). Ask them to recommend back only after 4+ posts exist.
  4. Join Notes (Substack's feed). Reply usefully on others' history posts. No link-dropping.
  5. Every month, export Substack subscribers and import them to Mailchimp with tag src-substack only if they opted in to our list. Otherwise keep the lists separate.
  6. Gate: after 8 weeks, keep it if Substack adds ≥20% of our monthly new subscribers (our own threshold); otherwise freeze it.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • It's a new public channel, so a PROPOSAL for the project lead (append to the project workspace). Not created by any run.
  • If approved:
  • The brand is "Cedwyn Estate". Posts are 3-line hook excerpts of research-section stories, each linking to the Cedwyn page and /ebooks/.
  • Editorial rules apply: never name the project lead; banned words; no insignia; nothing about family holdings.
  • Candidates for recommendations get checked first (topic fit, active in 2026).
  • Measure "Substack" as a source in the HDYHAU survey (self-reported-attribution-hdyhau-survey.md) and through the Cloudflare referrer substack.com.
