# St Dwynwen's Day (Dydd Santes Dwynwen, 25 Jan): a small Welsh-only gifting/free-book moment
Harvested 2026-10-28 (skills harvest). Topic: seasonal campaign / Welsh audience / e-book promotion. Index had 0 hits for "dwynwen".

## What the sources show
- **The day:** 25 January, Welsh patron saint of lovers (and of sick animals); Visit Wales promotes it as Wales' own Valentine's Day, popular "throughout Wales" and "little known" elsewhere (Visit Wales page, read).
- **Owner testimony (BBC News Wales, "Could St Dwynwen's Day be a big money-spinner for Wales?", read; article is from Jan 2016):** CadwynGifts (Pencader) - "It's not a big money-spinner at the moment ... not much publicity"; they sell "many more" love spoons for Valentine's than for Dwynwen; gifts sell better where Welsh is widely spoken. Gwin Dylanwad (Dolgellau) ran a Dwynwen offer (fizz, meringues, a rose, paper-heart message wall): "We are trying to push it. Not so many people know about it." Lesson: it is a **reminder to an existing Welsh audience**, not a source of new buyers - small but low competition (three weeks before Valentine's, January lull).
- **Agency playbook (Cambrian Web, Lowri Faber, 15 Jan 2025, read):** use it to break the dead weeks between Christmas and Valentine's; add a Welsh twist (love spoons, the legend, Ynys Llanddwyn); bilingual posts; can be folded into Valentine's plans. Advice, no numbers.
- **The heritage-body pattern (Cadw, read):** Cadw publishes a **free e-book "Santes Dwynwen: Welsh patron saint of love"** (EPUB/MOBI/PDF, Heroes and Heroines of Wales series) and posts it on X each 25 January ("Perfect reading for St Dwynwen's Day ... Download the FREE e-book"). A free e-book plus the day's hashtag is how a Welsh heritage publisher uses the date.
- Overlap check: Calan Gaeaf, St David's Day, Christmas gifting and the anniversary calendar are covered elsewhere; nothing in the index covered 25 Jan.

## How to do it
1. Put 25 Jan in the content calendar; prepare by 15 Jan.
2. One bilingual (cy/en) post per channel on 25 Jan with #DyddSantesDwynwen #SantesDwynwen, tied to a real story from your own material (not the legend retold).
3. One email to the list only if there is a real reason (a free book, a gift path) - not a discount by default.
4. Track with a UTM (utm_campaign=dwynwen) and compare clicks with an ordinary January send.
5. Do not invent "tradition" assertions; cite Visit Wales/Cadw for the legend if retold.
