Self-guided audio walk of Llandough on VoiceMap (paid) and izi.TRAVEL (free): a second product built from the e-book research

What the sources say (read 2026-10-30)

  • VoiceMap publishing plans (docs.voicemap.me, read):
  • The Basic plan is free. It includes the MapMaker CMS and a tour editor and audio engineer: script review and editing (7-day turnaround), audio clean-up, a cover image, and SEO on the title and description.
  • Every tour gets QR codes, a short URL, a promo design pack, keyword research and a group marketing consultation.
  • A Viator/TripAdvisor listing is a $29 add-on on Basic. Voucher codes allow private distribution; download credits cost $2.50 each, with 10 included per tour.
  • Paid plans: Priority $35/month (Viator/TripAdvisor included), Pro $75/month (free tours, AR/360, Klook and Musement), Premium $188/month.
  • Free (unpriced) tours need Pro. On Basic the tour has to be sold.
  • Royalties (VoiceMap forum post from staff, Nov 2025, read): the standard royalty is 50% or 65% (which applies when is not checked). There was a promotional 80% for named destinations until 30 Jun 2026; it has expired. Staff say first royalties often arrive "within just a few weeks", especially "in smaller cities… with steady visitor demand but limited guided tour options".
  • Case studies (docs.voicemap.me publisher case studies, read; all are VoiceMap's own reporting):
  • TellBetter Tours (NYC) moved from his own white-label app: 83% of his 2022 sales came from VoiceMap, 17% from his own app.
  • Annie Sargent (podcast): sales 6× in 2022 vs 2021; half her listeners buy more than one of her 5 tours.
  • Lynn Momboisse: earnings 5× in 2020 vs 2019, then 4× in 2021.
  • Johnson County Library: a 90-minute history drive built from interviews and a book. This is the closest match to our records-based tour.
  • A VoiceMap YouTube talk puts typical tour prices at $15–$30 (search snippet only, not checked).
  • izi.TRAVEL (app store listings and the izi help centre, read):
  • free CMS and app with about 25,000 tours in 2,500 cities;
  • outdoor tours and "Free Walking" mode;
  • text-to-speech voice-over in the CMS;
  • QR codes, a website widget, visit statistics, reviews, quizzes, sponsor slots;
  • paid content is supported (terms not checked).

Use it for a free bilingual taster walk that links to the paid VoiceMap tour and the e-books.

The playbook

  1. Route: 45–75 minutes, 8–12 stops, public roads and footpaths only. Each stop is one record: a date, what the document shows, and a 60–120 s script.
  2. Write from the e-book chapters. The research is already done; a tour script is about 1,500–2,500 words. Record it in English first, then Welsh (and Spanish later). VoiceMap's editor reviews the script for free on Basic.
  3. Publish on VoiceMap Basic (free) at a price in the $8–$12 range (below the $15–$30 typical; this is a short village walk). Add the $29 Viator/TripAdvisor listing only after the first 10 sales.
  4. Free taster on izi.TRAVEL: a 3–4 stop "Llandough in 4 records" in cy/en using the CMS text-to-speech or our own voice recording. Its last stop points to the full VoiceMap walk and to /ebooks/. Embed the izi widget on a Cedwyn page.
  5. Cross-sell both ways. The e-book's last page gets "Walk it: QR → VoiceMap". The tour's final stop gets "Read the records: /ebooks/". This is Annie Sargent's "half buy more than one" effect.
  6. Distribute: QR code on the guide handout (see wales-tourist-guides-wotga-cpd-partner-route.md), the walking-festival listing (vale-of-glamorgan-walking-festival-valeways-heritage-walk.md), and Visit Wales/Visit Cardiff listings where self-guided tours are accepted (not checked).
  7. Measure monthly: VoiceMap downloads and royalties (CSV export), izi visit statistics, and /ebooks/ visits with utm_source=voicemap|izi.

Rules that apply

  • No family holdings or addresses: the farm and its house are not stops, and nothing points walkers onto private land. Say "former manorial house" where needed.
  • Scripts are public text, so the editorial file applies: never name the project lead; no banned words; no insignia; no accusations about named people.
  • Archive images in the tour: the NLW licence bars material alteration and has term and print-run limits; Glamorgan re-use fees apply (see archive-image-permission-terms-ai-animation-paid-versions.md). Prefer our own photos and reconstructions.
  • Publishing a new paid product and setting its price is a PROPOSAL for the project workspace (the project lead decides). Zero spend: Basic and izi are free; the $29 add-on needs his OK.
  • Creating accounts on VoiceMap or izi in the project lead's name is outward. Only do it on his GO.
