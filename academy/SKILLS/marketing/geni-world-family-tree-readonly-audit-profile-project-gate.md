## Evidence
- **Scale (geni.com blog, read):** the World Family Tree passed 200 million profiles (post of 10 May 2025); the home page preview now states "over 210 million". It is one shared tree: each historical person should exist once, and duplicates are merged.
- **Curators (Schoenblog, written by a Geni curator, read):** "almost 200 volunteer curators", experienced genealogists who usually have added 5,000+ profiles; they merge duplicates and resolve disputes. Profile managers and discussions on a profile are the route to fix errors.
- **Free tier (Geni home page and Geni help answers, search previews):** Basic accounts include unlimited profiles, merging duplicates, collaboration, Consistency Checker and 1 GB media; full search is a paid (Pro) feature; public profiles can be viewed free. Pro is paid: out of scope under the never-pay rule.
- **Etiquette (Geni project "Working with the World Tree", preview):** "Many users consider it rude to create duplicates of profiles that already exist on Geni." Check before adding.
- **Projects (geni.com preview):** a project groups profiles, descriptions, photos and discussions in one place. There is a "Glamorganshire - Main Page" project family including "Historic Buildings of Glamorganshire, Wales", "Historic Glamorgan" and "People Connected to Glamorganshire" — the natural home for a manorial farmhouse page.
- **Search visibility (site: search, read today):** Geni profiles and projects rank for place + surname searches: a site:geni.com search for Llandough Glamorgan Williams returned profiles naming Llandough (a 1959 death at Llandough; a Williams widow who "settled at Llandough", 1761). Which Llandough is meant is not checked.
- **Access note:** geni.com project and profile pages answered our plain fetch with a bot wall (Incapsula), so project contents were not read; the facts above are from the blog (read) and search previews.
- **No traffic numbers** found for referrals from Geni profiles to outside sites. Treat reach as not checked.

## Why it matters
- People researching Llandough, Leckwith or Williams lines land on Geni profiles from search. A correct, sourced profile is where a stranger first meets our family; a wrong one spreads into every tree that copies it.
- A building project entry gives the house a page that genealogists browse by county.
- Merged, sourced profiles are reused by other trees: accuracy compounds.
