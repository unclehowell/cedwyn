YouTube channel Home tab: trailer for new visitors, Spotlight for subscribers, sections as the documentary shelf

Harvested 2026-10-30 for the documentary push (the project lead 13:42: "mention the Premier documentry in the making and scenes are free to watch now"). Premieres/watch-page trailers are covered in youtube-premieres-archive-films-decision.md (watch-page trailers need >1,000 subs) — the CHANNEL trailer below has no such gate.

What YouTube documents (Help 3219384, "Customize your YouTube channel layout", read 30 Oct 2026)

  • Channel trailer (for people who haven't subscribed): Studio > Customization > Home tab > Add section > Channel trailer. By default no ads run on it unless it contains third-party claimed content. Once a viewer has watched it, it is not shown to them again.
  • Spotlight / featured video (for returning subscribers): same path > Add section > Spotlight; can be any video on YouTube; also not shown again once watched.
  • Featured sections: up to 12 custom sections; default layout is 4 (Short videos, Uploads, Created playlists, public Subscriptions). Section types: For you (personalised mix; can restrict to last 12 months), Videos (videos/Shorts/lives), Playlists (single/created/multiple), Channels, Posts, Top community clips, Memberships, Effects. Drag to reorder; Home tab can be toggled off (keep it on).
  • Practitioner context (r/NewTubers thread, read via search snippet only, not a primary source): many small channels leave both slots empty; nothing measured — treat as a gap we can fill cheaply.

How to do it

  1. Trailer slot = the single best "what is this channel" video, short enough to finish (a finished trailer is never shown again to that viewer, so its job is one shot at a stranger). Prefer 60-120 s; open on the strongest image in 1-2 s; end on "Subscribe for the next scene" + where to read more (spoken/on-screen; links on Home aren't clickable from the video).
  2. Spotlight = the newest documentary scene (rotate on each new scene so subscribers land on what's new).
  3. Sections, in order: (1) "The documentary — scenes in date order" playlist; (2) "The story in 60 seconds" Shorts playlist in chronological order; (3) "Archive films" playlist; (4) Uploads; drop public Subscriptions if empty. Chronological playlists matter for a history channel: the shelf reads as a book.
  4. Channel profile links (up to 14, clickable beside Subscribe): first link cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/ebooks/ (UTM'd), second /movie/.
  5. Check monthly: Analytics > Traffic source "Channel pages" views and subscribers gained from the channel page.
