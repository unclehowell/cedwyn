# Heritage-site learning packs for Welsh learners: the Cadw "Deg Diwrnod Diddorol" model + the Learn Welsh resource library

Harvested 2026-10-30 (skills harvest run). Topic: product/content format – a free, level-graded heritage pack that turns a place's history into Welsh practice, as a lead magnet and a learner-community door.

## The evidence (read 30 Oct 2026)
- **Cadw "Deg Diwrnod Diddorol" (Ten Interesting Days)**: free PDF booklets for adult learners, one per national course level – Mynediad (Entry), Sylfaen (Foundation), Canolradd (Intermediate), Uwch (Advanced) – in North and South editions (8 PDFs). The South set (launched 10 Aug 2024) covers 40+ historic sites from St Davids to Chepstow, Gower to Abergavenny.
- Format: a "day" per site; QR codes link the booklet to online activities and videos (also on a YouTube channel); history, stories, quizzes and conversation prompts; **each day drills specific grammar points taken directly from the Dysgu Cymraeg course units**. Usable on site, in class or online.
- Reach/testimony: "used by Welsh learners worldwide, as far away as Australia" (Welsh Government press release). Tutor: "Diolch arbennig [i'r tîm] sydd wedi rhoi'r adnodd arbennig yma at ei gilydd". Learners: "full of local history expressed in useful Welsh", "a springboard for other trips on my own". Member: partner used the Canolradd booklet, "helped him learn new words… will help him read the Welsh information at your sites", now wants Uwch. (Cadw page + gov.wales release, read; no download counts published.)
- **Learn Welsh Online Resource Library** (National Centre for Learning Welsh, learnwelsh.cymru): searchable library filtered by level (Entry → Proficient, Taster), dialect (North/South), category (Reading, Podcasts, Families, Music…) and type (Book, Audio, Video, Website, App, **Partner**). Each entry states level and *the course unit it suits* – e.g. "Chwedlau Cymru: Y Tylwyth Teg", Amdani Entry, "suitable for learners who have completed Unit 19 of the Entry level course", with buy links (Local Welsh Shop, Ffolio). Whether outside/self-published resources can be submitted: not checked (no submit form found; "Partner" type exists). The Centre had 20,445 learners in 2024-25 (see welsh-learners-audience skill).

## Why it works (the pattern)
1. **Level is the product.** Learners pick by level, and tutors pick by course unit. A resource labelled "after Unit 19" gets used in class; one labelled "for learners" does not.
2. **Place + language practice** gives tutors a ready lesson and a trip; the history is the hook, the grammar is the reason to keep it.
3. **Free PDF + QR to video/audio** keeps the cost near zero and makes it shareable in learner groups worldwide (diaspora classes use it remotely).
4. Progression pull: a user finishing one level asks for the next – a natural sequence for email capture.

## Playbook
1. Pick ONE place and ONE level first (Sylfaen/Foundation is the widest useful band). 4–6 short pages: a 120–200 word Welsh text, a 15-word geirfa with English, 3 conversation prompts, a 5-question quiz, one archive image with source line, QR to a short audio/video reading.
2. Map grammar to the course: name the unit/patterns used (tutors check this first). Have a fluent Welsh speaker or tutor check the language before release – mark "language not checked" until then.
3. Publish as a free PDF on our own page with a short email opt-in *optional* (download must work without it), plus the audio.
4. Distribute through existing learner doors only: SSiW Book Club thread reply, learner Discord/Facebook groups by their rules, Merched y Wawr learners' nights, local Learn Welsh providers (Learn Welsh Cardiff / Vale) – each a HubSpot draft on owner's GO.
5. Ask the National Centre how a resource enters the library (one question, no pitch).
6. Measure: /r/ campaign path per door; downloads per level; opt-ins; clicks to /ebooks/. Stop rule (ours): < 25 downloads in 60 days = don't build the other levels.

## Gates / don'ts
- No machine-translated Welsh in a learner resource; a wrong mutation in a teaching pack costs trust with tutors.
- Images: only items with a clear reuse licence (see archive-image licensing skills); source line on each.
- Don't copy Cadw's name/branding or imply endorsement; this is our own pack in the same format.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Build (no spend):** "Diwrnod yn Llandochau" – a Sylfaen-level 5-page pack on the farm and parish from published Cedwyn evidence pages (neutral third person, never "tenant farmers", no living family names, no banned words), geirfa + prompts + quiz, QR to the existing Welsh documentary scene with its level badge. Language check by a fluent speaker before publishing: PROPOSAL to the owner (who checks it).
- Reuse it as the "free Foundation-level page" already proposed in the Welsh-learners and SSiW skills – one asset, three doors.
- **HubSpot draft (owner's GO):** one-line enquiry to the National Centre about listing in the resource library.
- If downloads pass the stop rule, add Mynediad and Canolradd versions; then a paid "learner edition" e-book bundle = PROPOSAL.
