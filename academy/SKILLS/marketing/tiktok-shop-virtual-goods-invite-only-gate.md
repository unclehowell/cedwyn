## What TikTok's own policy states
TikTok Shop US Seller University, "Virtual Goods Requirements", dated 09/01/2026, applies to the United States (read 30 Oct 2026):
- "Selling virtual goods is limited to **invite-only sellers**. Sellers must receive approval before listing or offering any virtual goods."
- Permitted for invitees (the list is "non-exhaustive"): software, digital games, in-game content, game and other digital subscriptions, online courses, virtual gift cards, virtual tickets and concert tickets.
- Subscriptions must be **prepaid**: "Recurring auto-billing subscriptions are not permitted."
- Online courses: assertions must be realistic. "Stored-value components, including course credits and gift certificates, are not permitted." In course clips used to promote a listing, "the creator must remain clearly visible on screen."
- **E-books and pay-per-view video are not named** on the permitted list.

UK:
- The UK Restricted Products Policy page (seller-uk, read) lists categories that need supporting documents. A search of its text found no virtual-goods or e-book section.
- I could not reach a UK virtual-goods page. Search previews (not checked) say virtual products sit on the prohibited list for the US, UK and Europe.
- Treat the UK position as **not checked, probably not available**.
