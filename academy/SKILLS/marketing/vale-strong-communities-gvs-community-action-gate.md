# Vale of Glamorgan Strong Communities Grant + GVS Community Action Fund: local small-grant gate

Harvested 2026-10-30 04:26Z. GATE + playbook. Read in full: the Vale of Glamorgan Council's Strong Communities Grant page.

## What the source states (read 30 Oct 2026)
- **Strong Communities Grant Fund (SCGF)**, Vale of Glamorgan Council:
  - Open to community groups, the voluntary sector, and town and community councils, for initiatives inside the Vale.
  - The 2025-26 pot was **£180,000**, in grants of **£5,001 to £15,000**.
  - **At least 20% match funding.** "Value for money is a key assessment factor... demand to be high."
  - **Now closed.** The last round decided in June 2025, with projects complete by 31 Mar 2026. No 2026-27 round is posted yet (it was the fifth round when it last opened).
  - How to apply: email a short paragraph to the SCGF team first. They check eligibility early and only then send the form.
  - Aims include seed funding with a sustainability story, consultation and feasibility work towards future bids, and kit and equipment.
  - The guidance and progress reports (2020, 2022) contain case studies with costings.
- **GVS** (Glamorgan Voluntary Services) runs the **Third Sector Small Grants Scheme, Community Action Fund**, for projects **up to £5,000**. Enquiries go to GVS.
- A third-party snippet (not checked) mentions a Vale micro-fund of £100-£400 for small community projects.
- No award rate was found.

## Generic playbook
1. Send the eligibility paragraph early. It is the cheapest test and the council invites it: project, who benefits in the Vale, cost, match.
2. Show the 20% match. In-kind volunteer time is usually accepted, but **check the guidance PDF first**; this is not confirmed.
3. Pitch value for money and life after the grant. The fund wants projects that cut future reliance on grants.
4. Use the progress-report case studies to size the budget.
5. For under £5k, go to GVS instead.
6. Watch for the next round: the page, the council's Facebook posts, and the GVS news page.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Not eligible as it stands.** It needs a constituted community or voluntary group, and individuals can't apply. This is the same vehicle decision already raised for Awards for All Wales and CFW (the project lead's call; a PROPOSAL via the programme office).
- **Hard rule:** a community council is an eligible applicant here, but **never** approach or partner with the community council (standing rule). Only a separate group would apply.
- The fundable shape is the one shared across the grant gates: free bilingual "Llandough memories" sessions (oral-history recording, scanning photos and papers, a guided walk, a small exhibition). The £5k GVS route fits a first pilot. SCGF's £5k-£15k fits a second year with "feasibility for future bids". E-books stay outside the grant.
- Zero-spend action now: none outward (contact nobody). Add one line to the project workspace: "Vale SCGF (£5,001-£15k, 20% match, email-first; closed, watch for 2026-27 round) + GVS Community Action Fund (<=£5k): needs a constituted group."
