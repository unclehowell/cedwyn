GATE: TikTok Shop for e-books, scenes and the Academy (not a route today)

Added 2026-10-30 (skills harvest). Why: TikTok is a connected channel, and 'tiktok shop' had 0 hits in the index. The existing TikTok skill (tiktok-search-discovery-small-history-account.md) covers the bio link gate (1,000 followers or a Verified Business Account) but not selling inside TikTok.

What TikTok's own policy states

TikTok Shop US Seller University, "Virtual Goods Requirements", dated 09/01/2026, applies to the United States (read 30 Oct 2026): - "Selling virtual goods is limited to invite-only sellers. Sellers must receive approval before listing or offering any virtual goods." - Permitted for invitees (the list is "non-exhaustive"): software, digital games, in-game content, game and other digital subscriptions, online courses, virtual gift cards, virtual tickets and concert tickets. - Subscriptions must be prepaid: "Recurring auto-billing subscriptions are not permitted." - Online courses: assertions must be realistic. "Stored-value components, including course credits and gift certificates, are not permitted." In course clips used to promote a listing, "the creator must remain clearly visible on screen." - E-books and pay-per-view video are not named on the permitted list.

UK: - The UK Restricted Products Policy page (seller-uk, read) lists categories that need supporting documents. A search of its text found no virtual-goods or e-book section. - I could not reach a UK virtual-goods page. Search previews (not checked) say virtual products sit on the prohibited list for the US, UK and Europe. - Treat the UK position as not checked, probably not available.

Decision rule

  1. Selling on TikTok Shop needs three things: a UK seller account, an invitation, and a permitted category. We have none of these today, so don't spend time on it.
  2. Recheck only if TikTok publishes a UK virtual-goods page, or someone gets an invite. Pages to check: seller-uk.tiktok.com, search "Virtual Goods Requirements".
  3. If it ever opens, the only fit is the Academy as a prepaid fixed-term pass, never the £49.99/mo recurring plan, which TikTok's rule bars. That pass would be a new product, so it is the project lead's call.
  4. Until then, TikTok's job is discovery. Purchases happen on /ebooks/ and /learn/ via Stripe/Gumroad. The bio link gate (1,000 followers or Verified Business Account) is the real next step on TikTok.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • No TikTok Shop listing, no seller application. This also follows the rules: prices are fixed and new channels go to the project lead as proposals.
  • TikTok captions point to the site with the shop line (purchases via /ebooks/), as on YouTube Shorts. Don't promise "shop on TikTok".
  • If the Academy ever lists anywhere invite-only: the creator-visible rule conflicts with the AI-twin narrator plan, so the disclosure rules in ai-founder-digital-twin-intro-disclosure-youtube-meta-tiktok.md apply.
  • Recheck date: with the Sunday growth report, at most monthly.
