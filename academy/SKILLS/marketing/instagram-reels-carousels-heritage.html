Instagram: Reels for reach, carousels for saves (heritage account playbook, 2026)

Harvested 2026-10-26. Evidence first, then steps.

What the data states

  • Buffer, State of Social Media Engagement 2026 (millions of posts): on Instagram Reels get 36% more reach than carousels, but carousels earn 12% more engagement. Reels also got 125% more reach than single images. "Instagram behaves like two platforms."
  • Socialinsider (quoted by blckalpaca, Oct 2026): average follower engagement rate 0.48% in 2025 (down 24% YoY); carousels 0.55%, Reels 0.52%. Metricool: carousels get about 9x the saves of single images.
  • Adam Mosseri, Head of Instagram (via Hootsuite 2026): the top signals for both connected reach (followers) and unconnected reach (non-followers) are watch time, likes and sends (shares by DM). Check sends per reach and likes per reach, not raw likes.
  • Mosseri's basics for a Reel to be recommended: strong hook, captions on, audio, original and not watermarked (a TikTok/CapCut watermark or recycled clip is down-ranked), 3 minutes or less, and the account in good standing (Account Status).
  • Our own baseline (8.md): best post so far is the c.1880 armour image (84 views); one Reel reached 101. That's tiny, so any test result under about 300 reach is noise.

The playbook

  1. Split the jobs. Reels find strangers. Carousels turn people who are already looking into savers and clickers. Per week: 2 Reels + 1 carousel + Stories on most days (Stories reach only followers, so use them for the link sticker).
  2. Reel recipe (15-45 s): - 0-2 s: the hook, as on-screen text plus voice. Lead with the strangest true fact: a date, a number, a document. "This 1824 map is the first time anyone wrote this farm's name down." - 2-30 s: one record on screen, a slow pan or zoom, captions burned in. - End: a question that makes people send it on ("Know someone from Llandough? Send them this"). - Export clean, with no third-party watermark.
  3. Carousel recipe (5-10 slides, 4:5 portrait 1080x1350): - Slide 1 is the hook. It must work as a single image, because the feed may resurface slide 2 to people who skipped slide 1. - One idea per slide; a document crop with a 1-line caption. - Last slide: "Save this for your next visit to the Vale" plus the shop pointer ("full story: bucklerfamily.estate/ebooks", typed — the bio has no link). - Saves are the carousel's job; ask for them.
  4. Caption: first 125 characters carry the hook (that's what shows before "more"). Use 3-5 specific keywords that people actually search for (Llandough, Vale of Glamorgan, Welsh history, Cardiff history). Instagram search now reads captions, so keywords beat a wall of hashtags.
  5. Link path: captions have no links. Use the bio link (a deep page, never the homepage) and the Story link sticker. Change the bio link to match the week's post, with UTM tags (utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=). This is an off-site link, so UTMs are correct here.
  6. Trial Reels (Professional accounts): Instagram can show a Reel to non-followers only first. Use it to test two hooks on the same clip without spamming followers. Keep the winner.
  7. Measure weekly (the project workspace): reach split (followers vs non-followers), sends per reach, saves per reach, profile visits, and link-in-bio clicks from GA4/Cloudflare. Kill a format after 4 posts with no movement, not after 1.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • The weekly quote-pack flywheel (Tue 19:00) already makes carousels. Add the save CTA on the last slide, make slide 1 stand alone, and put 4:5 crops of the records inside.
  • The daily Cedwyn movie scene gives free Reel material. Cut a 20-40 s clip with burned-in captions and a hook card from each door, post it as a Reel (watermark-free), and send it to the movie page via the bio link.
  • Hook bank (evidenced facts only, from the title chain and evidence catalogue): the 1824 Bute survey plan 19 "Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm"; the 1987 Court of Appeal case (wording: "BP, registered proprietor from 1982/83"); "Peerless" Jim Driscoll and Marconi/Lavernock as local hooks that bring in non-history audiences.
  • Public text rules apply: never name the project lead; banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken); no accusations about named people.
  • Success for phase 1 is follows, saves, sends and bio clicks, not sales (the project lead's funnel, 26 Oct).
