Publit and Publizon for a one-title UK publisher: fees, reach, and why neither gets La visita to Spain cheaply

Harvested 31 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). kobo-plus-vs-nextory-spanish-edition-subscription-gate.md found that Nextory takes small publishers only through Publit or Publizon (support.nextory.se, 28 Oct 2026). This file reads both on their own pages. Proposals only: nothing was signed up for.

What the pages say (read 31 Oct 2026)

  • Publit (get.publit.com, Swedish):
  • E-book retail: Adlibris, Bokus, Apple Books and Bokon. "Publit takes a commission fee of 10% of the wholesale price when a book is sold."
  • Streaming and libraries: these need the "Fixed Price Digital Books" agreement, from SEK 495 a month excl. VAT for up to 10 editions. It reaches Storytel, BookBeat, Nextory and libraries via OverDrive. The publisher keeps 100% of what the services pay, and the 10% retail commission falls away under this agreement.
  • Settlement: quarterly.
  • ISBN: a Publit-assigned ISBN may catalogue the edition with Publit as publisher at the National Library of Sweden. Using our own ISBN avoids that.
  • Fit for a foreign account: the privacy text names the Swedish Tax Agency and the "personal identity number" among the data it holds. Whether a UK company without a Swedish ID can sign up is not checked.
  • Spain: the page doesn't say whether the Nextory or Storytel feeds go to their Spanish catalogues. Not checked.
  • Publizon (publizon.com, Danish, run by WeDoBooks):
  • Joining fee: "Joining Publizon's platform costs €349 excl. VAT. It is a one-time cost" (publisher signup page, read).
  • Running costs (/prices, read): a monthly title fee per active title, a transaction fee on the net price of each sale, and a streaming fee per title per service per month, each capped per country. The amounts aren't published ("Contact us"; the price examples read "xxxx").
  • Libraries: listing in Biblio (SE/FI) and eReolen (DK) libraries is free.
  • Coverage (FAQ, read): "At the moment we cover Denmark, Sweden, Norway and Finland". DACH and English-speaking markets are "soon". Spain isn't covered.
  • Nextory Spain exists: Nextory launched in Spain after buying and merging Nubico (Cision press release, read as a snippet). Its signup country list includes Spain. The route to that Spanish shelf for a small UK publisher is unclear on both distributors' pages.

Numbers for one title

  • Publit Fixed Price: SEK 495 a month ≈ SEK 5,940 a year excl. VAT. At roughly SEK 13 to the pound (rate not checked today) that's about £450 a year, recurring, for Nordic subscription reach.
  • Publizon: €349 up front plus unpublished monthly fees, Nordic only.
  • For comparison: StreetLib One is €299 once and lists Nextory, Storytel and BookBeat, plus Gandhi and Casa del Libro (streetlib-one-299-eur-no-free-plan-gandhi-listed-bajalibros-not.md).
  • At the estimated ~€0.10 per full read on subscription shelves (Kobo Plus file), Publit's £450 a year needs about 5,000 full reads a year to break even. For a Spanish heritage guide in Nordic markets, that won't happen (our estimate).

How-to (decision rule for any subscription aggregator)

  1. Write down which countries' catalogues it feeds, not just the brand names. "Nextory" in Sweden is not Nextory in Spain.
  2. Price it per title per year, including recurring fees, and divide by a realistic per-read payout to get a break-even read count.
  3. Prefer one-off fees to monthly ones for a single-title backlist.
  4. Use our own ISBN, so the national catalogue lists our imprint.
  5. Check signup eligibility (tax ID, country) before any form is filled in.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • PROPOSAL 1 (no spend): skip Publit and Publizon for La visita.
  • Publizon doesn't cover Spain.
  • Publit costs about £450 a year recurring, sells only through Swedish stores plus Apple (which we can reach directly), and states no Spanish reach.
  • Neither meets the "Spanish-speaking readers in Spain, Argentina and Mexico" goal.
  • PROPOSAL 2: if subscription shelves are ever wanted, StreetLib One (€299 once, owner's decision) dominates both. Record that in the project workspace beside the StreetLib proposal; don't add a second one.
  • PROPOSAL 3 (zero cost, later): an English or Welsh-interest title could go free into Nordic libraries via Publizon's Biblio/eReolen. That needs the €349 joining fee, so it's spend for the owner; it is noted only, not recommended.
  • Cuts against: neither distributor's per-read figures are published, and the Nextory Spain route remains unread. A direct question to Nextory Spain about publisher intake is an owner-sent email, not drafted here.
