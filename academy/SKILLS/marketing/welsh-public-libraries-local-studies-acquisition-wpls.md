## What the sources say (read 29 Oct 2026)

**Welsh Public Library Standards, 7th framework 2025-28** (Welsh Government PDF, read)
- QI on acquisitions: authorities must buy at least **250 items per 1,000 population** a year below 25,000 people, **225** at 25,000-50,000, and **200** above 50,000.
- QI A5, Welsh-language resources: at least **4% of the materials budget** must be spent on Welsh-language material.
- Electronic downloads (e-books, e-audio, family-history resources) are reported per service.
- Entitlement WPLSCE 8: access to high-quality Welsh-language resources.
- So every authority has a yearly buying target and a Welsh-language spend floor. A Welsh / bilingual local title helps them meet both.

**CILIP Local Studies Group toolkit: "Book stock"** (localstudieslibrarians.org, read)
- Local studies libraries choose whether, and on what criteria, to collect works by local authors.
- "Creative works set in the area": most local studies libraries would want to include these.
- Collections need multiple copies of heavily used titles for current and future use and for branches.
- Librarians rely on intelligence from branch staff with community contacts, and fill gaps from dealers' lists.

**Vale of Glamorgan Libraries** (valeofglamorgan.gov.uk Family and Local History page, read)
- The local and family history collection runs through Barry and Penarth libraries: trade directories, census transcripts, free Ancestry/Findmypast.
- Penarth Library advertises local and family history support. Its role address is [address kept in the project workspace] (Penarth Library Facebook post, snippet).

**US counter-example** (sspl.org policy, snippet)
- Some library systems do not buy unsolicited or unreviewed self-published books.
- Reviews in established journals are the usual gate. review-copies-welsh-local-history-journals.md covers that route.

## The playbook

1. **Make the title easy to buy:**
   - ISBN;
   - print edition available through standard library supply (via the Books Council of Wales Distribution Centre or Gardners/IngramSpark);
   - e-book on BorrowBox;
   - a one-page title information sheet: title, ISBN, price, format, pages, subject (local history, Llandough / Penarth / Vale), a Welsh-language or bilingual element if there is one, and one review quote.
2. **Offer free reference copies to local studies.** Two print copies to the local studies collection: one for reference, one for loan or branch. This follows the toolkit's multiple-copies point. A donated reference copy is the low-friction first step. Ask for it to be catalogued under the place name.
3. **Ask the authority to buy for branches.** Send the information sheet to the library service's stock/acquisitions contact, noting the acquisitions-rate QI and, for a Welsh/bilingual title, the 4% Welsh-language floor. Keep it to one paragraph with one attachment.
4. **Pair it with an event.** A talk at Penarth or Barry library (and Cardiff Central Library Hub) gives branch staff the community intelligence the toolkit states drives selection. It also puts the book in front of local readers.
5. **Get one review first.** A Morgannwg / local society journal review answers the self-published gate.
6. **Follow up once after 4 weeks**, then stop.

## Kill rule

No catalogue record within 3 months of the reference copies arriving: stop chasing purchase and keep the reference copies as the outcome.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn

- **Only once a print edition with an ISBN exists.** Until then the e-book route is BorrowBox (see the gate skill). This skill is not a reason to print.
- **Draft, then the project lead's GO.** One HubSpot draft task ("[BIZDEV] Penarth Library local studies: reference copies + stock sheet"), addressed to the role address only ([address kept in the project workspace]) and to Cardiff Central Library local studies. the project lead's sign-off; never sent by us.
- **Information sheet built from the e-book metadata**, at /ebooks/library/ as a printable page. Bilingual title and blurb via the free Welsh translation route (Helo Blod skill), so the title counts toward the 4% Welsh-language spend.
- **The two reference copies cost money** (printing and postage), so they are a proposal in QUEUE-after-framing.md. Zero spend until the project lead approves.
- **Measure:** catalogue search for the title on the Vale and Cardiff library catalogues monthly (absence watch), recorded in the change-watch log.
