# MetaFilter Projects: one self-link post for an open local-history archive (measured, $5 signup = owner GO)

Written 31 Oct 2026 for the Cedwyn e-book and archive work. Topic: a long-running, link-sceptical community that has exactly one place where members may post their own work.

## The rules, from MetaFilter's own pages (read 31 Oct 2026)

- **Signup is a one-time US$5**, paid by PayPal. It is the only cost. (The new-user page offers a waiver for real hardship. Don't use that route for a project account.)
- **Self-links are banned everywhere except Projects and Music.** A self-link in a front-page post means removal and a ban with no refund. Ask MetaFilter removes "thinly veiled promotion" and bans the accounts involved.
- **The new-user page is blunt:** "MetaFilter is not a site for self-promotion, SEO, PR, marketing, or advertising… If you're joining specifically for any of these purposes, stop now."
- **Projects has no posting interval** (MetaFilter front page: 12 h; MetaTalk: 1 week). New accounts wait **one week** and must comment in a front-page thread before posting there; Projects has no such wait.
- **How a project spreads:** members vote it up, or press **"post this project to mefi"** on its page. That puts it on the main MetaFilter front page, posted by **someone else**, which is the only way our link can ever reach the front page.

## What a Projects post actually gets (our count from the live pages, 31 Oct 2026)

- **Volume:** the Projects front page held about **50 posts from 3 Sep to 30 Oct**, so roughly 25 a month. Each post stays visible for weeks, not hours.
- **Votes, top post per month** (Popular page): Oct **4**, Sep **10**, Aug **11**, Jul **8**, Jun **10**. Most posts get **0–3 votes** and **0–5 comments**.
- **Close analogues that did well:**
  - "Happy Foot Sad Foot sign (a Wikipedia article)": a member's article on a local landmark, **10 votes** in June.
  - "Babel Nexus: a public domain repository for books with reading trails" (June).
  - "A Book Of Small Science Fiction", **7 votes** (Jul). "New PlanetSlade paperback recalls glory years of UK punk, pub rock & ska", **4 votes** (Jul), from a member who posts there regularly.
- **The honest read:** this is a small, thoughtful, long-lived audience, not a traffic source. What it can bring is a handful of careful readers, comments from people who know archives, and a slim chance a member puts it on the front page. We have no measured click-through figure. The pages show votes and comments, not visits.

## How to do it

1. **Be a member first.** The account holder reads and comments in ordinary threads (history, Wales, archives, maps) for **at least 3–4 weeks** before posting. A first-day account whose only act is a self-link reads as exactly what the new-user page warns against.
2. **Post the free, open thing, not the shop.** Link to the free record pages (a timeline, the evidence catalogue, a free first-edition e-book), never to /ebooks/ or a price.
3. **Title it like the good ones:** say what it is, plainly, with the place. For example: "An open archive of one Welsh farm's records, 1500s–1980s, with every source page linked".
4. **Body: 2–3 sentences, then "more inside".** Say what you made, why, and what's unusual (scans, a parish-by-parish timeline, bilingual). End with a real question for MetaFilter readers ("what would you want from a site like this?").
5. **Reply to every comment** for the first 48 hours, as the maker, warmly and briefly. No follow-up links to the shop.
6. **Once only.** One Projects post per real milestone (for example a complete, new section), months apart. Never ask anyone to vote or to "post it to mefi".
7. **Tag the link** with `utm_source=metafilter&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=projects` so any visits show up.

## Gate

- **Before:** the owner approves the $5 spend and the account. The account holder has commented for 3+ weeks.
- **After 30 days:** keep the account for occasional ordinary participation if the post drew **3+ votes or 3+ comments, or 10+ tagged visits**. Otherwise make no further Projects posts. A front-page pickup by another member is a bonus, never a goal.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn

- **Status: PROPOSAL needing the owner's OK.** The US$5 signup is a spend, and the account must be a real person who takes part. Agents never sign up, pay, post or comment.
- **What agents prepare:** a draft title, a 3-sentence post and "more inside" text pointing to the free record pages, plus 3 example threads where the account holder could usefully comment. Keep it in the folder until the GO.
- **Public text rules apply:** never name family members, use none of the banned words, no addresses or holdings, no accusations about named people. The post describes the archive, not the case.
- **Best timing:** when a free, finished section is live and checked (for example the book-search CSV page or the timeline once its broken links are fixed). A post that sends careful readers to a broken page wastes the one shot.
