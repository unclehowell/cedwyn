YouTube Partner Program ladder 2026-27 and Hype: what a 6-subscriber history channel can and can't unlock

Harvested 27 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). Every rule below comes from a YouTube page I read in full that night. No creator testimony with numbers was readable (Reddit's thread on Hype results blocked the fetch), so there are no outcome figures for Hype here: measure our own.

The facts (YouTube's own words)

Expanded YPP (UK is on the list) — Help 13429240 and the YPP creators page: - Tier 1 (fan funding + Shopping): 500 subs + 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days + either 3,000 qualified long-form watch hours in 12 months or 3M qualified Shorts views in 90 days. Unlocks Channel Memberships, Gifts, Supers (Super Thanks/Chat/Stickers), Shopping, Creator Partnerships. - Tier 2 (ads + Premium revenue): 1,000 subs + 4,000 watch hours (12 months) or 10M Shorts views (90 days). - Needs: 2-Step Verification on the Google account, ONE AdSense for YouTube account created inside Studio, channel follows the monetisation policies. Review "typically in about 1 month", the whole channel is reviewed.

Change announced 10 Aug 2026 (YouTube blog), live 1 Feb 2027: - New applicants for ads/Premium revenue need 8,000 qualified watch hours (365 days) or 20M qualified Shorts views (90 days). Existing partners are not affected. - Fan Funding and Shopping entry thresholds stay unchanged (so Tier 1 above still stands). - Shorts ad/Premium revenue sharing only for channels with 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days; below that, Shorts earn nothing from ads but "milestone" incentives (Shopping bonuses, brand-deal incentives, trend boosts) are promised, details not published. - Practical meaning: the ads tier is now a long way off for a small channel; the realistic prize is Tier 1 (memberships, Supers, Hype eligibility). Long-form watch hours are the honest route; Shorts views don't count toward watch hours.

Hype — Help 15509925, 15334500, 15333273; blog 18 Sep 2024 and 26 Aug 2025: - Only for channels in YPP with 500–500,000 subs; UK is one of 39 countries. - Viewers hype long-form videos in their first 7 days; up to 3 free hypes a week each. Not eligible: Shorts, unlisted/private, made-for-kids, age-restricted, videos with copyright assertions, live streams/Premieres while live (the archive is eligible afterwards). - Points carry a small-channel multiplier (Help 15333273 example: a hype on a 500,000-sub channel = 50 points; a small channel's hype is worth far more). Weekly country leaderboard, top 50, by topic, refreshed every few minutes, same for everyone in the UK; "Hyped" badge for 7 days; the hyper's hype is posted publicly to their own Posts tab. - YouTube: hyping does not influence recommendations or search. The gain is the leaderboard, the badge and fans' public posts. - Beta scale (blog, Sep 2024): 5M+ hypes across 50,000+ channels in 4 weeks; 75%+ of 2,500 surveyed viewers (US/JP/DE) said they want to help small creators grow. Scale, not an outcome per channel.

Monetisation review gate: "inauthentic content" (policy page, renamed 15 Jul 2025): not allowed — "image slideshows, templated storylines or scrolling text with minimal or no narrative, commentary or educational value" and "AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates, giving the impression of mass production". Reused content (archive clips without substantive commentary) is reviewed channel-wide; one bad batch can block the whole channel.

How to do it (routine)

  1. Read the gate monthly (Studio > Earn, or the Data API channel stats): subs, public long-form uploads in 90 days, watch hours 365 days. Log one line in the project workspace Don't apply before both numbers clear 500 subs and 3,000 hours.
  2. Grow watch hours, not Shorts views. Shorts are discovery (and their links aren't clickable: see youtube-shorts-non-clickable-links-related-video-bridge.md); long-form watch time is what counts toward the 3,000-hour gate. Each Short points to a long-form video through Related video.
  3. Pre-review clean-up (before applying): go through the channel as a reviewer would. Any upload that is a still slideshow with read-out captions, or an auto-made templated clip with no narration or insight, gets narration and context added, or is set to private. Titles and descriptions should say what is original (our research and voice, which records were used).
  4. When eligible for Hype: publish long-form on a fixed weekday; in the first 48 hours ask the email list and community "if it was worth your time, tap Hype under the video (free, 3 a week)". Never offer anything in return for a hype (that is clickbait/incentive territory); never ask for hypes on Shorts.
  5. Measure: Studio mobile shows hypes, points and the weekly data story; compare 7-day views of hyped against non-hyped uploads over 6+ uploads before believing it.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • The channel stat on file is 6 subscribers (an older JSON in work/, not re-read tonight), so YPP, Hype, memberships and Supers are all out of reach for now. The working target is Tier 1 (500 subs + 3,000 long-form hours). After 1 Feb 2027 the ads tier needs 8,000 hours, so don't plan revenue on ads.
  • Risk to fix now, not later: the movie and auto-social pipeline produce OmniHuman talking shots and still-image clips. Each upload needs real narration and a distinct educational point (the policy's own test), or it can block the whole channel at review. Note this in the movie RUNNER as a check, not a change of method (the project lead chose OmniHuman).
  • Until 500 subs: put effort into long-form "door" videos (scene episodes, record explainers) and the Related-video bridge, not into Hype asks.
  • Nothing here costs money. Applying for YPP needs the project lead's AdSense account and 2-Step Verification: that's his decision when the gate is met (a PROPOSAL, not an action).
