Spotify for Authors: getting an audiobook edition onto Spotify (and the redemption-code launch)

Date: 2026-10-26 (skills harvest 23:24). Status for Cedwyn: PROPOSAL only. Publishing on a new storefront is the owner's decision. It depends on an audio file, and the route to that is google-play-auto-narrated-audiobooks-en-es.md.

What the evidence shows (primary pages read 26 Oct 2026)

  • Direct publishing (Spotify for Authors blog, 1 Aug 2025, read): self-published authors can upload an audiobook, set up a payment profile and publish straight to Spotify. The dashboard shows geography, demographics and "affinity" data (which podcasts and authors your listeners also play). Wider distribution is an opt-in through Voices by INaudio from the same upload. Royalty rate for direct uploads: not checked (not on the pages I read). UK eligibility for direct upload: not checked.
  • AI-voice audio is accepted (Spotify newsroom, 20 Feb 2025, read): ElevenLabs-narrated audiobooks go to Spotify through Findaway Voices after a standard review. Spotify also accepts Google Play Books digital-voice audio. Every such title gets this first line added to its description: "This audiobook is narrated by a digital voice."
  • Free creation tools (Spotify for Authors blog, dated 9 Sep 2026, read): ElevenLabs voices, up to 5 audiobooks free, no exclusivity, English only. They are open to self-published authors in the United States only, so we cannot use them from the UK. Spotify's figures on the same page: 40 million+ e-books exist worldwide against about 1 million audiobooks.
  • Redemption codes (Spotify for Authors blog, dated 2 Sep 2026, read): royalty-free codes that unlock the audiobook on Spotify, including before release day (advance copies for reviewers). The suggested uses: giveaways for newsletter subscribers, reader magnets, book-club packs, a countdown page listeners can pre-save, a book playlist, and an embeddable web player with a "Listen on Spotify" button.
  • Market size (Spotify newsroom, 15 Oct 2025, read): Spotify quotes Publishers Association data that UK audiobook revenue hit a record £268m in 2024, up 31% year on year. More than half of eligible Premium users in English-language markets have started an audiobook, and 52% of Spotify's audiobook audience is 18-34. Premium includes 15 hours of audiobook listening a month (spotify.com, snippet only; UK plan details not checked).
  • One author's real numbers (Amie McNee, via her husband's Substack, read): two self-published backlist novels from 2021-22, with no promotion. In Q1 2025 Spotify listening was 519.8 full-book equivalents, up 440% on Q1 2024 (118). Audiobook royalties were USD 1,535 in Q1 2025, against USD 537.53 a year earlier. That is about USD 3 per full listen on Spotify, compared with USD 1.97 per Audible credit and about USD 5.1 per library listen. Audio was about 70% of her indie revenue. His caveat: Spotify pays from a pool, so the payout per listen falls as total listening rises. His explanation for most of the growth is discovery inside Spotify's genre shelves. This is one author in a different genre (historical romance), so it is not a forecast.

How to do it (steps)

  1. Make the audio first. From the UK the realistic free route is Google Play auto-narration (English and Spanish), or a human or ElevenLabs file you already own. Check that Google Play's terms allow the file to be sold elsewhere (the Play skill notes a condition that it must also stay on sale on Play).
  2. Create a Spotify for Authors account and check whether direct upload is offered for a UK payment profile. If it is not, use Findaway Voices or INaudio. Pick the retailers deliberately and leave out any that bar digital voices.
  3. Metadata: use the same title chain and subjects as the e-book, the digital-voice disclosure, and a 3000x3000 square cover (square cover spec not checked for Spotify).
  4. About 3 weeks before release, set up the countdown page so listeners can pre-save.
  5. Send redemption codes in small batches: newsletter subscribers who replied, ARC readers (arc-team-zero-cost-advance-readers.md), two or three reading groups (book-club-reading-group-guide-heritage.md), and Welsh-Patagonian societies for the Spanish edition.
  6. After launch, read the affinity data monthly. It tells us which podcasts our listeners already play, which feeds podcast-guesting-heritage-history.md.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Best first title: a narrative, low-image e-book with the text already finalised. Image-heavy evidence catalogues are a poor fit for audio.
  • Bilingual angle: a Spanish audio edition puts Welsh Patagonia on a platform that Chubut listeners already use. Welsh is not offered by Google auto-narration. Note it as a gap; do not fake it.
  • House rules collide: Cedwyn has no outbound links, so no Spotify embed or "Listen on Spotify" button on Cedwyn pages. Any mention of the Spotify edition stays as plain text on /ebooks/, or lives on social channels and in emails.
  • Price ladder: direct /ebooks/ sales stay the main route at GBP 9.99. Spotify is a discovery channel that pays per listen, not a replacement for direct sales.
  • Codes as a list-builder: "Reply to get a free listening code" in the welcome sequence gives a reason to reply. Replies help deliverability (reader-replies-and-small-community.md).
  • Gate: hold until (a) the owner approves a new storefront, and (b) one title's audio file exists. Queue the proposal in the project workspace, not here.
