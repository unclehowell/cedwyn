The town and the parish¶

There are two places called Llandough in the Vale of Glamorgan. Tŷ Mawr stood at Llandough-juxta-Penarth (CF64), beside Penarth and Cardiff. Llandough near Cowbridge (CF71) is a different community about 17 km to the west, with its own Great House Farm. The forms below are the names the records use for the town, the parish and the authorities over it.
