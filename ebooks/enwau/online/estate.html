The estate and the parcels¶

The wider estate, its woods and valleys, and the parcels the farm was later divided into each carry several names and numbers. A title number or a plan number is a name for a piece of land, not proof of who held it. Parcels A and B are our labels for the two parts of the farm, used throughout our case.

The Cedwyn estate¶

Source: Book of Llandaf, pp. 202 and 279, in Evans and Rhys, The Text of the Book of Llan Dav (1893)
Written by: Scribes of the Book of Llandaf

Our note: The Old Welsh form of the personal name Cedwyn. Neither place is Llandough: it shows the name, not this estate.

Cydfin Farm or Ty Mawr Farm, (107 a.) · No Tŷ Mawr date: the range 1552–1824 is the Bute catalogue’s

Source: NLW Bute Estate Records D219 catalogue

Our note: A Bute estate catalogue entry. Its heading and its date range are the cataloguer’s; the leases have not been read. It is not a dated mention of Tŷ Mawr, and its application to Tŷ Mawr and its dating are under forensic examination.
