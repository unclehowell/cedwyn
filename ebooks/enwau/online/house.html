The house and the farm¶

Every form below names, or has been taken to name, the house and farm beside St Dochdwy’s Church at Llandough-juxta-Penarth. Tŷ Mawr is Welsh for “great house”. The first record we have found that names Tŷ Mawr is David Stewart’s 1824 survey of the Bute Glamorgan estates, plan 19, “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm” (EV-0211). Forms dated earlier come from Bute or other third-party papers and catalogue headings; each is shown with its date and source, and each is under forensic examination, not counted as a record of Tŷ Mawr.
