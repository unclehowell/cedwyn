0.1.0 (v0.01, free) — 26 October 2026¶

First edition. 108 entries in three parts (the house and the farm; the town and the parish; the estate and the parcels), a chapter on plays on names and dates, and the sources. Built from the Cedwyn archive’s Nomenclature register (version 1.0.1), the Cedwyn e-book’s name records, and the Ordnance Survey FOI correspondence of June–October 2026.

Open for v0.02: the originals of “Ked Fen” and “Red Fen”; the TNA OS 35/2503 name books; any record of 1667; the 1881 census image; the RCAHMW deposit naming “Ty Mawr; Great House Farm”.
