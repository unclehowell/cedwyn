Forensic Assessment — Ministry of Justice

Scope: MoJ as present policy/records oversight, not as the historic court deciding the Great House Farm dispute.

Role

The MoJ may be relevant to historic judicial-record policy, retention architecture and any current administrative channel concerning justice-system records. It is not treated as having decided the 1955, 1962, 1974 or 1987 merits.

Forensic control test

Separate policy responsibility from individual court administration. If a historic file cannot be recovered, identify the actual custodian and retention rule rather than attributing its absence to MoJ.

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the Ministry of Justice. This is not a finding that no MoJ-related records or functions are relevant.

Human action

Use MoJ only where HMCTS identifies a policy/retention issue requiring departmental escalation. Preserve relevant retention schedules and formal responses.

Acceptance: any MoJ role is tied to a named policy/function and concrete record.
