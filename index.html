Cedwyn · Reconstruction workspace

Architecture

Plans, dimensions, materials, photographs and geometry belong in one source-linked workspace. This app is designed to support the proposed Cedwyn Estate reconstruction in Argentina, south of Puerto Madryn, while keeping each measured fact separate from an interpretation.

Candidate 0.1.0 Source-led Catalogue-linked

Recording rule

No dimension is treated as measured unless it points to a source artifact and records units, measurement method and confidence. Derived geometry and illustrative alternatives remain clearly labelled as such.

Floor plans

Drawing register

Store each plan by level, orientation, scale, source, revision and drawing status. Keep measured plans distinct from proposed layouts.

Elevations and sections

Vertical geometry

Index facade elevations, section cuts, roof profiles, floor heights and the evidence supporting each line or dimension.

Dimension schedule

Units required

Record value, unit, source location, measurement method, confidence and whether the number is measured, derived or illustrative.

Materials and fabric

Specification

Connect material descriptions, visible surface clues, repair notes, construction assumptions and proposed equivalents to their sources.

Photo register

Image provenance

Use catalogue identifiers and original image links. Record viewpoint, date when known, depicted area and whether the image supports geometry or material interpretation.

Geometry and massing

Model inputs

Describe footprint, roof volume, openings, orientation, proportion and relationships between spaces before committing to a 3D model.
