Response-status terms

  • Disclosed / partially disclosed — cite the letter and identify the documents supplied.
  • Refused — cite the decision, legal provision and review or appeal route.
  • No substantive response / review pending — record the date and procedural status; neither status proves the underlying complaint.
  • Closed — cite the final decision or acknowledgement on which closure is based.

Record the request as sent, each enclosure, date of receipt, response wording and next applicable deadline. Do not substitute a paraphrase for the original correspondence.
