Your request has been handled under the FOIA. 
 
I can confirm the MOJ does not hold any information in the scope of your request. 
This is because the information you have requested, if held, cannot be obtained from 
the administrative systems held for the public authority purposes of HMCTS and 
MoJ.  
 
If the cases took place, any records that still exist may be limited - because of their 
age - and would be held by Glamorgan Archives.  Such information is not held by the 
MoJ, and so not subject to the FOIA.  
 
The FOIA does not oblige a public authority to create information to answer a 
request if the requested information is not held. The duty is to only provide the 
recorded information held.  
 
To advise and assist you, information about how to contact Glamorgan Archives can 
be obtained from the link below: 
https://glamarchives.gov.uk/
