The purpose of an Internal Review is to assess how your FOI request was handled in the first 
instance and determine whether the original decision was correct. This review has been 
conducted independently by a reviewer who was not involved in the original handling of your 
request. 
 
The original response stated that the Ministry of Justice (MoJ) does not hold information within 
the scope of your request for the purposes of HMCTS and MoJ administrative functions. While 
records of this nature may previously have been held by the court service, in accordance with 
applicable records management and retention schedules, court records are retained for 
specified periods and may subsequently be destroyed or transferred to an approved place of 
deposit. 
 
Having carefully reviewed the handling of your request and the rationale for the original 
response, I am satisfied that the original decision was correct and compliant with the 
requirements of the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA). 
 
Statutory Deadline 
 
The statutory deadline for your request was the 3 September 2026, and the response was 
provided on the 12 August 2026. Therefore, the response was compliant with the timeliness 
requirements of the FOIA.   
 
Outcome 
 
An FoIA Internal Review re-considers all aspects of the original response, including the decision-
making process; therefore, in the interests of fairness, I have considered the original response 
provided to request FOI 260813002 alongside the decisions made to provide this response.   
  
I have also considered relevant Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) guidance, which can be 
found in full here: Freedom of information guidance and resources | ICO and here: How to 
access information from a public authority | ICO.  
 
Having completed my review, I remain satisfied that the Ministry of Justice does not hold 
recorded information falling within the scope of your request for the purposes of section 
1(1)(a) of the FOIA.

I have also considered the age of the records requested. In accordance with relevant retention 
schedules, such records may have been destroyed or transferred to an approved place of 
deposit following the expiry of the applicable retention period. 
 
The FOIA provides a right of access to recorded information held by a public authority at the 
time a request is received. It does not require a public authority to create information, recreate 
records, or obtain information from another organisation in order to respond to a request. 
 
As the records referred to in your request are 37 years old, it is likely that any records which 
were retained beyond their operational purpose would either have been lawfully disposed of in 
accordance with retention arrangements or transferred to an archive repository.  
 
Accordingly, I remain satisfied that the Ministry of Justice does not hold the requested 
information for the purposes of section 1(1)(a) of the Freedom of Information Act 2000. 
 
In accordance with our duty to provide advice and assistance under section 16 of the FOIA, 
you may wish to contact Glamorgan Archives, which may hold historical records transferred 
from HMCTS. Further information can be found on the Glamorgan Archives website.
