Thank you for your correspondence of 21 July 2026. 
 
The ICO is responsible for enforcing and overseeing Data Protection 
legislation, the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA), the 
Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (the EIR) and related 
legislation. We are unable to assist with matters of 
maladministration or similar issues. 
 
FOIA gives individuals the right to access recorded information 
held by public authorities. The EIR gives individuals the right to 
access environmental information held by public authorities. FOIA 
and the EIR do not require public authorities to generate 
information or provide explanations in order to respond to requests 
for information.  
 
From your initial complaint to us, it appears that you would like our 
assistance and referral to “appropriate referral bodies” and for us to 
us ensure that certain records are preserved for a legal matter. 
Unfortunately, these matters do not fall within the our remit and so
Read more
we are unable to take further action in relation to these aspects of 
your complaint. 
 
It also appears that you would also like us to consider whether 
certain public authorities should hold information that they have 
told you they no longer hold. Unfortunately, we cannot decide 
whether a public authority should hold particular information as it 
does not fall within our remit. We can only consider whether a 
public authority has disclosed the recorded information it actually 
holds within the scope of a request made under FOIA or the EIR. 
 
If you would like to complain about a public authority’s handling of 
a specific request for information that you have made under FOIA or
