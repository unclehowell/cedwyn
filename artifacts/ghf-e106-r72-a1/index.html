This email is a request for information retrieval of recorded information captured by the following NPCC 
FOI request, forwarded to you in your capacity as staff officer/representative to the NPCC policing

To remove any further doubt, I now expressly request the following recorded information under the Freedom 
of Information Act 2000. 
 
A. 1988 operation 
 

 
 
 
- "SAS Style Operation" in ITV footage not published until 2025 - https://youtu.be/4GXmR7mAas0 
 
Any recorded information held by the NPCC, NaCTSO or any relevant NPCC function concerning the eviction, 
policing, security arrangements, occupation, enforcement of possession or demolition of Great House Farm, 
Llandough, South Wales, in 1988. 
 
This includes, where held: 
 
- operational orders or plans; 
- briefing material; 
- intelligence assessments; 
- incident or operational logs; 
- correspondence; 
- reports; 
- minutes; 
- debriefs; 
- photographs or other recorded media; 
- records identifying participating or supporting organisations; 
- records concerning the use or proposed use of specialist, armed, military or security personnel; 
- and records concerning liaison with other public authorities or private organisations. 
 
B. BP Properties Ltd v Buckler 
 
Any recorded information held concerning BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2, insofar as the case, 
the parties, Great House Farm, or the resulting possession/enforcement operation was considered by NPCC, 
NaCTSO, ACPO or any predecessor/successor national policing structure. 
 
I am particularly interested in any recorded material connecting the judgment with the subsequent 1988 
operation. 
 
C. Police and security involvement 
 
Any recorded information identifying, or describing the involvement of: 
 
- South Wales Constabulary; 
- South Wales Police; 
- ACPO or any ACPO committee, working group or national policing function; 
- specialist police units; 
- military personnel or military units; 
- private security companies, bailiffs or enforcement agents; 
- or any other police, security or government body 
 
in planning, intelligence, security, support or execution of the 1988 operation. 
 

 
 
 
I am not asking the NPCC to confirm that any particular allegation is true. I am asking whether records 
concerning these matters exist and, where not exempt, to communicate them. 
 
D. Predecessor and successor records 
 
I recognise that the NPCC did not exist in 1988. 
 
The relevant national predecessor organisation was the Association of Chief Police Officers (ACPO), which 
existed from 1948 until 2015. 
 
I therefore request any recorded information held by the NPCC concerning: 
 
1. records inherited, transferred or otherwise acquired from ACPO which refer to the matters above; 
 
2. any ACPO committee, working group, national policing portfolio or other predecessor body which dealt 
with the relevant matter; 
 
3. any later NPCC or NaCTSO record which refers to, indexes, summarises, preserves, archives or otherwise 
records the existence of earlier material concerning the 1988 operation. 
 
I am not suggesting that NPCC or NaCTSO themselves conducted the 1988 operation. 
 
The purpose of this part of the request is to establish the chain of custody and present location of any 
surviving national policing records. 
 
E. Searches and record location 
 
If you conclude that no substantive records are held, please confirm whether the appropriate NPCC functions 
and repositories were searched, including the Counter Terrorism Coordination Committee and any relevant 
historical/predecessor record holdings. 
 
Please also confirm, where possible, whether relevant records were: 
 
- transferred to another police force; 
- deposited with a local or national archive; 
- transferred from ACPO to another body; 
- destroyed under a retention/disposal schedule; 
- or otherwise ceased to be held by NPCC. 
 
I am not asking you to create new information or conduct an investigation. I am asking for information that is 
already recorded, including records showing the transfer, retention or disposal of relevant records. 
 
The ICO's records-management guidance recognises the importance of records showing where information 
was transferred, archived or destroyed, including the applicable disposal arrangements.

Our integration

NPCC response to FOI 3204/2026. It gives the search terms, repositories and teams used for questions 1–3, states questions 4–6 are not held, and supplies, outside the Act, the retrieval email sent to policing leads (Annex B). The results were "captured via email".

Why it matters: this is the most detailed account of a search on file from any body. It shows what a full answer about a search looks like, which is useful when reading the Wales Office's digital-only search (GHF-E106-R58-A1).

What cuts against the case: the search was real, documented and found nothing. That supports the NPCC's earlier answers (GHF-E106-R70-A1).
