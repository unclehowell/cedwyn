Dear Requester,  
 
Thank you for your enquiry of 29th August 2026 in which you requested access to 
information held by the Wales Office concerning the Great House Farm. 
 
We have treated your enquiry as a request for information under the Freedom of Information 
Act 2000.  
 
Your request  
 
In your email you requested the following information:  
 
• 
Records of any communication or outreach between Cadw, or its predecessor 
functions, and the Office of the Secretary of State for Wales / Welsh Office 
concerning Great House Farm during the period July–December 1988.   
• 
Records of any recommendation, briefing, submission, notification or formal appeal 
concerning the protection or listing of Great House Farm submitted to the Secretary 
of State.   
• 
Records of any communications with the occupiers and owners of the property 
before the refusal decision of 5 December 1988. 
• 
Any surviving destruction, disposal, transfer or accession documentation relating to
Read more
the Great House Farm 1988 assessment file, including certificates, dockets, lists, 
database entries, retention schedules, disposal authorities or other contemporaneous 
records held by the Welsh Office, its predecessors, Cadw, or any repository to which 
relevant records were transferred. 
• 
Information regarding a decision made in relation to Great House Farm in 1988, 
including the evidence that was used to support that decision. 
 
Our response 
We have carried out a search of our digital records and have determined that the Wales 
Office does not hold the information requested. Any paper files previously held by the former 
Welsh Office relating to this matter would have been transferred to the National Assembly for 
Wales in 1999, and subsequently to the Welsh Government in 2007. You may therefore wish 
to request this information from the Welsh Government. Freedom of information requests 
can be made to the Welsh Government through this email address: 
[email redacted].  
 
If you are dissatisfied with the outcome of your request or the manner in which it was 
handled you have the right to request an internal review. Such a request should be 
submitted within two calendar months of the date of this response and should be addressed
