The Land Registration Act 2002 is the principal legislation governing 
land registration in England and Wales. It sets out provisions for 
keeping a register of title to land and property, and in accordance 
with Section 66 & 67 of the Land Registration Act, it is a requirement 
that the Chief Land Registrar makes this information (including any 
documents held on the register of title) available for public 
inspection.  
 
How to access information held on our Registers  
 
The information you have requested is available to access under 
our statutory protocol under section 66 & 67 of the Land 
Registration Act 2002.
