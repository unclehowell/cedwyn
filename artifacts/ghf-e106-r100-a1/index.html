Dear Requester  
 
Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Request – 260607013 
 
Thank you for your request, received on 7 June 2026, in which you asked for the 
following information from the Ministry of Justice (MoJ):   
 
“... Please advise on the current location and access options for records of 
proceedings involving [... information noted but removed...] ([1915 wording removed by Cedwyn editors: no record of an eviction in 1915 is found], 1955/1962 possession orders). Focus on title/equitable interest 
evidence...” 
 
Your request is being handled under the FOIA. 
 
The MoJ does not hold the requested information.  This is because there is no legal 
or business requirement for MoJ to do so. If information is held by a court, for the 
purpose of court proceedings only, it is not the held MoJ as a public authority and, as 
such, is not held for FOIA purposes.
Read more
The FOIA does not oblige a public authority to create information to answer a 
request if the requested information is not held. The duty is to only provide the 
recorded information held. 
 
To advise and assist you on a discretionary basis, outside the FOIA, I can inform you 
that court proceedings relating to evictions and possession orders are dealt with in 
the County Court.  However, it's unlikely that the County Court will hold information 
from 1915, 1955 and 1962.  This is because the County Court’s records are held for 
no longer than necessary, in accordance with the Record Retention and Disposition 
Schedule that’s available from the following link: 
https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/682216a253add7d476d81917/county-
court-family-court-court-protection-rrds.pdf

Under the Public Records Act, older County Court records that are selected for 
permanent preservation (after 20 or 30 years) are transferred to an approved 
archive. You may wish to contact The National Archive (TNA) or local archives, such 
as Glamorgan Archives, to see if they can help. You can obtain information about 
them from: 
• https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/
