6 November 2026Journal entryInternal review: no information held (upheld)ref South Wales Police FOI 1188/26 (internal review of 719/26)

Email of 6 November 2026, 13:15 UTC, from the South Wales Police Data Management and Disclosure Unit with a readable internal review response dated 6 November 2026 (FOI 1188/26, review of FOI 719/26) and the force's complaints procedure. The requester asked for the review on 27 September 2026; a first response on 26 October 2026 came as a password-protected PDF with no password, and the requester asked for an unlocked copy on 31 October. The review upholds the original answer that no information is held about police attendance at the 1988 events at Great House Farm.

Assessment (our reading): The review is completed and readable, names the departments consulted and gives a plausible reason why 1988 paper records would not survive. It does not show what was searched for or where beyond the archive deposits, so whether the search could find the record the request describes is not settled by the response.

What they did

  • Upheld the 16 July 2026 position that no information is held, stating the review was conducted 'in a fair and impartial manner'.
  • Named the departments consulted: Information Governance, Joint Legal Services, Heritage and Communications, and Professional Standards.
  • Explained that records from 1900 to 1993 would have been paper, that a search of the force's deposits at Glamorgan Archives found nothing, and that crime, occurrence and intelligence records follow Management of Police Information (MoPI) retention and are deleted unless transferred to an archive.
  • Supplied a readable copy after the requester's 31 October email and gave the Information Commissioner as the next route.

What they did not do

  • The response does not describe the search terms used or mention the alternative farm names the requester supplied on 29 October 2026.
  • No search of the force's own surviving paper stores, registers or occurrence books from 1988 is described, other than the Glamorgan Archives deposits.
  • The original request is summarised as 'Request for 3rd party information'; the heading refers once to '719/25' and elsewhere to '719/26'.
  • No MoPI retention group or period is identified for the record types in question.

Fair note

  • The section 45 Code of Practice expects internal reviews within 20 working days; the 26 October response fell inside that period, and the readable copy followed the request for one.
  • Deletion under MoPI is lawful routine practice, and records that predate a force's IT systems often do not survive unless transferred to an archive; consulting four departments including Heritage is a reasonable spread.
  • A 'not held' answer upheld on review leaves the Information Commissioner as the statutory route under section 50 of the Act.

Open questions

  • Which search terms and which paper series were searched, and were the alternative farm names of 29 October used?
  • Which MoPI retention group would a 1988 attendance or occurrence record fall in, and is any disposal record kept?

4 November 2026Journal entrySubject access response issued (five days after the recorded date)ref South Wales Police SAR 1293/26

Two emails of 4 November 2026 from the South Wales Police Data Protection Team (SAR 1293/26): at 14:43 UTC a response to the subject access request as a password-protected PDF portfolio, and three seconds later the password in a separate email. The request, sent on 28 September 2026 with a crime-recording request and a search note on FOI 719/26, was followed by clarification exchanges on 29 and 30 September and a chase on 21 October 2026.

Assessment (our reading): The response has been issued, slightly after the recorded one-month date and in a secure form. Its contents are personal and are not journalled; whether it answers every part of the request is the open point.

What they did

  • Issued a response to the subject access request as an encrypted PDF portfolio, with instructions for opening it on computers, phones and tablets.
  • Sent the password separately.
  • Responded on 4 November 2026, five days after 30 October 2026, the date recorded for the one-month period following the clarification exchange.

What they did not do

  • The contents are personal data and are not described here. Whether the response covers the crime-recording and search-note parts of the 28 September email is not checked from the cover email.
  • The cover email does not say whether any information was withheld, or under which Data Protection Act 2018 exemption.

Fair note

  • UK GDPR Article 12(3) allows one month, extendable by two further months for complex or numerous requests if the requester is told within the first month. A response a few days past the month is a minor delay, and police forces handle high volumes of subject access requests.
  • Delivering special-category personal data encrypted, with the password in a separate message, is good practice.

Open questions

  • Does the response cover every part of the 28 September request?
  • Was any information withheld, and under which exemption?
