6 November 2026Journal entrySearch estimates and charges given; vestry records not heldref Glamorgan Archives 2026/4669B and 2026/4669C

Two emails of 6 November 2026 from Glamorgan Archives. At 16:24 UTC (ref 2026/4669B, replying to the requester's email received 12 October) an archivist gave a table of eight record series with estimated search times of one to three hours each, judged none of them likely to contain a 1969 root deed, a 1938 conveyance or a notice served on the occupying family, and quoted searches at £50 an hour plus VAT. At 16:48 UTC (ref 2026/4669C) the archive answered a location request for Llandough-juxta-Penarth parish records.

Assessment (our reading): Clear, itemised and candid about likely yield before any spend; the archivist's judgment is that the documents sought are outside these series. The vestry records are not held, with no onward pointer.

What they did

  • Listed each series with a description and an estimated search time: Llandough parish rate books (UC/73/1-5), Public Works and Planning Committee papers (DCVG/C/4/36/76-77 and /94), a rental book and book of plans (D696), Bute trustees' lease counterparts (DBDT/18/1, DBDT/79/1) and a Cardiff County Court funds ledger (ACA/8/2).
  • Gave its own view that none of the eight series is likely to contain the documents sought, before any charge is incurred.
  • Stated that the parish vestry minutes and churchwardens' accounts are not held, that the tithe map and apportionment are held at P35/9-10, and that any enclosure award would be within the Bute Estate plans DB/E/1-2, viewable by appointment.

What they did not do

  • No search of the listed series was carried out without the fee.
  • The reply does not say where the vestry minutes and churchwardens' accounts might now be, if anywhere.

Fair note

  • A charged research service at £50 an hour is standard for county record offices; giving an estimate and a likelihood judgment before charging is good practice.
  • The 'likely to contain' column is the archivist's assessment from catalogue descriptions, not a search result.
  • The first reply came a little over three weeks after the email was received; the archive's service standards, not the Freedom of Information Act timetable, apply to research enquiries.

Open questions

  • Are the Llandough-juxta-Penarth vestry minutes and churchwardens' accounts recorded as deposited elsewhere or as lost?
