4 November 2026Journal entrySearches confirmed; all held is on Archwilioref GHF/HENEB/2026-09-20 (HER enquiries)

Two replies from the Heneb HER team (09:43 and 09:44 UTC, 4 November 2026) to the requester's two emails of 2 November 2026. The first email asked Heneb to reconsider its three answers of 2 November as it would under regulation 11 of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004 (EIR), to say on what basis if it considers the EIR do not apply to the HER, and to confirm whether the searches covered the paper HER files and the GGAT archive for 1983 to 1989 under the listed names. The second asked for the full HER file for PRN 02038s with its source documents, the three documents formerly attached to that record on Archwilio (02038s_70255.pdf, 02038s_70256.pdf, 02038s_70257.pdf, which returned a 'No such file' error after 24 October 2026), any 1983 to 1989 planning consultation correspondence, site notes or photographs about Great House Farm or Church View Close, the restoration of the three files and a route to copies.

Assessment (our reading): Prompt and courteous. Heneb now states plainly that both the digital and the paper HER records were searched and that everything held is on Archwilio, which answers one long-standing point. It gives a reason for the offline Archwilio documents and a normal copyright reason for the journal article. It does not engage with the question of which regime applies to the HER, gives no date for the documents' return and no interim copies, and leaves the GGAT archive (as distinct from the HER) and the 1983 to 1989 site notes unaddressed. On the regime question, the next step identified by the requester's own email is the Welsh Ministers.

What they did

  • Replied two working days after the requester's emails of 2 November 2026 (18:11 and 18:12 UTC), well inside the 20 working days of EIR regulation 5(2) and the 40 working days of regulation 11(4).
  • Stated that all reasonable searches were made within the digital and paper HER records, and that everything on file is available on Archwilio. This is the first reply in the exchange to state that the paper records were searched.
  • Stated that the full HER entry for PRN 02038s is on Archwilio.
  • Stated that a related journal article cannot be included for copyright reasons and that a copy may be bought from the publisher.
  • Explained that links on Archwilio have been disabled while security concerns linked to a wider PHP software issue are resolved, with the intention of reinstating them as soon as practicable.
  • Stated that the 1983 to 1989 planning consultation correspondence is not held in the HER.

What they did not do

  • Did not answer whether Heneb considers the EIR to apply to the HER, or give the basis for its view, which the requester asked for so that the same requests could be put to the Welsh Ministers.
  • Did not state whether the reply is a reconsideration under EIR regulation 11, or give a reference or a route to the Information Commissioner.
  • Did not state whether the GGAT archive for 1983 to 1989 (as distinct from the HER) was searched, or which names and grid references were used.
  • Did not give a date for restoring the three Archwilio documents, or offer copies of them by email while the links are disabled.
  • Did not name the journal article withheld for copyright reasons or its publisher.
  • Did not address the request for GGAT site visit notes or photographs from 1983 to 1989.

Fair note

  • HERs in Wales are kept for the Welsh Ministers under the Historic Environment (Wales) Act 2023. Information on cultural sites and built structures falls within EIR regulation 2(1)(c) and (f), and information held by another person on behalf of a public authority is held by that authority (regulation 3(2)(b)). Whether a request goes to Heneb or to the Welsh Ministers is a fair question, and an answer on it is normal practice; silence leaves the requester without a route.
  • Pointing to Archwilio is consistent with EIR regulation 6(1)(b), which allows an authority to refer to information already publicly available in another form. While the documents on Archwilio are offline, that ground is weaker for those three files, and regulation 9 (advice and assistance) would normally lead to an offer of copies by another route.
  • Withholding a third-party journal article for copyright reasons is common practice: the EIR give a right to information, not a licence to copy, and copyright is normally handled by pointing to the publisher.
  • Disabling links during a security fix is an ordinary operational step and is not, in itself, a withdrawal of records. The replies credit the earlier availability of the files and give a reason.
  • The confirmation that the paper HER records were searched is a direct answer to a point the requester had raised since 19 October 2026 and narrows the open questions.

Open questions

  • Does Heneb consider the EIR to apply to the HER it keeps for the Welsh Ministers, and if not, on what basis?
  • Was the GGAT archive for 1983 to 1989, beyond the HER, searched for Great House Farm and Church View Close, and under which names?
  • When will the three PRN 02038s documents return to Archwilio, and will they match the copies downloaded on 24 October 2026?
  • Which journal article was withheld, and who is its publisher?
