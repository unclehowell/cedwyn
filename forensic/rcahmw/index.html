RCAHMW holds the H. J. Thomas survey material for Great House Farm, including a 1974 architectural survey/floor plan documenting a sub-medieval/late-seventeenth-century building, and related photographic/record evidence.

Forensic control test

Determine what RCAHMW held by 1988, what was made available to Cadw, whether the demolition was notified, and what further photographic or survey records survive. A statement that an item exists today does not by itself prove who saw it in 1988.

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by RCAHMW. This is not a finding that RCAHMW had no involvement; the page addresses the particular custody and heritage-record questions identified. The key issue is whether surviving survey material can prove what heritage information was available to decision-makers before demolition.

Human action
