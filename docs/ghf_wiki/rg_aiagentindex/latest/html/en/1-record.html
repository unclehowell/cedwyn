Mandatory agent operating rules#

All AI agents and editors must follow the Site Agent Operating Rules. In particular: every substantive factual statement must be source-linked where possible; repeated statements must link to their canonical source; agents must distinguish documented fact, testimony of named family members, reconstruction, inference and unresolved material; and material edits must pass the site’s evidence, hyperlink and change-control checks before publication.

Current controlling Parcel-A case — 9 October 2026. Use this scope across all agent summaries: Parcel A is the full 1877 house/quarrying estate (farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and west-side yard, garden, forest north of the house, and ground south-east of the house/garden later registered as VG41); Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928. The already-obtained 1939 agreement/plan depicts Parcel B only. [SOURCE PENDING] The 4 July 1955 enforcement of Parcel B also seized the Parcel-A north forest and south-east green under “whole farm” wording. Mary’s defence of the remaining house/buildings/garden group did not narrow the full Parcel-A claim. Trace the two seized land areas through later registration/title and remedies; do not reopen the settled 1939 Parcel-B-only result or treat missing records alone as proof of fraud.

Source handling principles#

  • Documented facts are distinguished from testimony of named family members, reconstruction and interpretation.

  • Catalogue identifiers beginning GHF-E identify evidence entries used by the Wiki’s evidence catalogue.

Agent instruction — confidential threshold review. Any artifact or pattern that may materially affect the legal history of Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr or the 1987 proceedings must first be preserved and assessed privately. A discrepancy, missing record or unanswered request is a research lead, not a finding.

Preserve the original artifact, provenance, exact wording and source ID. Create or update a confidential HubSpot review task with the evidence supporting and cutting against the interpretation, relevance, materiality, causation, what would confirm or refute it, and the next record needed. Do not add unreviewed incidents to a public log, publish accusations or contact counsel or another third party automatically. External action or public wording requires an explicit owner/authorised professional decision. Full procedure: Snowball protocol · Projects.
