On Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 witness statement (Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony), the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 when quarrying and rent to Thomas ended, transferring the full Parcel A to the Williams family. Parcel A includes the farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and west-side yard, garden, forest north of the house and the south-east ground later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The 1939 agreement/plan is already obtained and depicts Parcel B only. [SOURCE PENDING] The 1949 effort to make Mary’s Parcel-A occupation permissive failed (no written record of the 2 February 1949 re-grant; GHF-E122). Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 witness statement (Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony) records that the title papers disappeared in 1950–52. [SOURCE PENDING] WGR’s 4 July 1955 enforcement of Parcel B also seized Parcel-A forest and green under “whole farm” wording (writ and execution record sought: GHF-E126). Later documentary work must corroborate/precisely map that account and trace those areas into later titles; it must not re-open Parcel-A/Parcel-B identity or treat the 1939 plan as unknown. The alternative limitation case (twelve years from 1928 potentially maturing by 1940 if legal conditions were met) is separate from underlying title. Missing originals and register gaps are evidential issues, not by themselves proof of fraud. 🕒
