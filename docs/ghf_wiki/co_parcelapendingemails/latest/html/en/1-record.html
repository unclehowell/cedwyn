Dear Hugh James,
I understand that Hugh James acquired Loosemores Solicitors of Cardiff. The published Court of Appeal judgment in BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2 (31 July 1987) records that the appellant, Mr W. Buckler of Great House Farm, Llandough, near Penarth, was represented by counsel instructed by Robbins Olivey & Blake Lapthorn, “agents for Loosemores, of Cardiff”.
Mr Buckler died in 1990. I write for his family, who are reconstructing the record of the case.
Could you tell me:
1. Whether Loosemores’ file (or files) for Mr W. Buckler in the possession action brought by BP Properties Ltd (writ 1984; High Court, Cardiff, judgment of Hollis J 24 July 1986; Court of Appeal 31 July 1987; House of Lords petition refused 3 March 1988; enforcement November–December 1988) passed to Hugh James, or what happened to it.
2. If any part survives, its reference and what it contains in outline (pleadings, witness statements, the trial bundle, title documents, correspondence with BP’s solicitors Linklaters & Paines).
3. If it was destroyed, the date and the retention policy applied, and whether a file index or closure record survives.
4. What proof of entitlement you would need (for example from a personal representative or next of kin) before releasing a copy.
5. Please keep anything that survives while this request is open.
Our case, prospective. Not a finding of any court.
Kind regards,
Cedwyn editors

Historic England internal review, ref 26/04323 — Mark.Wilkins@historicengland.org.uk, cc FOI@historicengland.org.uk
Dear Mr Wilkins,
Your reference: 26/04323
Request for internal review under regulation 11 of the Environmental Information Regulations 2004
Thank you for your reply of 21 October 2026. I ask for an internal review of it.
1. Your reply says nothing is held within the scope of my request (regulation 12(4)(a)). It then says Historic England does hold a record of “Archaeological Reports on microfiche” transferred to RCAHMW on 12 January 2018, without an itemised list. Items 1, 2 and 6 of my request asked for the record of that transfer, how the material was acquired, and where anything went if it was transferred elsewhere. Please disclose the record from which the date and description in your reply were taken, or tell me what that record is and where it is held. Regulation 12(4)(a) cannot apply to information that is held.
2. Items 1 and 4 asked for covering correspondence and the record of the decision to include the item in the 2018 transfer. Please check for any authorisation, agreement, email or note between Historic England and RCAHMW about the transfer, even if no itemised list was made, and for the date and role of whoever authorised it. If nothing is held for an item, please say so for that item separately.
3. Item 5 asked for any other information about Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, including photographs, survey notes and correspondence with Cadw, RCAHMW or the Glamorgan-Gwent Archaeological Trust about its listing or demolition (1986-1989). Your reply says only that you searched “paper and electronic records”. Please give me the search record: the systems and collections searched (including the Historic England Archive and the National Buildings Record), the search terms used, the date of the search and the team that carried it out.
4. Item 6: the microfiche transfer may itself be what item 6 asks about (“transferred elsewhere”). Please tell me under which retention schedule or policy the material was transferred rather than kept, and whether RCAHMW acknowledged receipt.
5. Date: my request referred to a transfer on 15 January 2018. Your reply gives 12 January 2018. Please tell me which date your records show and what the source is.
6. Your reply refers to the Coflein search for archive number 6500905. Please say whether Historic England holds any record that links that number to its transfer.
7. Regulation 9: if a narrower or differently worded request would locate the record, please tell me what it should say.
8. Please preserve all records within the scope of my request while this review is open.
Please acknowledge this request and tell me the date I can expect the outcome. Regulation 11(4) allows 40 working days.
Yours sincerely,
Sion Buckler
