Negative-findings control — as at 10-10-2026: Statements in this register that an authority holds no information, an attachment was not located, or a record remains outstanding must be attributed to the relevant authority/search or to the documented mailbox/file review, with the applicable response or review date. “Not held”, “not found in the material reviewed”, and “not yet obtained” are not interchangeable and do not establish that a record never existed. This date marks the Wiki’s assessment, not the historical event. Reassess and date the entry when new evidence is reviewed; preserve verbatim source wording.

Attachment status: Published means the original attachment is linked and mirrored with a SHA-256 manifest. No substantive attachment means only signatures, logos or previously supplied images were present. Not yet retrieved means the attachment is identified but has not been extracted or obtained. Where an original is not published, that limitation is stated.

Attachment check, 5 October 2026. The replies included in that check were opened and their attachments extracted; corrections are marked (corrected 5 Oct). Subject-access (SAR) letters are not published because they concern personal data. Three large emails remain listed at Not yet retrieved. Inferred — not evidenceWGR is a party, so in 1965 it held, or had to release, an estate in the school land; the development company was most likely the contracting vendor.
