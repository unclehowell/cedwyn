EV-0002 — Sir George Herbert grants Vaughan a 1,000-year lease of Cydwin

How we treat outside records. Tŷ Mawr is first named in the record we have found in David Stewart’s 1824 survey (EV-0211). In the family’s account, Tŷ Mawr was acquired in 1667. Earlier documents on this page that come from the Bute estate or other third parties, such as the Cydwin lease or a Bute catalogue heading, are not records of Tŷ Mawr: their application to it is under forensic examination, and we test them rather than repeat them.
