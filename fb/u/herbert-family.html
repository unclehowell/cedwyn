How we treat outside records. Tŷ Mawr is first named in the record we have found in David Stewart’s 1824 survey (EV-0211). In the family’s account, Tŷ Mawr was acquired in 1667. Earlier documents on this page that come from the Bute estate or other third parties, such as the Cydwin lease or a Bute catalogue heading, are not records of Tŷ Mawr: their application to it is under forensic examination, and we test them rather than repeat them. About Sir George Herbert of Swansea (d.

Sir George Herbert of Swansea (d. 1570) held the manor of Llandough by 1545; the Herberts of Cogan Pill after him. The first name in the farm's root title.

Hurst & Jones is now friends with The Herbert family
1767 · Life event · marriage

Inheritance by marriage: William Hurst and Calvert Richard Jones had married two heiresses of this branch of the Herbert family, and by 1767 held the manors of Dinas Powys, Llandough East and Cogan.

Cardiff Records: from 1545 the manor passed from Sir George Herbert to his grandson Sir William Herbert, and stayed with the Herberts of Cogan Pill for several generations.
