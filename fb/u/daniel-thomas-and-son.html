About The limeworks firm that took the 1877 agreement from the Marquess of Bute over land at Great House Farm.

The limeworks firm that took the 1877 agreement from the Marquess of Bute over land at Great House Farm. The Bute deeds describe “land (33 a.), formerly part of Great House Farm, for the Llandough Limeworks (33 a.), 1876–1912”. Our position is that the 1877 agreement is the start of the farm’s division into two parcels.

Daniel Thomas of Penylan dies

Daniel Thomas of Penylan, a contractor under Lord Bute and the owner of “valuable lime kilns at Llandough”, dies; his obituary appears in Tarian y Gweithiwr. In April 1888 a local man is described as “a labourer at Llandough Quarry”: the works beside the farm carry on.

Agents and editors: follow the Editing Protocol.
