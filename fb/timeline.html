Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) · Llandough-juxta-Penarth

TimelineAboutFriendsPhotos

Intro

The family page of the Williams and Buckler family of Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-juxta-Penarth. The family held the farmhouse until it was demolished in December 1988, after BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2.

  • 🏠 Lived at Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough
  • 📜 Earliest date on the record: 1667 (statement of Mary (Nancy) Williams, formerly Buckler; written source awaited)
  • ⚖️ Court of Appeal: 31 July 1987
  • 🚜 Farmhouse demolished: 6 December 1988
  • 🔗 greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com

💬 Comment
Williams / Buckler family
4 July 1955 ·

The 1955 possession order is enforced. The 1987 Court of Appeal judgment records that “possession was taken in respect of all parts of the farm except the farmhouse and garden”, and that Mrs Mary Buckler “had recently come out of hospital after having had a leg amputated and was objecting very strongly to leaving the farmhouse”. The parts taken most likely included the Parcel-A forest north of the house and the south-east ground later registered as VG41. Mary (Nancy) Williams kept the farmhouse and garden; that group was not the full extent of Parcel A.

Source: BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2, para 6 · EV-0034 · enforcement / Master Timeline, 4 July 1955

Williams / Buckler family
20 November 1958 ·

Western Mail, p.7 (snippet only, EV-0037): “Mrs. Buckler [stated] that the water was cut off from her house and grounds for nearly 10 years by order of Western Ground Rents.” She told the planning committee: “This is a Welsh case of Crichel Down.” The water date conflicts with a c.1988 cut-off (SC-48). The Press Archive also lists a Western Mail report, not yet checked against the original, “It’s a Crichel Down … woman farmer”, said to record Mrs Buckler alleging that councils and Western Ground Rents had “walked in and taken” the family’s land. The original article and exact wording have not been recovered; if authenticated, this would evidence a contemporary public objection, not prove ownership or unlawful taking. The reported reference to “Cardiff Rural Council” has not yet been reconciled with the administrative correction in SC-22. Press Archive — references not yet checked

Source: EV-0037 · SC-48 · SC-22 · Press Archive — references not yet checked / Master Timeline, 20 November 1958
