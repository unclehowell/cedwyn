Forensic Assessment — HM Courts & Tribunals Service

Scope: HMCTS as present court-records administrator/custodian, not as the historic merits decision-maker.

Role

The current HMCTS record-search programme is important because the relevant litigation predates current electronic case management. A search of a modern system cannot by itself establish that a historic paper file never existed.

Forensic control test

Obtain the search scope, central/national archive search, retention schedule, transfer/disposal history and any surviving index for the 1955, 1962, 1974 and 1984–87 proceedings. Record precisely what was searched.

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish misconduct by HMCTS. This is not proof that every historic file or records-search issue has been resolved. The active issue is records recovery and a defensible explanation where a file cannot be located.

Human action

Follow the outstanding HMCTS request, seek escalation to historic records/archive staff where necessary, and place every response into the evidence-access chronology. The existing 2 October 2026 extension should be tracked to substantive outcome.

Acceptance: a file-by-file search/retention outcome exists for every requested historical proceeding.
