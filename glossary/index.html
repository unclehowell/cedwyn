Leasehold / lease

Holding land for a fixed term from the freeholder, usually in return for rent. A ground rent is the rent paid on a long lease.

Licence (to occupy)

Written or spoken permission to occupy land. A licence is not ownership, and in law it stops time running for adverse possession.

Tŷ Mawr / Great House

Tŷ Mawr (“Great House”): the former manorial house at Llandough near Penarth, demolished in December 1988. First named in the 1824 David Stewart survey of the Bute estates as “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm”; the wider estate is known as Cedwyn.
