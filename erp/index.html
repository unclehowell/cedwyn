Lights

  • RedConcluded with a refusal, not held, no reply, or an excuse: escalate.
  • AmberConcluded with a late, partial or doubtful answer: escalate.
  • No lightNot reached yet. There is no green: no answer is taken as satisfactory, which is why every stage escalates to the next.

Excuse icons

  • ✈Away on holiday / staff absentFOIA 2000 s.10(6): only weekends and UK bank holidays stop the 20 working days; leave does not. Next move: Note the due date; at the lawful maximum, escalate.
  • £Paywall / fee demandedFOIA s.9 fees notice and s.13; EIR reg 8 (reasonable charge); UK GDPR Art 12(5): a SAR is free unless manifestly unfounded or excessive. Next move: Ask for the fee basis in writing; the clock pauses only for a valid fees notice.
  • 🌰That old chestnut (stock excuse)Any stock form of words offered instead of a search; each one is catalogued at /that-old-chestnut/. Next move: Quote the words back and ask for the search made.
  • 🔥Fire, flood or other lossCode of Practice on the management of records (FOIA s.46): records kept, disposed of and logged. Next move: Ask for the incident record and the disposal or loss log.
  • Records destroyed or presumed destroyedFOIA s.77 and EIR reg 19: destroying or concealing records after a request is an offence; s.46 Code: disposal must be logged. Next move: Ask for the destruction schedule entry, its date and authority.
  • ∅Not heldFOIA s.1(1)(a); EIR reg 12(4)(a): the body must say whether it holds the information, after an adequate search. Next move: Ask where and how it searched (ICO: the search must be reasonable).
  • 🧮Cost limit / too much workFOIA s.12 appropriate limit (£600 central government, £450 others, Fees Regulations 2004); EIR reg 12(4)(b) burden limb. Next move: Ask for the estimate and for advice to narrow (s.16).
  • 🛑Vexatious or repeatedFOIA s.14(1) vexatious, s.14(2) repeated; EIR reg 12(4)(b) manifestly unreasonable. Next move: Internal review, then ICO: the burden is on the body to show it.
  • 🔗Publicly available elsewhereFOIA s.21; EIR reg 6(1)(b): only for information the body itself holds and that is reasonably accessible. Next move: Ask for the exact location of the item said to be available.
  • ✂Scope narrowed / request re-readFOIA s.16 duty to advise; s.45 Code of Practice: requests read objectively. Next move: Restate the request in full with every name the place is known by.
  • ❓Clarification demanded (clock reset)FOIA s.1(3); EIR reg 9(2)-(4): the clock runs again from our answer. Next move: Answer the same day; log the restart date.
  • ↪Sent elsewhere (not our remit)EIR reg 10 transfer; FOIA s.45 Code of Practice: advise or transfer, do not just signpost. Next move: Ask whether it holds anything at all, then open the named body's pipeline.
  • 🏢Not subject to FOIFOIA s.3 and Sch 1; EIR reg 2(2)(c)-(d): bodies with public administrative functions are covered by the EIR. Next move: Use the EIR test, a SAR or the body's own policy instead.
  • 🔒Withheld as personal data ("GDPR")FOIA s.40(2); EIR reg 13; DPA 2018 s.3(2): personal data means data about a living person. Next move: Ask which living person; records of the dead are not personal data.
  • 📭Lost in post / mailbox not watchedFOIA s.8: a request by email is a valid written request; the clock runs from receipt. Next move: Keep the delivery record; resend to a second published address.
  • ⏳Volume of work (delay)FOIA s.10(1) / EIR reg 5(2): 20 working days whatever the workload. Next move: Note the due date; at the lawful maximum, escalate.
  • 🏠No reply by the due date (the dartboard's red house)FOIA s.10(1); UK GDPR Art 12(3): the time limit is the limit. Next move: Escalate the day after the lawful maximum.
  • 🐟Late or part answer (the dartboard's red fish)FOIA s.1(1), s.10(1), s.17 refusal notice for anything withheld. Next move: Ask for the refusal notice for the part not answered.

How the forecast works

  • Every stage takes its clock plus the longest extension the law or policy allows.
  • A body that misses its deadline does not lengthen the forecast: the day after the lawful maximum is the day we escalate.
  • Laps run one after another; inside a lap, stages that depend on each other run in order and the rest run side by side.
  • Worst case: the body asks for clarification on the last day and the clock starts again from our answer.
  • Working days skip weekends and any UK bank holiday (FOIA s.10(6), EIR reg 2). Bank holidays are published to 2028-12-26; later dates count weekends only.
  • Where no time is published, the seeded ladder's assumption is used and the stage is marked inferred.
