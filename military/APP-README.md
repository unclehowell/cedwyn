# Military Involvement (webapp 48 of 59)
Parts: index.html (site shell: shared header boot, slot bar "48 of 59"), military.css, military.js (renders `data/timeline.json`
with strand and label filters), data/timeline.json (the timeline; schema below), worker/preview.js (preview Worker, files inlined).

## Strands
1 Military presence before the 1897 Lavernock / Flat Holm trials · 2 Williams and Marconi (the Post Office engineer) ·
3 The German observer at the trials · 4 War Agricultural Executive Committee and Parcel B · 5 The 1958 Crichel Down remark · 0 later context.

## Entry fields
id, strand, date, title, quote (verbatim), source, url (shown as text, not linked), label (record | testimony | hypothesis), kind,
status (NEW | KNOWN against the evidence catalogue on the build date), ids (catalogue IDs), cuts (what cuts against it), next (the free request).

## Rules for editors
Add entries; never rewrite an existing quote. New evidence goes to the evidence catalogue as a candidate first. Neutral wording;
the project's banned-word list applies. Write "not checked" where something has not been confirmed. No outbound links on the page.
