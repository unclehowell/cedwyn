Cardiff Records, vol. 4 (survey of Sir William Herbert's manors, Landoche entry) Context
Landoche Juxta Cardif cam to this gent, by Inheritance from his greate graunde mother who was doughter and sole heire to Sr Mathew Cradocke k: Yt hath free tenaunts demeanes and coppy houlds, and it is distant westwards from Cardif theire m'ket towne one mile.

J. Hobson Matthews (ed.) · 1903 · New

Question: tenure direction · Row type: finding · What was opened: page image (stage 6)

Citation: Internet Archive scan (full link in the CSV)

Manor-level survey text in full; the register's existing Cardiff Records vol. 2 row carries only the editor's abbreviation of it. The manor had free tenants, demesnes and copyholds, so lordship of the manor is not ownership of every holding in it. 'Juxta Cardif' and 'westwards from Cardif one mile' place this manor on the Cardiff side, not at Llandough near Cowbridge. Next: find which free or copy holding, if any, became Cydfin / Ty Mawr.

Landoch came to Sir William Herbert from his great-grandmother, daughter and heiress to Sir Matthew Cradock, Knt., which, after the death of Richard Herbert, Esq., married Sir William Bawdrip, Knt. In this lordship was the chief dwelling-house of Sir Matthew Cradock, Knt. [see Llandough Castle].

Thomas Nicholas · 1872 · New

Question: tenure direction · Row type: finding · What was opened: page image (stage 6)

Citation: Internet Archive scan (full link in the CSV)

Parish-identity trap. The editor's bracket sends the reader to Llandough Castle (the Cowbridge parish), but the same survey printed in Cardiff Records vol. 4 p. 135 names 'Landoche Juxta Cardif', one mile west of Cardiff. Our inference: the bracket is the editor's own and joins the wrong Llandough. Do not use Nicholas's cross-reference to place this manor.
