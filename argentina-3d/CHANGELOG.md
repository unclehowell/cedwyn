# Changelog
- v0.4.0 (8 Nov 2026): Tŷ Mawr (Great House) now comes from the latest Tŷ Mawr model (section/revival-ar models/tymawr.glb v0.2.3, scored against the photographs by the model loop), cut to the house group: house, pantile range, stable range, west barn, yard walls, gravel and gate. The church, lane, hedges, meadow, garden, trees and survey markers are left out, so the house stands alone on bare steppe. models/tymawr.glb, 191,652 bytes; GLTFLoader vendored (three r160). The drawn farm stays as the fallback if the model cannot load. Also opened from the Patagonia hub's 3D map tile and at /argentina-3d/ on cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate (cedwyn#528). (Commit 0050842 calls this 0.3.0; it is 0.4.0.)
- v0.3.0 (6 Nov 2026): own data store. data/central.json holds the catalogue fields (id, kind, title, date, status, page) for the record IDs in data/sources.json (EV-0631, EV-0630, AR-0002, AR-0004), pulled read-only from the central catalogue by tools/sync_central.py every 6 h (.github/workflows/sync-central.yml). records.js lists them in the Sources dialog with links to https://bucklerfamily.estate/catalogue/; the page reads only its own store. Served at https://bucklerfamily.estate/argentina-3d/ from this repo's main. — Cedwyn editors
- v0.2.0 (4 Nov 2026): Tŷ Mawr rebuilt from three reference photographs (aerial from the SE, house front, stables from the yard): house with stone gable-end bay, slate roof, three chimneys (east one red brick), gabled porch; attached red-pantile range; lime-washed stable range with green doors and dressed-stone barn with gabled cart porch to the west; dry-stone walls, gravel yards, field gate; church to the NE; poplar windbreak. Laid out as photographed (front to the south). Views: Helicopter (from SE looking NW) and Ground (looking NNW at the stables). Procedural pantile and brick textures. — Cedwyn editors
- v0.1.0 (4 Nov 2026): first version. Separate Patagonia world: flat steppe with tussock grass and low shrubs, real sun and sky for Puerto Madryn at the chosen hour, haze; Tŷ Mawr (footprint from the 1974 survey; walls, roof, chimneys, porch and front windows from album 096/098), north wing, east additions, walled front garden, stable range and cart shed, threshing barn, stable row, entrance gate. Sources dialog marks evidence vs best guess. — Cedwyn editors
