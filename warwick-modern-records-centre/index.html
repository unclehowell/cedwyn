Forensic Assessment — Warwick Modern Records Centre

Scope: Warwick Modern Records Centre as a custodian/discovery route for historical BP-related corporate records.

Why it matters

The BP archive is potentially the best surviving route to the private corporate record behind the 1969 acquisition, 1982–83 registration and 1984–87 litigation. The public court judgment cannot answer what internal documents BP possessed but did not put before the court.

Forensic control test

Identify the BP group collection, access restrictions, file references, depositor, date of deposit and scope of search. Priority subjects are Western Ground Rents, BP Pension Trust/BP Properties, Great House Farm/Llandough, the 1969 conveyance, 1971 abstract, Land Registry applications, 1984–87 litigation and 1988 development/demolition.

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish archive wrongdoing. This assessment is limited to the custody/search trail identified here. The archive’s importance is evidential: a surviving corporate file can establish who knew what and when without relying on inference from public records.

Human action

Complete the targeted BP archive/successor preservation request already recorded as HubSpot P1-24 and request precise collection/file references. Record every negative result with its search scope and access reason.

Acceptance: BP/WGR archive search complete, file references captured and each relevant item classified for disclosure value.
