Assessed 14 October 2026. Scope: documented appearance in one 1965 conveyance. Glamorgan County Council is a historic authority; its records may sit with Glamorgan Archives or successor councils. Inclusion is not a finding of fault.

Documented appearances

DateAppearanceSource
11 June 1965Party (3) to the conveyance of land for Llandough Primary School; consideration £16,800. The Vale of Glamorgan Council says it holds the conveyance and contract, has no record of a gift or undervalue, and no valuation or committee report, and that older committee records may be at Glamorgan Archives.Reply register R23 (FOI 00211027), 14 September 2026

Current finding

As at 14 October 2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing. The Vale’s statements describe what it holds. They do not establish what the County Council’s own committee papers contain.

Next step

Search the Glamorgan Archives for County Council committee minutes and valuation papers around the 1965 purchase; separately identify and search planning/development-control committee records and general council minutes from June 1988 to February 1989 for any reference to Great House Farm, Tŷ Mawr, listing/retention or demolition. The National Archives’ DCVG catalogue identifies general minute volumes DCVG/C/1/33 (June–November 1988) and DCVG/C/1/34 (December 1988–February 1989), but this does not confirm that the specific planning-committee minutes are in those volumes or that the farmhouse was discussed. See DCVG catalogue and New Archival Leads, section 8.4. Acceptance: the correct minute series is identified and searched; either the relevant page/minute is cited or a dated, scoped negative search is recorded. Absence from general minutes alone does not prove no discussion occurred.
