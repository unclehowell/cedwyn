Purpose

Audit public-authority decisions, powers, duties, records and responses without asking an authority to determine private title.

Register

  • Authority and function at the relevant date
  • Statutory power/duty and actor/office
  • Event, document and decision
  • What the family knew and what the authority appears to have known
  • Missing record or contradiction
  • Current responsible body
  • Complaint, scrutiny, disclosure and independent oversight route
  • Correction, acknowledgement or other remedy sought
  • Response, deadline and escalation

Relevant strands include planning, heritage, archaeology, registration, enforcement and police records.
