Purpose

Give each institution a fair, documented opportunity to investigate, disclose, correct and remedy before escalation or proceedings.

Remedy ladder

  1. Issue identified
  2. Evidence sufficiently particularised
  3. Institution formally notified
  4. Disclosure/investigation requested
  5. Institution accepts, partly accepts or denies
  6. Remedy proposed
  7. Remedy completed and evidenced
  8. Unresolved/escalated
  9. Pre-action
  10. Proceedings

Gate

Preserve evidence → request disclosure → notify institution → use appropriate complaint/review route → request correction → seek proportionate remedy → record response/deadline → consider independent route → obtain legal advice and protect limitation.
