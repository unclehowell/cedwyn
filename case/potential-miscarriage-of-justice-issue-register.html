Definition used here: “potential miscarriage of justice” is a scrutiny label for an unresolved claim that the combined process produced an unjust result through error, missing evidence, procedural unfairness, or a material misunderstanding. It is not used here as a finding that a miscarriage of justice has been established. 🕒

Issues presently requiring evidence review

  • the timing and service of HMLR notice in 1982–83;
  • the position of Mary (Nancy) Williams at the time of registration and the effect of her death on 26 March 1983;
  • the fact that two relevant titles were still live in the 1986 litigation chronology;
  • the 2 February 1987 HMLR amalgamation during the appeal period;
  • the documentary title gap recorded in BP v Buckler;
  • the later use of possession and planning records to describe the site as one estate.

What would convert an allegation into a finding?

Each issue needs a primary document, a verified date, a clear actor, and a demonstrated connection to the claimed outcome. Where the evidence does not establish the point, the Wiki should say “not proved” rather than fill the gap with inference. 🕒
