Current finding

As at 14 October 2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by DCMS. A nil return is a statement about what the department holds on the question asked. It does not show that no relevant record ever existed elsewhere, and it is not a finding about the 1988 events.

Forensic control test and next step

Check the exact scope of each request and the search described in each response against the museum-side responses (Amgueddfa Cymru, National Library of Wales). Acceptance: the question of who holds, or held, the movable property is routed to a named custodian or closed with a dated result.
