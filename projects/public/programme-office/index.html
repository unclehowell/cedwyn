How the score works

Same formula every week

score = 50 x share of milestones done (in-progress counts half) + 30 x mean KPI progress to target (capped at 1) + 20 x momentum (actions done since last review / actions planned, 0.5 when no prior review) - 5 per open blocker that only the Executive can clear (max -15). RAG: 60+ GREEN, 30-59 AMBER, under 30 RED.
