Five kinds of organisation that look like parts of Cedwyn were studied from their own accounts, official statistics and the platforms' own data. Eight factors recur. Cedwyn has the story and the archive; it lacks an audience, a low-risk first step, a legal vehicle, a schedule and partners.

Heritage and guided walking tours

Unseen Tours, free-tour operators, Welsh guide fees
  • Unseen Tours CIC (London, since 2010): more than 450 tours a year for about 6,500 guests, 20+ guides, 60% of ticket revenue to the guide; tickets £15-18.
  • A large free-tour operator expects 2 million travellers a year; 45% of free-tour guests buy another product.
  • Wales Official Tourist Guide Association, 2026: £208 a half day, £343 a full day.
  • Wales, 2024: 157.84 million leisure day trips worth £5.50 billion.
  • Marketplaces take about 20-30% per booking.

Community and small independent museums

Tenby, Thackray, Beamish, the Welsh and English sector surveys
  • Tenby Museum (independent, volunteer-run): income £93,195 against spending £117,768 in 2025.
  • Thackray Museum: 70,000 visitors for the first time in 2023/24 after redevelopment; visitor income about 55% of total.
  • Beamish: 838,630 visitors in 2024; Art Fund Museum of the Year 2025.
  • Wales: 160 museums; 4,235 volunteers gave 404,676 hours. England: only 36% of museums earn any income from visitors; 31% are wholly volunteer-run.

Community land trusts, buyouts and community businesses

Eigg, Gigha, Tafarn Sinc, Tafarn y Fic
  • Isle of Eigg, 1997: bought for £1.5 million with 10,000 donors; population from about 64 to 110.
  • Isle of Gigha, 2002: £4 million, then £1 million repaid by about 100 residents within two years.
  • Tafarn Sinc, Pembrokeshire: over £325,000 in community shares from 471 people.
  • Tafarn y Fic, Llithfaen: run by villagers since 1988.
  • Rural community businesses: 94% long-term survival; average community-share stake £395.

Heritage crowdfunding and community shares

Hastings Pier, heritage grants
  • Hastings Pier: £14 million rebuild, £470,000 in community shares, Stirling Prize 2017, administration in November 2017: capital without an operating model.
  • National Lottery heritage grants: £10,000 to £250,000, decisions in about 8 weeks, paid 50/30/20, judged on four principles including organisational sustainability.

Indie history publishers and authors

Welsh presses, author earnings, e-book prices
  • Y Lolfa (Talybont, since 1979): publishes history and also prints for other authors, a second income.
  • Typical local-history print runs are under about 700 copies; bookshops expect 33% trade terms.
  • Median earnings of full-time UK authors: £7,000 (2022).
  • E-books: $3.99 earned most per title and box sets earned most at $9.99 (fiction-heavy data); a free first book lifts the series.

Benchmarks Cedwyn will be measured against

Feeds the weekly score
  1. First pilot walk within 8 weeks; then two public walks a month in season.
  2. Public walk price £15-18 a person; private groups from the £208 half-day guide fee.
  3. About 14 guests per public walk.
  4. At least 70% of bookings direct.
  5. A free entry product, with 45% of its users buying a paid item.
  6. An e-book price test (proposal).
  7. The books numbered as one series, the free first title kept everywhere.
  8. A 200-300 copy print run and three local stockists.
  9. 'Working towards Accreditation' before any museum capital bid.
  10. A legal vehicle able to issue £100+ shares.
  11. At least 400 named supporters before any share or crowd offer.
  12. One heritage-grant enquiry drafted with a revenue plan.
