WP-DIG-NOM-02 · Sourced name-origin articles and site-wide wiki links

  • Change ID CR-DIG-2026-10-31-01
  • Product PROD-DIG-NOM-02 — Cedwyn Nomenclature Wiki Layer
  • Control model PRINCE2 7, tailored · cross-cutting work package
  • Raised 31 October 2026
  • Status AMBER In progress; live acceptance open
  • Budget tolerance £0 external spend

Extend Cedwyn’s source-aware name register into a connected encyclopaedia. Important names and concepts should lead to dedicated pages explaining their meanings, histories, usages and source trails. Each page must distinguish recorded forms from scholarly hypotheses, later folk explanations and unresolved search spellings. A shared lexicon and predictable linking rules make the pages discoverable across Cedwyn rather than leaving isolated lists.

Programme boundary: this is a cross-cutting product/work package, not a fifth project. It supports existing research and evidence products and does not change the programme structure, title/ownership positions or any legal gate.

Where no etymology is supportable, state that it is not established and list the next source needed.
Canonical dataUse the nomenclature lexicon for concept IDs, names, variants, page URLs and link terms. Keep central data/nomenclature.json and data/glossary.json aligned with the independent nomenclature lexicon so all themes resolve consistently.
Article standardState standard/current forms; dated historical spellings with document locators; etymological and alternative explanations; evidence status and limits; inline references; related internal articles. Identify indexes and summaries as secondary sources where underlying primary records have not been checked directly.
Hyperlink ruleFirst meaningful occurrence of a curated name or concept becomes a real, keyboard-accessible internal link. Use longest-match precedence and skip existing links, headings, navigation, forms, buttons, code, scripts and expressly excluded content. Keep dotted underline/focus treatment restrained and consistent.
Build routesMain Cedwyn build: existing build-time tools/wikilinks.py consumes central nomenclature/glossary data. Independent nomenclature pages retain their existing lexicon-powered term modal. Shared header and Projects build additionally load the lexicon-backed direct-link runtime. Other distinct deployment branches must be listed and checked individually.
Non-goalsNo automatic inference of ownership, boundaries, legal effect or geographic identity from a shared name; no unsupported etymologies; no external spend; no replacement of WP-DIG-NOM-01 or other PRINCE2 baselines.
