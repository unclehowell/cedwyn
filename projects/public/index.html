Master mandate and operating rules

Work can progress in parallel without collapsing project boundaries

Purpose: preserve and reconcile surviving records about Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr; develop a feasible, source-led museum proposal in Argentina; and create an accessible, evidence-graded AR tour for Llandough Parcel A and its historic setting.

Independence: P1 can begin source grading, interpretation and concept planning; P3 can begin route mapping, source-location checking and wireframe specifications; P2 continues retrieving and preserving material in Wales. New records update P1/P3 through versioned change control. P2 is complementary, not a blanket start condition.

Historical accuracy: all interpretation distinguishes established source facts, testimony/recollection, regional context, inference and hypothetical reconstruction. The Roman villa/farm reported south of the church must not be shown as on Parcel A without site-specific evidence. The early ecclesiastical/monastic attribution must be source- and location-checked before it is depicted as an established structure.

Authority: publishing this working baseline is not Executive acceptance, spending authority, permission to acquire land/build, permission to publish private witness material, or authority to file legal proceedings. Each project needs explicit gates, tolerances, acceptance tests and approvals.

Cedwyn Snowball — programme-wide operating flywheel

Approved 31 October 2026 · one parent mandate, ten specialist work packages

Purpose: reconstruct the Welsh family estate, Williams/Buckler matriarchy, landholding, people, history and heritage around Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr / Llandough-juxta-Penarth; establish parcel by parcel what each person owned or occupied, by what instrument or legal interest, when it changed and what rights or heritage survive. Investigate documented institutional decisions and potential failures without presuming negligence, incompetence, concealment, fraud, scandal or government involvement. The proposed Patagonia Great House Farm II is a future feasibility and heritage concept, separate from the historic Welsh estate.

Recursive snowball formula: S(n+1) = S(n) + V + R + G + A + P + B - D - U - W. V = new verified artifacts/propositions; R = validated relationships; G = genuinely resolved research gaps; A = actionable follow-on leads; P = approved public/educational outputs; B = earned support or capability reinvested through approved procurement; D = duplicates removed; U = unsupported claims quarantined; W = avoidable rework. These components are reported separately. This is a management measure, not a measure of historical truth: copies, unverified leads and raw pageviews do not increase it.

The loop: new record or activity → deduplicate and preserve → canonical source/provenance record → relate people, bodies, events, parcels and titles → independent challenge → rank missing records → HubSpot task → specialist agent claim in Hive → bounded work → QA → approved public update → metric, changelog and next research question. Every loop must yield a verified record, resolved gap, approved output or explicit blocker.

Ten work packages in HubSpot:

  1. Estate/title and matriarchy: parcel-by-parcel title, succession, possession, maps and interests.
  2. FOI and records recovery: archive holders, Gmail, spam/archive checks, responses, attachments and follow-up.
  3. Documentary bible and master script: complete, source-linked narrative with contradictory evidence and corrections retained.
  4. Sequential scene production: nominal two-minute scenes, one at a time, with screenplay, evidence map, set/prop/wardrobe, choreography, camera, sound, continuity and version history; licensed OmniHuman access to be verified before use.
  5. Hive / Docs / WebMCP: one top-level agent instruction, specialist routing, claims, handovers and machine-readable entry points.
  6. Data architecture: one canonical writer, reconciled D1/graph/search stores, typed GraphQL and derived views.
  7. Audience and business development: social, copywriting, SEO, website, e-commerce, subscribers, contributors and partnerships.
  8. Procurement and funding: public, costed procurement register, spend limits and financial controls.
  9. Patagonia Great House Farm II: staged land, planning, museum, governance and operating feasibility.
  10. Quality and red-team: provenance, contrary evidence, privacy, legal/editorial checks, site QA and continuous improvement.

Sub-flywheels: research/title; FOI/archive and email; missing-evidence recovery; evidence grading/red-team; documentary/script/scene production; social and editorial; website/development/SEO/WebMCP; e-commerce/sales; community/contributions; procurement/funding; Patagonia feasibility. All are children of the same programme and use the same canonical records and work register.

Authority and safeguards: possible legal triggers are private by default. Agents preserve the artifact and create a confidential HubSpot review task with evidence both for and against, materiality, causation and what would confirm/refute. They do not publish allegations, update a public incident log or contact counsel automatically. Agents may research, draft, classify, challenge, QA and coordinate; legal determinations, external contact, sensitive publication, payments, transfers, debt and irreversible decisions remain with the owner/authorised professionals. No private keys, seed phrases or spend-capable wallets are exposed through MCP/WebMCP. Any disbursement requires explicit custodian approval within limits.

Control locations: HubSpot is the task/relationship/procurement register; GitHub is the versioned source; canonical data has one writer; D1, graph/search indexes and webapp views are derived; Hive is coordination; Projects is the public portfolio; Docs/Library holds the master protocol; WebMCP and the collapsed agent footer point back to the same instructions.
