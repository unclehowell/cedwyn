Daily routine

One post a day at most
  1. A Cedwyn page not yet covered is chosen.
  2. A short post is drafted from that page's own words; every date, number and name must appear on the page.
  3. The wording rules are checked; a post that fails is held as a draft and never published.
  4. Search engines and feed hubs are told about the new post.
  5. Owned channels get one native post each, at least four hours apart; communities and private messages are never used.
