One research project. Three delivery projects. One flywheel.

  • Structure v2.0.0 · 30 October 2026

  • Nations Wales · Argentina · online

Research (A) feeds the online legacy asset (B), the Argentina estate (C) and the Wales tour (D). Revenue from deliverables released as v0.0.2 (the first paid semantic-version release of each deliverable) flows back to the research. Every release adds evidence-led material that makes the next release better.

Bands: Progress is published only in four bands: 0%+ (initiated), 33%+ (first deliverable live), 66%+ (paid v0.0.2 releases live or the physical/field pilot running), 100% (closed and handed over). Bands are set by stage evidence, not task counts. Detailed tasks live in the task board, not here.

How value flows

Research → releases → revenue → research

Project A produces the evidence record. Projects B, C and D turn that record into things people can watch, read, play, visit and stand inside. Each deliverable is released first as v0.0.1 (free) and then as v0.0.2, the first paid release. Revenue from v0.0.2 releases funds the next round of research, which improves the next releases.

Project boundaries stay separate: a delay in one project does not stop the others; Project A's case reopening gate (A1) stays dormant until its legal test is met and separately authorised.
