Dependencies and critical path

Do not create false blocking dependencies
  • P1 and P2: start research and concept now; P2 source recovery later updates a versioned reconstruction.
  • P3 and P2: start route mapping and source checks now; exact-location wireframes wait for locational evidence, not for all unrelated retrieval to finish.
  • P1 and P3: share source IDs, provenance and confidence conventions; each project has its own products and acceptance gate.
  • P4 from P2: a potentially relevant lead is preserved under P2, but P4 needs independent legal review and written authority before any delivery work.

Work-package minimum fields and reporting

Unit of execution

Every authorised work package must state ID, milestone, purpose, outputs/product description, accountable owner, task IDs and states, planned dates/checkpoint, dependencies, tolerances, risks/issues, source links, acceptance test and approver. A task list without an acceptance criterion is not a complete work package.

At agreed intervals: checkpoint update and project highlight report. Forecast tolerance breach: exception report. At stage boundary: end-stage report plus next-stage plan. At closure: end-project report, product acceptance and benefits review. Dates and budget limits are proposed until the Executive records a decision.
