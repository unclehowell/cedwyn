Executive summary

Free routes first; £0 spent; review at G0

Recovering, or formally ruling out, every surviving record is the precondition for any title, remedy or accountability route. Option 1 (free routes first) was selected on 18 October; cash spent in the project is £0. The return is non-financial until reparations can be quantified. The case is reviewed at G0 (18 December 2026): continue, revise or stop.

Reasons

Recover or rule out every record

The Mandate (16 Oct 2026) requires every surviving record recovered or formally ruled out. The aim behind it is to reopen BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2 and seek reparations; that needs evidence meeting the Takhar v Gracefield [2019] UKSC 13 test or concealment under the Limitation Act 1980 s.32 (Canada Square v Potter [2023] UKSC 41).
