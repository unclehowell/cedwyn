Grant eligibility screen

Verify each call before application
  • Chubut cultural-space funding: the linked notice is a previous call. Monitor for a new open round and confirm whether the applicant, space and expenses qualify.
  • Activar Patrimonio: assess only if a qualifying museum/heritage institution and an eligible applicant route can be established.
  • Global EbA Fund small-size grants: the 8th call advertises a 26 October 2026 deadline. Consider it only for a genuinely eligible ecosystem-based adaptation package with an appropriate applicant; do not repurpose the house build as an environmental project.
  • Patagonia International Grants: focus is environmental campaigning, not general museum development or capital land/building costs. Treat as a likely poor fit for construction.

A funding register must record call status, deadline, eligible applicant, geography, capital restrictions, match-funding, reporting burden and written outcome. Never include a grant in the baseline before award.

Finance controls and next actions

Set before spending
  1. Approve separate cost centres for the four workstreams above.
  2. For each proposed spend, record purpose, supplier, quote, currency, tax treatment question, payment date, source of funds and acceptance output.
  3. Separate capital, one-off research, recurring digital costs and operating costs.
  4. Build three cases for the Argentina project: digital-only, existing-building adaptation and new-build land option.
  5. Do not publish donation receipts, charitable status or tax deductibility until legally confirmed.
  6. Report monthly on budget, actuals, commitments, forecast and stage-gate decisions.

First approach pack

Before requesting meetings or support
  1. Prepare a one-page bilingual outline: purpose, history, proposed public benefit, present stage, what is and is not decided, and the first questions.
  2. Start with a listening meeting, not a funding request. Ask what local priorities, constraints and existing initiatives the concept should respect.
  3. Offer specific low-burden collaboration options: source review, a school-resource review, a public talk, a cultural programme or an introduction to a qualified local professional.
  4. Record contact date, public contact route, consent to follow up, next step and status in the private partner register.
  5. Use names, logos and statements of endorsement publicly only after written permission.
