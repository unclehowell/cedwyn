Quality management approach

Build check, live check, privacy sweeps
  • Build every site locally before pushing; the build fails on personal contact data, banned phrases and (here) any link to the hosted wiki.
  • Verify live after each deploy at 390 px and desktop.
  • Independent privacy sweeps across all public sites.
  • Records: Quality Register.

Issue management approach

Git, Cloudflare and wiki change control
  • Git: fetch and rebase before every push; no force-push; serving branches only; no history rewrite without the Executive.
  • Cloudflare: add-only and reversible; every change logged with its undo step and a before-state export.
  • Hosted wiki: back up each page before editing, re-read it just before saving, change only the intended text, one change-log row per change set.
  • Dormant assets are not touched without the Executive.
  • Record: Issue Register.

Risk management approach

1–5 scales; escalate at 15

Risks scored 1–5 for probability and impact, reviewed at each highlight report and escalated to the Executive at 15 or more, or when the response needs spending or an outward step. Record: Risk Register.

Keep substantive evidence, source/provenance details, quotations, necessary caveats, scope tests and analysis. Remove redundant purpose prose only; never remove evidence or case-specific context. Agent instruction — confidential threshold review. Any artifact or pattern that may materially affect the legal history of Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr or the 1987 proceedings must first be preserved and assessed privately. A discrepancy, missing record or unanswered request is a research lead, not a finding.

Preserve the original artifact, provenance, exact wording and source ID. Create or update a confidential HubSpot review task with the evidence supporting and cutting against the interpretation, relevance, materiality, causation, what would confirm or refute it, and the next record needed. Do not add unreviewed incidents to a public log, publish allegations or contact counsel or another third party automatically. External action or public wording requires an explicit owner/authorised professional decision. Full procedure: Snowball protocol · Projects.
