Create a guided, location-aware AR tour showing what stood on Llandough Parcel A and explaining the longer history of its setting through source-linked digital wireframes. Existing research and browser-based storytelling give the project a starting point, but an AR experience, exact-site historic models, device testing and user pilot remain to be delivered.

Mandate and brief

Accessible heritage interpretation without false certainty

Core products: mapped tour route; source-linked waypoints; confidence-coded spatial layer; digital wireframes/historic scenes; guided script; AR-capable phone/browser prototype; device/accessibility tests; and a documented pilot/release acceptance decision.

Location control: the Roman villa/farm documented south of the church must remain at its evidenced location. Do not depict it as being on Parcel A unless site-specific evidence supports that placement. Nearby archaeology may be shown as a separate contextual waypoint.

Early monastery/ecclesiastical history: check the source, date and exact location before showing a structure on Parcel A. Where the evidence supports only a broader landscape interpretation, show it as context or a visibly hypothetical reconstruction.

Independence: P3 can begin now; new P2 records are useful dependencies for refinement but do not block route planning or the initial prototype specification. The Argentina museum is separate, and the tour has value without a museum building.

Product acceptance and controls

Location confidence before visual detail
  • Every waypoint has source reference, location method and confidence status.
  • On-parcel evidence, nearby context, regional history and hypothetical scenes use visibly different labels/styles.
  • Start with a device-agnostic phone/browser model; choose AR glasses only if a test demonstrates user benefit.
  • Confirm lawful route access, positioning accuracy, route safety, accessibility, privacy and low-connectivity fallback before a pilot.
  • Keep version history so new P2 material can revise a wireframe without destroying the prior accepted source/model state.
