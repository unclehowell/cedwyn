Parcel A (Phase 1): Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr evidence recovery. Derived from the Project Mandate of 16 October 2026; restructured 18 October; submitted to the Executive, acceptance not yet recorded.

Project definition

Background, objectives, scope, constraints, tolerances, users, interfaces

Background

Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, part of the Cedwyn Estate. Possession: BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2 (Court of Appeal, 31 July 1987; EV-0080). Farmhouse first registered 1983 (WA240304), merged into WA231076 on 2 February 1987 (EV-0066, pending). Our case is that the judgment should be reopened on grounds of concealment; no court has so found.

Project Product Description: a closure-tested evidence position.

Project approach

Request, follow up, complain; court only after advice
  • Map every route against each holder’s policy and the law; then request, follow up, complain, and apply to court only after legal advice (DEP-03).
  • Catalogue, check provenance and verify everything obtained (evidence pages).
  • Research cycle every 6 hours for new sources.
  • The Executive authorises external contact; the Project Manager researches, drafts and maintains the record.

Project management team structure

One-person Project Board

Project Board: Executive (also Senior User), Senior Supplier. Project Manager reports to the Project Board; Team Managers report to the Project Manager. Project Assurance and Project Support as below.
