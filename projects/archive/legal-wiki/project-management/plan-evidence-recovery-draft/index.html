Project Plan — Evidence Recovery

Derived from: Project Mandate and Project Brief (both 16 October 2026)
Baseline: to be set at G0, 18 December 2026
Stage: S0 Start-up & Initiation
Document status: Working plan. Open questions are tracked as work packages below.

1. Ultimate outcome

A complete, auditable evidence position: every surviving record or artefact either obtained and verified, or formally closed as destroyed, missing, classified, or ruled out.

2. Structure

One project under the umbrella programme layer, with work packages grouped by custodian type: public archives, public authorities, private holders and courts.

3. Stages (outline)

  • S0: mandate, brief, registers, and this plan authorised at G0.
  • S1: route mapping and request execution.
  • S2: follow-up, complaints remedy windows, and court applications.
  • S3: catalogue, verification, and closure logging against the five-status closure test.
  • S4: end-of-phase report to the executor.

4. Closure test

Each item receives one status: Obtained, Destroyed, Missing, Classified/withheld, or Ruled out — lost forever. No item remains open without one of these five. Potential evidence destruction or loss of access is escalated immediately.

5. Controls

PRINCE2 7 tailored: Risk, Issue & Change, Quality, Lessons and Decision registers; monthly Highlight Reports; zero tolerance on limitation and preservation deadlines. Trigger events (fraud, concealment, government involvement) monitored continuously and escalated immediately if a threshold is crossed.
