Backdating and evidence rule

These reports are deliberately retrospective reconstructions. Each is backdated to month-end for project-control continuity, using the dates and timestamps in the existing Records Access Chronology and related project records. A reconstructed report is not evidence that a formal PRINCE2 monthly report existed at the historical date. 🕒

Reporting discipline

At month end, the next report carries forward every unfinished task group and every active external dependency, and re-baselines it against actual performance, new evidence and external dependencies. The Performance section must state what is evidenced, not what was merely intended. The Issues/Risks/Concerns section must identify material blockers and ageing. The final section becomes the next month’s controlled plan.

October 2026 — New analytical control themes

The October reporting cycle must track the new cascade-of-accepted-premises paradigm, the property-description mutation chain, the authority/delegation chain, evidence-before-court matrix, and the separate title/alteration/indemnity/fraud/reparation routes. Institutional reports remain isolated; a cross-reference is not a joint finding.
