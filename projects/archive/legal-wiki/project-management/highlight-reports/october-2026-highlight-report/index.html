October 2026 Highlight Report

Reporting date: 5 October 2026. Stage: S0 Start-up & Initiation. Next gate: G0, 18 December 2026. Overall status: AMBER.

1. Executive summary

The programme remains within its current S0 governance structure and has a functioning PRINCE2 control architecture. The principal management change this period is the reconciliation of the public dashboard with the live HubSpot execution ledger. The raw HubSpot ledger contains 94 task records. Twelve are explicitly cancelled administrative/draft records and two are sample records marked for deletion, leaving 80 live delivery/control tasks. Of those live tasks, 4 are complete, 17 are in progress, 30 are waiting and 29 are not started.

2. Progress since the September baseline

  • Evidence, archive and press material continue to be catalogued as discrete evidence records.
  • The Wiki now contains a mature PRINCE2 management hierarchy: Mandate, Brief, Plan, Delivery Breakdown, PIDs, S0 Control Register, Control Registers, Analytics and Highlight Reports.
  • HubSpot has become the private execution ledger, while WordPress remains the public programme/evidence record.
  • New P1 work packages have been added for Glamorgan Archives, BP successor/corporate records, House of Lords papers, ITV/broadcast records, Cotswold Archaeology and photographic records.
  • Preservation and deadline controls remain the immediate priority.

4. Finance and resource

The current Phase-1 control envelope remains £0–£800. Actual expenditure is not inferred from the HubSpot deal amount. Unknown professional or external costs remain TBC until quoted or incurred. No budget variance is asserted without a recorded actual.

5. Quality

The acceptance rule remains: a task/product is complete only when its defined acceptance test is satisfied. External acknowledgement, administrative closure and receipt of a response do not by themselves establish evidential completeness. Evidence records must retain provenance and distinguish documented fact, family evidence and inference.

6. Next reporting period

  • Resolve the 6 October and 9 October deadline controls.
  • Progress P1 evidence-recovery work and the master evidence-capture protocol.
  • Maintain the HMLR/ICR, woodland and external-response controls.
  • Complete the four working PIDs and prepare the G0 decision pack.
  • Reconcile the risk, issue/change, quality, lessons and decision registers against live execution.

7. Gate forecast

G0 remains forecast for 18 December 2026. The gate decision should be based on completed PIDs, justified Business Cases, accepted roles, defined products and quality criteria, established control registers, a credible Stage Plan and resolution or explicit acceptance of outstanding exceptions.
