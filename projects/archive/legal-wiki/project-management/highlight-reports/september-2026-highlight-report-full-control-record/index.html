Phase-1 evidence exhaustion principle

Learn more

September establishes the programme as an evidence-gathering and exhaustion exercise before later material is destroyed, lost, dispersed or becomes harder to recover. Exhaustion means documenting each search and request, reconciling each response, preserving surviving material, identifying what was not found, recording search limitations, and closing only those avenues for which the project has a documented basis to stop. 🕒
