1. Project definition

Background

Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth. Possession: BP Properties Ltd v Buckler, Court of Appeal 31 Jul 1987 [EV-0080]. Farmhouse first registered 1983 (WA240304), merged into WA231076 on 2 Feb 1987 [PENDING: EV-0066].

Objectives

Desired outcome

A complete, auditable evidence position: every item obtained and verified, or closed as destroyed, missing, classified/withheld, or ruled out.

Scope and exclusions

  • In: FOI/EIR, subject access requests, complaints with remedy routes, archive and HMLR copies, court production/preservation applications where holders refuse.
  • In (CH-004, 18 Oct): the whole Cedwyn Estate, about 40 parcels.
  • Out: action on any fraud, concealment or government-involvement finding that meets a threshold — separate parallel project (P2).
  • Out: legal advice. Research support only.

Constraints and assumptions

See PID and Plan — assumptions.

Tolerances

See PID — tolerances.

Users and interfaces

  • Users: the Executive and family.
  • Interfaces: HM Land Registry and the ICR; Glamorgan Archives; NLW; TNA; Vale of Glamorgan Council; Llandough Community Council; Cadw / Welsh Government; Heneb; Wales Office; MoD; HMCTS / MoJ; solicitors (not engaged).
  • Systems: this wiki (public record); HubSpot (private execution ledger, D-002).
