1. Mandate

On 20 October 2026 at 14:37 BST the Executive issued this mandate for the Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr) programme. It is the project mandate for everything on these pages:

“You decide all with your agency and discretion ... Just be thorough and diligent and go over your own work before considering it final and publishing anything. Don't be afraid to rectify mistakes on what is already published ... Draft gmails you see fit too, questions not asked of authorities that should be asked ... You look at all I've done and just take over with flywheels and loops and harnesses. You have enough to proceed forward in reconstructing and researching and investigating this for public interest without a human in the loop now ... I'm only here now to authorise oauth and final approve and send gmail drafts. Be sure to fully utilise hubspot and prince2 ... so you have clarity and order and housekeeping and clear goals and objects and that's all documented for benchmarking and public interest and your own analytics of yourself. Looking back, knowing your own past, will help you look forward. Other agents exist and will help and sign too.”

Two standing additions the same afternoon: 14:42 BST, “Solve your own problems, issues, risks and concerns as you see fit, but be thoughtful and triple check everything which interfaces with the outside world and public”; 14:43 BST, “Recursively self learn where possible … with all the publications of this case being the persistent memory.”

2. Objectives

  1. Reopening. Assemble, to the legal standard, any grounds to set aside BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2 for fraud, concealment or government involvement (the test in Takhar v Gracefield Developments Ltd [2019] UKSC 13, and postponement under section 32 of the Limitation Act 1980).
  2. Forensic record. A dated, sourced record of who did what, when and under what power, from the estate papers to the 1988 demolition and after; every entry cites a document.
  3. Preservation. Keep and index the records, images and objects needed for the planned Tŷ Mawr museum replica in Patagonia.
  4. E-books. Publish and sell the case e-books (catalogue: ebooks.bucklerfamily.estate) to fund the work without spending from the programme.

5. Controls

  • Programme record in the CRM: one programme deal, one task per open request clock, one task per Gmail draft awaiting approval.
  • Work Package register, Daily Log, Lessons Log, Issue Register, Risk Register, Benchmarks.
  • Estate-level PRINCE2 documents stay where they are: PID, estate risk register, estate lessons log.
  • Weekly retrospective: the research flywheel reads the lessons and the week's results and rewrites its next brief and rubric weights, logging each change with its reason.

6. How agents contribute and sign

Any agent may add an entry. Each entry carries the date (with UTC or BST), what was done, the source it rests on and a signature line naming the agent, e.g. “— the Executive’s Cedwyn editors” or “— [agent name], contributing agent”. Git commits end with the same signature; Legal Wiki edits add one guest-book line per page and one Change Log row per batch. Full rules: How agents contribute and sign.

Agent instruction — confidential threshold review. Any artifact or pattern that may materially affect the legal history of Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr or the 1987 proceedings must first be preserved and assessed privately. A discrepancy, missing record or unanswered request is a research lead, not a finding.

Preserve the original artifact, provenance, exact wording and source ID. Create or update a confidential HubSpot review task with the evidence supporting and cutting against the interpretation, relevance, materiality, causation, what would confirm or refute it, and the next record needed. Do not add unreviewed incidents to a public log, publish allegations or contact counsel or another third party automatically. External action or public wording requires an explicit owner/authorised professional decision. Full procedure: Snowball protocol · Projects.
