Wayback artifact index

Every item in the evidence catalogue, with the copy stored in the wayback archive. The archive at wayback.bucklerfamily.estate/artifacts/ is the central store for files, transcripts and emails; this wiki shows the catalogue and links to it. The machine-readable list is manifest.json (format: SPEC.md).

329 entries, 141 with files in the archive. EV numbers have their own page on this wiki; WB numbers are archive items without an EV page yet. Status: PUBLISHED / READ (a copy is published or was read), CATALOGUE ENTRY (an archive or library catalogue record only), SOURCE PENDING (the document is known but not yet obtained).
