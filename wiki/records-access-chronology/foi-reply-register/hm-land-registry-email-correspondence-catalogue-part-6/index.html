Record significance: HMLR confirms an internal application to investigate and correct any error that may have been made. This is an administrative investigation step; it does not establish that an error occurred, still less fraud or concealment.

15 October 2026, 23:00 UTC · User submission seeking Schedule 4 alteration and Schedule 8 indemnity consideration

From: requester · To: HMLR Wales Office and Customer Support
Subject: Z084NQV / N610CGV – WA240304 and WA231076 – application to alter the register (Schedule 4 LRA 2002) and follow-up on the 1983 first registration
Attachment: none identified in mailbox result.

The email asked HMLR to add the submission to Z084NQV and consider alteration under Schedule 4 and indemnity under Schedule 8 of the Land Registration Act 2002. It stated that the submission addressed the 1983 first registration of WA240304 and events through 1987, did not deal with the 1982 application or eastern land, and did not waive questions about either.

It asked about the 1969 conveyance, 1971 abstract, root of title, the 1975 deed and plans, the 1983 application, the first-registration examination and the amalgamation. The email was sent to historic email addresses. HMLR had already explained that legacy email addresses were no longer supported for routing; receipt and addition to Z084NQV are not confirmed by a substantive acknowledgement in the mailbox material inspected.

21 October 2026, 15:15 UTC · Requester follow-up on 260928-6762800 and parcel enquiry

From: requester · To: HMLR Customer Support
Subject: Re: Further to Your Enquiry: Ref: 260928-6762800

The message asked HMLR to clarify the status of the separate enquiry 260928-6762800, preservation of the first-registration and complaint files, and the outstanding factual question about whether the 1982–83 records distinguish the farmhouse, garden and curtilage from the eastern agricultural parcel. It also referred to statutory and review routes.

Correction for future citations: the 21 October email described 260928-6762800 as the SAR. The dedicated SAR reference is D260928. The two references are not interchangeable. The route distinction is corrected in the Forensic Assessment.

27 October 2026, 12:01 UTC · HMLR requests identity verification for SAR D260928 only

From: HM Land Registry, Information Rights Team
Reference: D260928
Attachment: one inline logo image only; no substantive attachment from HMLR was identified.

HMLR said it needed to be reasonably satisfied of the requester's identity and asked for photographic identity evidence (for example passport or driving licence) together with a recent utility bill. It said it would make a file note but would not retain copies of the documents.

Scope: this request applies to SAR D260928. It does not apply to FOI F261030, EIR F261029, N610CGV, Z084NQV or enquiry 260928-6762800.

Privacy: identity documents, utility bills and residential-address evidence are excluded from this public catalogue and must not be uploaded to the public site or HubSpot. The reason both documents were needed, acceptable alternatives, secure transfer method and clock-start date should be confirmed. The ICO says identity checks must be reasonable and proportionate and that the SAR clock starts when the controller has the identification information it needs; therefore the 28 October date stated in HMLR's 1 October acknowledgement should not automatically be described as a missed deadline after the 27 October identity request. ICO right-of-access guidance.
