Enwau: the names of Tŷ Mawr

A forensic register of every name and identity of the house, the town and the estate · Cofrestr enwau · Registro de nombres

Tŷ Mawr, Ty-mawr, Great House, Great House Farm, Cedfin, Cydfin, Cydwin, Ked Fen, Red Fen, Llandough Home Farm, Church Farm; Llandough-juxta-Penarth and Llandough near Cowbridge. The story has been scattered because the place has so many names. This register puts 108 of them in one place, each with its form, its date, the source document, who wrote it and our note on the confusion it carries.

Tŷ Mawr is first named in the record we have found in David Stewart’s 1824 survey of the Bute Glamorgan estates. Mary Williams’s c.1974 statement reports 1667 as the date of the Williams family’s acquisition of Tŷ Mawr; no 1667 instrument has been located in the present record set. Bute and other third-party papers dated before 1824 are listed with their dates and sources and are under forensic examination; how we treat outside records.
