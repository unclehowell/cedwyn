Sir Keith Joseph MP

Minister of Housing and Local Government and Minister for Welsh Affairs, 1962–1964
Era in the records: 1962–1963

Photograph: Geoff Charles · 28 November 1963 · CC BY-SA 4.0 · Source (as text): https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sir_Keith_Joseph_visits_Llangefni_and_Caernarfon_(1469270)_Crop.jpg · period photograph (28 November 1963). Shown as published; resized.

On the record

Hansard records that a Western Ground Rents Ltd director sent correspondence to the Minister on 3 Dec 1962, and that the Minister met the company's chairman and general manager. He answered in the Commons in early 1963. The texts do not mention Llandough or the farm.
