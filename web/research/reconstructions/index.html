Scene reconstructions

Modern interpretive reconstructions of documented scenes, one place, time and scene each. Every image is an artist's impression made from quoted records — held as a reconstruction, never as evidence. Each entry keeps the record-grounded description separate from the openly qualified interpretation, and pairs the scene with a 2D wireframe illustration for the Wales tour.
