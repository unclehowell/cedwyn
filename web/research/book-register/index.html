Book research register

Printed books, journals and edited records that carry lines about Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough-by-Penarth, Leckwith and Cogan, the Vaughans, the Bute estate and the Williams family. Each entry gives the line as printed, the page, where the book can be found, how far it was checked, and what it does and does not show. Searches that found nothing are kept too, so the gaps are visible.

The whole register as a spreadsheet: book-research-findings.csv. Source addresses are shown as plain text; copy one into a browser to open the book.
