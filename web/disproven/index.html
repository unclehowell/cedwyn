Disproven theories

What we stated, what the record shows, and when we corrected it · 4 entries · updated 27 October 2026

This register lists statements we once published or relied on and later found wrong. Each entry gives what we stated and where, what the record shows with its source, when we corrected it, and links to the corrected pages. Corrections stay on this page: owning them is part of keeping a reliable record.

A statement enters the register only when a record contradicts it. Questions the records leave open are kept in the register of what the records don’t say, not here.

DP-001Corrected 27 October 2026

Parcel A included the green registered by the council in 1970

What we stated
Our research pages and the infographic “The Land Changing Hands” showed Parcel A as split into three parts: the woodland, the house and yard, and the village green VG41. The research layer described the green as “south-east ground of Parcel A”. Shown on the infographic and in the Research Library (“Three Parts of Parcel A”) until 27 October 2026.
What the record shows
The family’s marked-up Ordnance Survey plan of the holding shows the Parcel A line drawn round the farmstead, with its south edge along the lane north of St Dochdwy’s Church: the line ends before the green begins. The green is a separate registration, village green VG41, registered by the council in 1970 under the Commons Registration Act 1965 (catalogue EV-0044). The certified register map shows the green as a small roadside wedge (catalogue EV-0263).

DP-002Corrected 27 October 2026

The house was demolished in November 1988

What we stated
One record we relied on, the RCAHMW plan card NA/GL/89/208 (H.J. Thomas measured plan, dated 26 April 1974 and 12 December 1988), is typed “House demolished Nov. 1988”. Our documentary scene notes carried November 1988 as the date (frame F082), and we tested whether it placed the demolition before the December hearing (27 October 2026).
What the record shows
The RCAHMW record NA/GL/88/49 gives the demolition as 6th December 1988, and the building still stood at the early-December hearing (Western Mail, 6 December 1988; South Wales Echo, 3 December 1988). The two RCAHMW entries still differ on their face. The record shows possession was taken before 3 December, so “Nov.” most likely records the month of the eviction, not the demolition. The difference is kept as an open question, not a finding.
Corrected
27 October 2026

DP-003Corrected 27 October 2026 (script line now reads “Sutton, down the Vale”)

Sutton, and the farm’s setting, “on the coast”

What we stated
The documentary script for scene F005 placed Sutton “on the coast”, and working drafts of the film’s settings leaned on a coastal look (script drafts to 27 October 2026).
What the record shows
Tŷ Mawr stood inland, on the hill at Llandough above the River Ely: H.J. Thomas measured plan and sketch, NGR ST 1680 7331, 26 April 1974 (RCAHMW NA/GL/89/208). Sutton lies down the Vale, not on the coast.
Corrected
27 October 2026 (script line now reads “Sutton, down the Vale”)

DP-004Corrected 27 October 2026

A modern “Proposed Ground/First Floor” plan as a Tŷ Mawr record

What we stated
A modern “Proposed Ground/First Floor” house plan arrived among the Tŷ Mawr archive images on 27 October 2026 and was first read with them as part of the Tŷ Mawr material.
What the record shows
The plan is of another property, outside the Tŷ Mawr scope. It shows no feature of the 1974 measured plan (Hall, Passage, Kitchen, Dairy; RCAHMW NA/GL/89/208). It is parked for later inspection as Kissed Frogs KF-49.
