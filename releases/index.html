Cedwyn

The Release Machine

A music-maker for publication dates: each line is a document related to Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough and the Cedwyn research, placed at its publication date between 1987 and 2026. Every populated title is hyperlinked to its source.

Press play and the playhead sweeps the years like a music maker; each pad that lights is that line's publication date. Tap a lit pad (or the title) to open the source. "Related chronology" adds lines whose date is not a publication date (a court decision, an archived capture, a catalogue record's creation, a scan upload) - clearly labelled, and never mixed into the publication lines.

How to read the dates

  • Every default line is a publication date: the document's own print or online publication date. Where only the year is known, the pad sits on the year and the line says "(year only)" - no day or month is invented.
  • Source links open through this site's Wayback Vault, which shows its captured copy first and always offers the live website and the Internet Archive copy.
  • Each line names its source basis. A library-catalogue or publisher page verifies the book and its date; it is not a full text. Full-text scans are labelled as such.
  • The optional related chronology lines are not publication dates: the BAILII line is the date the judgment was handed down; the Amazon line is the date of an archived capture; the ADS line is when the catalogue record was created; the Internet Archive line is when the scan was uploaded. They are kept on separate, labelled lines so the two kinds of date are never conflated.

A discrepancy, missing record or unanswered request is a research lead, not a finding.

Preserve the original artifact, provenance, exact wording and source ID.
