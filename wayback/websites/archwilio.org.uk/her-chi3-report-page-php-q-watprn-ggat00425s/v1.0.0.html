Roman Villa, Llantwit Major (Caermead)
Primary Reference Number (PRN)
: 00425s
Trust

Unitary authority

NGR

Site Type (preferred type first)
: Roman - Villa
Status
: UA Local List (Adopted)
Summary :
Large, fully Romanised villa with a complicated structural history between the 2nd-4th century AD, and possibly built on the site of an earlier Iron Age farmstead. Ultimately of double-courtyard plan.
Description :
Villa of complicated structural history, not fully understood, certainly from mid 2nd century, and possibly on site of earlier Iron Age farmstead; including apparently a period of decline in the early 3rd century, followed by the main floruit extending into the 4th century. Ultimately of double-courtyard plan, with ranges of buildings around an inner courtyard on the W and an outer yard on the E; the main house comprised the N and W ranges round the inner courtyard, the W range including baths. Built in local limestone and sandstone with some decorative detail in Bath stone; Pennant sandstone roofing slate with some ceramic roofing tile; window glass; mosaic, stone and opus signinum floors; at least some walls plastered. Also evidence for metalworking (in part of disused baths) and burials, including those of what appear to be victims of massacre. The area occupied by the villa is grass-covered with numerous banks, mounds and hollows; fragmentary walls are visible and are best preserved at the SE corner.
The site is a Scheduled Monument (GM020) and is included on the Vale of Glamorgan List of County Treasures.
