Early Medieval Cross, Llandough Churchyard

Primary Reference Number (PRN) : 00076s
Trust : Glamorgan Gwent
Community : Llandough
Unitary authority : Vale of Glamorgan
NGR : ST16797325
Site Type (preferred type first) : Early Medieval - Cross
Status : UA Local List (Adopted)

Summary :
Composite pillar-cross consisting of a two-part shaft with massive central collar and splayed base but lacking the crosshead, standing in St Dochdwy's Churchyard, Llandough-Juxta-Penarth.

Description :
Composite pillar-cross consisting of a two-part shaft with massive central collar and splayed base but lacking the crosshead, standing in St Dochdwy's Churchyard, Llandough-Juxta-Penarth. Upper and lower sections both have recessed panels of interlace between bulbous angle mouldings; all sides on square upper section, with plain mouldings; front and back on rectangular lower section with decorated mouldings. Collar formed of five horizontal bands of decoration; decoration on base includes human figures. Late 10th early 11th century. (RCAHMW 1976, 61-2 no.938).
Evans 2003: GGAT 73 Early-Medieval Ecclesiastical Sites Project database
