Listed Buildings in Llandough, Vale of Glamorgan

  1. II Barons Court PH [image: Photos]
    On the W bank of the Ely River and its late C20 road bridge, adjacent to the roundabout linking the A4055 Barry Road and A4160 Penarth Road.
  2. II Church of St Dochdwy [image: Photos]
    Prominently sited on high ground overlooking Cardiff Bay to E and at the junction between Llandough Hill, Penlan Road and Leckwith Road with small village green in front and within a large walled chur
  3. II Former National School and School-house [image: Photos]
    Situated on a corner, the former school fronting Penlan Road and the schoolhouse fronting Lewis Road
  4. II Pound Cottage
    Situated opposite the church and village green, by the junction of Penlan and Leckwith Roads and Llandough Hill.
