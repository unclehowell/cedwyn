How an order works

  1. Pick an item and press the button. It opens in our print partner's secure store, where you choose size and colour and pay.
  2. The item is printed to order by a print partner near you (UK items print in the UK where available).
  3. It is posted to you, with tracking. Delivery cost and time are shown at checkout before you pay.
  4. If it arrives damaged or misprinted, it's reprinted or refunded. See terms, delivery and returns.

Prices are in US dollars, the store's base currency; your card converts at checkout. They cover the print, the payment fees and little else. The project runs at cost, not for profit.

Anti-boilerplate rule (mandatory): Do not explain a page’s purpose in its body, add a “Purpose” paragraph, write a generic introduction, or add an AI-style overview. Use one sentence only — “What this page is about → Read the Docs” — linked to the closest matching page-type anchor in Read the Docs (for example #evidence-pages, #catalogue-pages, #forensic-assessments, #chronology-pages or #analysis-pages). Keep substantive evidence, source/provenance details, quotations, necessary caveats, scope tests and analysis. Remove redundant purpose prose only; never remove evidence or case-specific context. See Editorial standards.

Mandatory page-local change log: before finishing an edit, append a row to the page-bottom “Agent page change log / guest book” table. If missing, install it and log the installation. Agent instruction — confidential threshold review. Any artifact or pattern that may materially affect the legal history of Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr or the 1987 proceedings must first be preserved and assessed privately. A discrepancy, missing record or unanswered request is a research lead, not a finding.

Preserve the original artifact, provenance, exact wording and source ID. Create or update a confidential HubSpot review task with the evidence supporting and cutting against the interpretation, relevance, materiality, causation, what would confirm or refute it, and the next record needed. Do not add unreviewed incidents to a public log, publish accusations or contact counsel or another third party automatically.
