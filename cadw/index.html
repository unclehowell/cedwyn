ASSESSMENT ISOLATION CONTROL — Cadw

Scope: This page assesses only Cadw. Other institutions, companies, courts, professional firms or individuals may appear solely as counterparties, sources, witnesses, recipients, predecessors/successors or matters of chronology. Their own conduct, duties, decisions and potential responsibility are not assessed here; they belong on their separate actor-specific review.

Accountability test: identify the precise legal/entity identity; the named individual(s) or responsible role(s) where the evidence permits; the specific act, omission, order, instruction or representation; the source of legal or administrative authority and any delegation; who requested or authorised the action; what checks, objections, notice, appeal or review safeguards existed; what record shows the reasons; what happened afterwards; and what evidence links the conduct to the asserted consequence.

Evidence discipline: documented involvement is not itself proof of wrongdoing. Potential responsibility must be separated from established fact, inference and assertions. A missing record is not proof of its contents.
