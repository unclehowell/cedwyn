Parcel-scope correction — 9 October 2026. WA240304 is the farmhouse first-registration file/strand; do not treat the title number or this file label as synonymous with the full Parcel A. On the family’s established title case, Parcel A from the 1877/1928 arrangement also included the forest north of the house and south-east ground later registered as VG41. [SOURCE PENDING] These two areas were seized on 4 July 1955 while WGR enforced possession of Parcel B. The WA240304 file must be read and its plan mapped against that full Parcel-A geometry, the separate Parcel B and the later title chain.

Forensic capture objective

Establish farmhouse title, title class, original register, D23 examination material, correspondence, notices and plan. The post-death dates require chronology but are not themselves evidence of improper motive.

Required capture

  • Complete copies and transcription where lawful.
  • Original register/title class and application form.
  • All notices, service evidence, requisitions and applicant responses.
  • Plans, acreage and parcel references.
  • Provenance, custody and contradictions.

A missing or contradictory record is not itself proof of fraud or concealment.
