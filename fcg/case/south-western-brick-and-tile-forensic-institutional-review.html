Current finding

As at 14 October 2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the company. The catalogue records the deed; its operative terms and plan have not been read for this review, so what land it concerned and how it relates to the farm parcels is not established here.

Next step

Obtain the deed through HM Land Registry (Form OC2) and read its plan against the two-parcel map. Check Companies House and the limeworks material for the company’s operations. Acceptance: the deed’s land and purpose are stated and sourced.
