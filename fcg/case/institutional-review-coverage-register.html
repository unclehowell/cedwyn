Coverage check — 14 October 2026. Every institution, company, court, archive or media body that appears in the wiki’s evidence, reply register or correspondence now has its own forensic assessment page, linked below. Appearance in this list records a documented connection, not fault. Individuals are handled under the named-individual protocol on the Forensic Assessments index, not here.

Maintenance rule: when a new body appears in a reply, letter, evidence page or news report, add its assessment page and a row here in the same edit.

Deliberate boundaries

  • Individuals (councillors, officials, journalists, photographers, presenters) are covered by the named-individual protocol, not by an institutional page.
  • Personal-support correspondence and unrelated matters that appear in the family’s mailbox are deliberately not given public assessment pages.
  • Platforms used to send requests (for example WhatDoTheyKnow) are channels, not parties.
