Purpose

Record negative searches so that “not found” is auditable rather than assumed.

For every search record

  • Custodian/institution and responsible office
  • Date searched and systems/series searched
  • Names, geographic variants, dates and reference numbers used
  • Repositories, databases and physical holdings checked
  • Restrictions or exclusions
  • Found/not found result
  • Searcher’s explanation and completeness assessment
  • Alternative custodian or duplicate route
  • Preservation/destruction/transfer follow-up

Closure rule: a nil result is not final while a credible alternative holding, retention explanation or transfer trail remains open.

Change history is maintained on the Master Change Log; no separate page-local version footer is maintained.
