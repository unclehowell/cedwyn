Forensic Assessment — NCTSO

Scope: National Counter Terrorism Security Office role only where a specific historical record is identified.

The reviewed Great House Farm record does not presently establish an NCTSO decision or operative role in the title, possession, heritage or demolition history. The organisation should therefore not be treated as part of the historic accountability chain merely because it appears in the project universe. 🕒10-10-2026

Current finding

No NCTSO wrongdoing or operative historical role is established. 🕒10-10-2026
