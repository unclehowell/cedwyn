Evidence

  • SOURCE PENDING: an HMLR notification is reported to have arrived after Mary (Nancy) Williams’s death; no named family member’s statement of it is yet on the wiki. The corrected date used on this Wiki is 26 March 1983.
  • GHF-E180: placeholder for the reported letter. The original has not been produced on this page.
  • GHF-E158, E161, E162, and Land Registry Titles: registration context.

Description

The reported letter, addressee, title reference, dispatch date, receipt date, envelope/postmark, service record and applicable objection or appeal deadline have not been verified from the original documents.

Interpretation

The alleged late notification remains SOURCE PENDING and a record-retrieval lead. The available register chronology does not establish when a notice was sent or received, whether a deadline had expired, or whether HMLR deliberately withheld notice. The letter and registration/service file are needed before those conclusions can be drawn.

Added 15 October 2026 — keep notice and title as separate issues: the reported post-death notification remains a family-evidence lead until the original notice and service record are produced. It does not, by itself, establish a mistake in the underlying title. The separate substantive question—what conveyancing evidence supported registration of the farmhouse-and-garden parcel under WA240304—is analysed at 1983 First Registration: Parcel A Title Basis and Alleged HMLR Mistake. The two lines of enquiry should be pursued together but not conflated.
