Forensic Assessment — European Court of Human Rights / Archives

Scope: the ECHR archive/custodial function relating to Application 14464/88.

Established role

The archive record is relevant to the survival, destruction and metadata of the historic application. The current project record states that the underlying case file was destroyed under the Court’s retention policy.

Forensic control test

Separate three things: (1) what the application and admissibility decision establish; (2) what archival metadata survives; and (3) what the destruction policy means. Destruction of a file is not evidence that its contents favoured either party. 🕒

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish archival wrongdoing. This is not a finding that no archive involvement or records gap exists. The proper response to the destroyed record is controlled archival reconstruction, not invention of its contents.

Human action

Retain the application number, decision date, archival confirmation, retention/destruction policy and any surviving index or correspondence. Legal counsel should determine whether any present Strasbourg route is procedurally viable; domestic title and registration work remains primary.

Acceptance: a complete surviving archive trail is catalogued with explicit limits on what the destroyed file can no longer prove.
