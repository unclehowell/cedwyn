Forensic Assessment — Forest of Cardiff

Scope: Forest of Cardiff’s role as present woodland holder/charitable owner and its statements about acquisition and disposal.

Current evidence

[SOURCE PENDING] Woodland was historically connected to the farmhouse parcel. Forest of Cardiff has stated in its correspondence that it bought the woodland from Ideal Homes (now Persimmon Homes) after due diligence, sold a small western section about 20 years ago and has observed nearby garden extensions. The charity has also made a private sale proposal for the remaining woodland.

Forensic control test

Do not collapse this into the historic title claim. The key question is whether the woodland now held by Forest of Cardiff is the same physical land as the historic Parcel A woodland, and what conveyance/title documents support the charity’s acquisition.

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by Forest of Cardiff. The assessment is limited to the acquisition statement and identified title-chain evidence. Its acquisition statement is useful counter-evidence to the family’s remembered 1955 transfer narrative and creates a concrete title-chain question about Ideal Homes.

Human action

Obtain the current woodland title, 1994/2005 onward conveyances, the Ideal Homes transfer and Forest of Cardiff’s acquisition records. Compare plans before considering any proprietary or indemnity claim.

Acceptance: a parcel-specific woodland chain identifies the exact land, titles, transfers, consideration where available and current possession.
