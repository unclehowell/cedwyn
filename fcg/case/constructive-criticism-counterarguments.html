Controlling scope correction — 9 October 2026. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 and the full Parcel A passed to the Williams family: farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and west-side yard, garden, north forest and south-east ground later registered as VG41. Parcel B was the separate eastern agricultural fields. The 1939 agreement/plan is already obtained and depicts Parcel B only. [SOURCE PENDING] The 1949 permissive-occupancy attempt failed. Mary (Nancy) Williams said the title papers went missing in 1950–52 (witness statement c.1974, Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony). [SOURCE PENDING] On 4 July 1955 enforcement of Parcel B also seized the Parcel-A north forest and south-east green under “whole farm” wording. Mary’s defence of the remaining occupied house group does not narrow the title claim. The primary title case and the alternative limitation case (12 years from 1928 potentially maturing by 1940 if legal conditions were met) are distinct. 🕒

How the source record is used on this Wiki

  • Evidence description: the entry states what the source records, with its date, reference or provenance where known.
  • Interpretation: the interpretation is kept separate from the source description and is tied to the evidence recorded elsewhere on the Wiki.
  • Open question: where the underlying record has not been found, the missing record is identified rather than supplied with invented content. 🕒
