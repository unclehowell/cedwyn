The legal framework

Existing judgments and proceedings

31 July 1987 — BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2. Court of Appeal decision. BAILII case record.

1988–1989 — European Commission of Human Rights application 14464/88. The Wiki records the application as introduced on 3 May 1988, registered on 12 December 1988 and declared inadmissible on 14 April 1989. HUDOC.

Final appeal reopening

CPR 52.30 — Reopening of final appeals. The rule states that the Court of Appeal or High Court will not reopen a final determination unless the requirements in CPR 52.30(1)(a)–(c) are satisfied. Civil Procedure Rules, Part 52.

Practice Direction 52A, section 7 — Reopening appeals. Paragraphs 7.1–7.4 specify the permission procedure and supporting written evidence. Practice Direction 52A.

Fraud, concealment and limitation

Limitation Act 1980, s.32. Section 32 concerns postponement of limitation in cases based on fraud, deliberate concealment or mistake. legislation.gov.uk — section 32.

20 March 2019 — Takhar v Gracefield Developments Ltd [2019] UKSC 13. Supreme Court judgment concerning an application to set aside a judgment obtained by fraud. UK Supreme Court case page.

15 November 2023 — Canada Square Operations Ltd v Potter [2023] UKSC 41. Supreme Court judgment concerning deliberate concealment under s.32 of the Limitation Act 1980. UK Supreme Court case materials.

Land registration

Land Registration Act 2002, Schedule 4. Provides the statutory framework for alteration of the register, including rectification. legislation.gov.uk — Schedule 4.

Land Registration Act 2002, Schedule 8. Provides the statutory framework for indemnity. legislation.gov.uk — Schedule 8.

Land Registration Act 2002, Schedule 1, paragraph 2. Lists interests of persons in actual occupation among the interests capable of overriding first registration, subject to the statutory conditions. legislation.gov.uk — Schedule 1.

Human rights

Human Rights Act 1998, Schedule 1. Sets out the Convention rights given effect in domestic law, including Article 6 and Article 1 of Protocol 1. legislation.gov.uk — Schedule 1.

How the source record is used on this Wiki

  • Evidence description: the entry states what the source records, with its date, reference or provenance where known.
  • Interpretation: the interpretation is kept separate from the source description and is tied to the evidence recorded elsewhere on the Wiki.
