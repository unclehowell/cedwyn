Forensic Assessment — Senedd Culture Committee

Scope: present democratic scrutiny/accountability only.

Control test

Any referral must distinguish private title litigation from systemic lessons about heritage protection, records preservation and public accountability. The Committee should not be asked to determine private ownership.

Human action
