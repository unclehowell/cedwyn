Forensic Assessment — Cotswold Archaeological Trust

Scope: the archaeological contractor’s own work, instructions and records. No conclusion is drawn from GGAT’s role merely because the bodies interacted.

Role

The archaeological record places Cotswold in the later intervention programme. The forensic question is what work it was actually commissioned to perform, under what planning/condition mandate, what it reported, and how its records were preserved.

Control test

Reconstruct the commissioning authority, scope, condition numbers, briefing, fieldwork, finds/osteology transfer, report chain and subsequent archive deposit. The 1994 mandate/condition 17 issues identified in the wider forensic material must be tested against the original documents.

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish personal or institutional negligence or fraud. This is not proof that all records or factual issues have been resolved. The current record justifies document recovery because the archaeological intervention is an independent factual layer relevant to what remained on the site and what was known.

Human action

Obtain the original contract/brief, condition references, field records, report, finds register, archive deposit and correspondence with the planning authority/GGAT.

Acceptance: a source-complete archaeological intervention dossier with a clear authority and records chain.
