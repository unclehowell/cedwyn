Assessed 14 October 2026. Scope: the Member of Parliament’s office as a point of escalation for the family’s correspondence. The office is not a decision-maker on title. Inclusion is not a finding of fault.

Documented appearances

DateAppearance
12 June 2026The family writes to the MP about Great House Farm (now Church View Close).
27 July 2026The family lodges a complaint that earlier correspondence had no substantive response.
5 August 2026The office replies that it searched its systems, regrets the lack of earlier replies and takes up the matter.
20 August 2026The office reports further enquiries made on the family’s behalf.
18 September 2026The office forwards a response from the HM Land Registry Chief Executive and Chief Land Registrar (register entry R44). The family asks to meet and sends further material on 18 and 19 September. The office says it is still awaiting the Ministry of Justice.

Sources: the family’s Gmail correspondence and the reply register (R44).

Current finding

As at 14 October 2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by the office. After the 27 July complaint the office engaged and made enquiries. Whether the Ministry of Justice has now replied is not recorded here.

Next step

Log the Ministry of Justice response date when received and record whether a meeting took place. Acceptance: the outstanding MoJ answer is received or a dated chase is on file. See the Ministry of Justice review.
