The standard

Clearance measures how an institution has handled requests for records about Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough: prompt, full, free and straight answers score; delay, partial answers, “not held” with no record, cost and repeat refusals, fees, diversion, contradiction and silence cost points.

Scale

Every institution starts at 500 of 1000 (held between 0 and 1000). The bar is high: from 500, clearance needs a net +400, for example ten on-time, fully disclosed replies with nothing deducted.

Point rules

Fixed rules. Points move only on a dated email or letter; each change on the ranking shows its date and a link to the message in the case-mail archive and its wayback copy where one exists. No points are given for opinion.

Statutory clocks

  • FOI (FOIA s.10) and EIR (reg. 5(2)): 20 working days.
  • FOI internal review: 20 working days (ICO guidance; 40 only in exceptional cases).
  • EIR internal review (reg. 11(4)): 40 working days.
  • Subject access (UK GDPR art. 12(3)): one calendar month.
  • Letters and preservation notices: 20 working days for a reply.
  • Working days exclude weekends and bank holidays in England and Wales; day 1 is the first working day after receipt. A fair clarification pauses the clock. Overdue points grow each working day a reply is late.

Method

  • Record: the per-institution forensic assessments of 21 Oct 2026, the correspondence register (fee notes) and the clarification ledger. Automated mailbox categories are context only and never scored on their own.
  • An item whose date or meaning is uncertain is not scored, or is marked “to confirm”.
  • Requests still inside their deadline score nothing until answered or overdue.
  • Re-scored every day at midday from new case mail. Data and scorer: clearance.json.
