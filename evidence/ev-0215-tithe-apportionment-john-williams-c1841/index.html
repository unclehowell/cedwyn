Quotation (index): Occupier “Williams John”; landowner “Bute the Marquess of” (33 parcels) and “Evans the Revd James (Glebe)” (3 parcels); no. 209 “Courtyard &c”; no. 196 “Cottage & garden”.

Proves: c.1841 John Williams occupied 36 parcels, 119a 2r 30p by the sum of the indexed areas: 33 owned by the Marquess of Bute (119a 0r 6p; 6 of them noted Leckwith, 14a 3r 12p) and 3 glebe parcels of the Revd James Evans (2r 24p).

Does not prove: Any lease, its term or rent; that this holding is the 1824 plan 19 farm (parcel-to-parcel match not made); any boundary after c.1841; the 1870s acreage (c.76a, EV-0212, PROVISIONAL). The estate-wide theory (H15) remains HYPOTHESIS (1/5).
