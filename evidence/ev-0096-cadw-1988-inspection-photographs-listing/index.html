Establishes: The 11 June 2026 Welsh Government response says Cadw inspectors visited and assessed the house for listing, that it did not meet the listing criteria, and that the only inspection record then held by Welsh Government was the two photographs. The official PDF includes a farmhouse photograph and a barn photograph, both dated 29 July 1988. Read the official response and photographs (PDF). Does not establish: that no related file survives elsewhere, the identity of every decision-maker, a full rationale beyond the brief response, or the specific transfer or destruction history of any associated record.

Does not prove: The disclosure does not establish that no file survives outside Welsh Government. It says other associated records may have been transferred to RCAHMW for permanent preservation or destroyed under the Public Records Act 1958, but does not identify specific records or confirm which route applied to each item. Whether records E137, E139 or E143 were considered remains unknown. The meaning of the July 1988 “YYY” code remains unresolved.

Catalogued, not yet viewed: Glamorgan Archives D1824/4/1, a Penarth Civic Society file headed ‘CADW’ (November 1990 to March 1992), and D1824/3/7, the society’s correspondence of 1988–1992. The catalogue descriptions have been read; the files have not been seen, so whether either refers to the house, the July 1988 inspection or the December 1988 listing decision is not checked.
