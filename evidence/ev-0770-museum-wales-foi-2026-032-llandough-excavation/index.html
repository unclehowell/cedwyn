Provenance and catalogue treatment

The 39-page PDF was disclosed by Museum Wales on 22 September 2026 under FOI 2026-032. The archive file is identified as wp-1790112812516.pdf. EV-0134 remains unchanged and continues to describe only pp.1–2. This additive record inventories pp.1–39 and links the earlier entry.

The file contains redacted personal names. This record uses institutional names and role descriptions only. It does not infer the identity or authority of a signatory beyond the wording visible in the document.

Other catalogue observations

  • All finds-list headings use “Llandough, Penarth” (pp.5–15), distinguishing this site from Llandough near Cowbridge.
  • The red-rectangle stickers on p.36 require a crosswalk to the published radiocarbon sample and burial identifiers.
  • The box counts on p.37 are 183 new boxes and 221 long boxes.
  • The CAT Access Graves database and four-table Box Catalogue are named at p.38 but are not included in the disclosure.
  • The 4 December 1998 meeting notes, 1999 Collections Impact Assessment form, 23 July 1994 Museum letter and cross photograph, 1996 assessment of 16 skeletons, and full bone report are named or referenced but not held in this file.

Public-source cross-check

  • Excavation area and plan: CAT Report 94213, printed p.2, describes 0.22 ha immediately north of St Dochdwy’s; its plan figures are at printed pp.23–27. The 2005 report, pp.4–5, gives the published locality ST 1681 7331.
  • Cross: CAT Report 94213, printed p.4, describes the cross in its present position south of the church and records an earlier photograph showing it west of the church. EV-0770 p.3 refers to the separate Museum letter and photograph of 23 July 1994.
  • Burial totals and condition: CAT Report 94213, printed pp.6–7, records 858 inhumations, 152 disarticulated groups and condition categories including 434 good, 153 fair and 271 fragmentary. The 2005 report, pp.41–42, publishes 1,026 burials and the later examined total.
  • Radiocarbon: CAT Report 94213, printed p.13, lists four Beta Analytic determinations and notes two samples with insufficient collagen for conventional analysis. The 2005 report, pp.41–42, publishes the expanded Llandough sample set, including Waikato determinations. Public results answer the general laboratory/results question; the remaining question is the p.36 sticker-to-sample crosswalk and any unpublished determinations.
  • HER coordinates: Heneb HER GGAT02272s and GGAT03583s use ST 1683 7334. The Museum/CAT locality ST 1681 7331 is the same reference used on the RCAHMW 1974 farmhouse plan. The archived HER event record GGATE003875 carries a separate grid reference, ST 16739 73315.
  • Burial 631: Museum Wales’ 2 May 2007 article “The suffering of burial 631” gives a public interpretation; the 2005 report provides the excavation and radiocarbon context.
  • Isotope publications: The 2013 Journal of Archaeological Science paper (vol.40, pp.2352–2359) reports 33 individuals across four cemeteries; its accessible abstract does not isolate the Llandough count. The 2017 American Journal of Physical Anthropology paper (vol.162, pp.423–440) reports 36 Llandough individuals for carbon/nitrogen analysis (Table 1, p.427; methods, p.430) and 32 sulfur results (Table 4, p.433). Published tables should not be requested again.
  • 2023–26 leads: The 2023 BABAO Annual Review, pp.41–42, concerns dental microwear/calculus work, not new radiocarbon or isotope results. The 2025 “Rural Landscape and Settlement in the Dinas Powys Environs” paper concerns the Nant yr Argae pollen sequence and identifies “Unlocking Llandough” as forthcoming; it does not publish new Llandough cemetery results. The linked 2026 Cambridge thesis could not be retrieved in this pass. A social-media reference to 14 new dates was not used as evidence.
  • Burial licence chronology: EV-0131, p.39, records a licence issued 21 March 1994, expiring 21 April 1994, with no renewal recorded by 3 May. EV-0131, p.42, records a 5 May 1994 letter stating that a renewal extended the licence to 20 July 1994. The original licence and its conditions are not in these records.

Parcel overlay: method and limits

The published 0.22 ha excavation plan is not georeferenced to title boundaries and contains no survey-control schedule. The Museum/CAT locality ST 1681 7331 converts to approximately 316810,173310 (EPSG:27700; 51.452638, -3.198617). The HER site point ST 1683 7334 converts to approximately 316830,173340 (51.452910, -3.198337), about 20 m east and 30 m north (approximately 36 m) of the Museum/CAT point. The separate HER event point ST 16739 73315 converts to approximately 316739,173315 (51.452672, -3.199640), about 71 m west of the Museum/CAT point.

A point-in-polygon check against the current reconstructed map shapes places the Museum/CAT point inside the approximate A2 farmhouse-and-yard shape and the HER site point inside the approximate A1 north-wood shape. The HER event point falls outside the four current parcel shapes. These are location references only; none is the 0.22 ha excavation boundary.

Hypothesis: the two site-reference points are consistent with a locality in the reconstructed Parcel A A1/A2 area, with no demonstrated Parcel B overlap. This is not a parcel determination. The WA231076 and WA735527 plans are not georeferenced to the excavation plan; the WA240304 title plan is referenced as item 9 in EV-0065 but is not held; the 1939 tenancy plan EV-0023 covers Parcel B only. Until the 0.22 ha footprint and title plans can be tied to common survey control, the excavated area remains unclassified as Parcel A, Parcel B or both.
