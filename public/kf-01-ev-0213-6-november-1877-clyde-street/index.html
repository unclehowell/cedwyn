Not our farm 6 Nov 1877 · Clyde Street, Roath, Cardiff

Why it is not ours: A Roath building plot leased to a builder, not the farm.

Record or assertion tested
EV-0213 — 6 November 1877 Clyde Street, Roath counterpart
Disposition
Ruled out as the farm lease. Photographs identify a house plot in Clyde Street, Roath; the lessee is John Williams, builder, of System Street, Roath; term 99 years from 29 September 1877, rent £2 12s 6d.
What it does and does not establish
Explains why the exact date and 99-year term are a dangerous false friend. It does not identify the lessee as the Great House Farm tenant, prove a Llandough lease, or prove wrongdoing. A 99-year term from 1877 ends around 1976, not 1987.
Next step / retained route
Keep the photos. Get the archive reference/bundle and Plan No. 15 so the catalogue identity can be authenticated; do not treat it as farm-title evidence.
