Cardiff Corporation's injunction against Llandaff & Dinas Powis Rural District Council over Ely effluent (Local Government Board inquiry, 1904)

Out of scope Jun 1904 · River Ely and Cardiff's western sewer

Why it is not ours: A dispute between two councils about waste draining into the sewer; it does not concern the farm or the 1905 grant.

The record

  • Cardiff Corporation sought an injunction against Llandaff & Dinas Powis RDC over Ely effluent draining into Cardiff's western sewer; the matter came before a Local Government Board inquiry (Evening Express, 15 Jun 1904; Weekly Mail, 18 Jun 1904). No outcome was read.

What it does and does not establish

Background to the western sewer scheme only. Both parties are public bodies.

Next step

None.
