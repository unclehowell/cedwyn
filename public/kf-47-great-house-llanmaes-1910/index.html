“A. M. Thomas, Great House, Llanmaes” (show prize list, 1910)

Not our farm 12 Aug 1910 · Llanmaes, near Llantwit Major

Why it is not ours: A Great House at Llanmaes, a third Glamorgan farm of the name.

The record

  • Glamorgan Gazette, 12 Aug 1910, p.5, show prize list: “A. M. Thomas, Great House, Llanmaes.”

What it does and does not establish

A third Glamorgan Great House with a Thomas family. A Thomas “of Great House” in Vale press is not, by name alone, connected with Llandough.

Next step

Check the parish before citing any “Great House” item.

Sources

  • Glamorgan Gazette 12 Aug 1910 p.5 (Welsh Newspapers Online)
