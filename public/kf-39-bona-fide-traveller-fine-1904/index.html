“John Williams, farmer, Llandough, was fined 20s.” (Penarth police court, Oct 1904)

Out of scope Oct 1904 · Penarth

Why it is not ours: A licensing fine; which John Williams of Llandough is not settled, and it has no bearing on title.

The record

  • The court's view: “John Williams, farmer, Llandough, was fined 20s. … for falsely representing himself to be a bona-fide traveller” (Evening Express, 12 Oct 1904, p.3; read from a search snippet).

What it does and does not establish

Nothing about the farm. Several Williamses farmed in Llandough parish, so the name alone does not identify the holding.

Next step

None.

Sources
