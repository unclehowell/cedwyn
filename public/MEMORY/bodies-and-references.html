The case

  • BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2; (1988) 55 P & CR 337; [1987] 2 EGLR 168.
  • The reopening test used by our reopen-loop: the three limbs set by the Supreme Court in Takhar v Gracefield Developments Ltd [2019] UKSC 13. Our scores are our own working assessment, prospective, and never a finding of any court.

ID schemes

  • EV-#### — evidence items in the research catalogue.
  • MR-#### — missing-record pages: records that should exist (by statute, practice or cross-reference) but are not held or not found.
  • Semver — every draft, e-book, page and protocol carries a version (for example v0.02, v3.9.3).

Title words

  • Lord of the manor: manorial lordship only, not freehold.
  • BP: registered proprietor from 1982/83.
  • Courts' wording: shown as the court's view.
  • A rent-book entry does not identify the Tŷ Mawr parcel unless it names it.
