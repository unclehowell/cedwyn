“John Williams, Great House Farm, Llandough, was also fined £1” for travelling without a fare (Barry magistrates, Mar 1903)

Ours — outside the title question 12 Feb / 6 Mar 1903 · Barry Railway, Grangetown to Cogan

Why it is not ours: Our farm's name in a railway-fare case: no bearing on title or tenure.

The record

  • The court's view: “John Williams, Great House Farm, Llandough, was also fined £1 for travelling between Grangetown and Cogan on the Barry Railway on the 12th ultimo, without having previously paid his fare.” (Barry Dock News, 6 Mar 1903). The Barry Herald of the same date gives “John Williams, jun.”

What it does and does not establish

It places a John Williams at Great House Farm in 1903, on the Penarth side (Grangetown to Cogan). That is all.

Next step

None. Kept as a dated occupancy reference.
