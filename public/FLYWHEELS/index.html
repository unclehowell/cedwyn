FLYWHEELS

Every self-running loop on the project. Each has a runner, state, a log, a fixed metric and a cadence; each schedule spawns at most one worker at a time (it checks the goal prefix first). New wheels copy the schema.

Flywheel Cadence Purpose
Great House Farm research cycle every 6 hours Find evidence and artifacts not already in Cedwyn that bear on reopening BP Properties Ltd v Buckler (1987): concealment or government involvement.
Reopen-the-case autoresearch loop every 4 hours Run small one-hypothesis experiments against a fixed metric (the three Takhar limbs) to rank grounds for reopening the 1987 judgment.
Nightly drafts engine daily 05:30 UK Keep every due information-request draft and chaser written, in thread, ready for review.
Contacts flywheel daily 03:30 UK Find institutions and groups not yet contacted that may hold records, work out what each may hold, and open a hive draft for each.
Hive check-in every 5 minutes Take this agent's turns on hive drafts: one material review or permitted route action per turn, then pass the turn on.
Daily site flywheel daily 12:00 UK Measure every section and apply safe fixes, verified live at 390 px; log everything else as proposed.
First-seen detector every 6 hours Detect any record about the farm that quietly appears online and register it with its first-seen date.
Farm page recapture every 5 days Capture outside pages about the farm's history before and after each cycle so changes are on record.
Business development (plan + execute) plan daily 09:00 UK; execute every 2 hours 10:00–22:00 Grow e-book sales and community for Cedwyn: plan the next 24 hours as weighted HubSpot tasks, then execute them with care.
Skills harvest every 15 minutes Add 2–4 new practitioner-sourced sales and marketing skills per run, each with sources and how it applies to Cedwyn.
Sales fix every 3 hours If no e-book has sold, find out why and apply the top-priority fix.
Growth flywheels (on-page, quote pack, visibility, weekly report) weekly (Mon 07:30 visibility, Tue 19:00 quote pack, Wed 13:30 on-page, Sun 18:00 report) Make Cedwyn findable and shareable: markup audits, quote cards from the records, search and AI visibility checks, and a weekly measured report.
Cedwyn film — daily scene daily 06:00 UK Make one two-minute scene a day from the chronology, in Welsh, English and Spanish, and open the next door on /documentary/.
Morning brief daily 08:00 UK Tell the project lead what finished overnight, mail that needs him, today's calendar and deadlines due soon.
Intelligence flywheel daily 04:15 Europe/London Normalise events and actors, test patterns honestly, map who answered and who stayed silent, and publish missing-record (MR-) pages.
Email ingest every 2 hours, 07:00-23:00 Europe/London Turn inbound project correspondence into site updates: the dartboard lanes, missing-record and catalogue candidates for the research cycles, and dated notes on the matching case tasks.
Programme office weekly, Monday 06:00 Europe/London Keep the objectives register (Wales tour, Argentina land and museum, chronology, e-books, the case) scored, mirrored into projects, and moving.
Cedwyn Academy (this section) every 6 hours (proposed) Keep the academy current: read new memory notes, lessons, runners, skills and worker reports; distil the public-safe parts; regenerate the work board from real gaps; rebuild, scan, publish, log.
Problems flywheel every 30 minutes Take every blocker, question or problem an agent would otherwise raise with the project lead and loop on it until it is solved.
Online gap finder every 6 hours Find public information about the farm, its places, people and the case that is not yet on the site, and queue each find as a task for the right page.
Correspondence flywheel every 2 hours at :30, 07:30–23:30 Europe/London Keep a hive draft task for every research or disclosure email that needs a reply, a chase, escalation, forwarding or first contact; housekeeping between emails.
Repository and project-board sync every 6 hours Keep the project board in step with the repository and the live site: what was done, and what still needs doing.
Data-driven webapp migration one webapp at a time, per the programme plan Move each webapp to pages generated from its own view of the central catalogue, keeping the existing design (standing order 7s).
