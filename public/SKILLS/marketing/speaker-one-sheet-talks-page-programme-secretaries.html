Speaker one-sheet + speaker page for booking local talks (2026)

Topic: the one forwardable PDF (and the matching web page) a programme secretary needs to put a talk in front of their committee. Harvested 2026-11-01 (skills harvest ~02:45Z). New in the index: the podcast file has only a one-line podcast one-sheet; nothing covers talk bookings.

Why it matters (practitioner testimony)

  • Brilliant Author (2025-26 template guide): teachers, librarians, Rotary coordinators and festival programmers "don't want to hunt through your website or piece together information from five different pages"; the common ask is "Do you have info I can share with the committee?", so send ONE clean PDF rather than a list of links. One page for a beginner speaker; two pages once there is a book and a track record.
  • Communicator Academy (Writing at the Red House): organisers often ask for the one-sheet after a yes-in-principle, and look for it on the speaker page first; bio 75-100 words; if there is no client list yet, rename the section (e.g. "Where I have spoken") or leave it off rather than pad it.
  • Meredith Liepelt (TWIBC): the sheet answers the planner's four questions: what do you speak about, how does it help our audience, who else have you spoken for, will our audience take to you. Post it as a downloadable PDF and offer it when you meet anyone with access to an audience.
  • Build Book Buzz (Erin Flynn Jay, via the podcast file): headline tied to a hook, talking points as bullets, a link not an attachment.
  • No source gave a booking-rate lift from a one-sheet; treat it as removing friction, and measure replies.

What goes on it (one page, A4, prints in greyscale)

  1. Talk title + one-line hook (the question the talk answers).
  2. Three talk options, each with length (20 / 30 / 45 min), format (slides, walk, objects to handle) and the one thing the audience leaves with.
  3. Who it suits: local history and civic societies, u3a, Probus, Rotary, libraries, WI, family-history groups; online or in person; Welsh, English (or both).
  4. Speaker bio, 75-100 words, written for hosts, not readers: what you research, the archive sources used, the book.
  5. Where it has been given (or "first season 2026-27" if new; never invent).
  6. One host quote once you have one, with the host's permission and role.
  7. Practicals: needs (projector/HDMI or none; power; table for books), fee policy (e.g. "expenses only" or "free to local societies"), travel area, lead time, whether a QR/handout and a book table are offered and that the host can say no to selling.
  8. Booking contact: one role email, a link to the speaker page, a QR to it.
  9. One image that reads at thumbnail size (a map or record, not a logo wall).

The speaker page (same content, web)

  • One URL (e.g. /talks/) with the PDF link at the top, the three talks, a booking form or role email, past and upcoming dates, and a 60-90 s clip once one exists.
  • Event JSON-LD only for in-person dated talks (see event-structured-data-in-person-talk-page.md).
  • Keep the PDF and the page versioned together (semver in the footer), so a forwarded old copy is identifiable.

How to use it

  • Every talk pitch (society, u3a, Probus, Rotary, library) carries the page link in the body; attach the PDF only when asked.
  • After each talk, add the venue to "Where it has been given" and ask the host for a one-line quote in the thank-you email.
  • Refresh each season (Aug and Jan, when most societies set programmes).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Build one /talks/ page plus a one-page PDF in the shop theme (backgrounds #f7f2e7 or #fbf8f1), three talks drawn only from what is already on the site: the estate through its records; the 1994 dig and the in-between scenes of the documentary; reading old maps and records yourself.
  • Speaker named as the site's own author voice; never the owner's name; banned words checked (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken); no outbound links on the page; no family holdings or addresses.
  • "Where it has been given" starts empty: "First season 2026-27". Fee line and travel area are the owner's to set (PROPOSAL in QUEUE-after-framing.md); default wording "free to local societies; expenses only further afield" only after his GO.
  • The PDF link replaces attachments in every HubSpot talk-pitch draft (society, u3a, Probus, Rotary, library and Insole Court routes already in the index); the hive reviews drafts as usual; nothing is sent without the owner.
  • Pair with in-room-ebook-sales-qr-payment-link-vs-tap-to-pay-uk.md (the "book table offered" practical) and shared-talk-deck-table-handout.md.
