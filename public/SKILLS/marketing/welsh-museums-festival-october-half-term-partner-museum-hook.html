Welsh Museums Festival (October half-term) and museum partner hooks, including the Museum of Cardiff

Skills harvest 2026-10-31. Before this run, "welsh museums festival" and "museum of cardiff"/"cardiff story" had 0 hits in INDEX.md. MuseumWeek (June hashtags) and museum guide apps are covered separately.

What it is (read 31 Oct 2026)

  • Welsh Museums Festival / Gŵyl Amgueddfeydd Cymru, organised by Museums Federation Cymru (charity 1102270) with Welsh Government funding. It runs every October half-term; 2026 was 24 Oct to 1 Nov, the 12th year.
  • 2026 had "more than 60 museums" taking part, "the highest number since the festival began" (Federation president Ken Griffin, gov.wales release, 20 Oct 2026). The Federation awarded grants of over £12,000 to 18 museums so they could take part. Activities are free or low-cost and bilingual, and the 2026 theme was "Sustainable Hwyl" around Calan Gaeaf.
  • Earlier figures: 2025 had "more than 50 museums" with joy as the theme (Museums Journal, 28 Oct 2025, snippet), and one year had "400 events ... in 43 museums" (MA Facebook post, snippet, year not checked).
  • Who can take part: museums. Participation runs through the Federation (members, a festival newsletter and a festival events page that is updated daily). A private author or website cannot list an event of its own; the way in is as a partner of a participating museum that adds the event to its own festival listing.
  • Museum of Cardiff (formerly Cardiff Story, The Old Library, The Hayes CF10 1BH; [address kept in the project workspace]; free entry; Mon–Sat 10–4) took part in the 2026 festival. Its City Showcase gallery shows community-led exhibitions: "Ely Memory Lane", 11 Jul to 31 Oct 2026, was a six-month co-production of the Endeavourers over-60s group (Ely), Cardiff West Community High School pupils and Cardiff University postgraduate researchers, with oral histories, photos, toys and a co-designed video game. It also runs talks and local-history walks (Visit Wales page, snippet). On 31 Oct 2026 its homepage said it was closed because of a leak, with no reopening date.

Why it matters

  • It is a nationally promoted week (a Minister launches it and Welsh Government issues a press release) during which local museums want extra content, bilingual and family-friendly. A partner who brings a ready-made activity costs them nothing.
  • The City Showcase model shows the Museum of Cardiff gives its gallery to community groups working with researchers. That is the shape of a Great House Farm / Llandough display: older residents' memories, a school and a researcher. A private seller would not fit; a community-led project would.

How to do it

  1. Watch `museumsfederation.cymru from 1 Jun 2027 for the 2027 dates (October half-term) and the participating-museum list. Subscribe the Cedwyn inbox to the festival newsletter (no outward message needed beyond the signup form; still check with the owner first, because it gives out an address).
  2. Shortlist participating museums within reach of Llandough and Leckwith: Museum of Cardiff (once reopened), Penarth / Vale museums on the list, St Fagans. Read each one's 2026 festival events to see what kind of activity it hosts.
  3. Offer a free, ready-to-run bilingual activity: a printable "Old farms of Llandough and Leckwith" trail or colouring sheet (Calan Gaeaf or harvest theme), sourced from public records with no case advocacy. The museum runs it under its own listing and credits the source line plus the QR code. Ask nothing, sell nothing on the sheet itself; the QR goes to a free page on Cedwyn.
  4. For a City Showcase-type display, the pitch has to be community-led: a residents' memory group plus a school or a university researcher. Tab can prepare the pack, but the group has to exist first.
  5. Measure with utm_source=wmf2027&utm_medium=print&utm_campaign=<museum> and count sessions in the festival week against the week before.
