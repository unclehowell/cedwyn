# Instagram Collab posts with heritage partners (2026)

Topic: co-authored Instagram posts (one post, up to 5 co-author accounts, shown to every co-author's followers) as the cheapest way for a tiny account to reach an established heritage audience.

## What the evidence shows
- **Emplifi, H1 2026 analysis of Instagram brand profiles** (blog by J. Skalicka, 15 Sep 2026, read in full):
  - More than 73% of brands in the dataset published collab posts in H1 2026.
  - Two kinds: *owned* (we publish and invite the partner) and *accepted* (the partner publishes and invites us). Both beat the same brand's standard posts; **accepted collabs give the larger uplift**.
  - **XS profiles (<10,000 followers): accepted collab posts earned 5x the interactions of their standard posts**, the largest relative uplift of any size band.
  - Shares and reposts are higher in every vertical; accepted collabs reached more than 5x the amplification of non-collab posts in ecommerce/retail.
  - **Reels gain most** from collaboration; **owned carousel collabs gain least**.
  - Small brands do best with **a few carefully chosen partners**; large brands with many (to cut audience overlap).
  - Brands with more user posts tagging them also see stronger collab results.
  - Caveat in the source itself: collab users tend to be bigger, more active brands, so this is association, not proof of cause.
- **Schöttl (2026), Journal of Digital & Social Media Marketing 13(4):363-376**: 100 collab posts across athletes, creators, companies and community accounts, measuring reach for each co-author. Only the abstract was read; its findings are **not checked**.
- **Instagram Help Centre** (help.instagram.com): up to 5 collaborators; the creator is the primary author. The page returned an error from this box, so this is from the search snippet and **not checked** at source (SocialBee's help page gives the same limit).

## How to do it (steps)
1. **Pick 3-5 partners whose audience overlaps but isn't yours**: local history societies, archives, museums, heritage walks, Welsh-language heritage accounts. Organisations only.
2. **Offer them the better deal: they publish, we accept.** Accepted collabs perform best, and the partner keeps control. Bring them a finished Reel (vertical, own footage/scans, 15-45 s, source line on screen) that fits *their* feed and voice.
3. **Make it a Reel**, not a carousel, when the goal is reach. Keep carousels for detailed explainers.
4. **One clear record per post** and a source line. The caption names the record and the archive it came from, so the partner's audience gets value even if they never click.
5. **Invite through Tag people → Invite collaborator** (owned) or accept the partner's invite. Both accounts must be public; the invite can be accepted after posting.
6. **CTA = follow / save / share**, not a hard sell. Link in bio (UTM `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=collab&utm_campaign=<partner>`).
7. **Measure for each collab after 7 days**: reach split followers / non-followers, shares, saves, profile visits, follows. Compare with our median Reel. Two collabs with no follower lift for us means we change partner, not format.
8. **Reciprocate**: share their events/posts to Stories, credit them by name, and offer them the next one.

## Pitfalls
- Asking a big partner to co-sign a post that is really our ad. They decline, or their audience ignores it.
- Too many co-authors at a small size. Emplifi found small brands do best with few partners.
- Contested framing. Partners won't co-sign anything that reads as a dispute; keep it to dated records.
- Third-party footage (Facebook/Instagram originality rules: see the facebook-reels-originality skill).

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **PROPOSAL (needs owner GO, because it contacts third parties and adds a partner channel):** a "Records in 30 seconds" collab series. Each Reel is offered to one heritage organisation to publish, with Cedwyn as collaborator.
  - Reel 1: the **1824 David Stewart survey**, the first record mention found of Tŷ Mawr, plan on screen with its archive reference.
  - Reel 2: field names from the survey, with a map overlay.
  - Reel 3: "The family acquired Tŷ Mawr in 1667 (1974 newspapers; ITV News 1988)", source cards on screen.
- Partner shortlist to build (not yet contacted): registered organisations only (local history societies, record offices, heritage-walk groups, Welsh-language heritage accounts). No private individuals, no council accounts while council threads are open, nobody with an open correspondence thread.
- Outreach goes as a HubSpot draft only; nothing is sent without the owner's GO.
- Fits the existing 6 posts/day cap. A collab counts as one of our posts only when we publish it.
- Success bar after 3 collabs: ≥1 collab beats our median Reel reach by 3x **and** brings ≥10 follows. Otherwise stop.
- Public text: no owner name, no banned words, no Bute-era dates.
