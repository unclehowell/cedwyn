Episodic Reels after "Link a Reel" was retired (28 Oct 2026): Series test, Facebook Reels playlists, and the series home on our own site

Harvested 30 Oct 2026 ~11:46Z. Index had 0 hits for "linked reel", "link a reel", "reels playlist" and "instagram series" before this run. Neighbours already covered: youtube-serial-show-shorts-series-door-a-day-documentary.md (YouTube side) and trial reels.

What's true now

  • Meta "Series" (TechCrunch, 2 Jun 2026, read in full; Meta told TechCrunch):
  • Select creators can group new and old Reels into episodes, on Instagram and Facebook.
  • Each series gets its own hub and a tab on the profile. Viewers who find an episode in the feed can tap into the full series, watch in order, pick up where they left off, and save it.
  • The test is for creators who already post serialized content. Meta is "considering ways to monetize". TikTok has had Series since 2023.
  • Status 14 Oct 2026 (CreatorFlow, a practitioner; it cites Meta and in-app screenshots, read):
  • Still a limited test, with no application, waitlist, follower minimum or country list.
  • Check the profile "+" menu and the Professional dashboard.
  • The episode limit isn't confirmed (tutorials say 25).
  • Facebook labels a series with an episode count. Facebook Reels playlists (up to 500 Reels) are live now.
  • "Link a Reel" retired 28 Oct 2026:
  • Several creator posts quote Instagram's in-app notice: the button is gone and existing links disappear, while Reels, views and insights stay.
  • Instagram's own help page is a Meta help page, which is on our blocked list, so this is not checked against a primary page. The posts agree on the date.
  • CreatorFlow's 14 Oct text still calls Linked Reels live, which is now out of date.
  • No numbers: nobody has published lift figures for Series or playlists. Meta states only that serialized content is "taking off". Treat this as structure, not a promised gain.

How to do it

  1. Stop relying on Link a Reel. Any old "watch next" chains are gone, so don't plan around them.
  2. Number every episode the same way in the cover text and the first caption line, e.g. "Lost Farms · Ep 7 of 12 · 1916". Viewers and any future Series hub can then follow the order.
  3. Facebook now: put every episode into one Reels playlist on the Page, in order. Re-check the playlist after each new episode.
  4. Instagram Series: check the "+" menu and the Professional dashboard monthly. When it appears, build the series from the existing episodes in order. Test 3–5 episodes first, then add the rest.
  5. One series home outside Meta: each caption names one page on our own site where the whole series lives. That page doesn't depend on Meta features that come and go.
  6. Measure: per-episode plays and follows from Insights, plus visits to the series page from l.instagram.com and facebook.com referrers. Log them weekly. Without these, "series" is just a label.
