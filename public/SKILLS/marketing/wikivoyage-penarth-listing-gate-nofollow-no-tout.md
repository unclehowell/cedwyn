# Wikivoyage (Penarth / Vale of Glamorgan): a gate, NO for promotion
Harvested 2026-10-29 21:14Z by the skills harvest. Free wiki travel guide; checked as a possible free listing for the /wales/ walk and the e-books.

## Facts (Wikivoyage policy pages + Wikimedia pageviews API, read 29 Oct 2026)
- **"Wikivoyage, not unlike cathedrals, is not for advertising"** (Welcome, business owners). Owners may add *traveller-useful* information to the right city section (See/Do/Buy/Eat/Drink/Sleep), but "promotional listings will be removed". Only one listing per business is allowed.
- **"Marketers and chain employees may not add listings"**. They may only suggest one on the article's talk page. Most **tour listings** are "inappropriate", per the separate tour guideline.
- **External links:** strict. No booking, review or other-guide links. **Every link is rel="nofollow"**, and the Don't tout page states outright that "there is no SEO benefit to listing a website here".
- **Traffic (Wikimedia pageviews API, users, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026):**
  - en.wikivoyage Penarth: 1,494 views (≈4/day)
  - Vale of Glamorgan: 1,973
  - Cardiff: 21,336
  - For comparison, the en.wikipedia Penarth article got 57,641 and Llandough 209.
- The Penarth article has See items for Cosmeston Country Park and Cosmeston Medieval Village. It has **no Lavernock, Marconi or Llandough** entries.

## Verdict
- **No listing for the e-books or the website.** It's commercial, nofollow, and about 4 readers a day, so any upside is too small to justify a conflict-of-interest edit that would likely be reverted.
- **The /wales/ walk** is a free self-guided route, but it is our own product, so the touting rules apply to us. An edit would be a public action in the business's name. **Not proposed**: the value is too low to spend a the project lead GO on.
- Optional, only if a volunteer (not us) is interested: the Lavernock Point Marconi 13 May 1897 site is a genuine gap on the Penarth page. We do not edit it ourselves.
