What the library is

  • Biblioteca Popular Agustín Álvarez, Trelew (Chubut). CONABIP registration no. 1819 (snippet, CONABIP directory title). Address: San Martín 38, first floor, Trelew. Phone 2804431836 (snippet, membership post). Celebrated its 92nd anniversary in October 2026 (snippet, IG @bibliotecapaatw). The person in charge in the 2026 TV clip is Estela Zaldivia, "encargada de la Biblioteca" (snippet, TVeo Trelew video title).
  • Channels: Facebook page "Biblioteca Popular Agustin Alvarez | Trelew" (facebook.com) and Instagram @bibliotecapaatw. No own website found; it announces everything on these two and in El Chubut.

How outside authors actually get in (evidence)

  1. Book presentations are a regular, free, weekday-afternoon format. El Chubut, 2 Sep 2026 (page read): the library "anunció la presentación de dos obras literarias", Friday 11 Sep 2026, 17:00, at San Martín 38, first floor, two Patagonian authors (Gustavo Chaina, relatos; Anwar Chaina, verse on Patagonian legends, a first book). The library itself wrote the announcement, the paper ran it.
  2. Welsh-colony subjects are welcome there. The library hosted the presentation of the third edition of "Galesas" by Leonardo Javier De Bella, "una obra que recupera historias fundamentales" (snippet, IG reel DZfYlHEBeg1 and FM Tropical 106.3 FB post). Other presentations seen: Julieta Magdalena Prada; "De amores y terruño"; a book "04 Veces no, Chubut" on a Thursday 27 Nov at 17:30 (snippets). So: one or two presentations a month, local or regional authors, 17:00–17:30 slots.
  3. Annual literary contest (not a talk route but a visibility route): VI Certamen Literario de Relatos Cortos y Poesía 2026, entries until 30 Sep 2026, short story up to 2 pages, poetry 20–50 lines, original and unpublished, first prize per category ARS 100,000 cash and winners edited (snippets, IG Db-rpG-EVFV, FB post). 2025 edition closed 5 Sep 2025. Expect the VII edition call around Aug 2027.
  4. The library needs money for its building: it ran a book sale at ARS 1,000 a book to fund building works (snippet, IG reel DcByQalx5Bc). A donated print copy and a talk that brings people in are welcome; never offer or imply payment either way.

The playbook

  1. Pitch 8–12 weeks ahead, by Facebook page message or Instagram DM written as a short email-style note in Spanish (an agent drafts; a human sends). There is no form and no published call.
  2. Offer exactly: a 40-minute presentation in Spanish (remote by video, or in person if someone is travelling), title + one-line synopsis, why it belongs in Trelew (the Welsh link), a ready-made flyer (1080x1350, their logo space blank) and a press paragraph they can forward to El Chubut and Jornada. The Chaina announcement shows the paper prints the library's own text.
  3. Offer two dates on a weekday at 17:00 local (20:00–21:00 UK). Avoid January–February (summer) and the Eisteddfod del Chubut week (late Oct; date per the Eisteddfod file in this index, not re-checked here).
  4. Leave one print copy for their shelves (Spanish edition if there is one) plus a free e-book code for members.
  5. After: ask for photos/video and the post URL; log in HubSpot; one thank-you; nothing more until a new book.
  6. Kill rule: no reply in 30 days -> one follow-up; no reply in 60 days -> park until the next book.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Venue fit: Trelew grew from the Welsh colonists' railway (the library's own anniversary post ties the town's history to "la gesta de los colonos galeses", snippet). A Cedwyn presentation on a South Wales manorial house, its records and the Welsh who went to Chubut is in the same lane as "Galesas".
  • Make one HubSpot draft task "[BIZDEV] Biblioteca Popular Agustín Álvarez (Trelew): propuesta de presentación" with the Spanish pitch above, two dates in Apr–May 2027 (before winter, after summer), a flyer from the movie/e-book art, a UTM link ?utm_source=bp-agustin-alvarez&utm_medium=talk&utm_campaign=trelew-2027 to /ebooks/ with the es landing.
  • Combine with the Trevelin/Esquel and Gaiman routes already in the index into a single "Chubut library tour (remote)" week so one deck serves all.
  • Public text: never name the family member behind the project; banned words list applies; stick to the records; no holdings or addresses.
  • Contest: only if someone writes an original unpublished piece in Spanish; it is not a promo channel. Low priority.
  • Status: drafts only. Sending or travel needs the project lead's GO. Zero spend.
