Review copies to Welsh and local-history journals (Morgannwg, Welsh History Review, The Local Historian, FHS journals)

Checked 29 Oct 2026. Topic: PR for a specialist heritage title, aimed at the readers who buy county history. Status for Cedwyn: PROPOSAL. Every cover note is outward, so it's drafted in HubSpot only and sent only on the project lead's GO (press needs his GO).

Practitioner evidence (numbers)

  • Bob Trubshaw, How to Write and Publish Local and Family History Successfully (Heart of Albion, 2005; read in full). He has self-published 16 books and edited 60+ titles and nearly 50 magazine issues.
  • "A fairly specialist book about Leicestershire will probably sell about 250 copies in the first year. A further 50 copies or so will go out as 'freebies' for reviews and gifts."
  • His costing: "At least 30 copies are likely to be sent out free for reviews".
  • In his worked example the 50 review and complimentary copies sat alongside 100 mail-order sales at full price and 150 shop sales at 35% discount. His point is that reviews in specialist and society periodicals drive mail order.
  • Timing: allow about 4 weeks between delivery and launch "to allow review copies to be seen and reviewed ... in time for publicity to coincide with the launch".
  • "Never send a book to a newspaper ... without addressing it to a specific person by name and including a personalised letter."
  • Make sure the reviewed edition shows the buying details, because "few reviewers will add the crucial information".
  • Counterpoint from the same book: one title sold 250+ copies with no review copies at all, all from demand in shops. Reviews help but aren't the only route.
  • Library acquisition policy (The Book Place guidelines for self-published authors, read): libraries are "more likely to consider adding a self-published book ... if it has been reviewed in a major review journal". Paid reviews are not considered. So a journal review also opens library stock.

How to do it

  1. One list in HubSpot: outlet, named reviews editor, format accepted (print, PDF or both), deadline or volume date, and status.
  2. Ask before sending files. A two-line cover note offers a PDF review copy or, where hard copy is required (WHR), a print copy once one exists. Academic reviewers rarely take unsolicited e-books.
  3. Lead with what a reviewer needs: subject, period covered, sources used (archive catalogue refs), page count, price and where to buy, plus a short press note on the contents (Trubshaw).
  4. Make the edition self-selling: the buy URL inside the PDF on the title and colophon pages, so a review that skips it still converts.
  5. Budget for long lead times. An annual journal may print a review 6-12 months later. Treat it as a slow, durable channel: a library citation and a credibility line for the sales page.
  6. Track: tag the buy link in each review copy (?utm_source=review&utm_campaign=<outlet>) and log responses.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Best first targets: the Morgannwg reviews contact (county fit: Llandough and Leckwith are in historic Glamorgan) and the Glamorgan FHS journal. Then the WHR via ISWE only once a print edition exists, since WHR asks for hard copies (see ffolio-cymru-books-council-wales-listing.md for the ISBN and print route). After that, The Local Historian.
  • Pick the most source-led e-book (record lines with catalogue references) as the review title. Avoid any title touching the litigation, because scholarly reviewers will judge tone.
  • Drafts: the CORRESPONDENCE or BIZDEV workers write one HubSpot draft task per outlet as [BIZDEV] Review copy offer: <outlet>, with a named editor and verified address. Never send, and nothing goes out without the project lead's GO.
  • Public text rules apply to any press note: never name the project lead; banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken); no insignia; no family holdings; no outbound links.
  • Copies needed: 4 PDFs now, plus 1-2 print copies later. The cost is £0 for PDFs, which fits zero spend. Any print copy is a spend PROPOSAL.
  • Kill rule: if no outlet replies within 8 weeks of the offers going out, stop chasing and keep the list for the next title. One reply from a reviews editor is enough to keep going.
