The playbook

  1. Only real, dated, public events qualify (a guided walk with a date, a talk at a Penarth/Llandough venue, a launch reading). An e-book on its own is not an event.
  2. Email tourism@ with: title, date/time, venue with postcode, price (free or ticket link), 60-word description, booking URL, one landscape image (1920x1080, no text) and image credit, organiser contact. Send 4–6 weeks ahead (their social calendar is planned).
  3. Same week: post your own photo/video on Instagram tagged #VisitTheVale and @visitthevale for the UGC galleries (permission is requested through their software; say yes).
  4. For anything with crowds, a road or a stall: email the Events Officer first (ESAG); for Cedwyn's small walks this is a courtesy note.
  5. Log in HubSpot; track utm_source=visitthevale&utm_medium=listing.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • The Llandough heritage walk (when a date exists and the accreditation gate in visit-wales-tpd-listing-walk-accreditation-gate.md is respected: list it as a talk/heritage walk led by a qualified guide or as part of the Valeways festival) and any Penarth talk go to tourism@ as a HubSpot email draft for the project lead to send.
  • UGC is the cheapest win: Cedwyn's Instagram (@cedwynestate) posts of Llandough/Leckwith views can carry #VisitTheVale (one per fortnight, no pricing in the caption, shop link in bio only).
  • Inspiration pitch idea: "Llandough's lost manorial houses" from published records only — wording neutral, no assertions, no named individuals' disputes, no holdings, never name the family member behind the project; banned words apply. Inspiration pages are council editorial; accept their edits.
  • Craft stall at council events (e.g. Christmas fayres): only with print copies on hand; proposal for the project lead's GO; check any pitch fee first (zero spend).
  • Skip: Vale Events Network fee, paid targeted promotion.
