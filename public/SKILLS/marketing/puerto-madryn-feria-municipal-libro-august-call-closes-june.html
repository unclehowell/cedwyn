Puerto Madryn Feria Municipal del Libro: the June call for the August fair

Checked 31 Oct 2026. Puerto Madryn is Cedwyn II's town and has the Asociación Cultural Galesa.

The facts

  • 5th edition: 13–16 Aug 2026, SUM de la Escuela 710. Run by the Municipalidad through its Subsecretaría de Cultura (subsecretary Lic. Herman Müller). Local press reported 90 free activities, and Müller called the result "altamente positivo". Visitor and sales numbers: not checked; none were found in text.
  • Invited headliners 2026: Selva Almada, Martín Kohan, Reynaldo Sietecase, César González and the illustrator Chanti.
  • Open call: closed 30 Jun 2026, about 7 weeks before the fair. It covered stands, publishers, bookshops, workshops, short works ("obras breves") and school activities. Forms were posted on the Municipalidad's and Subsecretaría de Cultura's social accounts. In-person help at Casa de la Cultura, Mon–Fri 8–14. Email: [address kept in the project workspace], which answers questions and sends the sign-up link on request.
  • Who applied: the organiser said the first 20–30 sign-ups came from across Chubut and Patagonia, including Neuquén, Río Negro and Comodoro Rivadavia, and from out-of-town bookshops. So regional outsiders do get in. Nothing in the call text restricted applicants to Madryn residents, though the full rules weren't published in text: not checked.

The playbook for an e-book publisher

  • Use the activity slots, not a stand. A stand needs physical stock and staff. A talk or workshop needs only a speaker. Use the Gaiman pattern: a 1-page abstract and a 10-minute slot.
  • Remote is not checked. Ask in the email whether a recorded or video-link talk is accepted. If not, the speaker has to be a local partner, such as the Asociación Cultural Galesa Puerto Madryn or a Welsh teacher.
  • Leave-behind: a printed QR card to /ebooks/ in Spanish. Per the Gaiman rules, authors don't sell on the floor, so sales happen online after the talk.
