# Cymraeg 2050 action plan 2026-27: no Patagonia line, no author or heritage fund

Harvested 2026-10-31. Read in full: the Welsh Government action plan page (HTML, 45 KB of text).

## What the plan states
- "Cymraeg 2050: Welsh language strategy action plan 2026 to 2027". First published 24 March 2026. A statutory annual plan under section 78 of the Government of Wales Act 2006 (read).
- The plan presents itself as a continuation of the 2025-26 plan, because the Senedd term was ending. "It will be for the next Government to set new priorities" in an updated Cymraeg 2050 strategy, which will respond to the Welsh Language and Education (Wales) Act (read).
- The "Wales and the wider world" section names the NPLD, the British-Irish Council, the UNESCO Decade of Indigenous Languages, MoUs with Cornwall, Brittany, Catalonia, the Basque Country and Ireland, and the Urdd Message of Peace and Goodwill 2026 (read).
- Word counts on the page (read): "Patagonia" 0, "Argentin" 0, "Wladfa" 0, "Chubut" 0, "British Council" 0, "Books Council" 0. "book" appears once, in boilerplate. The plan's money is for education, early years, Welsh-medium capital (£15m a year, subject to budget), the Coleg Cymraeg Cenedlaethol, and the National Centre for Learning Welsh.
- So the plan has no Patagonia activity and no fund an outside author or heritage project could apply to.

## Where Welsh Government money does reach Patagonia
- The Welsh Language Project (British Council Wales, since 1997) is where Welsh Government money reaches Patagonia. Its page states it funds a permanent Welsh Teaching Co-ordinator based in Patagonia and supports about 20 staff in schools and adult learning (search snippet of wales.britishcouncil.org; the page itself was read in an earlier run, see spanish-patagonia-audience.md).
- This is a teaching programme. It has no publishing, book-promotion or author-visit strand. Whether it has any budget for materials was not found.
