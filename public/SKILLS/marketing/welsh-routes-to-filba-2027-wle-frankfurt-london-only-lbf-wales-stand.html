Welsh public routes to FILBA 2027 without co-funding: what exists, what doesn't

Harvested 31 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). Read-only; nothing sent. Settles lead "Welsh Government / Wales Arts International / Wales Literature Exchange routes into FILBA 2027". Verdict

No Welsh public body takes titles to FILBA. There is no Wales stand at Buenos Aires and no Welsh call for it. The zero-cost Welsh route is indirect: get the title in front of Wales Literature Exchange (WLE) and the Books Council of Wales (BCW) at the London Book Fair, 16-18 March 2027, five weeks before FILBA (22 Apr-10 May 2027), and go to FILBA ourselves on the free professional registration already documented.

What the primary pages show (read 31 Oct 2026)

  • WLE "Book Fairs" page: "We attend the Frankfurt book fair and the London Book Fair. To make an appointment with the Exchange at forthcoming book fairs, please contact us at [address kept in the project workspace]" Past entries: LBF as "part of the Wales national stand"; Frankfurt 2019/2021/2026 (a Czech guest-of-honour event 10 Oct 2026). No Buenos Aires.
  • WLE has done Argentina before, outside fairs: "Translating Wales, Reading the World: Chile and Argentina" (5 Dec 2014) - translation journeys to Argentina and Chile organised by WLE, Wales Arts International and the British Council, published as a supplement with Taliesin's "Gwladfa" issue (Welsh translations of Jorge Aulicino, Jorge Fondebrider and others; Mererid Hopwood, Karen Owen; Ned Thomas, Owen Martell). So WLE treats Patagonia as a literary-exchange theme, not a sales fair. No 2026-27 Argentina programme found -> not checked.
  • BCW at Frankfurt 2026: "Thanks to funding from Welsh Government through Creative Wales, the Books Council of Wales has organised the Wales presence at Frankfurt... Over 15 Wales-based publishers and organisations are attending to... discuss collaborations, rights and licensing" (books.wales, Oct 2026). Creative Wales earlier ran Frankfurt trade missions (13 companies). The fee to join, if any, is not published -> not checked (ask BCW).
  • LBF 2027: 16-18 March 2027, ExCeL London (londonbookfair.co.uk); the Books Council of Wales is listed as an LBF exhibitor.
  • Comparison (England, not Wales): ACE + Inpress funded 12 small publishers at LBF with bookable meeting tables and a £500 bursary - shows shared-stand tables exist; not available to us unless Wales offers the same (not checked).

Why this is the right way round

  • FILBA's international rights business is small compared with Frankfurt/London; Welsh agencies concentrate their money where rights deals happen. A Spanish-language Welsh-heritage title gets to Argentine publishers fastest via (a) WLE's network, which covers Spanish-language translators and the 2014 Argentina contacts, and (b) our own free FILBA professional credential.
  • Everything here is free: an email for an appointment, a one-page rights sheet, and a free LBF visitor/trade pass (pass price for 2027 not checked; buy nothing without the project lead's GO).

How to do it

  1. Now - Jan 2027: one-page rights sheet (EN + ES; title, extent, Welsh-Patagonia angle, Spanish edition status, sample link to our own site). Text only, no assertions about named people.
  2. Jan 2027: email [address kept in the project workspace] asking (i) for a 20-minute appointment at LBF 16-18 Mar 2027, (ii) whether WLE has current Argentine/Chilean contacts from the 2014 journeys, (iii) whether the title can sit in the Wales stand's rights materials. Owner sends; we draft only.
  3. Same month: ask BCW ([number kept in the project workspace] / post@ address on books.wales) whether a Wales-based micro-publisher can join the Wales stand at LBF 2027 at no cost, and on what terms.
  4. March 2027: LBF meetings -> collect Argentine/Spanish-language contacts.
  5. April-May 2027: follow up at FILBA via the free professional registration (Business Center directory), naming the WLE introduction.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Proposal: two HubSpot draft tasks for the project lead to approve and send himself: "[BIZDEV] WLE - LBF 2027 appointment + Argentina contacts" and "[BIZDEV] Books Council of Wales - Wales stand LBF 2027 terms". Due 15 Jan 2027. Drafts only; never sent by us.
  • Rights sheet lives as a private draft in the project workspace (not on the public site until the project lead approves).
  • Never-pay rule: if BCW or LBF quote a fee, park it and log it as a proposal; do not pay.
  • Public text rules apply (never name the project lead; banned words; no family holdings).
  • No new Cloudflare Worker/KV/D1 load: email drafts + a document only.
