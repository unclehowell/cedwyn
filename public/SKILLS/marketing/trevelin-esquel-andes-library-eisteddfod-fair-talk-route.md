# Trevelin and Esquel (Andes Welsh settlements): library talks, the Trevelin Eisteddfod and the Esquel book fair

Harvested 2026-10-31 (skills harvest). Read: bibliodetrevelin.com.ar, elvalleonline.com.ar (Eisteddfod Trevelin entries). Everything marked (snippet) comes from search previews of Facebook, Instagram and LU17 pages that block scripted reads. Nothing here has been checked with the organisers.

## What is there (facts with sources)
- **La Biblio de Trevelin = Biblioteca Popular Perito Moreno**, CONABIP no. 3773, founded 1951, Libertad 267, Trevelin. Open Mon–Fri 10–13 and 17–20. It runs talks and screenings (for example a philosophy-workshop talk-debate, May 2022), a reading-volunteer scheme ("Libro Vivo"), and sends members to FILBA's popular-library days. It has a WhatsApp, Facebook (bibliodetrevelin) and Instagram contact. Its board since March 2024 has nine members (read on its site). CONABIP's own page states it sends out digital "Bibliocápsulas", so it already handles digital material (snippet).
- **The Trevelin Eisteddfod** (separate from the Eisteddfod del Chubut in Trelew/Gaiman and from the Eisteddfod de la Juventud in Gaiman). The 2026 edition was 1–2 May 2026 at the SUM of Escuela no. 57 (LU17, snippet). 2025 was 2–3 May (programme .docx, snippet). Competitions include music, choirs, poetry and literature in Welsh and Spanish, recitation, dance, visual art, photography and "escritura y traducción". Main prizes: the Bardic Chair (Welsh poetry), the Poet's Crown (Spanish poetry) and a silver medal. Entry rules, deadlines and the form come out on its social accounts, or by email from [address kept in the project workspace] (elvalleonline, read). The festival's Instagram also shows a "19 y 20 de noviembre" date of unclear year (snippet). Check which date applies before planning.
- **Asociación Galesa de Trevelin y Esquel and the Museo Regional Trevelin (old mill)**: the museum publicly thanked the association for a book presentation it hosted (Instagram, snippet). The 2026 Eisteddfod prizes were also unveiled there (snippet). So in the Andes, a Welsh-heritage book presentation goes through the association plus the museum.
- **Esquel Feria Municipal del Libro, 3rd edition, 7–9 May 2026**, Centro Cultural Esquel Melipal. It has publishers, writers, workshops, talks and music, with nightly cultural closes at 21:00 (Cultura Esquel Facebook + eqsnotas, snippet). There was also a 23 April book release outside the library at San Martín 636 (snippet). No open call for authors has been found yet.
- **Precedent in the Valley**: "Galesas" by Leonardo Javier De Bella was presented at Biblioteca Popular Agustín Álvarez (Trelew) on Fri 12 June at 17:00 in its 3rd edition (FM Tropical 106.3 post, snippet). Welsh-women heritage titles do get library launch slots in Chubut.

## How an outside author gets a slot (playbook)
1. **Timing.** The Andes literary calendar sits around late April to early May: Día del Libro on 23 April, then the Trevelin Eisteddfod and the Esquel fair in the same fortnight. Pitch by early February 2027 for May 2027.
2. **One route per venue, never cold pitches to all of them at once.**
   - Library talk: write to La Biblio de Trevelin through its contact page or email. Offer a free remote talk (Zoom, 40 min, in Spanish with Welsh place names) plus one printed copy for the shelf if the project lead approves print. Libraries schedule against their own calendar, so offer two date windows.
   - Museum and association presentation: ask the Asociación Galesa de Trevelin y Esquel whether it would host or co-host a presentation at the Museo Regional. This matches the format the museum already thanks people for.
   - Esquel fair: ask Cultura Esquel (Municipalidad) in February how authors are invited for the 4th edition (May 2027). Say plainly that it would be a remote talk.
3. **The Trevelin Eisteddfod is not a promo slot.** It is a pseudonymous competition, the same model as Trelew's. If a "traducción" category allows outside entrants (not checked), a translated extract would earn credibility, not sales. Entering needs the project lead's GO.
4. **Material.** Reuse the one Spanish abstract (150 words) and bio already used for Gaiman/Madryn/Trelew/Comodoro. Add one Andes hook, for example the Welsh settlers' 1885 Rifleros expedition to Cwm Hyfryd/Valle 16 de Octubre, linked to our Welsh-estate story only where the sources support it. Add a QR code to a Spanish landing page with `utm_source=trevelin-biblio` / `esquel-feria` / `museo-trevelin`.
