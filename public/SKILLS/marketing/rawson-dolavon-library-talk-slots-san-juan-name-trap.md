# Rawson and Dolavon libraries: who to ask for a talk slot (and two name traps)

Harvested 2026-10-31. Checked against CONABIP's register and local press. (read) = page read, (snippet) = search preview only.

## Name traps (from reading the pages)
- "Biblioteca Popular Rawson" (CONABIP reg. 707) is in Santa Lucía, San Juan province, NOT Chubut. Its CONABIP wall (book launches such as "Bajo la piel") is a San Juan library (read).
- municipioderawson.gob.ar is the Municipality of Rawson, San Juan. Its "Feria Provincial de la Cultura Popular y el Libro" (17th edition, 2-3 Oct 2026, Plaza Bicentenario) is a San Juan fair (read; San Juan 264 phone code).
- So check the province before using any "Rawson library" or "Rawson feria" result.

## Rawson, Chubut (provincial capital)
- Biblioteca Popular Asencio Abeijón, CONABIP reg. 3235, Avda. Guillermo Rawson 1200, Rawson, Chubut (read).
  - (snippet) The Instagram account @biblioabeijon announced a "Ciclo de charlas y conferencias" from 29 Mar 2025, every 15 days at 10:00 in the library. The 2026 status was not checked.
- Biblioteca Pública Provincial at the Centro Cultural Provincial (Rawson).
  - (snippet) It runs a "Ciclo de Charlas y Literatura" through the year: the 1st meeting in Women's Month (March), the 2nd around the Día de la Memoria/Libro, and book presentations such as "Mariano Epulef".
  - (snippet) A "literatura patagónica" programme of literary meetings and book presentations runs 3-6 Nov. The year and the host library were not confirmed.
- Feria Municipal del Libro de Rawson, 4th edition, 3-5 Sep 2026, Centro Cultural José Hernández. 23 provincial writers (from Rawson, Playa Unión, Trelew, Gaiman, Lago Puelo, Esquel, Puerto Madryn) plus three national authors (Saracino, Moglia, Moscoloni). Run by the Subsecretaría de Cultura (Leopoldo Zanelli), Dirección General de Acción Educativa (Gabriela Locaso), and an organising committee (Mirta Prado) (El Chubut 28 Aug 2026, read).
  - The author line-up is invited and provincial. No open call or form was found for 2026. The press note names organisers, not an application route.

## Dolavon, Chubut
- Biblioteca Popular Domingo Faustino Sarmiento, CONABIP reg. 2414.
  - The CONABIP register gives Av. Gral. Roca 188 (read). The library's own 21 Aug 2026 note gives Sarmiento 36 (El Valle Online, read). Use the newer address and confirm.
  - It completed its CONABIP "Alta de Beneficiario" on 11 Aug 2026, which re-opens subsidies for book purchases, training and programmes (read).
  - (snippet) It hosts author presentations; one author donated a signed copy at her launch. A small, newly re-registered library, so a donated talk plus a print copy is the offer that fits.
- Dolavon also has its own Eisteddfod (es.wikipedia, read). Avoid its week for asks.

## How an outside author gets a slot (inferred from the pages; no published procedure found)
1. Ask the institution, not a person. A library's comisión directiva decides; the municipal fair goes through the Subsecretaría de Cultura.
2. Timing:
   - Rawson municipal fair: line-up announced 28 Aug for 3-5 Sep, so ask by June-July.
   - Provincial library cycle: from March.
   - Abeijón cycle: fortnightly Saturdays.
3. Offer: a free 30-40 min talk in Spanish (remote by video is the realistic option from the UK). Give a print copy to the collection (see conabip-libro-percent... and biblioteca-nacional... skills: e-books do not enter catalogues by donation). No selling at the talk unless the host proposes it.
4. One follow-up only. Spanish first, with one Welsh line (welsh-patagonian-societies-chubut-outreach.md).
