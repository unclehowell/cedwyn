# Instagram Live and Notes for a small account: what's gated, what's usable (2026)

Harvested 2026-10-29 20:58Z (skills harvest). Topic: Instagram growth for @cedwynestate.

## The facts (with sources)
- **Live is closed to us.** Since about 1 Aug 2025, only *public* accounts with **1,000+ followers** can start an Instagram Live. Instagram confirmed it to TechCrunch, and its Help Centre has the same line. For comparison: TikTok also needs 1,000 and YouTube needs 50 subscribers.
- **Notes reach very few people today.** A Note is up to **60 characters** of text. It sits at the top of the DM inbox for **24 hours**, and only **mutual followers** (people you follow who follow you back) or your **Close Friends** list see it. You can't see who viewed it. Replies arrive as DMs (Hootsuite guide, 2026).
- **A test may widen Notes.** On 7 Apr 2026 Instagram began testing Notes that *all followers* can see. Social Media Today reported it from a Threads post by app researcher Hammod Oh. The test isn't on every account. Notes on Reels and feed posts were tried earlier, removed for lack of use, and now appear again in some 2026 guides, so check in-app before relying on it.
- **Carousels are the better use of effort.** Buffer analysed more than 4 million posts (guide updated 23 Oct 2026). Per person reached, carousels earned about **109% more engagement** than Reels, and single images about **34% more**. Instagram also re-shows a carousel starting from the first slide the person didn't swipe to. Watch time, likes and **sends** count the same in every part of the app.

## How to do it
1. **Live:** don't plan it. Re-check eligibility only once the account passes 1,000 public followers. Until then, a "live" moment means a carousel or a Reel.
2. **Notes (2 minutes, free):** on days with a real hook, post a Note of 60 characters or fewer that asks a question people can reply to in a DM. For example "Which 1890s Llandough name should we look up next?" Never put a sales line in a Note.
   - If the all-followers test is on for the account (the composer asks "share with followers"), pick that option. Otherwise mutuals only, which is a few accounts.
   - Answer every DM reply by hand within 24 h. **No DMs to strangers.** Only reply to people who wrote first.
3. **Measure:** log each Note's DM replies in the social log (date, text, replies). Kill rule: **0 replies across 6 Notes means stop.**
4. **Put the saved effort into carousels.** Use a record-image first slide, 3–6 slides, the source on the last slide, and the shop link only in the first comment, as the quote pack already does.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Live: NO** until @cedwynestate has 1,000+ public followers. Read the follower count in the Sunday growth report and log it as "IG N/1000 (Live gate)". The same gate shape is already used for Facebook Stars (N/500).

- The real lever stays carousels from the weekly quote pack, so no change is needed there.
