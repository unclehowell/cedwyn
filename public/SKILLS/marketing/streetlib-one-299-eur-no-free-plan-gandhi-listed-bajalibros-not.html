StreetLib in Oct 2026: the free Basic plan is gone from the English site (StreetLib One costs EUR 299 once). Gandhi is listed, Bajalibros isn't

What StreetLib's own site states now (read 31 Oct 2026)

streetlib.com is a JavaScript app; the copy below was read from its live bundle, in English and Italian. - English pricing page (/pricing, SEO title "StreetLib One Pricing: 299 EUR, Pay Once"). Only two plans: - StreetLib One: "299 EUR once", "No subscription, no renewal, no annual fee". 85% of net revenue. E-book and audiobook. "Retail sales on 50+ stores worldwide", subscription services, digital lending, pre-orders, retailer promotions, Write unlimited. - Enterprise: custom price. - The FAQ: "Every StreetLib One account gets the whole network ... There are no tiers or extra channels to pay for." - Also: "account approval is subject to StreetLib's review process, and purchasing a subscription does not guarantee approval or acceptance of your titles." - There is no free plan on the English page. - Italian pricing page (streetlib.it/prezzi-servizi) still shows "Digitale": "Nessun costo d'ingresso" (no entry fee), 70% of net, up to 100 free ISBNs, global e-book distribution, audiobooks, quarterly billing. - Its comparison table: Basic distribution is "Selezionata" (selected stores), with 10 titles. Pro is "Estesa" (extended), with 100 titles, at €99/yr or €299 one-off. - Our reading: the free tier may survive for Italian sign-ups or be a stale translation. Whether a UK account can still open a free plan is not checked; only the signup screen can answer that. - Distribution partners logo grid (same bundle, about 60 names): - Spanish-language outlets listed: Casa del Libro, Librerías Gandhi, 24Symbols, Odilo, Nextory, Storytel, BookBeat, Everand, Ubook, Tocalivros, Perlego, OverDrive, Skoobe. - Not listed: Bajalibros and Leamos. The 2026 aggregator comparison we relied on had them; StreetLib's own page doesn't. - The grid carries no per-plan flag, so whether Gandhi is "selected" for a free Digitale account isn't stated anywhere public.

What this changes

  • "StreetLib Basic is free and reaches Bajalibros" is wrong on the current site. Bajalibros isn't on StreetLib's own list. The free tier isn't offered in English, and where it survives (Italian) it is "selected" stores only.
  • Gandhi (Mexico) is on the list. The route to it is StreetLib One at €299 once, which also brings Nextory, Storytel, BookBeat, 24Symbols, Odilo and OverDrive. One fee for several shelves we can't reach directly.
  • Other routes to Bajalibros (for the owner to choose; not checked today):
  • Bajalibros' own publisher signup. Its "publicá con nosotros" page wasn't read this run.
  • Another aggregator whose 2026 list names it.

How-to: test before paying (zero spend)

  1. Open the StreetLib signup screen (owner's account; no agent sign-up). Note which plans a UK publisher is offered. - If a free "Digitale" plan appears, open it and read its store toggle list. Record Gandhi, Casa del Libro and 24Symbols as on or off, with a screenshot. - If only StreetLib One appears, stop: it's spend.
  2. Value test for €299 once: count the shelves we can't otherwise reach (Gandhi, Casa del Libro, Nextory/Storytel/BookBeat, libraries via Odilo/OverDrive). - Payback at about €3.50 net per sale (85% of net on a €5–7.99 list after store cut, our estimate) is about 85 sales. - At the Kobo Plus-style ~€0.10 a read (from the Kobo Plus file), subscription reads hardly move it.
  3. Don't double-list: leave Kobo, Apple and Google off in StreetLib wherever we go direct.
