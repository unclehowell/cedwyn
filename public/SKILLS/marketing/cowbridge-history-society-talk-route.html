Cowbridge History Society talk route (plus Cowbridge u3a History Group)

Date: 2026-10-26 (skills harvest 17:07). Pages read in full today unless marked.

What the society is (read at source)

  • Registered charity 1094061, run by volunteers, "about 90 members", serves Cowbridge and surrounding villages. Objectives include promoting local history "via a series of talks and exhibitions", research and publishing, Charter Day (13 March 1254), and a public local-study room.
  • Monthly talks: FIRST FRIDAY of the month, 7:30 pm, Maendy Hall, Cowbridge United Free Church, The Broad Shoard, CF71 7AQ. Visitors pay £2; membership £10/yr. Members get a quarterly newsletter and the society's publications at a discount.
  • 2026-27 programme is already full through May 2027 (Oct: Iolo Morganwg and the Druids; Nov: The Great Flood 1607; Dec: Iolo Morganwg: Life after Death; Jan: diary of William Thomas c.1727-1795; Feb: Turner in the Vale; Mar: Barbara Gamage, Coity heiress; Apr: Cowbridge in WW1; May AGM 7 May 2027 + Edwardian Cardiff talk). So the realistic slot is the 2027-28 programme (Oct 2027 onward).
  • The programme leans hard on Vale/Glamorgan estate, gentry and 18th-19th century records: exactly the register a Great House Farm records talk fits.
  • Programme Secretary is a named committee role (membership page). Correspondence goes via the Secretary: [address kept in the project workspace], [number kept in the project workspace].
  • History Room, Old Hall, High Street CF71 7AH, open most Wednesday mornings 10:00-12:00 in term. Almost all its archive is on People's Collection Wales (that platform is a separate skill; do not duplicate).
  • The society also offers talks and guided walks TO organisations (not individuals) by prior arrangement: it is a speaker source for other clubs, so a good relationship here feeds the Rotary/Probus/u3a circuit.
  • Publications list: 15 local titles priced £3-£10, sold by the Treasurer and in the History Room (e.g. "A Journey down the Thaw Valley" 2020 £10; "Cowbridge and District 1860-1930" 2024 £6). Shows the audience buys local-history print at £3-£10.

Cowbridge u3a History Group (read at source)

  • 3rd Thursday monthly, 10:00, Lesser Hall, Cowbridge Town Hall (green door, east side). Talks "last about an hour", by outside speakers and members, "many with a Welsh flavour". Group fee £10/yr covers speakers and room hire; visitors £2.
  • Recent topic: St Donat's Castle (Jan and Nov 2025). Daytime, older audience; different people from the Friday-evening society, so it is a second Cowbridge slot, not a duplicate. (General u3a mechanics are in u3a-speaker-circuit-local-history.md; this is the Cowbridge-specific route.)

How to do it (steps)

  1. Wait for the deck + handout (shared-talk-deck-table-handout.md) to exist and be tested once at a smaller venue.
  2. Draft ONE short email to the Secretary, for the Programme Secretary, offering a talk for the 2027-28 programme: title, 50-60 min, 2-line speaker bio, the record sources used, no fee (expenses only), slides + projector need. Put it in HubSpot as a draft task; send nothing without the owner's GO.
  3. Separately draft to the Cowbridge u3a History Group contact (via the u3a site group email) for a daytime slot; can be sooner because u3a groups book closer in.
  4. Offer the History Room a free copy/QR card of the free edition for its reading shelf (ask, do not assume).
  5. After any talk: Jodee Blanco's "catalytic outreach" ask (which other groups book speakers?) and log in HubSpot.
