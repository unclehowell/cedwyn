# Hardcover.app: list the e-books on the Amazon-free reader app (and its Author Spotlight)
Harvested 2026-10-29 20:45Z by the skills harvest. Free reader-catalogue listing; no outreach, no spend.

## Why it is worth 20 minutes
- Hardcover is the Goodreads/StoryGraph alternative built by a small part-time team ("around 10 people"), founder Adam Fortuna; positioned as Amazon-free (hardcover.app/pages/about).
- Its monthly Hardcover Report email goes to **50k members** (organiser's own figure, Jan 2026 report, read). From Jan 2026 each report carries an **Author Spotlight**: one book by one author, shared in that email, the blog post and a featured list. The first pick (John Wilker, *Space Rogues*) was chosen as "a long-time Hardcover member (and supporter!) who has been active on our Discord for years" - i.e. selection is by community presence, not a form (read, Jan 2026 report). No application route is published: not checked whether one exists.
- Goodreads importer keeps custom shelves as lists, so switchers arrive with full libraries; every new listing is visible to that migrating, engaged reader base.
- A third-party book record also gives search engines and AI answers another independent citation of each e-book (same logic as goodreads-storygraph-listings-ebooks.md).

## Rules that bite (Hardcover FAQ + librarian docs, read 29 Oct 2026)
1. **Adding a book:** the new-book form (hardcover.app/books/new, also at the bottom of search results). With an ISBN or other external identifier the book enters the database and becomes searchable at once. Without one, only Librarians and paid Supporters can use "Add edition by manually entering all data"; that edition is in your own library immediately but **hidden from search until a librarian verifies it**.
2. Hardcover separates the Book (work) from Editions; set the edition format to **Ebook**.
3. **No author claiming yet** ("Author claiming is not available yet"). Until then authors can apply to become a Librarian (stating they are the author) or ask a librarian on Discord.
4. Any logged-in reader can fill empty fields and upload missing covers; editing existing data needs Supporter or Librarian. Books with 5+ reads (impact_score >= 5) have edits queued for a Senior Librarian.
5. Referrals: every two successful referrals = one free Supporter month (so the Supporter edit access can be earned without paying - zero-spend compatible, but slow).
6. Bugs and data help go through the Hardcover Discord (#bugs, #librarians).

## Steps
1. Search hardcover.app for each title first (avoid duplicates).
2. If the e-book has an ISBN: add via the form with the ISBN -> searchable at once. If not: add with title/author/description/cover; then ask in Discord #librarians, politely and once, for verification of the manual edition (one message per book batch, no promotion).
3. Fill: description (our own wording, banned-words checked), cover, page count, language (en; cy where bilingual), publication date, format Ebook. Add the shop URL only where a field exists for it.
4. Put each book on a public list on the account (e.g. "Records of Llandough and Leckwith").
5. Track monthly in the Sunday growth report: shelves (Want to Read count) per book.
- Kill rule: if after 90 days the books together show fewer than 5 shelvings, stop spending time here; keep the listings.
