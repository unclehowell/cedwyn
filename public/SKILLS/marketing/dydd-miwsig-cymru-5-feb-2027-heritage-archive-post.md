Harvested 2026-10-31 ~19:30Z. Jan-Mar Welsh calendar gap: Hen Galan (welsh-midwinter-customs-...md), Dydd Santes Dwynwen (st-dwynwens-day-25-jan-...md) and St David's Day (st-davids-day-2027-...md) are already covered. This file adds the February moment that sits between them.

## Facts (with sources)
- **Date: 5 February 2027.** The Welsh Government page (read 31 Oct 2026) heads "Dydd Miwsig Cymru 2027 ... 5 February 2027 is Welsh Language Music Day". Earlier years ran on the second Friday (2026: Fri 13 Feb, Wales.com). Some third-party calendars give 12 Feb 2027, so **check the gov.wales page again in early January** before scheduling.
- **What it is:** a Welsh Government campaign, run "for over a decade" (Wales.com), celebrating all genres of Welsh-language music. Official tags are **#Miwsig / #DyddMiwsigCymru**, with the handle @Miwsig on Facebook, X, Instagram and YouTube. Official Spotify playlists include "Best of Wales" and "Welsh Miwsig for your business". gov.wales offers downloadable promo resources.
- **How organisations took part in 2026** (Wales.com, read): businesses and offices played Welsh-language music for the day, schools held sing-alongs and music-discovery lessons, and dozens of gigs were listed on the awni database. **Sain Records, one of Britain's oldest indie labels, used the day to put its 1990s-2000s Welsh-language archive online.** That is the heritage-brand pattern: release something from your archive on the day.
- **No numbers published.** I found no published stream or reach figures for 2025-2026 (search plus the gov.wales and Wales.com pages). Treat the uplift as unmeasured, and measure our own post instead (see below).
