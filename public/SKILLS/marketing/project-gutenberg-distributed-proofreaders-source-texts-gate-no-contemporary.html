Project Gutenberg + Distributed Proofreaders: a source-text route, not a shop route (GATE)

Checked 31 Oct 2026. Pages read directly: PG "Submitting Your Own Work", PG Licence, PG Copyright How-To, PG Volunteers' FAQ, and the Distributed Proofreaders (DP) home page. A live PG catalogue search was also run.

What the sources say (read)

  • PG no longer takes contemporary works. "Contemporary works are not being accepted for the collection." Copyrighted books were added in the early years "with permission of the author"; today PG "does not have, and will not have, the needed features" for living authors. Authors are pointed to self.gutenberg.org, the World Library Foundation's self-publishing portal (PDF accepted, author keeps ownership, free unlimited redistribution expected). => Our paid e-book (v0.0.2 £4.99) cannot go there. A free edition (v0.0.1) could, but only on the condition of free unlimited redistribution.
  • Clearance rules: PG has 7 active rules, among them pre-95-years and pre-1978 publication, post-1978 life+70 or publication+120, and Rule 10 for foreign works under US law. If unsure, submit the item for a clearance decision rather than ask in the abstract. PG clears by US law. The licence page tells users to check their own country's law, so a UK author who died under 70 years ago is not free in the UK even if PG hosts the work.
  • Reusing PG texts in a paid product (licence 1.E.8 + "Explained"): strip the PG licence and all references to Project Gutenberg, and the text is unrestricted in the US and most other places. If the PG name or licence stays attached, a 20% royalty on gross profits applies, due within 60 days of each tax-return date. => To quote a PG-hosted source in the e-book, cite the original printed edition (author, title, year, page), never "Project Gutenberg", and do not bundle PG files.
  • DP pipeline (read): 51,469 titles preserved; 106 in Oct 2026, 134 in Sep 2026. Each page is proofread 3 times, formatted twice, post-processed, optionally "smooth read", then submitted to PG. PG itself recommends DP as the way in for anyone who wants to produce e-books. Registered volunteers can also be content providers, and DP has forums for suggesting books to digitise.

Gap check (live PG search, 31 Oct 2026)

  • "Glamorgan": 1 hit (The Century of Inventions of the Marquis of Worcester, not relevant). "Cardiff": 2 hits (Cardiff: A Sketch-Book by D. S. Andrews, 546 downloads, and an unrelated one). PG holds almost no public-domain Glamorgan history.
  • Free Gutenberg mirror API (gutendex) timed out; it was not used.

How to do it

  1. As a source: before writing a chapter, search PG for the period source. Use it as a clean text, but cite the printed edition. Every quote in the e-book points to the printed original.
  2. As a content provider (the real gap): pick a 19th-century Glamorgan or Llandaff source that is out of copyright in the US and the UK (author died before 1956) and is not on PG. Find a complete scan (Internet Archive, usually already scanned). Propose it on DP's forums or register as a content provider. Each book takes many volunteer-months, so expect the PG release in months, not weeks.
  3. Smooth reading: registered volunteers can read pre-release e-books. That builds goodwill and gives early access to new Welsh texts, but it is not a promotion channel.
  4. No promotion inside PG texts: transcriber's notes are not adverts. Our gain is a free, citable, clean text of a source that our e-book and the evidence pages can quote and cite as the printed edition.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • No shop or listing action. PG refuses contemporary works, and self.gutenberg.org needs a new account plus free redistribution: that is the project lead's decision, and only for the free v0.0.1 (overlaps the Internet Archive / Open Library and Zenodo skills; pick one canonical free copy).
  • Useful now, at zero cost: the book flywheel and evidence work can check PG first for clean texts, then cite the printed edition. Never cite "Project Gutenberg" in paid text, to avoid the 1.E.8 royalty trigger.
  • Longer play: propose that DP digitise a missing, cleanly out-of-copyright Glamorgan source. It must be a neutral period source, not a family or case document. That needs a person's DP account, so it is a PROPOSAL for the project lead.
  • Kill rule: if no suitable cleared source is found in 2 searches, drop the content-provider idea.
