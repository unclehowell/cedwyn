# North American Welsh diaspora: Ninnau & Y Drych, WNAA/NAFOW, affiliated societies (2026)

Topic: reach Welsh-heritage readers in the US and Canada, an English-reading, genealogy-minded, older and higher-income audience the index had not covered (globalwelsh/Wales International file is UK-government-led networks; Patagonia files cover Chubut).

## What the primary sources say (with numbers)
- NINNAU & Y Drych, "The North American Welsh Newspaper" (read 30 Oct 2026): a 501(c)(3) non-profit run by volunteers. It is bimonthly, with a colour e-version and a black-and-white print version. Copy deadlines are the 1st of Feb/Apr/Jun/Aug/Oct/Dec (Dec 1 = Jan-Feb issue). It runs a free calendar of Welsh-related events in the US and Canada, and also events in Wales "if deemed of particular interest" to the North American Welsh community. The format is "City, ST — date — event — time/venue — cost — contact", sent to the main office by mail or email with a meaningful subject line, because its spam filter drops vague ones. Ads (paid, not for us now): full page $500, half $275, quarter $150, 1/8 $90, 1/10 $80.
- Welsh North American Association / North American Festival of Wales (read): the festival moves between the US and Canada each year. NAFOW 2026 ran 2-6 Sep in Springfield MA with ~500 expected attendees from North America and Wales (host Welsh Society of Western New England). It had a Marketplace and a dedicated "Anglesey roots" networking session, so a place-specific roots session is a format they already run. NAFOW 2027: hub at Hilton Cleveland Downtown, Ohio, with preregistration opening 1 March 2027 (festivalofwales.org snippet).
- WNAA Affiliated Welsh Organizations (AWO) directory (read): member societies get the Hwyl newsletter and event notices to share with their members. Updates go to the AWO directory contact. WSWNE also keeps a regional list of societies, e.g. St David's Welsh Society of the Capital District (Albany NY) with bi-weekly Welsh classes, and the Poultney Area St David's Society (VT).
- The University of Rio Grande Madog Center (Ohio) runs a monthly Zoom Welsh learners' book club (snippet). That bridges to the Welsh-learners skill.

## The playbook
1. **Lead with a genealogy story, not the product.** This audience joins for roots, hymns (gymanfa ganu) and place. The hook is "what records survive for one Glamorgan farm and family, and how to read them". The e-book link comes second.
2. **Use the free doors first:** (a) a calendar entry for any online talk, timed to the bimonthly deadline. A talk on 10 March needs copy by 1 Feb for the Mar-Apr issue. (b) Article or news submissions to the main office. (c) Event notices through AWO societies' newsletters, which only works via a society that agrees to share it.
3. **Time for US evenings:** an online talk at 19:00 Eastern = 00:00 UK. Use 13:00 Eastern Saturday (18:00 UK) so both sides can attend. Add an .ics link (see online-talk-add-to-calendar-ics-show-up-rate.md).
4. **Prices and checkout:** show USD alongside GBP where Stripe adaptive pricing allows (local-currency-pricing-stripe-adaptive-gumroad-ppp.md). Most of this audience pays by card from the US.
5. **Festival route (long lead):** NAFOW 2027 Cleveland preregistration opens 1 Mar 2027. A "Glamorgan roots" networking slot, modelled on the 2026 Anglesey roots session, would be a proposal to the organisers. It would be in-person and need travel, so for now it's a GATE (owner decision, spend).
6. **Measure:** `utm_source=ninnau|wnaa|<society>&utm_medium=diaspora`, plus USD-currency Stripe sessions as a proxy. If a calendar listing or article sends fewer than 10 visits in 60 days, stop repeating it. Our threshold.

## Pitfalls
- No paid ads (zero-spend rule). The ad prices are recorded only for a future owner decision.
- Societies are volunteer-run. Send one polite, ask-first note per society, never a list blast. UK PECR doesn't govern US email, but our own no-cold-contact and drafts-only rules do.
- Keep public text free of family holdings and addresses, and never present the case as settled. The diaspora press reprints copy verbatim.
