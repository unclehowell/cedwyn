# Skill #5 — Travel → heritage tourism (2026)
Written 26 Oct 2026. Entry #5 'Travel' mapped to heritage tourism for the /wales/ walking tour.
- Official listings: Visit Wales takes products/events via its industry route (Event Data Submission form); needs an operator contact.
- Free route platforms (Wikiloc, Komoot, AllTrails) rank routes by recorded GPS tracks, photos and reviews; a route without a real track doesn't get listed well.
- Local partners carry walkers: civic-society heritage trails (Penarth Civic Society), walking charities (Valeways), libraries' history talks.
- Rules: Vale of Glamorgan Council bodies are excluded from outreach; sole-trader B&Bs and pubs count as individual subscribers under PECR, so don't cold-email them.
