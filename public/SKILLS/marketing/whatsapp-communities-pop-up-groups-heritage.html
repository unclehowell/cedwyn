WhatsApp Communities and pop-up groups: the two-way layer beside a Channel

Harvested 29 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 22:39Z). Zero spend. Companion to whatsapp-channel-readers-uk-patagonia.md, which covers the one-way Channel. This file covers Communities (an announcement group plus linked sub-groups) and short-lived "pop-up" groups around one event.

Practitioner evidence (numbers)

  • El Tecolote, San Francisco, bilingual community paper (RJI, 2026, read in full):
  • Community launched Apr 2025. Six months of QR codes in print, Instagram Stories, in-article callouts, sign-up events and tabling: about 300 members.
  • One breaking-news moment, co-promoted with a partner outlet: 700+ joined the announcement group, taking it to about 1,000.
  • Pop-up groups: a teachers' strike group brought 70+ opt-ins, an Election Day group 50+. Members stayed in the wider network afterwards.
  • Partner event with a trusted grassroots group: about 15 sign-ups, small but "high-touch and influential".
  • Cadence: announcement posts Tuesday and Thursday mornings, more only during big events.
  • Every new member got a personal message asking why they joined and what they wanted. Photos, short videos and polls drew the most engagement.
  • Churn is the measure. A single post could make several people leave. Highly engaged posts didn't bring new members but reduced churn.
  • Privacy: in groups, members may see one another's phone numbers, depending on setup.
  • Reach plc: 90% open rate across its 80 WhatsApp Communities (Twipe, 12 Dec 2024, already cited in the Channel skill).
  • WhatsApp Help Center (snippets, not checked; faq.whatsapp.com refuses our fetch): up to 2,000 members per Community, counting all its groups and the announcement group. The community general chat is capped at 1,024, like any group.
  • Channel vs Community vs broadcast list (RJI): a Channel is one-to-many with emoji reactions only. A broadcast list sends one-to-one messages that readers can reply to privately. A Community is one-to-many announcements plus many-to-many sub-groups. Pick by how much conversation you can moderate.

How to run it

  1. Channel first, Community second. The Channel (no phone numbers shown, no cap) stays the main broadcast. Start a Community only once there is someone to moderate replies, at least 2 hours a week.
  2. Create it on the owner's phone in WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business: Communities tab, then New community, with a name, description and image. Tab's WhatsApp line is a Business API number and can't own a Community.
  3. Structure: announcement group for the twice-weekly post, Tuesday and Thursday mornings. Add at most 2 sub-groups, each with one clear purpose. Turn off "members can add others" and set the groups to admin-approved joining.
  4. Pop-up groups are the growth engine. Open a group for one dated moment, announce an end date, then close it to messages. Point its members to the Channel or Community. A dated moment with real questions works best.
  5. Welcome message: one personal line to each new member: "What brought you here, and what would you like more of?" Log the answers. They are the content plan.
  6. Post shape: a photo or short clip plus 1-2 lines, and a poll every couple of weeks. Add a link only when there's somewhere worth going.
  7. Measure weekly: members, leavers per post (the churn signal), replies and questions, and clicks via utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=<slug>. These visits show as "direct" without UTMs (see share-links-dark-social-web-share-utm.md).
  8. Never add people. Use invite links only (opt-in), and keep no list of numbers outside WhatsApp. That keeps the UK GDPR position simple.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • PROPOSAL (the project lead's GO; the owner creates it on his phone): no standing Community yet. Use pop-up groups tied to dated Cedwyn moments: the Llandough walk or a library talk (seed attendees by QR on the handout), St David's Day 1 Mar 2027 (st-davids-day-2027-random-acts-pethau-bychain-calendar.md), and each movie door opening. Each runs 7 days with a stated end, then points members to the Cedwyn WhatsApp Channel.
  • Language: English first, plus a separate Spanish pop-up only if the Patagonia Channel shows real Spanish followers. Never mix languages in one group.
  • Moderation and editorial: the editorial rules apply to every admin post. Never name private people, nothing about family holdings or addresses, no banned words. Member replies naming people are removed with a polite note. Never discuss the case in member threads. Point to the evidence pages instead.
  • Weekly numbers go into the Sunday growth report. Kill rule: if a pop-up gets fewer than 10 opt-ins from a live event, or more than 20% of members leave within its week, stop pop-ups and keep the Channel only.
  • No DMs to strangers. The personal welcome line goes only to people who joined by link, and only on the project lead's GO.
