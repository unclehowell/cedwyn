- **Calendar hooks** (from spanish-patagonia-audience.md): 28 July (Gŵyl y Glaniad), the Eisteddfod del Chubut (Sept-Oct; 2026 ran 19-25 Oct), 1 March (Saint David's Day). A pitch tied to a hook gives the desk a reason to run it that week.

## How-to
1. **Angle for Chubut readers:** a Welsh family house and its records, written for descendants of the 1865 settlers, not for UK readers. One dated record, one image, one short human line. No legal dispute, no accusations, no named living people.
2. **Write in neutral Argentine Spanish (press register, usted), checked by a fluent reader before the project lead sends**, 120-180 words, a headline, a one-line image caption with archive reference, and a link to the Spanish-language Cedwyn page (not the shop).
3. **Send window:** Tue-Thu, 12:00-14:00 GMT (09:00-11:00 Trelew). One outlet at a time; if no reply in 7 days, offer the same piece to the next outlet, not both at once (regional desks dislike duplicated copy).
4. **Order:** Diario Jornada redacción -> El Chubut "Redacción" form -> Radio Chubut (offer a short phone or WhatsApp-audio interview in Spanish, only if a Spanish speaker is available).
5. **Letters route for no-news weeks:** El Chubut's "Carta del lector" can carry a short reader letter about a record; it must be honest about who writes it.
6. **Track:** one HubSpot task per outlet (draft), UTM `utm_source=jornada|elchubut|lu20&utm_medium=press&utm_campaign=chubut-2027`, Cloudflare traffic from AR checked a week after any run.
