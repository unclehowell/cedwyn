# Merched y Wawr: Welsh-language women's branches, Y Wawr magazine and the South-East region

Harvested 2026-10-30 (skills harvest run). Topic: community/press route into a Welsh-speaking, older, book-buying audience through a national voluntary movement with local branches near the farm.

## What it is (read 30 Oct 2026 unless marked)
- Merched y Wawr ("Daughters of the Dawn"): voluntary, non-political Welsh-medium women's movement, founded 1967 at Y Parc when the WI insisted on English. Charity no. 506789, HQ Aberystwyth. (merchedywawr.cymru, read; Wikipedia, read)
- Size: home page: "over 280 branches and clubs in Wales" (read). Members: ~7,000 in 2016 and 10,000+ in the late 1980s (Wikipedia citing Swansea CVS 2016 and the Encyclopaedia of Wales; not checked further). Facebook page ~4,805 followers (search snippet only).
- Branches meet monthly with a guest speaker (gŵr/gwraig gwadd), trips and socials; every branch writes up its meeting as a news post on the national site (dozens per month in Sept–Oct 2026, read). Clwb Gwawr = younger clubs.
- History/oral history is in scope: the movement has collected oral history across Wales and campaigned for the Gwenllian memorial (Wikipedia, read); the region pages link to People's Collection Wales (read).
- Learner bridge, real testimony: Cangen Harlech a Llanfair invites learners/new speakers every February; Feb 2026 "10 o ddysgwyr/siaradwyr newydd" came with their tutor, mixed with members and chatted in Welsh over refreshments (branch post, read).

## Playbook (practitioner pattern, adapted)
1. Lead with the place and the records, in Welsh, not the product. A branch post reads like "we heard about X's history"; the talk is the thing they report, and the book is the take-home.
2. One concrete offer per contact: a 30–40 min Welsh-language illustrated talk (in person or on Zoom), learner-friendly glossary handout, no fee asked, no sales pitch in the room beyond a QR card.
3. Write the Y Wawr piece as a feature (600–900 words, 2–3 photos with source lines), not an advert. Women in the records is the natural angle for this magazine.
4. Time it: talks are booked for the season (Sept–May; branches start the year in September), so offers go out by early December for spring dates; St David's (1 March) issue copy by early January.
5. Measure: a short campaign link (/r/ path with utm_source=merchedywawr) on the handout and in the article. Stop rule (ours): fewer than 10 visits in 60 days after the issue lands = do not repeat.

## Gates / don'ts
- Women's organisation, Welsh-medium: the speaker should be a fluent Welsh speaker; a recorded or Zoom talk only if the branch asks for it.
- No paid ads (zero spend). No mass emailing of branches – one regional contact, one draft, owner's GO.
- Branch officers' personal emails/phones stay out of public files and the academy.
