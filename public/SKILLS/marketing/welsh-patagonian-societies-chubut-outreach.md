# Reaching Welsh-Patagonian societies in Chubut: who they are, how they like to be approached (2026 how-to)

Goal: build respectful, institution-to-institution links between the Cedwyn archive (Argentina half, Spanish) and the Welsh societies of Chubut, so our Spanish editions reach the community that cares most, without cold DMs to individuals. Complements spanish-patagonia-audience.md (audience size, dates, language) and heritage-anniversary-hashtag-calendar.md (28 Jul).

## The institutions (each society's own public pages, read 26 Oct 2026)
- **Asociación San David, Trelew**: founded 1892 (first as "Camwy Fydd", "Sociedad San David" from 1897). Its hall was built after St David's in Pembrokeshire, seats 1,000, first used for an Eisteddfod in 1913 and opened 28 Jul 1915 for the colony's 50th anniversary. Public contact on galesesenpatagonia.com.ar: institutional email + office phone, public hours 16-20 h. Host venue of the Eisteddfod del Chubut.
- **Asociación (Civil) Cultural Galesa de Puerto Madryn** (asociaciongalesamadryn.org): runs the restored historic house (Casa Histórica Maestro Toschke), dance group Her Siriol (10 years in 2026), a Welsh tea for the Landing, school visits, the photo exhibition "Aún estamos aquí" (shown in Gaiman's Museo Primera Casa, Aug 2026), a members campaign and a donations page. Public contact form and "Cómo asociarse". **Puerto Madryn is where the Argentina half places Cedwyn II**, so this is the nearest natural partner.
- **Asociación Eisteddfod del Chubut**: the 2026 Eisteddfod ran **19-25 Oct** in Trelew and Gaiman, main sessions 23-24 Oct at Salón San David (Radio Chubut, Patagonia Magazine). Gaiman's Youth Eisteddfod was mid-September 2026 (trevelin.info). Competitions include literature and translation; entries open months ahead.
- **Other societies listed on galesesenpatagonia.com.ar:** Comodoro Rivadavia (Asociación Galesa San David), Esquel, Gaiman, Trevelin (Asociación Galesa 16 de Octubre; Capilla Bethel). Gaiman also has the **Museo Histórico Regional** (Dirección de Cultura Gaiman shares documents and photos for Museum Day, 18 May).
- **Wales side: Cymdeithas Cymru-Ariannin / Wales-Argentina Society** (founded 1939; registered charity 507737 in England and Wales): funds teacher, student and minister exchanges and co-funds the Welsh Language Project with the Welsh Government and British Council Wales. Chosen as one of five "treasures" of Community Foundation Wales's first Global Giving Circle (folk-singing workshops in Trelew, Gaiman, Esquel, Trevelin). CFW's own estimate: at least a third of these towns' people have some Welsh ancestry.

## Our approach (inferred from how these societies present themselves; no published etiquette guide was found)
1. **Give before asking.** They publicise donations, members and events; they do not publish "partnership" forms. Offer something they can use: a free Spanish PDF for their library, a dated record image for their 28 July or 1 March posts, a talk recording. No ask for promotion in the first message.
2. **Write to the institution, not a person.** Committee lists are public on their pages; still use the society's email or contact form, addressed to the Comisión Directiva. Never DM members or follow-up through personal profiles.
3. **Spanish first, a line of Welsh second.** Short, formal-warm Rioplatense Spanish ("Estimada Comisión Directiva"), a Welsh greeting line (e.g. "Cyfarchion o Gymru"), signed by the archive, not a private individual. No attachments in a first email; a link to the free Spanish PDF page.
4. **Their calendar, not ours.** Never write in Eisteddfod week (Oct), 28 July (Landing) or 1 March. Best windows: Feb-Apr (programmes being planned) and Aug-Sep (Eisteddfod entries).
5. **Culture first.** Lead with the shared Welsh heritage of the house and its names. Our case material and criticism of public bodies stays on the site's Argentina pages; it is not the subject of a first letter to a cultural society.
6. **Join, then contribute.** A member fee or a donation is spending: the project lead's decision only. Following their public pages and sharing their posts costs nothing.
7. **One follow-up at most**, after 3-4 weeks; silence is an answer.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Targets, in order:** (1) Asociación Cultural Galesa de Puerto Madryn (Cedwyn II's town); (2) Asociación San David, Trelew; (3) Wales-Argentina Society (from the Wales side, in Welsh/English); (4) Museo Histórico Regional de Gaiman (records exchange). Institutions only; HubSpot **draft tasks only** via the contacts flywheel; nothing sent without the project lead's sign-off. Check contacted.json first so no one gets two first letters.
- **What we offer:** the Spanish edition of the names register (Enwau: every form of Tŷ Mawr's name with its date and source) as a free PDF/EPUB for their library, plus one record image per 28 July post.
- **Every example follows the project lead's 11:45 rule:** 1824, the David Stewart survey, is the first record mention of Tŷ Mawr we have found; 1667 is the family's acquisition (1974 newspaper reports of Mary Williams' statement; ITV News 1988); Bute estate documents are never presented as Tŷ Mawr records before 1824.
- **Draft opening (Spanish, for HubSpot):** "Estimada Comisión Directiva: Cyfarchion o Gymru. Desde el archivo Cedwyn, sobre una casa galesa de Llandough y sus nombres, les ofrecemos sin costo la edición en castellano de nuestro registro de nombres para su biblioteca. El enlace está aquí: [página /ebooks/enwau/ en español]. Con gusto les enviamos imágenes de documentos fechados para el 28 de julio. Saludos cordiales, Archivo Cedwyn." (Banned-word grep before saving; never name the project lead.)
- **Measure:** UTM per society (utm_source=asoc-madryn etc., see utm-discipline-ga4-funnel.md); log replies in HubSpot; count Spanish PDF downloads by source.
- **2027 hooks:** Eisteddfod del Chubut translation or literature categories (check the 2027 rules when published, around mid-year); Museum Day 18 May with Gaiman.
