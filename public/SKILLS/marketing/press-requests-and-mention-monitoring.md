# Skill: Press requests and free mention monitoring for a heritage story (2026)
Written 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). Topic: earning coverage by answering journalists, and catching every mention / share (the project lead's "press monitor").

## What the evidence shows
- Steve Blears, Marvellous Media Training (UK), "How to use #JournoRequest to get media attention" (21 Nov 2023): search X for **#JournoRequest** and **#PRrequest**; most requests are B2C case-study asks and seasonal; only answer when the request matches your real expertise - irrelevant replies waste time and burn goodwill. Reply fast, short, on-brief.
- BuzzStream, "Journalist Requests: How to Find Them and Respond" (buzzstream.com): request platforms (Qwoted, Featured, HARO's successors) work when responses are fast, specific, quotable and include credentials; generic pitches are ignored.
- ResponseSource (UK, responsesource.com) sends journalist enquiries by email; it is a paid service - note only, we do not buy it.
- Talkwalker Alerts (talkwalker.com): free, email-delivered web mention alerts, "trusted by over 600,000 users"; reviewers (mentionkit.com, mentiondrop.com 2026) call it the closest free complement to Google Alerts. Google Alerts alone misses many mentions; run both.

## How-to
1. Monitoring (free, set once): Google Alerts + Talkwalker Alerts on: "Great House Farm" Llandough; "Ty Mawr" Llandough; cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate; "Peerless Jim Driscoll" + Llandough; Lavernock Marconi. Deliver to the Tab mailbox ([address kept in the project workspace]) so runs can read them; log each new hit to the project workspace (date, url, outlet, sentiment, linked y/n).
2. Own-site signals: share-button clicks (GA4/Cloudflare events) and referrers from facebook.com / x.com / news sites = "non-purchase engagement" for phase 1 of the funnel.
3. Journalist requests: once a day search X (logged-in browser) for #JournoRequest / #PRrequest + Wales, history, heritage, farming, land, archives, boxing, Marconi. Only respond where the archive genuinely has an evidenced record.
4. Response shape (<= 150 words): one evidenced fact with its date and archive reference, one line on what the archive holds, one deep link to the specific Cedwyn page, offer of images/scans. No accusations, no named living people, banned words list applies.
5. Track in HubSpot: each journalist as a contact (media organisation = corporate subscriber), the request URL in the note, outcome later.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Press contact stays on the project lead's GO** (standing rule): runs may monitor and DRAFT responses to journalist requests as HubSpot tasks; nothing is sent to press without his go.
- Alerts setup needs a Google account action (Google Alerts) - doable in the connected Gmail account's browser session or by the project lead in two minutes; Talkwalker Alerts signs up with an email address (the Tab mailbox).
- Weekly: count new mentions, shares and press hits into the Sunday growth report.
