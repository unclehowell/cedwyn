WhatsApp Channel for e-book readers (UK + Spanish-speaking Patagonia)

Skill harvested 26 Oct 2026. Topic: a one-way WhatsApp Channel as a free, algorithm-free broadcast list for Cedwyn readers, especially Spanish-speaking readers in Argentina where WhatsApp is the default app. Supports today's plan item "Draft a WhatsApp Channel plan for Cedwyn readers" (the owner creates the channel; this file is the how-to).

What the practitioner evidence shows

  • Financial Times (Nieman Lab, 7 Nov 2023, read in full): "It took a year for us to grow an audience of 35,000 on Telegram ... we [grew] a similar-sized following around markets on WhatsApp in two weeks" (Rachel Banning-Lover, head of social). FT posts a handful of top stories a day; links are free to read.
  • La Nación, Argentina (same article): 392,500 followers at the time. Chief social editor Andrés Krom: more than nine out of ten Argentinians with internet access use WhatsApp; the tone is "plain and friendly"; posts are one or two short paragraphs so followers get the point without leaving the app; links kept to a minimum and reserved for in-depth pieces. Metrics available to admins: approximate views per post and counts of each emoji reaction, nothing more.
  • Echobox VP Product in INMA (read, public part): Reach PLC sends to 2M+ people via WhatsApp; some readers reach up to 14 pageviews per session. Niche channels beat general ones (Daily Mail "Kardashians News" 2.7M followers, more than its general news channel). Noticias Telemundo (820,000 followers) uses short videos and polls and WhatsApp became a bigger traffic source than X. Limits: channels sit in the Updates tab, followers must opt in to notifications, no built-in monetisation, real manual workload.
  • Twipe (12 Dec 2024, read): no follower cap; admins never see followers' phone numbers (simpler GDPR position than Groups/Communities); Reach saw a 90% open rate across its 80 WhatsApp Communities (Communities, not Channels).
  • WhatsApp's own pages (FAQ: create a channel from the Updates tab, share the channel link on other websites; Blog: admins can record 60-second videos, voice-message transcripts on channels): search snippets only, not checked (faq.whatsapp.com and blog.whatsapp.com refused our fetch).
  • No e-book or small-heritage channel case study with sales numbers was found. Treat this as a reach and loyalty channel; sales impact is untested.

How to run it

  1. Create in the WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) app: Updates tab -> + -> New channel. Name + short description + square image. Done on the owner's phone; the WhatsApp line Tab uses is a Business API number and cannot own a channel.
  2. Choose a niche promise (niche beats general): one sentence of what followers get and how often.
  3. Cadence: 2-4 posts a week, never 10 a day (Nieman: heavy senders train people to mute).
  4. Post shape (La Nación): 1-2 short paragraphs, one image or a 60-second clip, the point complete inside WhatsApp; a link only when the reader wants the full piece. Use a poll every couple of weeks to learn what people want.
  5. Two languages, two channels if the Spanish audience is real: an English channel and a Spanish one, rather than mixing.
  6. Grow it with what you own: the channel link on /ebooks/, the site footer, the e-book back matter, the Mailchimp welcome email, Instagram/Facebook bios, and the QR on the talk handout. No paid follower services.
  7. Measure weekly: follower count, views per post, top reactions, and clicks via utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=channel on every link.
  8. Rules: broadcast only (no DMs to strangers); never add people's numbers; one clear call to action per week at most.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Status: PROPOSAL only. The owner creates the channel(s) on his own phone and gives the GO before anything is posted. Tab drafts the name, description and first posts into the plan task; nothing is sent.
  • Channel 1 (English): "Cedwyn: Tŷ Mawr in the records" - "Short notes from the records of a lost Vale farmhouse, twice a week."
  • Channel 2 (Spanish, for Chubut/Welsh-Patagonian readers): "Cedwyn: la granja en los registros" - same notes in Spanish; link to the Spanish pages on Cedwyn.
  • First three posts (drafts): 1. "1824: the David Stewart survey is the first record mention found of the farm. Here is the page." (image of the extract, link to the evidence page) 2. "The family acquired Tŷ Mawr in 1667 (1974 newspapers; ITV News 1988)." Short paragraph, archive cutting image. 3. Poll: "Which should the next note cover? A) the 1974 newspapers B) the 1988 demolition C) the survey maps".
  • Sales link, once a week at most: the free first edition or the £31.99 bundle on /ebooks/ with ?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=channel&utm_campaign=cedwyn-en (or -es). No price changes here.
  • Report: follower counts and best post go into the Sunday growth notes.
