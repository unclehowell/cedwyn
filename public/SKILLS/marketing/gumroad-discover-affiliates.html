Gumroad Discover + affiliates: the marketplace and referral levers

Skill harvested 26 Oct 2026. Free to use: Gumroad takes a fee per sale only.

Facts (Gumroad's own pages, read 26 Oct 2026)

  • Fees: 10% + $0.50 on sales through your profile or direct links. 30% when a new customer finds you and buys through Discover. Gumroad has been merchant of record since 1 Jan 2025, so it handles sales tax and VAT worldwide.
  • Account eligibility for Discover (help article 79): 1. Payout settings completed. 2. A balance of at least $100 from genuine sales. Self-purchases and friends' purchases don't count. 3. A pass of Gumroad's risk review, which "can take on average 3 weeks after you pass the $100 threshold".
  • Product eligibility, once the account qualifies: 1. At least one successful, non-refunded sale. 2. A category selected. 3. Ratings enabled. These are all set in the product's Share tab. Tags also drive Discover filtering (the article covers tags and filtering by tag).
  • Discover is crowded: about 1.6M products, about 20 shown per page (Gumroad's discover page; a Medium analysis of 36 "hot" products).
  • Affiliates (help articles 333 and 249):
  • Add an affiliate by email and set 1–75% commission per product. The commission is calculated on what the buyer pays, after discounts and before tax.
  • The affiliate link looks like gumroad.com Your own affiliates get a 30-day cookie; links through Gumroad's general affiliate programme get a 7-day cookie. The last click wins.
  • Gumroad takes its fee first, and the rest is split.

How to do it

  1. Ready the listings now, so they qualify the day the account does. For every product: choose a category (Education / History or the nearest match), turn ratings ON, add 5–8 tags (welsh history, local history, genealogy, archives, wales, patagonia, heritage, ebook), write a cover and a first line that state the hook, and set an honest page count.
  2. Get to $100 of genuine sales through direct links, at 10% + $0.50. Don't buy our own products and don't ask friends to: Gumroad excludes those sales, and it risks the review.
  3. After the $100 mark, allow about 3 weeks for the risk review. Then check whether the products show in a Discover search for the tags.
  4. Ask buyers for a rating with one line in the delivery email or the PDF's last page: "If it was useful, a star rating on Gumroad helps others find it." Ratings act as social proof on Discover.
  5. Affiliates, only with people who asked or already partner with us: offer 30–40% on the £31.99 bundle to a heritage society newsletter or a podcast host who covered the story. Never cold-recruit strangers by DM. Track sales in Gumroad > Affiliates.
  6. Weigh the 30% cut. Discover sales are customers we would not otherwise reach. At £9.99 we keep roughly £6.50 after Discover's 30% and card processing, against roughly £8.10 on a direct sale (rough figures, taking $1 as about £0.77). That is still positive, so leave Discover on.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Cedwyn's buy buttons use Stripe Payment Links (checked 26 Oct). Gumroad is the second shelf (the hotspotbnb store). Discover is the only part of our setup that brings in buyers we didn't reach ourselves, so it is worth meeting its requirements early.
  • Listing copy follows the Cedwyn rules:
  • The Williams are a family of standing.
  • 1667 is the family's acquisition of Tŷ Mawr, cited to the 1974 newspaper reports (Mary Williams' statement) and ITV News 1988.
  • No banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken), and the project lead is never named.
  • Owner steps: payout settings and identity for the risk review are the project lead's. An agent can draft the category, tags and descriptions as a checklist for him, or apply them if the Gumroad connection allows edits. Read the connection's product-update action first; change nothing priced.
  • Affiliate offers are outward messages, so they are HubSpot drafts for the project lead's OK.
  • KPI: the account reaches $100 of genuine sales, then products appear in Discover within about 3 weeks, then Discover-sourced sales show in Gumroad's sales by source.
