# YouTube Premieres for long archive films: when to use them, and when not to (2026)

Topic: deciding between a scheduled Premiere and a plain upload for longer Cedwyn films (movie doors, record explainers), and running one properly when it fits.

## What the platform documents (YouTube Help, read in full 26 Oct 2026)
- A Premiere creates a **public watch page before release**. It can be shared; viewers can set a reminder, comment and chat. Premieres "show up across YouTube just like regular uploads": search, home page, recommendations.
- **Not supported:** Shorts; output above 1080p; 360/VR180. A Community Guidelines strike freezes the Premiere.
- A **countdown** (theme and length chosen in "Set up Premiere") runs, then everyone watches together. Viewers can rewind but not skip ahead of the live point.
- Afterwards it is a normal upload. **Premiere views transfer to the video**, the **chat replay stays attached**, and Studio analytics show live vs later viewing.
- **Trailers** on the watch page (15 s to 3 min) need **more than 1,000 subscribers** and no strikes. Pre-roll ads only if monetised; Super Chat if eligible.
- **Instant Premiere** premieres as soon as processing finishes. **Live Redirect** sends a live stream's audience to the Premiere.

## What practitioners report
- **Voclify (A. St, 11/31 Jul 2026, read in full), opinion from creator discussions, not a controlled study:**
  - Premieres work when an engaged audience will turn up at a set time (music, gaming, tight niches).
  - For channels **under about 10,000 subscribers** with average engagement, plain uploads "tend to" gather views faster in the first 48 h. This is the author's pattern reading; **not checked** with data.
  - Anecdote: creators with 2,000 subscribers premiering to 11 people in chat.
  - A Premiere without promotion (community post, Shorts teaser, socials, pinned comment) is "just a delayed regular upload".
  - Search-intent videos (how-tos) are poor Premiere candidates.
  - The author also writes that the video is not discoverable "the same way" until it premieres. That sits uneasily with YouTube Help's statement above; treat it as **not checked**.
- **Reddit r/PartneredYoutube and r/letsplay threads** (snippets only; Reddit returned 403 here): several creators saw no gain or a smaller first week after Premieres. Anecdotal, **not checked**.

## Decision rule
Premiere only when **all** of these hold:
1. The video is long-form (≥4 min), 16:9, ≤1080p, and has an event hook (a new door, a new record).
2. We can name **≥15 people likely to attend live** (list subscribers, Facebook followers who engage, partners). Below that, the empty chat shows on the replay for good.
3. There are ≥48 h of promotion: a community post with the watch link, a Short teaser, a Facebook/Instagram post, a list email.
Otherwise: **plain upload**, scheduled for our usual best hour, with the same promotion pointing at the live video.

## How to run one (when the rule passes)
1. Upload as Scheduled → tick **Set as Premiere** → pick a short countdown (2 min). Schedule 3-7 days out at a local evening slot.
2. Share the watch page link at once (UTM on the link from our site: `utm_source=site&utm_medium=premiere`).
3. Promote at -72 h, -24 h, -1 h: community post, Short teaser (Shorts can't premiere, but can point to one), socials, email.
4. Be in chat from the countdown. Pin a first comment with the record source and the /ebooks/ link. Answer questions with dates and sources.
5. Afterwards: pin the best chat question as a comment and reply. Compare 7-day views/AVD with our last 3 plain uploads. Two Premieres that don't beat the median = stop.

- **Today the channel has about 7 subscribers**, so trailers are out (1,000-sub gate) and the attendance rule fails. **Default: plain uploads** for movie doors and archive films. No change needed to current jobs.
- **PROPOSAL (needs owner GO, as a new public event):** the first Premiere waits for a flagship film, e.g. a film on the **1824 David Stewart survey (the first record mention found)** or "1667: the family acquired Tŷ Mawr (1974 newspapers; ITV News 1988)". It only runs once the attendance list reaches 15 named likely viewers (list subscribers, engaged followers, partners). The Instagram collab and email list are how we get there.
- Movie doors are 720p webm ~2 min. That fits Premieres technically (≤1080p) but is short for an event. Combine 3-4 doors into one compilation before premiering.
- Never premiere a Short; keep Shorts as teasers that link to the long video.
- Public text: no owner name, no banned words, no Bute-era dates.
