# St David's Day (1 Mar 2027) campaign calendar: Random Acts of Welshness / #PethauBychain

Harvested 2026-10-29. Consolidates the St David's Day mentions scattered across other files (gifting, Random Acts listing) into one dated calendar.

## What the national campaign is (primary source, read)
- Welsh Government / Wales.com "Dydd Gŵyl Dewi 2026" partner pack: the theme is Dewi Sant's words "gwnewch y pethau bychain" (do the little things). The campaign "Random Acts of Welshness" asks people and organisations to do one small act that celebrates Welsh people or traditions and "makes someone's day... a little bit better", and post it on 1 March with #RandomActsOfWelshness and #PethauBychain.
- Partner steps in the pack: (1) choose and film/photograph the act before the day, and email the campaign team if it is notable (contact listed in the pack); (2) share with partner organisations and "friends of Wales around the world"; (3) follow and share @WalesDotCom content on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok; (4) post on 1 March with both hashtags.
- Example acts in the pack: fly the Draig Goch (AR at draiggoch.cymru), give a cwtsh, Welsh hat, miwsig, a gwledd (feast), Tippit pub game, daffodils, Welsh cakes (picau ar y maen), singing the anthem's second and third verses.
- Also running 2026: community social network Tŵt ran a six-week "Pethau Bychain" campaign ahead of its app launch on St David's Day (nation.cymru, snippet only, not read).
- No reach or engagement numbers were published in the pack (none found; not checked further). Treat this as a free piggy-back on a national hashtag, not a proven sales channel.

## Why it works for small heritage publishers (practice)
- A national hashtag day gives a small account discovery it cannot buy: the post is findable under the day's tag and can be reshared by the campaign's own channels if submitted early.
- The campaign explicitly wants "little things" that are kind and celebratory. Selling is off-tone; giving is on-tone. Lead with a free gift, keep any shop link secondary.
