# E-book pre-orders on free stores (Google Play Books, Kobo Writing Life)
Harvested 2026-10-26 (skills harvest). Internal how-to; nothing here is sent, listed or published.

## Why pre-orders, for a small author
A pre-order turns months of attention into one launch day: every order placed in advance is charged and counted on release day, so the book lands with a visible sales spike instead of a trickle. It also gives every post, talk and podcast mention before launch a real "buy" target rather than "coming soon".

## Google Play Books (Partner Center Help, "Enable pre-orders", read 26 Oct 2026)
- Set a future **release date up to 2 years ahead** and choose **Allow pre-orders: Yes** (Book info tab, or the bulk spreadsheet).
- **No book file is needed** to open a pre-order unless release is within 7 days. The file is **required 3 days before release**, or pre-orders **may be cancelled**.
- **Metadata and price lock 3 days before release.** Pre-orders cannot be created inside that window.
- If the price is changed after someone pre-orders, they pay the **lower** of the two prices on delivery.
- Buyers are charged on release day; **no sample is readable until release** (so the store description has to sell the book by itself).
- Sales reports use the **order date** (anticipation is measurable), but money appears in earnings reports only after delivery.
- Pre-orders open at midnight local time in each country; not available in India.

## Kobo Writing Life (Kobo's own blog and posts; the KWL help centre blocks this box, so these are from Kobo's published snippets)
- Kobo advises opening a pre-order "as soon as possible – ideally, 12 months in advance", and using the backlist to back it (back-matter links in earlier books pointing to the pre-order). Source: KWL blog "Five top tips for marketing your eBook and audiobook pre-orders".
- **Asset-less pre-orders:** KWL lets authors create a pre-order **without the e-book or audiobook file** (KWL blog, "now offers asset-less pre-orders").
- Kobo's own post: all pre-orders "count as sales on DAY ONE of your book's release", boosting visibility and ranking from the start (KWL Facebook page).
- A US author-services firm (Book Launchers) states that a Kobo pre-order counts **double** toward ranking. Not confirmed on any Kobo page we could read; do not repeat it as fact.
- Practitioner caution (r/selfpublish): for many wide authors all non-Amazon stores together are 5-15% of sales. Kobo pre-orders are a free extra shelf, not a launch engine by themselves.

## The routine
1. **Date it honestly:** choose a release date the text, cover and EPUB check (tools/epub_lint.py; epub-editions-sphinx-epubcheck.md) can meet with a week's margin. A missed Google deadline cancels orders, the worst possible first impression.
2. **Open the pre-order on Google Play and Kobo on the same day**, same price (at or above Kobo's UK £1.99 floor to keep 70%), same description.
3. **One link for readers:** a Books2Read universal link (free) that routes each reader to their own store, plus our own /ebooks/ page as the direct option (direct sale = higher margin; stores = discovery).
4. **Point everything at it:** back matter of the free first edition (ebook-back-matter-one-next-step.md), the welcome sequence, talks, podcast show notes, the anniversary calendar post nearest release.
5. **Release-day mail:** one email to the list: "It's out today" with the links. Pre-order buyers already have it; everyone else gets the day-one nudge.
6. **Measure:** Google's sales report by order date shows which promotion moved pre-orders; log each week's count in the project workspace

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Owner steps only the project lead can do (already listed in free-epub-storefronts-kobo-itch-books2read.md and google-play-books-partner-center-ebooks.md):** open the KWL account and the Google Play Partner Center account in his chosen publisher name with payout details. Google's account approval has taken ~18 days for one author, so a pre-order date needs that lead time too.
- **Zero spend:** both stores are free to list. Never Draft2Digital (activation fee). Kobo's paid promotions only with the project lead's explicit go-ahead.
- **Tab prepares:** EPUB, cover, description (en/cy/es), keywords and the Books2Read link in the project workspace; nothing goes live without the project lead's go-ahead.
