# Skill #3 — Website analysis, SEO & AI visibility (2026)
Written 26 Oct 2026 by the project lead's Tab agent.
## What practitioners report
- AI Overviews now sit above the blue links; Ahrefs' 2025 study found an AI Overview cut clicks to the top organic result by ~58% (321webmarketing / seoprofy 2026 summaries). Getting CITED in the overview or answer engine matters more than rank alone.
- What gets cited: pages with a clear one-line answer near the top, specific facts with sources and dates, a descriptive title and meta description, structured data (Book, Organization, FAQ), and crawlable text (not JS-only). Small sites win on specific long-tail questions they answer best.
- Hygiene practitioners repeat: no broken internal links on money pages, canonical tags, share cards (og:image) so links travel well on social and messaging, and a sitemap submitted in Search Console / IndexNow.
## How-to (Cedwyn)
1. Weekly: run tools/linkcheck.py and fix buy-path 404s first.
2. Every page: title ≤ 60–65 chars, meta description ≤ 155, canonical, og:title/og:description/og:image.
3. Research pages: open with one plain sentence answering "what is this" + date + source; this is what answer engines lift.
4. JSON-LD: Book (each e-book, with offers in GBP), Organization, WebSite; FAQ on /ebooks/.
5. Ask the answer engines (Grok, etc.) the fixed questions monthly and record whether Cedwyn is cited.
6. One committer at a time; patches go to the project workspace when the repo is busy.
