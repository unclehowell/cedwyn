# LoveReading / LoveReading4Kids (UK reader-review sites) — GATE: indie route is paid, rejected

Date: 2026-10-31 · Status: REJECTED for Cedwyn under the never-pay rule; one watch item

## Facts
- LoveReading (lovereading.co.uk, since 2005) is a UK consumer book-recommendation site; LoveReading4Kids is its children's sister site.
- **Indie route = paid.** "Get your self-published book reviewed by LoveReading ... From £120. Positive reviews listed free; negative feedback ..." (LoveReading's own page, search preview; the page itself returned a 522 timeout from this box today). Editors allocate the book to an Ambassador who writes the review.
- Practitioner write-up (Robert Prime, publishing.co.uk, 14 May 2026, updated 28 May, read): first-stage editorial review **£120**, turnaround **from 4 weeks**, **no ISBN needed to submit**, one needed only if accepted and listed. He discloses that he **co-owns the LoveReading network**, so his "best value" verdict is not neutral.
- Criticism thread: Absolute Write forum, "LoveReading UK - charging £120 for indie authors to be reviewed" (preview) — writers treat it as a paid-review service, i.e. the same class as Kirkus Indie and Readers' Favorite express reviews.
- Reader reviews on LoveReading4Kids need sign-up as a reviewer/Ambassador and are written about books already on the site (preview).

## Decision
- **Rejected:** the £120 review. Paying for a review breaks the never-pay rule, and a paid review is a weaker signal than a free one (see free-editorial-reviews-readers-favorite-gate-mbr-print-only.md, which covers the free Readers' Favorite tier).
- **No free indie door found.** Books reach LoveReading's editorial picks through publishers; a self-published digital-only title has no free submission route there (not checked beyond the pages above).
- **Children's angle:** LoveReading4Kids is out of scope unless a Cedwyn title is written for children; none is.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- Nothing to do now. Record LoveReading in the skip list so later runs don't re-research it.
- Watch item: if a print or trade edition ever exists through a publisher, the publisher (not Tab) can put it forward to LoveReading's editors at no cost to us. Until then, the free review routes are the Readers' Favorite free tier and reader reviews on the stores.
