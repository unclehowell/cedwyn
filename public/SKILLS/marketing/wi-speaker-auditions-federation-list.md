# WI speaker auditions: getting on a Federation speakers' list

Date: 2026-10-26 (skills harvest 16:37). Zero spend unless the owner agrees a fee or travel. Drafts only; nothing sent.

## What it is
Women's Institutes (WIs) book a speaker for most monthly meetings and print the year's programme in advance. Each county Federation keeps an approved speakers' list or yearbook, filled through **auditions**: applicants give a short version of the talk to an audience of WI members, who vote. Approved speakers go on the list that local WIs book from.

What Federation pages state (read in full on 26 Oct 2026):
- **Shropshire Federation:** audition system; "We plan to have auditions on average twice a year"; applicants not chosen are not contacted; max five talk titles; average WI size in Shropshire is 26 members, "many cannot afford high fees"; travel should reflect fuel costs; books may be sold only from the sales table with a 10% commission, agreed at booking; talks that look "purely a business opportunity for the speaker" may be excluded; once booked, honour the date.
- **Hampshire Federation:** five Speaker Selection Days a year (four daytime, one evening); members choose who goes on the HCFWI Speakers List; application details appear in Hampshire WI News a couple of months before each day.
- **Surrey Federation:** members "judge and vote" at auditions for the SFWI Yearbook; 2023 example ran morning and afternoon sessions at a village hall.
- **Staffordshire Federation:** Speakers' Book 2026-27 published for WIs to plan their year.

Practitioner testimony (pigpen.page, a traditionally published author, read in full): passed her audition in March 2023; the next list came out in 2024, yet "I received several bookings from representatives who had been there on the day"; she was paid a speaker fee and "I am yet to sell a book after an event". Lesson: the audition room itself is the first booking channel; book sales are a bonus, not the point.

## How to do it (steps)
1. **Find the Federation.** Our area is the Glamorgan Federation of WIs (glamorgan.thewi.org.uk; the site sat behind a browser check today, so its audition dates and form are not checked). Neighbouring: Gwent, Mid Glamorgan/other Welsh federations if they run separate lists (not checked).
2. **Get the application.** Usually a web form or a notice in the county WI newsletter. Fields seen (Shropshire): up to 5 talk titles, fee, travel expenses, availability, equipment, any WI bookings already made.
3. **Write the entry.** Short titles, a hook sentence each; avoid anything that reads as a sales pitch. One title should be a strong local story; a second can be a lighter "behind the records" talk.
4. **Audition piece.** Usually a few minutes (length varies by Federation, not checked for Glamorgan). Open with a story, one striking document image, end with a question for the room. No book plug in the audition.
5. **Fee and sales.** Set a modest fee or expenses only for small WIs. If selling print copies, agree the sales-table commission at booking. For e-books: a printed card with a QR code to the free edition, placed on the sales table.
6. **After each talk:** thank-you note; ask the WI secretary whether neighbouring WIs in the group would like the talk. Log to HubSpot.
