GIPHY brand channel: put archive GIFs and stickers into Instagram, WhatsApp and Messenger search

Date: 30 Oct 2026 · Skill harvest · Topic: Instagram/Facebook growth, heritage visuals, free reach

Why (read 30 Oct 2026)

  • GIPHY's search powers the GIF and sticker pickers in the big messaging apps. marketingexamples.com (Harry Dry, read) states: Facebook, Twitter and WhatsApp use the GIF API, while Instagram, TikTok and Snap use the sticker API (stickers have transparent backgrounds). He quotes GIPHY's own "7 billion served to 500 million people a day" (a vendor figure; don't repeat it).
  • Live heritage benchmarks (channel pages read 30 Oct 2026):
  • U.S. National Archives: 1.4K uploads, 9.6B GIF views
  • Smithsonian NMAAHC: 108 uploads, 342.5M views (32.3M in Cuseum's 2018 write-up)
  • Brooklyn Museum: 181 uploads, 76.6M views (1.4M from 48 uploads in 2018)
  • These are GIPHY's "views" (one per time GIPHY serves the GIF), not people and not clicks.
  • Harry Dry's small-brand example: Karla and Co., about 100,000 views a day from 16 stickers, by owning a phrase nobody else had tagged ("Ay Mama"). Low-competition tags beat crowded ones. (Single anecdote, not checked against GIPHY.)
  • Tate Britain's "1840s GIF Party" (Cuseum, 2018, read): 500+ public submissions, one GIF reblogged 100,000+ times, 25,000 new Tumblr followers, and 2,500 visitors to the Late at Tate event built around it.
  • Guessed UK handles came up with no live channel: nationalarchivesuk, britishlibrary, museumwales, amgueddfacymru, LlyfrgellGenedlaethol. That's a hint the Welsh heritage space is uncrowded, not proof (only those handles were tried).

The rules (GIPHY help pages, read via archived copies, 2025)

  1. The channel type matters. "Creator" is for original, non-commercial content. Anyone selling goods or services must apply as a Brand. Cedwyn sells e-books, so it's a Brand.
  2. What a Brand application needs: - an email on the company domain that matches the username - a working link to our own web domain (social profiles don't count) - a public social profile - a 250×250 avatar - at least 5 uploads first - one application per account
  3. Only accepted (upgraded) channels get their tagged GIFs into GIPHY search and the API. Harry Dry: "Only with a brand channel are your GIFs publicly searchable", and his non-original GIFs were rejected. Verification (the blue badge, @username search) is a separate, later step via GIPHY's contact form.
  4. Original content only. Use our own restored scans, movie-door frames and hologram tiles. Never other archives' footage (rights) and never a likeness of real family members (existing editorial rule).
  5. Tag for searches people really type that have little competition: Welsh words and place names (Llandough, Leckwith, Patagonia, Gŵyl y Glaniad, Dydd Gŵyl Dewi), period phrases, and record types ("1916 tenancy", "tithe map"). Check each tag by hand on giphy.com and prefer ones with almost no results.
  6. Branding inside the frame: a small "cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate" corner mark. GIPHY gives no link-back from inside the apps, so the watermark is the only route back. Keep text minimal.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Blocker only the project lead can clear: the Brand application needs an address on the bucklerfamily.estate domain that matches the username (e.g. a cedwyn@ alias) and acceptance of GIPHY's terms in his name. Log this in the project workspace as a decision, not a message, and don't create the account until he states GO (it's a new public channel, so it's a PROPOSAL per BRIEF/programme-office rules).
  • Prepare now, at zero cost:
  • Cut 8 loops of 2–4 s at 480 px from existing movie doors and restored scans with ffmpeg (already on the box; same pipeline as tools/email_video_thumb.py). Keep each under 2 MB.
  • Make 3 transparent-background stickers (WebP/GIF) of a Welsh word or date card for Instagram Stories.
  • Stage everything in the project workspace with a tags.csv (tag, giphy result count checked by hand, date).
  • Editorial rules: no banned words in tags or titles, never name the project lead, nothing about family holdings, no insignia.
  • Measure: GIPHY's channel analytics (views per GIF) monthly into the Sunday growth notes. Also watch site referrals and brand searches, since GIPHY views alone don't sell e-books.
  • Kill rule: if after 90 days the channel has under 50k views, or no change in branded search or /ebooks/ visits, stop uploading and leave it as it is.
  • Event hooks: stage a St David's Day (1 Mar) set and a Gŵyl y Glaniad (28 Jul) set ahead of time.
