Journalist query platforms: free tiers, measured hit rates, AI-flagged pitches (2026)

Harvested 28 Oct 2026 01:27Z (skills harvest). Extends press-requests-and-mention-monitoring.md (#JournoRequest on X + Google/Talkwalker Alerts) with the request platforms and their numbers. Zero spend.

What the evidence shows

  • Visibility Stack / Pushkar Sinha, "7 Journalist Query Platforms That Got Me Brand Mentions in 2026" (updated 1 Oct 2026, read): 1,000+ pitches in 12 months across 7 platforms -> 143 brand mentions (~14%), DR 20-93. Featured.com 18-20% approval (but they automated it, so the biggest sample); Qwoted ~6% approval with placements at DR 60-90; best single win a HubSpot (DR 93) quote via MentionMatch. Popular queries get hundreds of replies in the first hour and placements skew to fast responders. Their warning: most teams send ~100 pitches, win nothing and quit. Vertical = B2B SaaS, samples uneven (their own caveats).
  • Qwoted pricing page (qwoted.com, snippet read 28 Oct) + Community Guidelines: Free = 2 pitches/month with 2-hour delayed delivery; Pro $149/mo (35 pitches). Paid tiers excluded (zero spend).
  • Contentellect Qwoted review (read): since the Aug 2025 Pangram integration (Business Wire announcement), journalists see how much of each pitch was likely AI-written; Qwoted's help centre warns even light AI grammar edits can trigger it. Accounts are sometimes disabled without explanation (user reports). Winning pitches answer the question in the first two lines plus one detail only the source has.
  • SourceBottle (sourcebottle.com, read): free for sources to receive callouts (AU/NZ/UK strongest); paid "Expert Profile" $25/mo excluded. Medialyst comparison (vendor, not checked): free daily callouts with up to 10 keyword subscriptions.
  • ResponseSource Journalist Enquiry Service (responsesource.com, read): free for journalists to post, PRs pay per category (one category reported at "over £400 per year", Fleximize, not checked) -> excluded. Vuelio's own advice: be realistic, many responses get no reply, but relevant sources get filed for later features.

The routine (drafts only; press on the project lead's GO)

  1. Accounts are the project lead's decision: an expert profile is a named person speaking. Public text never names the project lead, so either the project lead approves being quoted under his own name, or the profile is "Cedwyn archive" (most journalists want a named human - expect lower acceptance). Log the choice before any sign-up.
  2. Free feeds only: SourceBottle (keyword alerts: Wales, Welsh history, heritage, farming, land registry, archives, local history, family history, Patagonia/Welsh Argentina), Qwoted Free (2 pitches/month - spend them only on UK/Wales history or land/property-records requests), plus the existing daily #JournoRequest search. Route alert mail to the Tab mailbox so runs can read it.
  3. Triage each request in under 5 minutes, 3 yes or skip: (a) we hold a dated record or image that answers it directly; (b) the deadline is > 3 h away and the request is < 1 h old (first-hour rule); (c) no named private living people, no accusations.
  4. Draft (<= 150 words, HubSpot draft task, never sent by us): line 1-2 answer the question; one dated fact with archive reference; offer scans; one deep link. Written in the source's own plain voice. Because Qwoted flags AI text, the final words on Qwoted must be the project lead's own (we supply the facts, he types or rewrites).
  5. Track: request URL, platform, sent y/n (the project lead), accepted, published URL, linked y/n. Expect ~5-15% acceptance; judge after 30 pitches, not 5.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Proposal for the project lead (one line in the next growth notes): "Free SourceBottle + Qwoted Free alerts for UK history/land-records requests; we draft, you send in your own words." Nothing is created until he states GO.
  • Best-fit request types: Welsh local history features, adverse possession / land registration explainers, family-history and archive stories, Welsh Patagonia anniversaries (28 July), Marconi/Lavernock and Peerless Jim Driscoll pieces.
  • Never use these platforms for e-book promotion copy; the quote earns a mention, the Cedwyn link (if given) does the selling.
