SSiW (SaySomethingin Welsh) learner community: forum, book club, graded readers (2026)

Harvested 2026-10-30 (skills harvest). This complements welsh-learners-audience-amdani-levels-learner-communities.md, which has no SSiW material. SSiW is the largest English-speaking adult Welsh-learner community with its own forum. Overseas learners use it too: the Sydney Welsh Society's Learn Welsh page links only to SSiW.

What the readers ask for (threads read)

  • "The SSiW Welsh Book Club" (topic 13975): opened 2018, 740 posts, 49,106 views, last post 12 Oct 2026. It is the forum's single standing thread for Welsh books at every level. Members review titles with photos and page samples.
  • "Graded reader recommendations" (Jan 2025, 425 views):
  • A learner finishing Level 1 found Olly Richards' Short Stories in Welsh too hard ("having to check a lot of words").
  • The replies recommend books with vocabulary on the same page (Stryd y Bont, Yn Ei Gwsg, Bywyd Blodwen Jones, C'mon Reff!) and the Amdani colour levels: yellow = Mynediad/Entry, green = Sylfaen/Foundation, blue = Canolradd/Intermediate.
  • One reply posts a photo of a first page "to help judge the level". That is the format that wins: show a sample page.
  • "Books for beginners" (2019 to May 2026, 2,992 views): learners want to see spelling of what they hear. Replies point to Gomer's "Fiction for Welsh learners" and to the vocabulary lists at the end of each SSiW lesson.

Playbook (from the threads + forum FAQ)

  1. Join the existing thread; don't start a promo topic. The forum FAQ (standard Discourse "civilised place" guidelines) asks members to browse first and "improve the discussion". In the Book Club, a sample page plus a level label is a contribution; a bare link is spam.
  2. Level label + on-page geirfa (word list) + first-page sample. These three things decided every recommendation above.
  3. Pair audio with text. SSiW is audio-first, and learners ask for spelling. A scene with its Welsh transcript and a slow-read option fits that gap exactly.
  4. Respond to the lull, don't fake activity. 25 posting users a week means one honest, useful post is visible for days. Never post twice.
  5. Measure: utm_source=ssiw-forum&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=learner-reading. Our stop rule (not an industry figure): if one post brings fewer than 15 visits in 30 days, don't post again.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Build first: a free Foundation-level (green) page of one documentary scene, with:
  • the Welsh transcript;
  • an on-page geirfa of about 15 words;
  • a level badge;
  • a link to the free v0.0.1 scene. This matches the level badge and geirfa proposal already passed to the documentary player owner.
  • Then, on the owner's GO only: one Book Club reply from an account the owner controls, never from Tab and never under the owner's name in public text. It shows a sample-page image, the level and the free link, in neutral third person and with no banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken). Draft it as a HubSpot task; Tab posts nothing.
  • Product hint for the e-books: a "learner edition" with vocabulary on each page beats a plain Welsh translation. This is the forum's most repeated request, and it is logged as a PROPOSAL for the e-book pipeline (QUEUE-after-framing), not a decision.
  • Skip: the Speaking Practice and Slack groups (practice spaces, off-topic for us); the US/Canada sub-forum (29 topics, too quiet).
