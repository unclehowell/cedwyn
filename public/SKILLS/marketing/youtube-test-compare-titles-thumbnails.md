# YouTube "Test & compare": A/B testing titles and thumbnails
Harvested 2026-10-26 (skills harvest). Internal how-to; nothing here is sent or posted.

## What YouTube itself documents (YouTube Help, "A/B test titles & thumbnails", read 26 Oct 2026)
- Test up to **3 titles, 3 thumbnails, or 3 title+thumbnail pairs** per video, in YouTube Studio **on a computer only**.
- Eligibility: **advanced features** must be on (phone verification: an owner step). Not available for **Shorts**, scheduled Lives or Premieres (a Premiere becomes eligible once it converts to a normal video); not for made-for-kids, mature or private videos.
- The winner is chosen on **watch time share**, not click-through rate: YouTube optimises for "overall watch time over other metrics, like click-through rate", so a thumbnail that oversells and gets clicked-then-abandoned loses.
- A test runs **a few days up to 2 weeks**. Results: Winner / Performed same / Inconclusive. With no clear winner, the **first** option uploaded stays, so upload your preferred version first.
- **Changing the title or thumbnail mid-test stops the test.** A small control group sees only the default.
- Thumbnails below 1280x720 force every option down to 854x480: always upload 1280x720 or larger.
- YouTube recommends testing **older videos first** and making options **clearly different**: near-identical options run longer and end Inconclusive.

## Practitioner testimony
- **Jamie Whiffen** (UK YouTube strategist; clients incl. Ali Abdaal): make **three genuinely different concepts** per video, then refine the winning concept with small variations (the MrBeast method he cites); one client video reached 900k+ views after a concept round then a variation round. He re-tests winners weeks or months later because early traffic sources skew results, and warns against testing seasonal videos out of season.
- A creator on r/PartneredYoutube (Jul 2025, 6 months of use; search snippet only, Reddit blocks this box): A/B testing improved the thumbnail on about **3 of every 4 videos**, with a **3-7% CTR lift** on the better ones. Treat as one creator's experience.
- Creators repeatedly note the tool reports watch-time share, not CTR (r/youtubers thread). Read Reach > "How your A/B test is going" for impressions and CTR alongside it.

## Small-channel reality
- The test needs impressions. YouTube's own FAQ: too few impressions is a main reason for "no Winner". On a channel with a few hundred views per video expect many Inconclusive results; that is still information (the options are equivalent, so keep the clearer, more honest one).
- Run **one test per video at a time**, and do not stack it with a title edit, a pinned-comment change or a new end screen in the same fortnight.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
1. **Which videos:** the long-form Cedwyn movie "door" clips (~2 min, 720p) and any walk or archive videos on the channel. Shorts are excluded by YouTube.
2. **Three concepts, not three colours.** For a door clip, for example:
   - A: archive-document close-up (a 1974 newspaper column) with a 3-4 word overlay, e.g. "Held since 1667".
   - B: the place: Tŷ Mawr's site in the landscape today, overlay "Demolished, 6 Dec 1988".
   - C: a face/character frame from the scene (only our generated cast; no likeness of real family members without the project lead's photos and approval).
   Upload the one we would choose anyway first (it stays if the test is Inconclusive).
4. **Thumbnails:** 1280x720 PNG/JPEG, made by the movie or quote-pack workers from frames we already own (no new renders needed). Keep text to 4 words; check legibility at phone size.
5. **Record each test** in the project workspace: video, the three options, result, impressions, CTR, and watch-time share. After 5 tests, write down which concept family wins (document / place / character) and default new uploads to it.
6. **Owner step for the project lead:** turn on advanced features in YouTube Studio if not yet on (Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility). Starting a test changes how a public video looks, so the first test waits for his go-ahead; after that it can be routine.
7. **Pair with chapters/captions** (youtube-chapters-captions-transcripts-archive.md): the test raises clicks, chapters and captions keep the watch time that decides the winner.
