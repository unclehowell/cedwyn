## What the sources say

**Welsh Language Commissioner, "Using the Welsh language on social media: a practical guide for businesses and charities"** (PDF, read 30 Oct 2026). It is practitioner testimony.
- **Jones Crisps:** "when people run through their feed, very often, Welsh language content gets more hits than English language content."
- **Save the Children Wales:** chose **one bilingual account** rather than two. "All messages would be bilingual and questions would be answered in the language in which they were asked." "We intentionally don't post the same message in both languages – we try to translate the meaning rather than the words. We have been praised for doing this." They limited themselves to two platforms because writing good bilingual posts takes time.
- **Galeri Caernarfon:** "The Welsh language comes first in any post or tweet, and subsequent conversations are held in the user's preferred language."
- **Artist Rhiannon Roberts:** "the business is growing quicker as I can expand my audience through bilingual marketing."
- **Commissioner's supermarket research:** 83% agree that using Welsh shows respect for Welsh culture, and 74% agree it shows support for the local community.
- **Guide's advice:**
  - One feed or two are both fine, provided both languages get equal status.
  - Use Welsh-specific hashtags in Welsh posts alongside international ones.
  - Images and GIFs should be bilingual or carry no text.
  - YouTube's free subtitles take several languages.

**S4C** (press release 12 Jul 2023, read):
- Weekly viewers in Wales were up 8%, the highest in five years.
- Viewers aged 16–44 were at their highest level in ten years.
- Hours watched on YouTube "almost doubled within a year".
- Moving onto digital platforms is what brought younger viewers.

What the evidence supports: Welsh-language viewing is moving onto YouTube and social, and practitioners report Welsh posts standing out in feeds. None of the sources publishes a controlled Welsh-vs-English engagement test. We have to run our own (step 5).

## The playbook

1. **One feed, Welsh first.** Keep one account per platform. Welsh goes in the first line, which is the part shown before "more". English follows as a meaning-for-meaning rewrite, not a gloss.
2. **Lead with what is already Welsh.** Clips whose audio is in Welsh get a Welsh caption first and burnt-in English subtitles, since autoplay is sound-off. Never put an English voice-over on Welsh dialogue.
3. **Reply in the asker's language.** A comment in Welsh gets a Welsh reply, one in English gets English. Questions we can't answer properly in Welsh go to Helo Blod advice (free) before we post. No machine Welsh in public replies.
4. **Hashtags: one Welsh, one place, one broad.** For example a Welsh history tag, a place tag and a broad history tag. Check each tag's live use before adopting it; dead tags are noise.
5. **Test it ourselves.** Alternate Welsh-first and English-first captions on comparable posts, at least 6 of each, same time slot, same clip type. Measure:
   - reach;
   - 3-second hold (or Skip Rate on Instagram);
   - shares;
   - UTM visits (utm_content=cy-first / en-first).

   Keep Welsh-first as the default unless English-first beats it by more than 25% on visits across the set. Equal results still favour Welsh, because of the respect and loyalty findings above.
6. **Limit the platforms.** Do two platforms well, as Save the Children did, before adding more. Bilingual posts take twice the care.
7. **Measure the audience, not just the posts.** Check whether commenters and followers include Welsh speakers (the language they comment in is a fair proxy). Log it monthly.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn

- **Natural fit:** the documentary's pre-1970 scenes have Welsh audio with cy/en/es subtitles. The 6/15/30-second teaser cutdowns from the free v0.0.1 scenes (see the teaser-cutdowns skill) go out Welsh caption first, with burnt-in English subtitles. The characters speak only to each other, as already required.
- **Channels:** Facebook "Great House Farm II" and Instagram @cedwynestate, through the existing auto-social flywheel. These are the two platforms. Add YouTube Shorts only once the A/B result is in.
- **Caption shape:**
  - Line 1: Welsh, place and date first ("Llandochau, tua 200 CC").
  - Line 2: English, the same meaning.
  - Then the link to /documentary/ with UTM tags.
- **Translation:** human-checked Welsh only, through the monthly Helo Blod batch. Machine drafts are fine internally but never published unchecked.
- **Test owner:** the auto-social flywheel logs `cy-first` / `en-first` in social-log.md. The Sunday growth report reads the result after 12 posts.
- No new accounts, no DMs and zero spend. Public text follows the editorial rules (no banned words, never names the owner).
