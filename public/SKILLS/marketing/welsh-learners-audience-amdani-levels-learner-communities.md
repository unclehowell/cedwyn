# Welsh learners as an audience: level-tagged reading, Amdani, learner communities (2026)

Topic: reach adults learning Welsh (a real, growing, reading-hungry audience) with Cedwyn's Welsh-first documentary scenes and e-books, without spend or cold contact.

## What the primary sources say (with numbers)
- National Centre for Learning Welsh, 2024-25 statistics (read 30 Oct 2026): 20,445 unique learners on Centre-run courses (+12% on 2023-24, +61% on 2017-18, highest since publication); 34,960 learning activities (+12%); 47% (10,040) learn in the community face-to-face or online, 39% (8,370) via workplace programmes; 4,110 learners aged 16-24 (+56% year on year).
- Duolingo 2025 Language Report (read): among its most committed learners, Welsh is the #4 language worldwide after Spanish, English and Italian ("less-commonly-studied languages with dedicated followings"). Duolingo publishes no Welsh learner count, so treat this as rank only.
- Books Council of Wales, Amdani page (read): a reading series written for adult Welsh learners, graded to the Centre's four levels: Entry, Foundation, Intermediate, Advanced. Each book uses that level's vocabulary and forms, adds illustrations, and lists the harder words in English.
- Learn Welsh, Amdani Festival of Reading (read): a virtual festival held each March since 2021 (2026: 2-6 March). In 2024 it added a national book club and 150+ learners signed up to discuss the series with its authors.
- Learner communities (snippets, 30 Oct 2026, not checked further): Lingo Newydd (Golwg) is a monthly print/digital magazine with level-graded articles, and tutors recommend it to classes (r/learnwelsh, SSiW forum). Sgwrs Dysgwyr Cymraeg Discord has ~1,590 members. There are Facebook groups for Duolingo Welsh learners and for "Welsh Learners and Speakers of North America". In Cardiff, Learn Welsh Cardiff and Menter Caerdydd run informal reading groups (Grŵp Darllen Cant a Mil, a council Clwb Llyfrau Cymraeg). The University of Rio Grande's Madog Center (Ohio) runs a monthly Zoom Clwb Darllen i Ddysgwyr.

## The playbook
1. **Tag content by level.** Learners filter by level first. Label each Welsh-language item Mynediad/Entry, Sylfaen/Foundation, Canolradd/Intermediate or Uwch/Advanced, judged by sentence length and vocabulary. Don't call a text "for learners" without a level.
2. **Give the Amdani-style supports:** a short geirfa (glossary) of 10-20 harder words in English per piece, toggleable cy/en subtitles, and a slow audio version where one exists. These supports are the product for learners.
3. **Make one free sample per level** (one short scene transcript or one page). It earns links and shares from tutors and forums. Paid e-books stay paid.
4. **Join through the community's own channels.** Answer learner questions in r/learnwelsh or the SSiW forum only where a Cedwyn transcript genuinely answers one, once, follow each venue's self-promotion rules, and prefer the free sample link. Never cold-DM learners.
5. **Use the calendar.** List or pitch a learner-friendly piece ahead of the Amdani Festival (early March), and alongside Dydd Miwsig Cymru (Feb) / St David's Day (1 Mar). Pitches to Lingo Newydd or Menter Caerdydd go out as drafts on the owner's GO only.
6. **Measure:** add `utm_source=<venue>&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=learners-<level>`. If learner-tagged pages show under 1% click-through to /ebooks/ after 30 days, stop investing in this channel. Our threshold, not a benchmark.

## Pitfalls
- Machine-translated Welsh shown to learners loses trust fast. Have Welsh lines checked (see helo-blod-free-welsh-translation-trilingual-copy.md) before tagging them for learners.
- Don't label historic or dialect Welsh (period quotations) as Entry level. Mark it "original wording", with a modern gloss.
- Amdani is the Books Council's series name, so don't use it as our label. Say "for Welsh learners, Foundation level".

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- The documentary scenes already ship Welsh audio with cy/en/es subtitles. Add a level badge and a 15-word geirfa under each scene card on /documentary/, plus a "Darllen yn araf / slow transcript" link to the Welsh subtitle text. Small template change; give it to the documentary player owner as a PROPOSAL.
- Make one free "Llandough mewn 10 gair" (Llandough in ten words) Foundation-level page from existing site text, linked from each Welsh scene and from /ebooks/.
- Draft (HubSpot only, no send) a short note offering Lingo Newydd the free Foundation-level piece, and an event listing for the Rio Grande Madog Center Zoom club if an online talk happens. Both wait for the owner's GO. Never name the owner publicly, and keep public text free of the banned words list.
- Track learner UTMs in the Sunday growth report.
