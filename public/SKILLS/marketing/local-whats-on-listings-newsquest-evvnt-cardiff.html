Local what's-on listings for a talk or event (Penarth Times/Newsquest via Evvnt, What's On In Cardiff, Visit Wales)

Written 26 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 16:07) by the Tab agent for the Cedwyn e-book work. Zero spend: free tiers only.

What it is

Free listings in local event calendars put a talk, exhibition or walk in front of people searching "what's on in Penarth/Cardiff/Vale" and, for the newspaper calendars, sometimes in print. Each listing is a dated, local page that links to the event page and (through it) to /ebooks/.

Facts read at the source (26 Oct 2026)

  • Penarth Times (Newsquest) "Submit an event": free listing in its local events calendar; events "go live on the day they are added and will likely appear in the newspaper the week of the event"; print is guaranteed only with paid credits. An image is required before starting; preview before publishing. Route: What's On > Local Events > "promote an event" (penarthtimes.co.uk).
  • The calendar runs on Evvnt. Evvnt's own support page: the free service publishes the event on ONE listing site and sends a report with the live link; syndication to its full network is the paid premium tier (excluded). Unlimited free submissions.
  • Newsquest sister titles share the calendar platform, so the same free route exists on other Newsquest titles (e.g. Barry & District News) - check each title's own Local Events page; the free tier still lists on one site per submission.
  • What's On In Cardiff (whatsonincardiff.net): free form; its own tips: 400-700 word description helps search, full venue address for Google Maps, allow 2 business days for review, short-notice events may not be listed.
  • Visit Wales: events go on visitwales.com through its industry Event Data Submission form and must meet Visit Wales' event criteria (criteria page not read in full this run - check before submitting; likely suits larger public events, so low fit for one talk).
  • No practitioner case study with numbers was found for local calendar listings this run. Treat results as unknown and measure them with UTM tags.

How to do it

  1. Only list a real, dated, public event (a library talk, an Open Doors event, an online talk). Never list the e-books themselves as an "event".
  2. Prepare once: 1200x675 image (no insignia), a 400-700 word neutral description, venue full address + postcode (or "Online"), organiser = the business name, a contact email on our domain, and the event link with ?utm_source=<site>&utm_medium=listing&utm_campaign=<event>.
  3. Submit 3-6 weeks ahead: Penarth Times (free tier, one site), Barry & District News if relevant, What's On In Cardiff, the venue's own what's-on page (library/hall), and Eventbrite (see free-online-talk-listings-eventbrite-meetup.md).
  4. Decline every paid upsell (featured credits, premium syndication). Record each listing URL in the project workspace the day it goes live.
  5. After the event: count registrations/visits by utm_source; keep only the calendars that sent people.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Accounts on Newsquest/Evvnt and What's On In Cardiff are owner decisions (business email, organiser name). Until the GO: draft text in the project workspace and a HubSpot task; submit nothing.
  • No site event at Tŷ Mawr itself without landowner consent.
