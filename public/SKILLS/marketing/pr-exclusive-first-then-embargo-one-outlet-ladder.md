# Exclusive first, then embargoed wide release: placing one story with one outlet

Read 27 Oct 2026. For small publishers with one real piece of news at a time: offer it to ONE best-fit journalist as an exclusive, with a deadline; if they pass, offer the next; only then send it wide under an embargo.

## The evidence (primary sources, read today)
- **Muck Rack, State of Journalism 2026** (1,044 journalists surveyed 30 Jan-2 Mar 2026; PDF):
  - What a pitch should offer: clear relevance to my beat 70%; interview access to relevant sources 58%; original data or research 40%; high-resolution images 37%; **exclusive or embargoed content 33%**; pre-written quotes 14%; social copy 3%.
  - Instant delete: misses their beat 88%; overly promotional 71%; looks like a mass email 50%; repeated follow-ups with no reply 46%; wrong name or outlet 35%; too long/unclear 40%; no source access 26%.
  - 86% say at least some of their stories start with a PR pitch (51% say 1-10% of stories; 34% say more than 10%).
- **Cision, 2025 State of the Media** (3,000+ journalists): 45% want embargoed or early-access information in a pitch, 47% want compelling data; 86% reject a pitch that is not relevant to their audience or beat; 84% say the best way to start a relationship is a short introductory email, even with no story. Newsletter journalists: ideal pitch 100-200 words; 62% want data.
- **Practitioner rules (Jaffe PR, PR Newswire guidance):** an exclusive = one outlet gets the story first, at an agreed time; spell out whether "exclusive" means that outlet only, or first-only (you may share with others once they publish). An embargo = many outlets get it early, all publish at the same time, on trust. Offering routine news as "exclusive" teaches journalists to delete you.
- Cross-check in our own index: `email-subject-lines-preview-text-mpp.md` has the pitch-email subject data (offering an exclusive: 54.16% vs 37.16% opens; small sample, not checked by us).

## How to do it
1. **Is it news?** Something new, dated and checkable: a first release, a record newly found, a premiere, a figure nobody had. If not, it's a listing or a feature idea, not an exclusive.
2. **Rank 3 outlets by fit,** not size: who covered this exact beat in the last 90 days (read their last 5 stories). Name the journalist, not the desk.
3. **Exclusive offer email** (100-200 words): one-line news hook; why their readers; what they get first (interview, images, the data or record, early access link); the exclusive terms in one sentence ("Yours first; we'll share wider after you publish"); the answer deadline (e.g. 48 h) and the publish time you need. No attachments over a few MB; a private press-kit link instead.
4. **No answer by the deadline = a pass.** One polite follow-up only (46% delete repeat chasers). Then offer outlet #2 with a fresh deadline.
5. **Wide release under embargo:** same kit to the rest of the beat list, "EMBARGOED until 00:01 BST <date>" in the subject and first line, identical time zone everywhere. Expect someone may break it; never send anything you can't afford to see early.
6. **After it runs:** link the coverage on the press page, thank the journalist, log who answered, how fast, and what ran (that list is next time's ranking).

## How we apply it to Cedwyn (PROPOSED; press only on the project lead's GO, nothing sent)
- Every press contact needs the project lead's GO first, and nothing goes to anyone with an open thread. This skill only shapes what we prepare.
- **Candidate stories** (dated and checkable, framed as concerns not findings, never naming the project lead): the bilingual/trilingual film scenes on /movie/ (Welsh, English and Spanish audio); the restored Patagonia NFT collection; e-book first editions free to read.
- **Fit-ranked outlet ladders (draft):**
  - Welsh-language angle: Golwg360 or a papur bro first; then BBC Cymru Fyw (see `welsh-language-media-papur-bro-golwg360-heno.md`, `bbc-wales-cymru-fyw-story-routes.md`).
  - Patagonia angle: Chubut press first (`chubut-spanish-press-jornada-elchubut-radio.md`).
  - Data angle: the dataset pitch in `data-led-digital-pr-heritage-dataset-pitch.md`.
- **Kit ready before any offer:** online press kit page (`online-press-kit-page-heritage-ebooks.md`) with 3 hi-res images (37% want them), a spokesperson line offering an interview (58%), and one checkable number or record (40%).
- Draft each offer as a HubSpot task in the usual hive format: one outlet per task, deadline in the body, signed changelog. Never a Gmail draft, never sent by us.
- Log: date offered, outlet, answer time, ran yes/no, referral visits to /ebooks/ (UTM `utm_source=<outlet>&utm_medium=press`).
