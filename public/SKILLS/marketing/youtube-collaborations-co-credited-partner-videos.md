# YouTube Collaborations: co-credited videos with partner channels

Date: 2026-10-26 (skills harvest 23:24). Status for Cedwyn: PROPOSAL. Inviting another channel means contacting an outside party, so each invitation needs the owner's OK.

## What the evidence shows
- **YouTube Help, "Invite collaborators to your video" (read 26 Oct 2026):**
  - You can invite **up to 10 creators** onto one video. It works for long-form videos and Shorts, and for an archived live stream, but not a stream that is live now.
  - Once a collaborator accepts, **each channel's name and its own Subscribe button** appear on desktop, mobile and TV.
  - YouTube's stated goal is to "recommend the video to the audiences of both the collaborators and the channel where the video was posted", with the caveat that "you may not notice changes in search or recommendations at first".
  - **Revenue is not split.** The uploading channel keeps all earned revenue.
  - Where it is: YouTube Studio > Upload > Details > Show more > Collaboration > Invite a collaborator.
  - A separate "paid promotion" brand tag links the video to a brand partner's Google Ads account. That does not apply to us.
- **Older figures:** practitioner write-ups from 2025 (revelator.com, scalelab.com, snippets and read) cite "up to five" channels. The Help page now states 10. Follow the Help page.
- **Practitioner evidence on collab payoff (scalelab.com, read):**
  - Fit beats size: audience overlap and a similar creative level predict results better than subscriber count.
  - Start from YouTube Studio > Audience > "Other channels your viewers watch".
  - Case: a new channel ("Solo Club") gained **399 subscribers in 24 hours** from one 7-second mention at the end of another creator's video (not checked independently).
  - Put rights, credit and the posting schedule in writing before filming.
- **Counter-evidence:** a 2019 r/PartneredYoutube poster said a collab with a 500k-subscriber channel brought "only 600 subs" (search snippet; Reddit blocked the page, so **not checked**). Big-partner collabs are not a guaranteed win; a matched audience matters more.

## How to do it (steps)
1. **Shortlist 5 channels.** Use "Other channels your viewers watch" once the channel has enough views to show it. Before that, use a manual list: Welsh local-history, Glamorgan heritage, archive-film, Welsh-language and Patagonia-Welsh channels with **similar size and a recent upload**.
2. **Pick a format where both channels add something.** A shared-source episode: their footage or knowledge plus our archive records, one topic, 6-10 minutes, plus one Short cut from it.
3. **Agree in writing** (an email is enough) who uploads, the credit line, who may re-cut Shorts, and when it goes live. Revenue stays with the uploader, so whoever uploads should be the one who benefits less from the other's audience.
4. **Upload with the Collaboration invite.** The partner accepts in Studio. Check that both Subscribe buttons show on mobile before announcing it.
5. **Cross-promote:** a Community post on both channels, a pinned comment from each side, and an end screen (youtube-end-screens-related-video-playlists.md) pointing at each channel's best next video.
6. **Measure for 28 days:** subscribers gained from this video (Studio > Advanced > Subscribers by video), the share of views from "Browse" and "Suggested", and new returning viewers. Keep the format only if it beats our median video on subscribers per 1,000 views.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Raw material we already have:** the daily Cedwyn movie scenes (the project workspace) and the archive-film decision file (youtube-premieres-archive-films-decision.md) give short, finished clips a partner can react to or build on.
- **Natural partners** (to verify, do not contact yet):
  - societies we already target for talks: Cowbridge, Penarth, Vale u3a (see the talk-route skills), many of which post lecture recordings;
  - Welsh-Patagonia cultural channels for a Spanish/Welsh co-upload (welsh-patagonian-societies-chubut-outreach.md);
  - People's Collection Wales contributors.
- **Editorial rules travel with the video:** no banned words, the owner's name never appears, nothing about family holdings, no accusations about named people. The partner sees our rules before filming.
- **Links:** YouTube descriptions may link to /ebooks/. Cedwyn pages still carry no outbound links, so the video lives on Cedwyn only as a self-hosted clip or plain text, never as an embedded YouTube player.
- **Gate:** first draft an outreach brief for one partner as a HubSpot draft task (never sent). The owner states GO before any message goes out. Log the result in the project workspace
