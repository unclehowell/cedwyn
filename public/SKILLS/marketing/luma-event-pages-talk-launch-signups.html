Luma (luma.com) event pages for a talk or launch: what actually drives sign-ups

Harvested 30 Oct 2026. Research only. No account was made and nothing was sent.

What Luma documents (help pages, read today)

  • Registrations are unlimited on every plan. Luma limits sends: the invites and newsletters you push out. A new calendar gets 15 sends a week. The free plan rises to 500 a week after an approved "unlock sends" request, which is also required before you can send newsletters at all. Luma Plus gives 5,000 a week.
  • If a newsletter's list is bigger than the sends you have left, it fails completely; Luma won't send to part of the list. Adding a guest directly also counts as a send. Messages to people already registered are unlimited.
  • Online events never appear on Luma's city pages or city search. They show up only through the app's "Virtual Events" filter.
  • Featuring: Luma's team picks events for city pages in 50+ cities. They only consider public, in-person events at a specific venue. Registration must stay on Luma. Other criteria: a 1:1 cover image, host profiles, a clear description, usually at least 5 guests already registered, and good invite acceptance. You can't submit an event for featuring; Luma picks on its own. Luma states some hosts got "over 5x" more registrations after being featured (Luma's own claim, no data; not checked). Tech, wellness and nightlife are favoured, and niche topics are less likely.
  • London has a city page (luma.com). I didn't confirm whether there is a Cardiff page (not checked).

What this means

  • For an online heritage talk, Luma is a tidy free registration page with a calendar feed. It won't bring discovery. Every sign-up comes from our own list and posts, just as with the Eventbrite route (see free-online-talk-listings-eventbrite-meetup.md, which notes Eventbrite does have discovery for online talks).
  • For an in-person talk, Luma's curation could help only in a featured city. Local-history talks are "niche", so expect nothing from it.
  • The send limits matter more than the page. Until the calendar is unlocked, you can only invite 15 people a week.

How to do it (if used)

  1. Create a free calendar, unlock sends on day 1, and build a 1:1 cover image.
  2. Write a description a stranger can follow: what, when, who is hosting (with a profile) and what they get.
  3. Get 5+ of our own people registered before posting widely.
  4. Put a UTM link on our posts pointing to the Luma page. Compare registrations and attendance against Eventbrite or our own /talks/ page.
  5. Send the reminder to people already registered (unlimited) about 24 h and 1 h before.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Verdict: secondary, not primary. For the online v0.0.2 launch talk, keep our own page plus add-to-calendar (online-talk-add-to-calendar-ics-show-up-rate.md) as the main route. Luma is optional, because it gives us no discovery for online events.
  • Worth a test only for a future in-person guided-tour preview in Cardiff, using public meeting points only (the Peerless Jim and Marconi statues). Even then, featuring is unlikely because Cardiff may have no city page.
  • Account: the project lead's own account if he chooses (owner step). No Luma Plus (spend), and no emails to lists without his GO. Outreach is on hold.
  • Measure: registrations, show-up rate, and e-book clicks from the talk page. Drop Luma if it brings fewer than 10% of registrations versus our own page after 2 events.
