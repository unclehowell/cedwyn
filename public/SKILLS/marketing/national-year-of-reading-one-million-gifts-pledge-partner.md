# National Year of Reading 2026: "One Million Gifts of Reading" (17 Nov to early Dec) and the Pledge Partner list

Harvested 2026-10-29 (skills harvest c3d2ef5b). Sources read on the day unless marked.

## What it is (facts, with sources)
- 2026 is the UK-wide National Year of Reading, brand "Go All In" ("If you're into it, read into it"). The National Literacy Trust is lead delivery partner (literacytrust.org.uk blog, 1 Jan 2026, read). It counts reading "in all its forms", **e-books named explicitly** (same blog; gov.wales page, read).
- **Wales runs it too.** Welsh Government page "National Year of Reading in Wales" (read) invites "publishers, cultural bodies, and community partners". It lists the Books Council of Wales, Libraries Wales, NLT Wales, Adnodd, BookTrust Cymru, CILIP and The Reading Agency. Events can go on the **Hwb events calendar**: [address kept in the project workspace] for education events, the Libraries Wales contact on that page for library events. Books Council of Wales takes ideas from organisations at [address kept in the project workspace] (books.wales page, read).
- **The year ends on "One Million Gifts of Reading", launching 17 Nov 2026** and running "into early December" (goallin.org.uk and The Reading Agency's key-moments page, both read). There are three ways to take part, and individuals and organisations of any size are welcome:
  1. **Give recommendations**: favourite reads turned into shared conversations.
  2. **Gift access**: give books, magazines, audiobooks, comics "and more".
  3. **Give time**: reading volunteering.
  The campaign toolkit PDF has logos, **printable gift tags**, key visuals and ready-made copy.
- **Pledge Partners** (goallin.org.uk, read): **1,022 partners, 30 million "anticipated reach", 48,997 pledged volunteers**. These are the organiser's own figures. The pledge form is free and for UK-based organisations, with a Welsh-language version. The NYR team reviews pledges. Accepted organisations are listed on the partners wall and contacted during and at the end of 2026. Small presses and individual authors are on the wall (e.g. "Afrori Books", "Amy Redfern (children's author)", "Austin Macauley Publishers").
- Hashtags in use: #GoAllIn2026 (NLT LinkedIn post) and #NationalYearOfReading2026 (NLT Instagram). Both come from search snippets and were not opened.

## Why it matters for a tiny e-book shop
- It is a **free, national, non-commercial gifting moment that lands in Christmas gift season**, with a toolkit we can use without asking anyone. Its "gift access" lane takes e-books, so a free Cedwyn sampler counts as a genuine "gift of reading".
- A Pledge Partner listing is a dated, public link from a national campaign site. That helps authority, but **only** if the pledge describes real activity.
- What it cannot do: it is not a sales channel. NLT copy is about reading for pleasure, so overt selling under the brand will read wrong. Treat it as reach and goodwill. It has no measured conversion lift for small publishers; I found none published.

## How to do it (practitioner steps)
1. Download the One Million Gifts toolkit PDF and read its brand-use rules **before** using the logo or gift-tag art. Use only what the toolkit permits.
2. Pick one lane. "Gift recommendations" is the zero-risk one: a list of reads with a reason for each.
3. Post on 17 Nov or the first days after, using the campaign wording ("a gift of reading") and the hashtags. Add one clear free action and make no price claim in that post.
4. If you pledge: the form asks for your plans, so describe specific, dated activity you will actually do. Use the same email for every pledge. NLT follows up at year end, so keep evidence such as post URLs and counts.
5. Measure with a dedicated ref/UTM and a kill rule.
