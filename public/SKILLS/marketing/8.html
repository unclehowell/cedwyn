Skill #8 — Social media content & publishing (2026), owned channels: FB page Great House Farm II, Instagram, YouTube

Written 26 Oct 2026.

Practitioner testimony

  • History niches grow fastest on Facebook with share-worthy, story-led posts and replying to EVERY comment; a publisher network reports 0→40k followers in 2 months for an Ancient Egypt page (with a $3/day boost) and states comment replies multiplied engagement (Ezoic live case study, 2024).
  • Newsletter growers report growth when content appears where people already are, each piece pointing to one destination (The Introverted Networker, 2025).
  • Our own data: best post so far is the c.1880 armour image (IG 84 views); a reel reached 101 on IG. Image/record posts beat text-only.

How-to

  1. One post per channel per day, built from ONE record fact (assets/facts-10.md) + the matching Cedwyn page (UTM: utm_source=facebook|instagram, utm_medium=social, utm_campaign=bizdev-YYYYMMDD, utm_content=).
  2. Lead with the most striking line; date and source in the post; no banned words; never name the family member who runs it; no accusations.
  3. Reply to every comment within the day; pin the best post weekly.
  4. Log every post to the project workspace with its URL and UTM.
  5. Instagram links are not clickable in captions: say "link in bio" and keep the bio link on /ebooks/ (the project lead sets the bio).
