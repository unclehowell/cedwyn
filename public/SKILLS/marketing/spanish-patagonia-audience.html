Spanish-language and Welsh Patagonia audience building (2026 how-to)

Goal: reach the Welsh-Argentine community of Chubut (Trelew, Gaiman, Rawson, Puerto Madryn, Trevelin/Esquel) and Spanish-speaking history readers. Do it as an institution, in Spanish first, through public channels and groups only. No DMs to private people.

The audience in numbers

  • Argentina online (DataReportal, Digital 2025 Argentina): 90.1% internet penetration; 32.2 million social media user identities (70.3% of people). Ad-reach figures, early 2025: YouTube 32.2M, Facebook 29.1M, Instagram 28.9M (63.1% of the population), TikTok 24.4M adults. Facebook and Instagram are the main networks, and WhatsApp is how people share links.
  • Welsh Patagonia (British Council Wales, Welsh Language Project): over 6,000 Welsh speakers in Chubut; around 50,000 Patagonians of Welsh descent (2024 press release); 970 registered learners in schools and adult classes (2023-24 report). It is a small, close community with strong institutions: chapels, the Eisteddfod del Chubut, the Gorsedd (founded in Patagonia in 1881), tea houses, museums and Welsh societies.
  • Calendar hooks (from public posts by Chubut tourism and local media): 28 July, Día del Desembarco Galés / Gŵyl y Glaniad (the 153 Mimosa settlers landed in 1865; the 2026 anniversary was the 161st); the Eisteddfod del Chubut in Trelew each spring (Sept-Oct; check the year's dates); 1 March, Saint David's Day; chapel and tea-house tourism all year. Local media and Chubut tourism accounts post heavily around these dates, so posts tied to them get reshared.

Playbook

  1. Write in Spanish natively, not by machine translation. Use Rioplatense register: "vos" is normal in Argentina, while neutral "usted" is safe for archive text. Keep Welsh place and chapel names as written locally (Capilla Bethel, Gaiman). Have a native check headlines before they go live.
  2. One Spanish landing page per theme, linked from /argentina/, with hreflang="es-AR" (plus es as a fallback) and Spanish <title> and description. Google ranks the Spanish page for Spanish searches only if it exists as a separate URL with its own title.
  3. Facebook first. Post from the Page in Spanish. Share into public groups only where their rules allow it (Patagonia history, Chubut nostalgia/photos, Welsh-Argentine groups); read and log each group's rules before the first post. Photos of documents with a dated caption work best; put the link in the first comment if the group asks.
  4. Instagram: carousels of documents and places, Spanish captions with a Welsh line, and tags such as #Chubut #Gaiman #Trelew #PatagoniaGalesa #YWladfa #GalesesEnLaPatagonia. Tag the institutions, never individuals.
  5. WhatsApp-friendly sharing: every Spanish page gets a share button and an og:image card with a Spanish title. Argentine readers forward links in family and community WhatsApp groups; a clean preview card is what gets shared.
  6. Institutions to inform (by email, institutions only, under the cold-email rules): Welsh societies (Asociación San David, Trelew; Asociación Cultural Galesa), museums (Museo Regional Pueblo de Luis, Trelew; Museo Histórico Regional, Gaiman), the Eisteddfod del Chubut committee, the British Council Welsh Language Project, Chubut tourism, and local newspapers' culture desks (Diario Jornada, El Chubut). Offer free material: dated documents, a free e-book chapter in Spanish, images with credit lines.
  7. Spanish SEO long tail: pages answering exact searches such as "colonos galeses Chubut 1865 documentos", "historia de Gaiman", "galeses en la Patagonia origen". Each answer page cites its source and links to the evidence page.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Use /argentina/ as the Spanish hub. Check that it has lang="es", an es-AR hreflang pair with the English page, a Spanish og:title/og:image, and a Spanish free-chapter sign-up. Fixes go through the on-page run as patches while the repair freeze is on.
  • Calendar: plan a Spanish post series for 1 March (San David), Eisteddfod week, and 28 July (Desembarco), each linking an evidence page and the Spanish free chapter. Add these to the project workspace
  • Weekly: 1-2 Spanish posts on FB and IG, plus 1 on Bluesky/Mastodon with #Patagonia #Chubut (see bluesky-mastodon-fediverse-heritage.md).
  • Outreach: institution emails only, from team@, logged in HubSpot, none to any private person, and none to anyone on the do-not-contact list.
  • Measure: GA4 sessions from Argentina; sessions to /argentina/ (Cloudflare analytics: in the 26 Oct sales-fix check, /argentina/ got 7 of 56 UK page views; track visits from Argentina separately); Spanish sign-ups.
  • Off-site links may carry UTMs (utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=chubut-28julio); internal links never do.
  • Banned public words also apply in Spanish (no "dice", "afirma", "presunto", "fraude", "robado"). Never name the project lead. No insignia.
