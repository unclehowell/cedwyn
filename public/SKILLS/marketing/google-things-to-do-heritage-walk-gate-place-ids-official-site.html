What the sources say (read 30 Oct 2026)

  • Google Ads Help "Introducing Things to do on Google" (answer 10723429): attractions, tours and activities can show inventory as free listings (Things to do Search) or as pay-per-click Things to do ads. Google tells operators to work with their restech (booking-system) provider or a connectivity partner. From Q3 2026 Travel campaigns migrate to Search campaigns for Travel (ads side only).
  • Tourpreneur episode notes (Peter Syme with Chaz Desousa and Vanessa Tobin of Things To Do Marketing, a Google connectivity partner) - practitioner testimony:
  • Google has stopped admitting direct partners with fewer than ~100 activities; a single operator's route is a connectivity partner or a booking system that sends the feed.
  • A listing qualifies only if the booking page shows one price from start to finish (no fee added at checkout), there is a dedicated landing page per activity, and it links to a Google Business Profile.
  • Placement is driven by Place IDs: attach every point of interest the route passes; a walk past an attraction can appear under that attraction as "admission not included".
  • It surfaces in 4 places: Search, Maps, the operator's Business Profile, the attraction's page. Being linked as Official site puts your own booking button on your own profile and pushes resellers off it (Orlando Bike Tours: before the feed, its profile's booking options belonged to a third-party gift reseller).
  • The admissions module gave their fastest results; travellers scroll the whole ticket list, so even a bottom listing sells, commission-free.
  • Ads: clicks about $0.10 to $2, no ad copy (built from the feed). A new listing can take a month or more to gain traction. Business Profile categories decide which modules you qualify for; update the profile weekly.
  • Visible Tourism guide (read): "free listings do not mean a cost-free project" - the booking provider may charge for the connection; the Official site label is Google's decision, not something a supplier can promise; test the guest journey (find -> booking option -> right seller and product -> total price clear).

The gate (all must be true before any setup)

  1. A real, bookable product exists: date(s), meeting point, duration, capacity, one all-in price (or explicitly free).
  2. A dedicated page per activity on our own site with that price and a working checkout.
  3. A verified Google Business Profile in the right category (e.g. tour operator / tour agency) linked to that site.
  4. A booking system or connectivity partner that sends the Google feed at zero cost to us (otherwise it's spend and needs the project lead's GO). If 1-3 are not true, do steps 1-3 only; they help ordinary Search/Maps anyway.

Setup order once the gate passes

  1. Map the route; list every Place ID it passes (church, castle site, listed buildings, viewpoint). Attach all in the feed.
  2. One landing page per product, price identical on page, feed and checkout; no booking fee added later.
  3. Pick the feed route: an existing booking system's built-in Google integration (check its price page; free tier only) or a connectivity partner's free trial - note the date the trial ends and cancel before any charge unless the project lead states GO.
  4. Request Official site linking; then check our own Business Profile shows our booking button, not a reseller's.
  5. Free listings only. Things to do ads are spend: proposal only.
  6. Measure: UTM on the feed landing URL (utm_source=google&utm_medium=things_to_do), bookings per week, and the Business Profile's "bookings" insight. Give it 6 weeks before judging (practitioners: a month or more to gain traction).

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Today: gate fails. The Wales guided walk (Programme Office objective) has no dated, priced, bookable product yet, and Great House Farm itself is a lost/demolished site, so a Business Profile must be for the walk operator (business at a service area), not the farm (see google-maps-gbp-demolished-heritage-site.md).
  • Do now, zero spend, no GO needed: (a) keep a draft route list of Place IDs along the Llandough walk for later; (b) when a pilot walk is booked, give it its own page under cedwyn with one all-in price and the Stripe link.
  • Needs the project lead (PROPOSAL, append to the project workspace, not a message): choosing a booking system or connectivity partner, any trial with a card, any Things to do ads, and creating/verifying a Business Profile in his name.
  • Public text rules apply to the product page: never name the project lead; banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken); "former manorial house" wording on the Wales side; nothing about family holdings or addresses (meeting point must be a public place).
