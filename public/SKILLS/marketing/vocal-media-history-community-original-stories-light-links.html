Vocal Media History community: original 600+ word stories, English-only, labelled AI, light links

Harvested 2026-10-31 (skills harvest). Topic: long-form syndication on a paid-per-read story platform with a History community. Before this run 'vocal.media' had 0 hits. Medium syndication is a different case (it allows republishing): see syndication-canonical-noindex-medium.md.

What the platform states (primary, read 31 Oct 2026)

  • History community (vocal.media/history): "Explore captivating stories, events, and perspectives from the past". It showed 19,290 stories and 7,316 creators when read, so it is a real, active niche.
  • No republishing: Vocal's own resource "How to Recycle Your Content on Vocal" states the submission guidelines "don't allow content that is published elsewhere online". You rework old material into new pieces instead. It also states: "Authors can post excerpts from their books and embed a link to the book to drive awareness and sales."
  • Community Guidelines:
  • English-only: other-language content needs a full English translation.
  • There are word-count minimums (several writers report 600 words for stories).
  • Spam: "a thinly veiled advertisement will be flagged; too many forced hyperlinks ... sales-y language" gets flagged.
  • Comments are not for dropping links to your own stories.
  • AI: any AI-generated text, image, audio or video, "whether it's a sentence, a section, or the full piece", must carry the AI-generated content tag. Not labelling it is "deceitful" and can mean rejection or suspension.
  • Stories that contain identifying information that is not already public are not published.
  • Pay (Vocal-hosted writer guides, 2025): $3.80 per 1,000 reads on the free tier, $6.00 on Vocal+ ($9.99/month). Minimum payout $35, paid through Stripe.

Practitioner testimony (secondary)

  • The most-viewed list on Vocal ("Top 20 Most Viewed Articles", Frank Massey) puts evergreen, search-led listicles at the top. It cites "How I Started Averaging Over $2000 a Month Writing on Vocal" as a case study. That figure is self-reported and not checked.
  • In a Facebook group (snippet), a writer's article was refused as plagiarism because their own copy existed on Medium. So publish on Vocal first, or only, never as a copy.
  • Vocal writer guides agree that reads come from Google and outside sharing more than from the in-app feed. Subreddit self-promotion rules apply when sharing (see reddit-forum-participation-heritage.md).

How to do it

  1. Free account (never Vocal+). Profile in the project's brand, with one link to the site.
  2. Write new 700–1,200 word stories built on one record each: one evidence item, one date, one place. Never paste site pages or e-book chapters.
  3. One link in the closing line to the matching evidence page or /ebooks/ with utm_source=vocal&utm_medium=syndication. No other links, and no links in comments.
  4. Put Welsh quotations inline with an English translation beside each. Tag AI on any piece that uses AI images, AI-colourised photos or AI-drafted sentences.
  5. One story a week for 8 weeks. Track Vocal reads and UTM visits in Cloudflare. Kill rule: under 5 site visits per story on average by week 8, or any rejection for promotion.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Proposal only: a Vocal account publishes in public, so it needs the project lead's GO (one line in the Sunday growth report). Zero spend: free tier only. Earnings would be pennies, so the point is search-indexed reach into a history audience.
  • Editorial rules carry over unchanged: never name the project lead; banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken; write 'not checked'); no accusations about named people; nothing about family holdings or addresses; Llandough Farm is not Great House Farm; no insignia.
  • Good first pieces: single-record stories that already have an evidence page, such as the South Wales Echo 18 Mar 1983 warehouse fire (EV-0068), Hansard 6 Nov 1951, or the 1879 exchange (MAF 11/55/5333), each told neutrally and closing on its evidence page.
  • Conflicts to watch: the no-republish rule means Vocal pieces can't also be cross-posted to Medium/Substack word for word. Keep a log of which outlet holds which original (the project workspace).
