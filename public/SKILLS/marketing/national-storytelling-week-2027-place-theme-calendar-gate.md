## The facts (read 31 Oct 2026)
- National Literacy Trust page "National Storytelling Week 2027" (added 29 Oct 2026, read): **Saturday 30 January to Sunday 7 February 2027**. Theme: **"Every story has a place and every place has a story."** The page frames it around stories that grow from "landscapes, rivers, mountains, towns and communities". Created by the **Society for Storytelling**; the Literacy Trust runs free classroom activities and virtual workshops for UK schools and home educators (early years to 14), 2026 edition supported by Amazon.
- Society for Storytelling (search snippet; page 403 to plain fetch): held from the **last Saturday in January**; ways to take part include hosting a session "in your school, library, workplace, community".
- Both are free to join: no registration fee or listing needed to post or host.

## How to do it (any small publisher)
1. Read the year's theme in early January (Literacy Trust page) and match one piece of content to it word for word.
2. Make one free, classroom-safe item: a 5-minute "story of a place" told from dated records (reading level checked), with the record images and a one-page teacher sheet (3 questions, 1 map activity).
3. Publish it on your own site a week before the start, tag posts #NationalStorytellingWeek plus the theme words, one post per day at most for the 9 days.
4. If you host anything (an online talk, a library reading), list it on your own events page with an .ics file; it costs nothing.
5. Measure: visits to the item tagged utm_campaign=nsw2027, teacher-sheet downloads, signups from that page.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- The 2027 theme fits us exactly: one farmhouse and its parish told through dated records. Working title: **"The story of one house: Great House Farm, Llandough, told from its records"** (5 min, Welsh/English/Spanish subtitles), built from the existing story-time format and catalogue images, no AI scenes for the school version.
- Teacher sheet (PDF, one page, free, no email gate): 3 questions using the tithe map and one survey photo, links only to Cedwyn pages. Fits the sixth-form/EPQ and Welsh-learner skills already in the index.
- Calendar: script by 9 Jan 2027, page live 23 Jan, posts 30 Jan-7 Feb (Bluesky, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Short), one line in the January newsletter. utm_campaign=nsw2027.
- Optional (owner decision, proposal only): a free online story session on Sat 30 Jan 2027 via the existing add-to-calendar route.
- Public text rules apply: never name the owner, banned words list, no family holdings or addresses, no accusations about named people; school version stays to dated facts.
- **Gate:** repeat in 2028 only if the page gets 50+ tagged visits or 10+ sheet downloads in the 9 days, or one school/library account reshares it.
- Status: calendar item; zero spend; no contact with anyone (tags only).
