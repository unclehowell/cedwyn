What it is (primary pages read 31 Oct 2026)

  • 42ª Feria Provincial del Libro y 22ª Feria Patagónica del Libro: Thu 4 - Sun 7 June 2026, Gimnasio Municipal de Gaiman (El Chubut, 30 May 2026). Organised by the Biblioteca Popular "Richard Jones Berwyn" (CONABIP 3041) with the Province's Secretaría de Cultura and Gaiman municipality. It receives "librerías, editoriales y expositores de distintos puntos del país"; 2026 programme had four training workshops (oral storytelling, children's literature).
  • Gaiman is the Welsh tea-house town of the Chubut valley; Berwyn is a Welsh colonist name. This is the one annual book event held inside the Welsh-Patagonian heartland (FILBA is 1,400 km away).
  • Next edition (43ª) expected early June 2027 - dates not announced; recheck Feb-Mar 2027.

The rules (2015 call, Diario Jornada 7 Feb 2015 - the only full call text found; 2027 call not checked)

  • Open to writers, researchers, critics, librarians, editors, booksellers, illustrators, translators, reading promoters, journalists. Registration closed 20 April for a June fair (about 6-7 weeks before).
  • Talks (ponencias/charlas/conferencias): send an abstract of no more than one page; panels max 10 minutes per speaker.
  • Workshops/seminars (literary, historical, photography...): send a work plan (time, sessions, group size, concept).
  • Book presentations: send two printed copies + admission form; the book must be recent and printed before 31 March of the fair year. Authors may not sell individually - sales only through bookshops with a stand, by prior agreement with them.
  • Literary prizes are given at the fair (Certamen "Gonzalo Delfino"; Certamen Internacional de Autobiografías "Ricardo Berwyn").

What this means for an e-book-only publisher

  • A book presentation slot needs a printed book -> not open to an e-book-only title (unless a short print run exists).
  • A 10-minute talk / panel and a historical workshop need no printed book -> that is our door. A talk can end with a QR code to the Spanish edition; no selling on the floor, which suits an online sale anyway.
  • In-person delivery needs a speaker in Gaiman in June. Remote participation was not mentioned in any call read -> not checked; ask in the registration email.
  • The library is a CONABIP popular library: a gift of the Spanish e-book (and later a print copy) to its collection is a natural first contact, before asking for a slot.

Practitioner evidence

  • Patagonian regional authors use the fair as their main presentation venue (El Chubut / Jornada coverage of each edition: workshops, presentations, prize anthologies). No numbers on books sold were found - mark sales impact as not checked; treat it as a relationship + local-press event, not a sales channel.

How to do it (timeline for 2027)

  1. Feb 2027: watch El Chubut / Jornada / the library's social pages for the 43ª call; note the closing date (historically ~20 April).
  2. By early April: draft a one-page abstract in Spanish, e.g. "De Llandough a la Patagonia: una casa galesa y sus registros (siglos XVIII-XX)" - archive-led, dated sources, no assertions about living people.
  3. Send to [address kept in the project workspace] from the publisher's own mailbox (owner sends; we draft only). Ask: talk slot, remote option, and whether a donated e-book/print copy is welcome.
  4. June: if accepted, prepare a 10-minute slide talk + QR to /ebooks/ (Spanish), and alert the societies (Asociación San David, Puerto Madryn association) and Chubut press the week before.
  5. Log outcome in HubSpot and the project workspace
