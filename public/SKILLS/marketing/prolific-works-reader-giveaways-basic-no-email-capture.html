Prolific Works reader giveaways: free Basic plan, opt-in only, what the free tier can't do

Harvested 2026-10-31 (skills harvest). Topic: e-book reader giveaways / reader magnets on Prolific Works (formerly Instafreebie). Before this run 'prolific' appeared only in INDEX.md as a suggested topic. Group promos in general (BookFunnel, StoryOrigin) live in author-group-promos-bookfunnel-storyorigin-free-organiser-run.md: this file covers only what is specific to Prolific Works (PW).

What the platform states (primary, read 31 Oct 2026)

  • Plans (bookbird.io's 2026 summary of PW's pricing, matching Jericho Writers' account): Basic free (unlimited giveaways and delivery, reader support, 1 pen name); Plus $20/month or $200/year (mailing-list sign-ups, MailerLite/Mailchimp sync, promotion to readers, giveaway tracking); Pro $50/month (branding, 5 pen names). All plans: download limits, expiry dates, optional DRM.
  • The catch: PW's own help page "View and Download Subscribers" states only Plus or Pro accounts can add subscribers to a mailing list and export them as CSV. On Basic, readers get the book but you do not get their email. Jericho Writers: "if you don't get those emails, this strategy is defunct".
  • Opt-in rule: since 25 May 2018, only optional opt-in giveaways are eligible for PW's newsletter features. All group giveaways must be optional opt-in (help page "How to Join a Group Giveaway").
  • PW's reader newsletter is genre-led fiction (Newsletter FAQ): Mon romance, Tue mystery/crime/thriller, Wed fantasy/paranormal/horror and sci-fi, Thu YA, Fri a recap of group giveaways including multi-genre ones. There is no history or non-fiction day. Individual features are chosen by staff, weighted by how much the author shares their own link ("You Share, We Share"). No fee for features.
  • Group giveaways: an organiser sets dates and genres, authors submit books, the organiser accepts them, and each author shares their own link. For a group to be featured in PW's newsletter it needs at least 10 participating authors, active PW links on the landing page, and a graphic sent one week ahead.

Practitioner numbers (secondary; treat as indicative)

  • J.N. Chaney, reported by Jericho Writers: Facebook ads at $23/day brought about 49 subscribers/day (about $0.51 each), while PW Plus at $20/month brought about 84 subscribers/day. That was fiction, years ago, on a now-paid tier. Not checked against Chaney's own post.
  • r/selfpublish (search snippets only; Reddit blocks this box): one author got about 150 sign-ups in the first month from 3 group giveaways (2020). Another (2024 thread) uses BookFunnel and StoryOrigin and "wouldn't bother again" with PW.
  • In a Facebook author group (snippet), freebie-site subscribers lowered click and open rates and raised unsubscribes. That is the usual cost of list-building through giveaways.

How to do it (zero-spend version)

  1. Free Basic account, one pen name (the project's public brand, never a person's name). Upload only material that is free anyway: the free first edition (v0.0.1) or a sample chapter EPUB.
  2. Inside the file, put our own sign-up and shop links on the first pages after the title (front matter), with UTM utm_source=prolificworks&utm_medium=giveaway. On Basic, the book itself has to do the email capture that PW won't do.
  3. Set an expiry date and a download cap (for example 500), so the run is one test rather than an open tap.
  4. Group giveaways: filter "Join a Group Giveaway" by the book's genre. Join only groups that are non-fiction, history or "multi-genre", and only if the landing page is relevant. Skip groups that need Plus (mandatory opt-in) and skip fiction-romance bundles.
  5. Measure for 30 days: PW download count (dashboard) against Mailchimp sign-ups and /ebooks/ visits carrying the PW UTM in Cloudflare. Kill rule: under 10 sign-ups per 100 downloads, or under 3 in 30 days, means stop and log it.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Fit is weak but cheap to test. PW readers are mainly genre-fiction freebie seekers and there is no history day in its newsletter. The value is the shared group-giveaway links, plus a long-tail listing.
  • Proposal only: creating the PW account and joining groups is an outward, public listing in the project's name, so it needs the project lead's GO (one line in the Sunday growth report). Zero spend: Basic only, never Plus (Plus is $20/month).
  • Asset: the free first edition EPUB already exists, so no new writing is needed. Check the front-matter link block against the editorial rules: never name the project lead; none of the banned words (states, assertions, per, second-hand report, not checked, reported, deception, taken); Llandough Farm is not Great House Farm.
  • Compare before choosing: StoryOrigin's free tier is file delivery only, and its group promos need the $10/month plan (D2D podcast EP189, in the BookFunnel/StoryOrigin file). That makes PW Basic the only zero-cost group-giveaway route found so far. Without email capture, though, all its value has to come through the links inside the book.
