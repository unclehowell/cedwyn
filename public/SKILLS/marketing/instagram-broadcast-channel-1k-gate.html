Instagram broadcast channel - gated at 1,000 followers (prepare, don't chase)

Read 27 Oct 2026 (skills harvest 01:22). Type: social / owned reach / zero-spend. Status for Cedwyn: GATED - Instagram lists channels for public accounts with more than 1,000 followers; our account had 5 followers on 25 Oct 2026 (the project workspace).

What it is

A one-to-many message thread inside Instagram DMs: only the owner posts (text, photo, video, voice notes, polls, question prompts); members react and vote. Members get a DM-style notification, which is why it reaches people the feed algorithm does not.

Evidence (how solid each is)

  • Instagram help centre (snippet; page renders by script): "Channels can be started by a public account with more than 1,000 followers." Some guides say Creator accounts only; a Reddit user with 1,142 followers on a creator account could not see the option (r/socialmedia, Nov 2024) - eligibility rolls out unevenly.
  • Instagram announcement (read, primary): launched 16 Feb 2023; UK availability 19 Sep 2023; features: polls, voice notes, collaborators, question prompts, sharing a channel preview to Stories, moderators, expiry.
  • Marketing Brew, 17 Oct 2025 (read, brand testimony): Shake Shack's channel ~4,000 members, used with its consumer insights team to poll on product names; Cava's "Tzatziki Town Hall" ~2,400 members - "allows secret information and news to get to [members] first"; Tony's Chocolonely used it to crowdsource a product rename. Shake Shack's social director: "This is very much a test ... we're still kind of in that place"; some brand channels "have gone dark".
  • Reading of the evidence: channels work as a superfan focus group and early-news list, not as a growth engine; they convert existing followers, so they are worthless below a few hundred engaged followers.

Playbook (for when the gate opens)

  1. Name it for the reader, not the brand (e.g. "From the Archive" / "O'r Archif").
  2. Launch post: what members get that the feed won't - first look at each newly found record, polls on what to research next.
  3. Cadence 1-3 messages a week; each message one thing: a record image + one-line caption, or one poll ("Which record next: the 1824 survey or the 1938 conveyance?").
  4. Use polls and question prompts as reader research; feed answers into the on-site survey themes (onsite-one-question-survey-buy-path.md).
  5. Sales: at most 1 in 5 messages carries an offer (new edition, bundle); link to /ebooks/ with UTM utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=broadcast_channel.
  6. Invite: Story sticker/preview + pin; never DM strangers to join.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Now: nothing to switch on. Spend effort on the follower base (instagram-reels-carousels-heritage.md, instagram-collab-posts-heritage-partners.md, instagram-search-seo-google-indexing.md).
  • Gate check: the weekly growth report reads ig_followers; when it passes 1,000, check the profile for the channel option (Creator account required in some reports) and propose the launch to the project lead as one line (new public channel = owner decision).
  • Until then, the same superfan role is served by the WhatsApp channel route (whatsapp-channel-readers-uk-patagonia.md) and the email list.
  • Public text rules apply: never name the owner, banned words, no insignia, no accusations about named people.
