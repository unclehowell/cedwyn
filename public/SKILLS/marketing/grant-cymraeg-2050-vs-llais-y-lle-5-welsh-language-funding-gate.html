GATE: Welsh-language grants for a self-published heritage writer: Grant Cymraeg 2050 vs Llais y Lle 5 (2026-27)

Harvested 2026-10-31 (skills harvest). Topic: is there Welsh Government / Welsh-language money a self-published heritage author can apply for to promote Welsh-language work? This is a gate file. Any application is outward and in the project lead's name, so everything here is a PROPOSAL only.

Bottom line

  • Grant Cymraeg 2050 is not open to an individual author. It is a Welsh Government subsidy scheme for public authorities and third-sector organisations already working in language planning, funded mostly on historical allocations. No open competitive small-grant round for 2026-27 was found.
  • The live route for an individual is Arts Council of Wales "Llais y Lle 5": up to £35,000, deadline 1pm Wed 13 January 2027, but only for creative individuals living in Wales, working with a community and in partnership with an arts, cultural or language organisation. It funds community creative work in Cymraeg, not marketing of paid e-books.
  • Perthyn (Cwmpas) is for community groups becoming social enterprises / community-led housing, revenue up to £10,000; it explicitly excludes "Individuals or organisations ... working solo". Not a fit.

1. Grant Cymraeg 2050 (Welsh Government) - read

  • Subsidy scheme SC11294 "Cymraeg 2050" (gov.wales, published 3 Apr 2025, read 31 Oct 2026):
  • Eligible: "Public authorities and third sector organisations." Sectors: education; arts, entertainment and recreation; other services.
  • Runs 1 Apr 2025 - 31 Mar 2029, total budget estimate £45,000,000, maximum per subsidy £9,500,000.
  • "The amount of the subsidy will be based on historical allocations"; support "normally up to 70% of eligible costs".
  • For "organisations that have significant contribution to the language planning field".
  • Action plan 2026-27 (read): "From 2026 to 2027 grant recipients of our core grant scheme will receive longer term grant funding" (mentrau iaith and the other core grantees). Our reading: this is multi-year funding for existing grantees, not a new open call.
  • History: the open, competitive small-grants round (up to £20,000 per project, £300,000 pot raised to £425,000 for 26 projects) ran in 2017-18 (gov.wales press release 14 Nov 2017, read). Past winners were organisations (universities, Cardiff and Vale College, Cered/menter iaith, small companies), not lone authors. No 2026 round was found in search; not checked with the Welsh Language Division.
  • Note: "Local Authority Education Grant - Cymraeg 2050" in council papers is schools money paid to councils. Unrelated.

2. Llais y Lle 5 (Arts Council of Wales, Lottery) - read

  • Who: "creative individuals, or groups of individuals, from all backgrounds who live in Wales". One person leads and holds the funding and reporting. Needs proven experience of working creatively with communities and Cymraeg.
  • Must have: a partner arts, cultural or language organisation (safeguarding, admin, support; a signed partnership agreement letter is mandatory); a community that co-designs the work; "wholly creative" plans with the arts central, "going beyond a series of workshops alone".
  • Money: up to £35,000 (access costs on top); ACW pays max 90%; at least 10% in-kind from the community, evidenced. Budget must include 2 people at each of 5 sharing days around Wales.
  • Timing: deadline 13 Jan 2027, 1pm, online portal. Projects run 15 months, April 2027 - June 2028 (all start 26 April). Diary-style progress reports plus content for the Ambobdim platform.
  • Scored on: fit and community method; strength of the Cymraeg/creativity/community plan; quality of partnerships; ACW priorities (equality, Cymraeg, climate justice, talent); artistic quality and team.

3. Other doors already covered (do not repeat)

  • NLHF £10k+ and ACW Create & Engage small (to £10k): see heritage-fund-arts-council-wales-grants-gate-2026.md.
  • Cymraeg 2050 2026-27 plan has no Patagonia line: see cymraeg-2050-action-plan-2026-27-no-patagonia-line.md.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  1. Drop "Grant Cymraeg 2050" from any plan or BIZDEV task as a funding route for the e-books. Record the reason: organisations only, historical allocations, no open call.
  2. Llais y Lle 5 is the only realistic Welsh-language pot, and it has three gates only the project lead can answer: - Does he live in Wales? (Required for the lead individual; not checked.) - Is there a partner organisation willing to sign: e.g. a menter iaith (Menter Caerdydd / Menter Bro Morgannwg), a local history society or a library service? Finding one means contact, so it waits for his GO. - Does he want public money near the case at all?
  3. Fit if it went ahead (proposal): a Llandough/Leckwith community project in Cymraeg, e.g. residents and learners co-making place-name walks, oral memories of Tŷ Mawr and the Ely valley, and bilingual audio for the free archive pages. The output is free public work; the e-books may be referenced but the grant can't fund promotion of paid products. Keep anything about the 1987 case out of a funded project.
  4. Calendar: if the project lead states GO by mid-November 2026, that leaves about 8 weeks to find a partner, collect letters and draft. After 13 Jan 2027 the next chance is round 6 (date not known).
  5. Log this in the BIZDEV grants shortlist (#82). No draft or contact happens before GO.
