# Live streams for a heritage archive: a scheduled live "reading from the records", then the replay

Harvested 2026-10-30 (skills harvest). Index check first: 'livestream' 0, 'live stream' 0, 'q&a session' 0 skill hits. Premieres, online talks on Eventbrite/LinkedIn and add-to-calendar are already covered; this skill is the live broadcast itself and what it feeds.

## The evidence (with numbers)
- **National Museums Scotland** began live streaming in 2016 for its *Celts* exhibition. By August 2017 it had gone live **36 times with 25 speakers**, for **120,348 views, 24,308 post clicks and a reach of 966,751** (museum's own statistics, reported by Cuseum on CultureHive, read). A heritage body with no stars, using staff as the talent.
- **Meta (News Feed FYI, Jan 2018, read):** "live videos on average get six times as many interactions as regular videos". The figure is old and from the platform itself, so treat it as direction only.
- **Van Gogh *Sunflowers* (2017):** five museums went live together and each curator had 15 minutes. The format: one object, several voices, a fixed slot (CultureHive).
- **The pattern across these:** a live is cheap reach while it runs, and the replay is the asset that keeps working. Plan the replay, chapters and a clip before the live, not after.

## YouTube rules to know before booking a date (Help, read 30 Oct 2026)
- **Before the first live:** the channel must be verified and have no live restriction in the past 90 days. The first stream can take **up to 24 hours** to be enabled, so switch it on days ahead. The host must be at least 16.
- **Mobile live needs at least 50 subscribers.** Webcam (Studio) or encoder (OBS) live has no subscriber minimum. Use the desktop route while the channel is small.
- **Dual stream:** one horizontal stream plus a vertical feed that is **discoverable in the Shorts feed**. It must be set before going live; you can't add it mid-stream. Subscribers get one go-live notification.
- **Feature differences:** clickable links in chat and the description work on the horizontal live only, not the vertical. **Live Redirect** (send viewers at the end to another live or a Premiere) and Premieres are horizontal only.
- **Other features:** members-only lives exist (a perk for a paid membership). Up to 10 active streams per channel. Policy warnings on a live can mean a 14-day live ban, so keep chat moderated.

## Facebook
Meta's own Live best practices (stay live at least 3 minutes so people can join; start up to 10 minutes before an event) sit on Meta help pages, which block our fetches, so they are not checked here. The live route on a Page is the same idea: schedule it, post an announcement, pin the replay.

## The playbook (small channel)
1. **One object per live, 20–30 minutes, same weekday and time each time.** Keep a fixed slot so it becomes a habit (NMS ran 36).
2. **Schedule 3–7 days ahead** so the watch page exists. Share its link everywhere with UTM tags, and add it to the calendar (.ics) per the add-to-calendar skill.
3. **Run of show:** a 30-second hook, then the document on screen (screen share or a camera over the page). Read it aloud and translate the hard words. Take two or three questions from chat. End with **one** ask (the e-book that contains this record, at /ebooks/), and use Live Redirect into the next documentary Premiere if one is scheduled.
4. **Moderation:** a second person or blocked-word list. Turn on slow mode. No links from viewers.
5. **Replay within 24 hours:** trim the dead air, add chapters (the existing YouTube chapters skill), captions in cy/en/es, and cut 1–3 clips as Shorts (from the vertical feed) for Instagram/Facebook.
6. **Measure:** peak concurrent viewers, chat messages, replay views at 7 and 28 days, and clicks on the /ebooks/ link (UTM: utm_source=youtube&utm_medium=live&utm_campaign=<object-slug>). Keep going after 4 lives if replay views beat a normal upload. Stop if not.

## How we apply it to Cedwyn
- **Gate (the project lead decides):** a live needs a real human voice in real time. Cedwyn's rules say no likeness of real family members and never name the project lead publicly, and the documentary uses an AI cast. So the only form that fits is **voice-over plus document on screen**, with no face and no name: "Live from the records: the 1926 conveyance", etc. Whether the project lead (or anyone) voices it, and on which channel, is his call. Log it as a PROPOSAL in the project workspace, never as a step already taken.
- **Groundwork that is safe now:** check in YouTube Studio that the Cedwyn channel is verified and live streaming is enabled. This only switches on the 24-hour wait and publishes nothing. Note the subscriber count (under 50 means desktop only).
- **First topic:** one record already on the evidence pages and in an e-book, so the single ask points at a real product. Keep public text inside the editorial rules: banned words, no accusations about named people, nothing about family holdings.
- **Welsh angle:** the documentary rule is Welsh spoken before about 1970. A bilingual live (read the record in Welsh, explain in English) is a natural fit for the cy audience, with es captions on the replay.
- **Academy:** a members-only live once a month is a perk the £49.99 Academy can list. Only after the first member joins.
