# Substack Notes as a discovery feed: what the 2026 data states converts
Harvested 30 Oct 2026 (skills harvest). 'substack notes' had 0 skill entries before (Substack appears only in paid-archive-vs-open-archive and incidental lines). This is a GATE + playbook: it applies only if Cedwyn runs a Substack publication; whether one exists or should is not checked here and opening one is a new public channel = the project lead's GO.

## What the sources show (read 30 Oct 2026)
- **WriteStack / Write Build Scale, "We analyzed 21 million Substack Notes"** (writebuildscale.substack.com; WriteStack sells a Notes tool, so vendor data, but large-sample and specific):
  - Paid subscribers per million impressions by Note type: **post embed 74.8, video 35.5, outside link 29.5, image 21.7, plain text 12.0**.
  - A Substack post embed reaches **0.92x** the writer's typical Note; per like it converts **6.56 free subs per 100 likes vs 2.22** for Notes without a link.
  - **Outside links cost reach:** 86% of writers got fewer likes on their own outside-link Notes. "Keep outside links out of Notes you want to travel."
  - Short wins per click: **1.14 free subs per 100 clicks under 200 characters vs 0.89 over 500**; holds for 72.5% of writers.
  - Strangers vs own audience: Notes seen mostly by strangers bring **1.98 free subs per 1,000 impressions vs 0.37**; paid runs the other way (12.5 vs 39.8 per million). "Strangers subscribe free. Your existing readers upgrade."
  - Open with a statement, not a question: question openers averaged **14.6 likes vs 18.2** for all Notes.
  - 2026 median writer converts **0.93 free subs per 100 clicks** (middle half 0.58-1.47; top tenth above 2.5).
- **Same author, "I analyzed 9,641 Substack Notes"** (April 2026 sample, 689 writers): top Note drove **111 free subscribers**; the median Note that converted anyone drove **exactly 1**. Gains are a long tail: a few breakout Notes do the work.
- **Wes Pearce, Escape the Cubicle (own testimony, 23 Apr 2026):** Notes bring him **500-700 new subscribers a month** from short daily Notes taking about **20 minutes a day**; after six months of exported data, **story Notes brought more subscribers than educational and community Notes combined**. His list size and niche differ from ours; treat as one practitioner's result.

## The playbook
1. **Decide each Note's job first:** reach/free subs (short story Note, no link) or upgrades (post embed to the paid edition's post).
2. **Daily, short, story-first:** under 200 characters where possible; open with a statement ("In 1841 the tithe map gave this field a name nobody uses now.").
3. **No outside links in reach Notes.** Put Cedwyn links in the publication's posts, not the Notes.
4. **One post-embed Note per new post** when the aim is paid upgrades.
5. **Sort by subscribers, not likes:** export Notes data monthly and keep the formats that convert; expect a long tail.
