## How to run it
1. **Account + credentials:** a real profile; a topic credential such as "Researches the records of a lost Vale of Glamorgan farmhouse". The credential is where the affiliation is disclosed every time.
2. **Find questions (30 min):** search Quora for: Vale of Glamorgan history, Llandough, Penarth history, Welsh farmhouses, Welsh land records, tithe maps, Welsh Patagonia/Chubut, and in Spanish: "galeses en Patagonia", "colonia galesa Chubut". Open each question's log/stats; keep ones with steady recent views and under ~30 answers.
3. **Answer shape:** answer the question fully in the first 2-3 sentences; then one concrete record (date, document, archive) and an image (a map extract or cutting); then ONE link to the most specific page that goes deeper, with plain disclosure ("I help run the site that publishes these records").
4. **Rhythm:** 2-3 answers a week, never the same paragraph twice, never a link in an answer that is not genuinely useful without it.
5. **Never:** ask your own questions to answer them, use sock-puppet upvotes, or drop bare links.
6. **Measure:** `utm_source=quora&utm_medium=answer&utm_content=<question-slug>` on every link; monthly look at Quora's views per answer and the site's Quora sessions.
