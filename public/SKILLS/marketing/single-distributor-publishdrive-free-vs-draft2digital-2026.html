Single-distributor decision for wide e-book retail: PublishDrive Free vs Draft2Digital (2026 fees)

Harvested 30 Oct 2026. Status for Cedwyn: PROPOSAL only. Prices are fixed (only the project lead changes them); this file changes no price and opens no account.

What changed in 2026 (read from primary pages)

  • Draft2Digital (D2D), announcement by Kris Austin, 14 Apr 2026 (read in full): first-ever fees. New accounts pay a one-time $20 activation fee. An annual $12 maintenance fee applies when net proceeds (after D2D's commission) are under $100 over the preceding 12 months; charged from 14 May 2026 on the account anniversary. Reason given: automated, low-quality account creation ("slop") and rising compliance/security costs.
  • D2D commission: practitioner write-up (Alexa Bigwarfe, Women in Publishing Summit / writepublishsell.com, read) puts it at about 10% of list price, equal to about 15% of what the retailer pays, so the $100 net threshold is roughly $167 of retailer payouts. Not checked against D2D's own FAQ this run.
  • PublishDrive help page "How much does PublishDrive Distribution cost?" (read): 0% commission on every plan, flat subscription instead.
  • Free: 1 e-book, $0 forever. For signups after 24 Feb 2026 it reaches 3 e-book channels (the pricing page names Apple Books, Barnes & Noble and Kobo).
  • Starter 3 books $16.99/mo ($13.99/mo annual, about $168/yr); Standard 6 books $24.99 ($20.99); Plus 18 books $49.99 ($41.99); Pro 48 books $99.99 ($83.99). 30-day money-back on paid plans. Unpaid subscription fees can be deducted from royalties.
  • Practitioner verdict (Bigwarfe): at low volume D2D's $12 is cheaper than PublishDrive Starter's ~$168/yr; long-term D2D's real cost is the 10% on every sale; PublishDrive's first-book-free plan exists "because it takes time to build a reader base".

The decision rule (low-volume author, one or two titles)

  1. One title, only Apple/Kobo/B&N wanted -> PublishDrive Free. No activation fee, no maintenance fee, no commission. The only costs are the stores' own cuts.
  2. Need more stores (Everand, libraries, Google Play via aggregator, print, audio) or more than one title -> D2D, if the expected net clears $100/yr (no $12 fee) or the $20 + $12 is accepted as the price of reach.
  3. PublishDrive Starter only when yearly retailer payouts across 3 titles exceed roughly $1,680 (where 10% D2D commission = $168 Starter cost). Below that D2D is cheaper.
  4. Write the break-even down before signing: (expected sales/yr) x (store payout per sale) x commission vs flat fee.

Worked break-even (USD, store payout per sale P)

  • D2D cost per year = 0.10 x list x sales + ($12 if net < $100) + $20 once.
  • PublishDrive Free cost = $0 (one title, three stores).
  • PublishDrive Starter = $168/yr annual billing.
  • With list = $6 and ~10 sales/yr: D2D ≈ $6 commission + $12 fee + $20 first year = $38; PublishDrive Free = $0.

Pitfalls

  • PublishDrive Free's 3-channel limit applies to signups after 24 Feb 2026; older articles describing wider free reach are out of date.
  • B&N's reach is mostly US; for UK readers Apple Books and Kobo carry the value.
  • Apple Books digital narration and Everand reads both require an aggregator (see the two files of 30 Oct): a PublishDrive Free title can use Apple digital narration through PublishDrive; Everand needs a paid PublishDrive plan or D2D.
  • Store payout rates (e.g. 70% bands) and UK e-book VAT (zero-rated since 1 May 2020) were not re-checked this run; confirm in each store's terms before quoting numbers.
  • Rolling version labels: one aggregator title slot should be the stable edition, not every version.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Direct sales on cedwyn.bucklerfamily.estate/ebooks/ stay primary (0% to anyone but Stripe). Wide retail is an add-on, not a replacement.
  • Recommended route if the project lead wants Apple/Kobo presence: PublishDrive Free with ONE title — the current paid edition at its fixed price (v0.0.2, £4.99), never the free v0.0.1 (stores need a price policy and the free edition already lives on-site). Cost £0; no commission; also opens Apple digital narration later.
  • D2D only if the project lead also wants Everand/library channels or a second title in stores, and accepts $20 + $12/yr unless net passes $100/yr. At today's zero store sales, the maintenance fee would apply.
  • No account is opened by agents; this needs the project lead's own account, tax details and his GO (store presence = new public channel). Logged as a PROPOSAL for the programme office queue.
  • Measure: if adopted, one line in the Sunday growth report: store sales by store, and whether the title has passed $100 net (the D2D threshold) — keeps the choice reviewable each quarter.
