The Small Awards 2027 (Small Business Britain): free entry, Digital Star / Heart of Gold, decide by Feb

Date: 28 Oct 2026 · Status: proposal, needs the project lead's GO (an application is submitted in the business's name)

Facts (thesmallawards.uk, read 28 Oct 2026)

  • Run by Small Business Britain (the same organisation as Small Business Saturday). Launched 2017.
  • Free to enter (Blaenau Gwent Business Hub news post on the 2026 round: "completely free to enter", 12 awards). Many other UK business awards charge £99-£250+ per category (business-awards.uk page, snippet), so this one costs only time.
  • 2027 timeline: applications open 1 Dec 2026; close midnight 28 Feb 2027; shortlist and judging April 2027; black-tie ceremony May 2027; winners' press May/June 2027.
  • Judges look for "strong performance as an ongoing business" and "evidence of strong community engagement".
  • Media: all shortlisted businesses get outreach to regional and trade media; winners get case-study pitches to national and SME press (print, web, radio).
  • Categories that could fit a small online heritage publisher:
  • Digital Star (best digital-only business): no storefront; judged on use of digital channels to engage customers, web design and functionality, growth in audience over time.
  • Heart of Gold (contributes most to its community): community "can be defined geographically or by sector"; open to interpretation.
  • New Kid on the Block: only for businesses under 18 months old at the cut-off; check DATRO Consortium's incorporation date first (company 13775817 is older than that, so this likely does not fit).
  • 2026 Digital Star shortlist: 8 finalists (e.g. PinkSEO, Inkfire, Ingenious Gifts, Bike Boxes). Heart of Gold: 8 finalists, mostly CICs and community groups.

What practitioners report

  • Shortlisting is the real prize. A Hereford agency (The DM Lab) still lists "The Small Awards Digital Star Finalist" among its credentials on its case-study page, beside other awards, years later. A shortlist badge is reusable trust material.
  • Scepticism is common. r/marketing threads ask whether paid awards are worth it; owners say awards rarely bring customers directly and paid awards can look like vanity. A free award with real press outreach is the better kind. (Thread titles and snippets only; the thread bodies were blocked.)
  • A local Facebook group post asked whether residents had noticed how many small businesses "win awards", and suggested looking them up. Readers notice award-badge inflation, so show one real shortlist, not a wall of badges.

The playbook

  1. Decide in December, write in January, submit by mid-February (not on 28 Feb).
  2. Pick ONE category where the evidence is strongest. A multi-category spray reads as generic.
  3. Evidence beats adjectives. Judges want growth over time and engagement, so collect numbers before writing: visitors (human-traffic proxy), newsletter subscribers and their trend, sales, volunteers, community events, free resources given away.
  4. Lead with one concrete story (a reader, a school, a society that used the free material).
  5. If shortlisted: badge on the About page only, one dated news post, one email line; pitch the regional-media angle the organisers offer. No badge on every page.
  6. If not shortlisted: no public mention; re-use the written evidence for other free awards and grant forms.

How we apply it to Cedwyn

  • Best fit: Digital Star (Cedwyn is digital-only: e-books, academy, online archive). Second: Heart of Gold, with "community by sector" = local-history and family-history readers, if free educational material and volunteer transcription are live by February.
  • Prepare in the project workspace: evidence.md (numbers from the weekly growth report, Oct 2026 to Feb 2027), story.md, draft answers. Public-safe wording only: never name the project lead, no banned words, nothing about family holdings, heritage angle only, nothing about the legal case.
  • Gate: the entry is submitted in the business's name (DATRO Consortium Limited trading as Cedwyn), so it waits for the project lead's GO. Note: Companies House shows DATRO's accounts and confirmation statement overdue; judges may check, so fix that before entering.
  • Calendar: put "Small Awards 2027: decide category" in the 1 Dec 2026 BIZDEV plan and "submit by 14 Feb 2027" in the Feb plan. Kill rule: if by 31 Jan the evidence file has no growth trend or community story, skip 2027 and reconsider for 2028.
  • Do not confuse this with Small Business Saturday's "Nation's Favourite Businesses" list (see small-business-saturday-uk-online-shop-no-discount.md). Different list, same organiser.
