## What exists (read today)
- **Victoria County History (VCH):** founded 1899 "to produce a history of every parish in England" (IHR page, search snippet; the live page shows a bot check). It covers England only. There is no Welsh VCH and no open VCH volunteer route for Glamorgan.
- **The Glamorgan equivalent is the Glamorgan County History**, six volumes (Wikipedia "Bibliography of the Vale of Glamorgan", wikitext read; Internet Archive metadata read):
  - I, Natural History (1936)
  - II, Early Glamorgan (1984)
  - III, The Middle Ages (1971, ed. T. B. Pugh, University of Wales Press)
  - IV, Early Modern Glamorgan from the Act of Union to the Industrial Revolution (Wikipedia gives 1936/Rees, but the series order suggests 1974; the title page is not checked)
  - V, Industrial Glamorgan 1700-1970 (1980, A. H. John and Glanmor Williams)
  - VI, Glamorgan Society 1780-1980 (1988)
  - Publishers: the Glamorgan County History Committee/Trust with the University of Wales Press.
- **The series is finished.** No new volumes are being written, so there is no contribution or correction route of the kind a live VCH county offers.
- **Copies:**
  - Internet Archive holds Vol III (bwb_KR-502-255), Vol V (glamorgancountyh0000arth) and a 1936 volume (glamorgancountyh0006unse). All three are controlled lending ("access-restricted-item: true"), so reading them needs a free archive.org login.
  - Searching inside them returned nothing without a login.
  - Vols IV and VI have Google Books records (snippet view at most).

## Why it still matters for marketing (credibility, not traffic)
- A county history is the reference that librarians, reviewers, Wikipedia editors and AI answers check first. Exact volume-and-page citations to it on our chronology pages make our pages read as sourced.
- Vol III (medieval manors and lordships) and Vol IV (Tudor-to-Georgian gentry and estates) are the volumes most likely to mention Llandough, Leckwith, Cogan and the Herbert/Lewis/Windsor holdings. That is our inference; nothing has been read in them yet.
- It also feeds the book flywheel: the controlled-lending copies can be searched once a login exists.
