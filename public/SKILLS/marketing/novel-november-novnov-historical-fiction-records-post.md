# Novel November (#NovNov, the NaNoWriMo successor): one research-records post for historical-fiction writers

Skill harvested 2026-10-29. Verdict: **low priority, one or two posts in November, no outreach.** Nothing to install.

## The facts
- **NaNoWriMo is gone**: the nonprofit announced its shutdown in March 2025 (finances, falling participation, row over its AI stance) (Lit Hub; Beloit Round Table, 1 Oct 2025, read in full). Pitches or hashtags aimed at "NaNoWriMo" now reach a dead brand.
- **Novel November** is run by **ProWritingAid** (UK-based writing software, former NaNoWriMo partner): 50,000 words in November, 1,667 words a day, free dashboard, daily sprints, "Preptober" in October, editing/publishing sessions in December (Round Table, read).
- **2025 results** (ProWritingAid's own recap by Hayley Milliman, Director of Community, 7 Dec 2025, read): **68,000 writers**, **353 million words**, **150+ events** across Oct-Dec, US$7,000+ raised for Room to Read. Its sign-up page now states "join 100,000 writers" for 2026 (organiser's figure). Scrivener (Literature & Latte) runs a Novel November 2026 guide page.
- Hashtags seen: #NovNov, #NovelNovember. Volume not checked.

## Why it is only a small win for us
- The audience is fiction writers, not local-history buyers. The overlap is **historical-fiction writers who need period detail** (place names, prices, farm life, court records, Welsh-language usage). They want sources, and our free evidence pages and e-books are sources.
- Organiser events are run by ProWritingAid with bestselling authors; there is no small-partner slot we found. Do not pitch them.

## How to do it
1. One post in the first week of November (FB + IG, original image of a record): "Writing a novel set in 19th-century Wales this #NovNov? Five dated records from one Vale farm you can use for period detail" -> link to a Cedwyn evidence/timeline page (`?ref=novnov-2026`), /ebooks/ named once.
2. Optional second post mid-month (around the 15th, when writers stall): "a real 1890s farm day in records" prompt card.
3. Prompts must be verbatim period-record lines with source lines (same rule as the quote pack); no invented dialogue presented as record.
4. Measure by `ref=novnov-2026` visits in the first-party beacon / CF analytics. Kill rule: fewer than 20 visits -> do not repeat in 2027.
