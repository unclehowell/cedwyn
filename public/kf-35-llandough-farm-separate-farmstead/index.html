Llandough Farm — a separate farm, not Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr / Cydfin Farm

Separate farm 1824–1940 (catalogue, survey plan, tithe, press, tenancy) · Llandough-juxta-Penarth

Why it is not ours: A separate farmstead with its own deeds: Llandough Farm, 166 acres, NLW D 219/9–13 (1824–1832), against 107 acres for Cydfin Farm or Tŷ Mawr Farm (D 219/1, 4–5, 8 et seq.). The 1824 survey plan labels Llandough Farm apart from Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm.

The record

  • The National Library of Wales catalogue of the Bute estate records (D 219) describes Llandough Farm (166 acres; deeds D 219/9–13, 1824–1832) separately from Cydfin Farm or Tŷ Mawr Farm (107 acres; D 219/1, 4–5, 8 et seq.).
  • The 1824 Stewart survey plan 19 (EV-0211) names “Llandough Farm” as its own place on the plan, beside the Village of Llandough, Wellspring and the River Ely, and apart from “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm”.
  • Llandough Farm was the Rees family farm in the 1841 tithe and in press reports from 1865 to 1910 (EV-0228).
  • The 1940 Western Ground Rents tenancy to David and Thomas Thomas concerns Llandough Farm (EV-0233).

What it does and does not establish

A positive identity correction: the two holdings are separate in the catalogue and on the 1824 plan. “Llandough Farm” is not a name of the property, and its 166 acres, its deeds and its tenancies cannot be carried over to Tŷ Mawr because both lie in the same parish.
