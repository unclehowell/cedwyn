Poaching on Great House Farm, Llandough, prosecuted for the Bute Estate (Barry police court, Jan 1908)

Ours — outside the title question Jan 1908 · Great House Farm, Llandough-juxta-Penarth

Why it is not ours: Our farm, but a game prosecution: nothing in it bears on title or tenure.

The record

  • The court's view: a Saltmead man “pleaded guilty … to poaching on Great House Farm, Llandough”; the underkeeper was John David; the sentence was two months (Evening Express, 11 Jan 1908).
  • “Mr L. Harris, solicitor, prosecuted on behalf of the Bute Estate” (Barry Dock News, 17 Jan 1908; read from a search snippet).

What it does and does not establish

It places the farm's name in a 1908 court report and shows the Bute Estate prosecuting game offences there. It does not show who held the freehold or on what terms the Williamses farmed.
