Record or assertion tested
“Cardiff Urban District Council” as the water authority (per Branwen, voice testimony)
Disposition
Name correction. No Cardiff UDC existed: Cardiff was a county borough. Llandough was in Penarth Urban District ( SC-22 ); the public water undertaker was Cardiff Corporation.
What it does and does not establish
Either Penarth UDC or Cardiff Corporation could be the council remembered. The 1958 report blames Western Ground Rents, not a council.
Next step / retained route
Penarth UDC minutes (UDPE) and Cardiff Waterworks Committee minutes, 1948–58.
