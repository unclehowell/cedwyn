Forensic Assessment — Barry Preservation Society / Civic Trust

Scope: civil-society preservation bodies only. They are not treated as public regulators or title decision-makers.

Role

The preservation movement is relevant as contextual evidence of the contemporary public significance attributed to Great House Farm and the attempt to prevent demolition. Its evidence can corroborate chronology, representations and who was advocating preservation.

Forensic control test

Identify exactly what records the Society/Civic Trust held or generated: correspondence, petitions, photographs, minutes, submissions, contact with Cadw or the Council, and any records of the 1988 campaign.

Current finding

No adverse institutional finding is established. Public campaigning or preservation advocacy is not evidence of title, and absence of surviving papers is not evidence of destruction by any particular actor. 🕒

Human action
