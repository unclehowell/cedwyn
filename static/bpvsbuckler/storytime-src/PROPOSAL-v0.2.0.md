# Storytime v0.2.0: costed proposal (nothing spent; for Sion's decision)

These are estimates from typical vendor list prices. They have not been checked against today's price lists. v0.1.0 cost $0: it uses the device's own speech voice, edge-traced wireframes, pencil renderings made locally, and machine translation done in-house.

1. Recorded narration (natural voices, the same on every phone, exact word timing for the highlighting)
   - Wales tour: 18 short scenes × 19 languages ≈ 75,000 characters. A multilingual TTS with word timestamps comes to roughly $10–15.
   - Museum (English, 18 long scenes ≈ 9,000 characters): about $1.50–2.
   - Patagonia (5 locations × es/en/cy ≈ 10,000 characters): about $2.
   - Option: Welsh and Spanish in the cloned family voice already on file. The clone exists, so this costs only the characters.
   Benefit: highlighting is exact on iPhone and Android, and narration no longer depends on which voices a phone has. Welsh voices are missing on most phones today.
2. Photoreal "after" images for the Wales wireframe fade: 18 scene recreations, one per scene, each tied to its catalogue entries and labelled "artist's impression". At about $0.20 per high-quality render plus one retake, that is roughly $4–8.
3. Church hologram base: one researched reconstruction of the medieval church that stood in the seventeenth century, rendered then traced as a hologram. About $0.50–1.50 with retakes. No image of that church has been found yet; v0.1.0 traces the oldest photograph found, the pre-1866 church.
Total if all three are approved: about $18–28. Nothing will run before 2026-11-02 00:00 UTC, and nothing runs without Sion's yes.
