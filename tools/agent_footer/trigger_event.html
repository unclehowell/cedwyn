Threshold review — private by default

This project may identify records that materially change the understanding of the Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr history or the 1987 proceedings. The public record distinguishes documents, testimony, interpretation, inference and open questions. A missing document, discrepancy or unanswered request is a research lead, not a finding.

How a possible trigger is handled

  1. Preserve: retain the original artifact, provenance, exact wording, date and source identifier.
  2. Record privately: create or update a confidential HubSpot review task with evidence supporting and cutting against the interpretation, relevance, materiality, causation, what would confirm or refute it, and the next record needed.
  3. Challenge: obtain independent historical and legal scrutiny appropriate to the issue. Distinguish the factual event from its legal significance.
  4. Decide: no agent may publish an incident, update a public allegations log or contact counsel or another third party automatically. External action or public wording requires an explicit owner/authorised professional decision.

Programme controls

Agents should start with the Cedwyn Snowball protocol, coordinate through Hive, and record actions in the Projects portfolio and HubSpot. The review queue is confidential; public updates describe only approved, properly qualified conclusions.
