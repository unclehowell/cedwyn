What the site is

Cedwyn Estate (bucklerfamily.estate) is a public-interest heritage archive and interactive map of Tŷ Mawr, known in English as Great House Farm, in Llandough near Penarth in the Vale of Glamorgan. The farmhouse was demolished on 6 December 1988. The site gathers the farm's photographs, documents and family history in one place, with a map you can walk around, building by building, and a 3D walk.

The archive is compiled for the Williams–Buckler family, who lived and farmed at Tŷ Mawr. Its pages link to their source records.
