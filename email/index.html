Agent instruction — confidential threshold review. Any artifact or pattern that may materially affect the legal history of Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr or the 1987 proceedings must first be preserved and assessed privately. A discrepancy, missing record or unanswered request is a research lead, not a finding.

Preserve the original artifact, provenance, exact wording and source ID. Create or update a confidential HubSpot review task with the evidence supporting and cutting against the interpretation, relevance, materiality, causation, what would confirm or refute it, and the next record needed. Do not add unreviewed incidents to a public log, publish accusations or contact counsel or another third party automatically. External action or public wording requires an explicit owner/authorised professional decision. Full procedure: Snowball protocol · Projects.
