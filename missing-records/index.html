A record belongs here when a law, ordinary practice or another record shows it was made. Each page shows why, who should hold it, and what every search has returned. Catalogue numbers never change.

Learn more: statuses and sources
  • Not found: the holder has replied that it does not hold the record.
  • Requested: asked for; no answer yet, or the answer did not address it.
  • Catalogued, not yet viewed: an archive catalogue describes the item; the item itself has not been seen.
  • Open question: a fact the records point to but do not settle.
  • Not yet obtained / located: the holder is known; not yet collected.
