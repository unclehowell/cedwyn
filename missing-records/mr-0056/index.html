Full title: loss record and fire report for the March 1983 warehouse fire that destroyed Glamorgan-Gwent Archaeological Trust finds, Llandough finds among them. A press report of 18 March 1983 (EV-0068) records a warehouse fire that destroyed the Trust's stored finds; finds from Llandough are reported among the losses. A trust that loses its stored finds reports the loss to its trustees and in its annual report, and the fire brigade that attended completed a fire report. These are the only records that would show which Llandough finds and site records burned, and what survived. Heneb, the Trust's successor, replied on 29 October 2026 that it holds no records on the fire or the finds' custody, so the record now sits with other holders. Parcel: not identified (whether the Llandough finds came from Cedwyn land is not checked).
