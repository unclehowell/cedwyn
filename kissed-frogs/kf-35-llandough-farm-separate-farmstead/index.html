Separate farm Catalogue; 1940 tenancy · Llandough-juxta-Penarth

Why it is not ours: A separate farmstead: 166 acres in NLW D 219 against 107 acres for Cydfin / Tŷ Mawr.

The record

Read more
  • The National Library of Wales catalogue keeps Llandough Farm (166 acres) apart from the 107-acre Cydfin / Tŷ Mawr lease group (NLW D 219).
  • The 1940 Western Ground Rents tenancy to David and Thomas Thomas (EV-0233) concerns Llandough Farm.
  • The 25 Oct 2026 search of every name the farm has gone by (Great House Farm, Great House, Tŷ Mawr / Ty Mawr / Tymawr, Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm / Cydfin / Cedwyn, Llandough Home Farm) treated Llandough Farm as a distinct holding, not an alias.

What it does and does not establish

A positive identity correction: the two holdings are separate in the catalogue. The 166-acre figure and the Thomas tenancy cannot be carried over to Tŷ Mawr because both lie in the same parish.

Next step

Examine the individual D 219 instruments and plans for exact boundaries.
