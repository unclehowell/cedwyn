The “1924 Stewart survey” line “Late Thos. Williams now John Gedrick Tent.” — it is the 1824 Bute survey

Dating correction 1824 (not 1924) · Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm, Llandough-juxta-Penarth

Why it is not ours: A dating slip: the record is ours, but it belongs to 1824.

The record

  • The Stewart survey of the Bute estate, DB/E/1 plan 19 and reference table (1824), “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm”: “Late Thos. Williams now John Gedrick Tent.” (EV-0211).
  • A working note had dated the line 1924. No 1924 survey with this line is on file. The 15 May 1924 Bute–Corbett drain agreement (EV-0015) is a drain agreement and plan with no tenant line.

What it does and does not establish

The line is a record of our farm, from 1824, and names a late Thomas Williams in the Cedfin or Ty Mawr entry. The present transcription (139a 3r 8p; the name “John Gedrick”) remains provisional, as the wiki records.

Next step
