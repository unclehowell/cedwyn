Disposition
Ruled out as a Llandough match. Physically examined; described in the archive correspondence as a Cardiff/Pembroke Terrace lease with no mention of Llandough, Great House Farm or Tŷ Mawr.
What it does and does not establish
It is a real Bute–John Williams lease, but it concerns Cardiff property. The record does not show which Williams, if any, was related to the farm family.
Next step / retained route
Keep as a negative control for name/date confusion. Use the archive’s corrected description, not older catalogue wording.
