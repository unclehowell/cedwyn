How we treat outside records. Tŷ Mawr is first named in the record we have found in David Stewart’s 1824 survey (EV-0211). The Williams family acquired Tŷ Mawr in 1667 (sources: Mary Williams’ statement in the 1974 newspapers; ITV News, 1988). Earlier documents on this page that come from the Bute estate or other third parties, such as the Cydwin lease or a Bute catalogue heading, are not records of Tŷ Mawr: their application to it is under forensic examination, and we test them rather than repeat them. Scope test. Every item is assessed against three distinct questions: (1) title and parcel identity; (2) an evidenced act of deception, concealment or knowing misrepresentation; and (3) a documented public-authority decision, handling step or involvement. A source may be irrelevant to title but useful to the government-involvement chronology. A source-code assertion, a repeated Wiki paragraph or an archive search result is not independent proof of the assertion it repeats.

Leads retained as in-scope or potentially in-scope — not “kissed frogs”

  • 1881 “Llandough Home Farm”(EV-0224): in scope. The census label; per family testimony it is Great House Farm. Directories put the same John Williams at “Great house” 1868–1901(EV-0225). No other source uses “Home Farm”.
  • 1824 DB/E/1 plan 19, “Cedfin or Ty Mawr Farm”(EV-0211): relevant to identity, survey extent and mapping; the present transcription of 139a 3r 8p and the name “John Gedrick” remain provisional. It does not prove a later lease or freehold.
  • 1872–1876 Llandough poor-rate books(EV-0212): potentially important because the provisional read shows John Williams rated for a farm under Lord Bute and Daniel Thomas & Son for a lime kiln/quarry. The volume reference, row alignment and link to Tŷ Mawr must be verified.
  • Reported Bute–Thomas arrangement and reported John Williams lease(EV-0006; EV-0008): core unresolved leads for the 1877 transaction. The original and matching plan remain unlocated; do not substitute a Cardiff lease.
  • 1983 registration files and the 1987 title-file amalgamation: core title/public-administration evidence. Obtain the actual applications, supporting instruments, plans, correspondence and scope of the register change before asserting an error, concealment or knowledge.
  • 1987 Llandough Community Council minutes(EV-0214): retain under the public-authority/government-involvement track for the recorded right-of-way, VG41 and BP-agent discussion. They do not by themselves prove ownership, the content of legal advice, or a wrongful act.

Current conclusion: This screening has excluded specific records as matches for the Llandough farm lease; it has not disproved a separate Llandough agreement. No item in this register, merely by being excluded or repeated online, proves wrongdoing, concealment or government complicity. Keep original evidence, record each reason for exclusion, and reopen a lead only when a new reference, full image, plan or independently documented fact changes the test.

Exclusion means “not this property” or “not in scope”, not that the source is false, and not evidence of wrongdoing by itself. The original register stays on the wiki: Kissed Frogs register and Excluded: not our farm.
