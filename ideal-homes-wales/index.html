Forensic Assessment — Ideal Homes Wales

Scope: Ideal Homes Wales Ltd and its successors as later recipients/developers of land within the post-1987 chain. It is not treated as responsible for the original title history without proof.

Established role

HM Land Registry’s WA231076 chronology records transfers from BP Properties to Ideal Homes and later corrections in 1993–94. The development stage therefore matters because it shows what land was represented as transferable after the 1987 amalgamation.

Forensic control test

Identify the exact transfer instruments, plans, consideration, title numbers, ownership certificates and planning representations for each relevant parcel. Compare the post-transfer plan with the pre-1987 WA231076/WA240304 material and the historical Parcel A/B map.

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by Ideal Homes Wales. This does not rule out unresolved downstream title or planning questions. The evidential importance is downstream: later transactions can show whether the conflated title description became embedded in subsequent conveyances and planning.

Human action

Obtain the 1993/94 transfer and correction records, relevant planning files and successor-company records. Preserve exact company identity and avoid treating “Ideal Homes” as a generic label.

Acceptance: every relevant transfer is tied to an exact title, plan, date and party.
