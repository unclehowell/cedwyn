Unless an entry explicitly gives a later review date, the Wiki’s assessment of each open status is as at 10 October 2026; the date of an authority’s response is separately stated where known. Status key

  • Outstanding target — specifically identified but not yet obtained.
  • Partial — some material exists but the complete record is absent.
  • Access required — holder identified but production/access remains outstanding.
  • Reported missing — institution says it does not hold the record; transfer, destruction or miscataloguing remains to be tested where appropriate. 🕒
  • Unconfirmed family report — retained as a lead, not treated as documentary fact.

MOD FOI2026/09190 — entry added 10-10-2026

Evidence: GHF-E169 — MOD internal review dated 10 October 2026.

The MOD states that its searches across MOD archives and targeted searches in the Army Secretariat and Counterterrorism and United Kingdom branches did not locate information matching the request. Separately, it neither confirms nor denies whether it holds Special Forces-related information under FOIA 2000, section 23(5). The review acknowledges delay and concludes that timeliness requirements were not met, without clearly identifying in its concluding paragraph which stage caused that finding. It says personal-information elements were referred for a separate subject access process.

Interpretive control: this is a dated record of the MOD’s statement, not proof that relevant historical records never existed or that military personnel were or were not involved. Keep FOI2026/09190 distinct from FOI2026/09019 unless the MOD confirms a relationship. Do not mark the subject access strand complete until its reference and substantive outcome are recorded.

M-055 — Loosemores / appellant 1986–87 client file. Scope: instructions, pleadings, papers, counsel briefs, correspondence, title material and advice connected with BP Properties Ltd v Buckler, including any file held by Robbins Olivey & Blake Lapthorn as agents. The judgment names these firms in the appellant-side instruction chain. The current route is to ask Hugh James to identify the custodian of historic Loosemores files; Hugh James announced that it joined with Loosemores on 3 July 2023. Status: prospective request item only; request not yet sent; custody, survival and retention unknown.

M-056 — Linklaters & Paines / BP Properties appeal file. Scope: BP’s 1986–87 litigation file, counsel instructions, court bundle, material on the farmhouse/garden and any separate title/registration file disclosed by the firm. The appeal judgment names Linklaters & Paines for BP Properties in the appeal. Status: prospective request item only; request not yet sent; custody, survival and retention unknown.

M-057 — Masons / Pinsent Masons possible separate BP file. Scope: only any relevant separate conveyancing, first-registration, title or enforcement matter for which a primary document identifies Masons or a predecessor acting for BP. The published appeal report reviewed for this update does not identify Masons as BP’s appeal solicitors. Status: attribution and existence unconfirmed; request should first ask whether any responsive matter exists.

Handling rule: these are not findings that files were destroyed or withheld. First seek written confirmation of custody, transfer, retention and disposal history, and ask for a preservation hold. Do not label the letters FOI requests: private law firms are not ordinarily subject to FOIA solely because they acted as solicitors. See the ICO guidance on FOIA scope and the request plan.
