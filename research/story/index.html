iOutside records
How we treat outside records. Tŷ Mawr is first named in the record we have found in David Stewart’s 1824 survey (EV-0211). The Williams family acquired Tŷ Mawr in 1667 (sources: Mary Williams’ statement in the 1974 newspapers; ITV News, 1988). Earlier documents on this page that come from the Bute estate or other third parties, such as the Cydwin lease or a Bute catalogue heading, are not records of Tŷ Mawr: their application to it is under forensic examination, and we test them rather than repeat them. Every line carries one of four tags: Record (what a document shows, with its source), Deduction (what the records together imply, with the reasoning), Hypothesis (a theory, with a likelihood and what would confirm or break it) and Missing (a record that should exist, where it would be and who holds it).
