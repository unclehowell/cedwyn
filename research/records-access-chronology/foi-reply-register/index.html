Negative-findings control — as at 10-10-2026: Statements in this register that an authority holds no information, an attachment was not located, or a record remains outstanding must be attributed to the relevant authority/search or to the documented mailbox/file review, with the applicable response or review date. “Not held”, “not found in the material reviewed”, and “not yet obtained” are not interchangeable and do not establish that a record never existed. This date marks the Wiki’s assessment, not the historical event. Reassess and date the entry when new evidence is reviewed; preserve verbatim source wording.

Vale of Glamorgan Council, FOI 00211027 — email of 14 September 2026, 16:07 (27 MB)

Two attachments. Entry: R23.

Catalogue: GHF-E106-R23-A1

Conveyance dated 11 June 1965.pdf — conveyance and plan of the land for Llandough Primary School, between (1) Lakeside and Llandough Development Company Limited, (2) Western Ground Rents Limited and (3) Glamorgan County Council; consideration £16,800 (facts from the covering email)

Placeholder: original not yet retrieved (email too large for the mail connector)
Inferred — not evidenceWGR is a party, so in 1965 it held, or had to release, an estate in the school land; the development company was most likely the contracting vendor. A conveyance of this kind normally recites the vendor’s title, so it should recite WGR’s root — on the Wiki’s reading the 1938 Bute conveyance, which carried Parcel B only. That makes this the earliest WGR title recital the family can get before the missing 1969 deed to BP Pension Trust. It cannot give WGR title to Parcel A: in 1965 the farmhouse and garden were occupied by Mary (Nancy) Williams, who refused WGR’s tenancy offer that year. Whether the school site was ever part of Great House Farm is itself unknown. Test: the plan against the 1939 tenancy plan and the 1841 tithe parcels; the recitals (dates, parties, acreages); and whether any part of the ~10 acres of Parcel A is included.
