From: Sion Buckler
To: information@south-wales.police.uk; cc Sally-Anne.Hillman@south-wales.police.uk
Date: 27 September 2026, 04:17 UTC
Subject: Internal review of 719/26 (no records held) and overdue request of 20 August 2026 — Great House Farm, Llandough, 1988

Freedom of Information / Data Management Team,

Request 719/26, and my request of 20 August 2026 (deaths of William Buckler
and Mary Doreen Williams)

1. Internal review of 719/26

I understand the Force's position is that it holds no records. I ask for an
internal review of that conclusion, because the search does not appear
capable of finding what the request describes.

South Wales Constabulary officers were present at the eviction and
demolition at Great House Farm (Tŷ Mawr), Llandough, between 29 November
and 6 December 1988. This is not a matter of recollection. It appears in
ITV Wales footage from the time, now public on ITV's archive channel, and
in contemporary newspaper photographs and reports, which I can supply. A
police presence on that scale would have generated records: a duty or
deployment record, an operational order or briefing, an occurrence or
incident log, liaison with the Under-Sheriff or bailiffs, press office
material, and possibly photographs.

For the review, please confirm in writing:

(a) Which systems, archives and paper holdings were searched, including
pre-1990s paper records of the former Penarth/Barry division and any South
Glamorgan command records.
(b) The search terms used. Records of that period may be indexed under
"Leckwith Road", "Llandough", "BP Properties", "Buckler", "Williams", "Ty
Mawr", "eviction", "breach of the peace" or the date, rather than "Great
House Farm".
(c) Whether records of that type from 1988 were destroyed under a retention
schedule, and if so which schedule and whether any disposal record survives.
(d) Whether any records were transferred to Glamorgan Archives or the South
Wales Police Heritage Centre, with the reference if known.

If records were destroyed, a record of their destruction is itself
information I am asking for. If they are held elsewhere, please tell me
where, under your duty to advise and assist.

I was present at the eviction as a child. So far as any record contains my
personal data, please also treat this as a subject access request under
Article 15 UK GDPR.

2. Request of 20 August 2026

This request was due on 18 September 2026 and I have had no response beyond
the automatic acknowledgement. Please give me its reference and the
response. If the records are not held, please say whether they were
destroyed or passed to the Coroner.

If I do not receive the internal review outcome within 20 working days, I
will complain to the Information Commissioner.

Yours faithfully,

Sion Buckler (Ret'd Sergeant)

To each recipient, in your own capacity as holder or likely holder of records. Please do not treat this as a joint reply. Answer only for your own holdings.

Sirs/Madam,

This is a preservation notice. It is not a claim form and not service of proceedings. It does not replace any FOI, review or subject access request already with you.

Land: Great House Farm / Tŷ Mawr, Llandough-juxta-Penarth, adjoining St Dochdwy's. Titles WA231076, WA240304 (closed), WA513690, WA735527, and any related title. Planning refs 89/01396/OUT and 92/00671/RES.

Please preserve, and do not destroy, transfer out of your custody without a written receipt, or dispose of, any file, plan, photograph, tape or electronic record in your possession, custody or control relating to:

1. The 1877 split, the 1916 and 1939 tenancies, and the 1938 Bute to Western Ground Rents conveyance.
2. The 1955 and 1962 possession orders, the 1974 proceedings, and BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2.
3. First registration: WA231076 application 30 November 1982; WA240304 application 23 February 1983; later amalgamation.
4. The 1988 possession (29 November) and demolition (begun 6 December), including listing, planning, police and any liaison papers.
5. Mary Doreen Williams (died 26 March 1983) and William Buckler, so far as your file concerns this land.

Also preserve, if you hold them, the following planning and land-charge classes. Absence from a 2026 file disclosure is not proof they never existed:

6. Any section 52 Town and Country Planning Act 1971 agreement, later section 106 instrument, deed, undertaking or other agreement regulating the development or use of this land, and any corresponding local-land-charge entry.
7. The historic planning-register entries required by the Town and Country Planning General Development Order 1988 for 89/01396/OUT and 92/00671/RES (applications, reserved matters, plans and drawings, and decision information).
8. The officer's report annexes to 89/01396/OUT, including Appendix B (further GGAT comments after discussions with the Trust), and Appendices C–E.
9. The condition chain on 89/01396/OUT: conditions 16–18; the Council's October 1990 confirmation of condition 17; GGAT's July 1992 archaeological warning; the September 1992 reserved-matters approval; and the 1993–94 papers on whether condition 18 had been discharged.
10. Any preliminary environmental-assessment / screening determination for the outline application (site described as about 3.4 acres / 1.38 ha).
11. Any record of the statutory consultation route used because the land was, or was treated as, a site of archaeological interest / SMR entry under the 1988 Order.
12. The complete 92/00671/RES file, and any sale, option, conditional-contract or transfer paper on the planning files covering BP Properties to Ideal Homes Wales, including the recorded transfer of 3 December 1993.
13. The application-receipt record for 89/01396/OUT (form dated October 1989; reconstructed Council receipt 8 November 1989).

Please stop routine deletion, including archived mailboxes, for anything in that scope.

The public evidence index is https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/ . The investigation distinguishes the farmhouse parcel from the eastern fields. The 1987 Court of Appeal decided possession, limitation and the October 1974 letters. It did not decide the documentary root of the farmhouse parcel. These records go to that question. This notice is not a finding against any of you.

Please acknowledge, name the officer responsible for the hold, and say what you hold in scope. If you hold nothing, say what you searched.

Nothing in this letter abandons any claim concerning title, registration, concealment, limitation, reopening or alteration of the register.

Yours faithfully,
