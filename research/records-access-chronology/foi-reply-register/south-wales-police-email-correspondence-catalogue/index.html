Editorial correction — Mary Williams / Buckler (not part of the correspondence below)

[PRESS REPORT] The Western Mail in 1958 identifies Mary as “Mrs Mary Doreen Buckler of Great House Farm”. Mary used both Williams and Buckler. The phrase “falsely named Mrs Buckler” is wording in an earlier correspondent’s message; it is not an established fact or this archive’s description. The correspondence is retained so the historical wording is not misrepresented.
