Q&A — History & the Two Parcels

Controlling parcel definition — 9 October 2026. Parcel A is the full 1877 house/quarrying estate: farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, forest north of the house, and the ground south-east of the house/garden later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928 when quarrying and rent to Thomas ended. The already-obtained 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement/plan depicts Parcel B only. Parcel-history guide. The pages below distinguish the established family case from the remaining documentary work to trace how the forest and green were described, registered and transferred afterwards.

What are Parcel A and Parcel B?

Parcel A: the full house/quarrying estate, not just the farmhouse or house-and-garden group. It included the farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, forest north, and south-east land later registered as VG41. Parcel B: the separate eastern agricultural fields. The distinction is central because later “whole farm” wording blurred two legally distinct interests.

What happened around 1877?

The family’s established title case is that the 1877 Bute–Thomas arrangement placed the full house/quarrying parcel on the Thomas/Williams side, with quarrying rights/arrangements continuing until 1928. The family relies on Mary (Nancy) Williams’s statement and the end of quarrying/rent to Thomas in 1928 as the point when that arrangement took effect in the family’s freehold. This account includes the north forest and south-east green ground, not only the occupied house and garden. Parcel B was the eastern fields retained by Bute and later dealt with through WGR’s agricultural tenancy.

What is the quarrying part of the story?

The 1877 arrangement is described in Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 witness statement (Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony) as linking the house/quarrying estate to the Thomas side while quarrying continued. In 1928 the quarrying had ended and the last rent to Alfred Thomas was paid. The family’s case is that the arrangement then took effect and the full Parcel A became Williams family freehold, including the forest north and south-east ground later registered as VG41.

Who were Daniel Thomas and Alfred Thomas?

Daniel and Alfred Thomas. The Daniel Thomas and Alfred Thomas record collects the surviving evidence concerning the Thomas transaction; the exact land and legal effect are tested against the 1877 agreement and later title records.

Why does 1928 appear on the timeline?

The family’s established chronology identifies 1928 as the point when quarry machinery had been removed and a final payment of about £4 was made to Alfred Thomas. The family’s title case is that this is when the 1877 arrangement took effect and the full Parcel A passed into the Williams family’s freehold. The alternative limitation case is that twelve years from the 1928 change would have matured by 1940 if the applicable legal conditions were met. The legal title basis and the alternative limitation calculation must be kept distinct.

What is the significance of 1938–39?

The 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement and plan are already obtained and analysed: the plan depicts Parcel B (the eastern agricultural fields) only, not Parcel A. That finding is closed and should not be re-opened as an acquisition question. The separate downstream task is to inspect the 1938 conveyance’s operative scope and compare later 1961, 1969, 1971, 1975 and 1982–83 instruments/plans to identify how the north forest and south-east green entered later descriptions and titles.

Why does the 1916 tenancy matter?

The 1916 tenancy helps track how “whole farm” language entered later descriptions. That terminology must be read against the established Parcel-A / Parcel-B distinction: it did not itself merge the titles or confer a right to Parcel A. The current work compares later title plans to trace how the Parcel-A north forest and south-east green were dealt with after 1955.

What does the family’s long occupation prove?

Occupation is evidence relevant to possession, title, knowledge and limitation, but it is not a substitute for examining the 1877/1928 title case and succession. The family’s alternative limitation position is that twelve years from 1928 would have matured by 1940 if the statutory requirements were satisfied. The 1949 attempt to make Mary’s occupancy permissive is said to have failed; the 1955 “whole farm” wording then blurred the parcel division and made occupation appear permissive from a later date. Both the underlying-title route and the alternative limitation route must be stated distinctly.

Historical records and downstream title questions

The 1928 title case and 1939 Parcel-B-only finding are closed premises for this phase. The remaining document work is to corroborate the account, trace the exact forest/green polygons into later titles and evaluate legal remedies.

  • What copies of the 1877 arrangement and associated Thomas/Bute records survive, and what operative terms, schedules or plans further corroborate the family’s established 1928 title case?
  • What do the 15 May 1924 Bute–Corbett drain agreement and 1905 / 1961 deed actually describe? Do not treat the 1924 drainage instrument as the missing 1928 title event unless the document proves a connection.
  • How did the 1916 “whole farm” wording become embedded in later conveyance, possession and registration descriptions after Parcel A and Parcel B were distinct?
  • Closed: the 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement/plan is already held and depicts Parcel B only. Do not re-request it or ask whether it includes Parcel A.
  • How do the 1975 conveyance plan and 1982–83 registration plans map against the full Parcel-A boundary, especially the north forest and south-east ground later registered as VG41?
  • How did the 1969 root deed, 1971 abstract, 1987 amalgamation and later woodland transfers describe the exact Parcel-A north forest and south-east green? The 1969/1971 file gap is not Parcel-A-specific proof.
  • What current title/registered proprietor covers each exact part of the forest and green, and which protective, rectification or indemnity routes remain legally available?
