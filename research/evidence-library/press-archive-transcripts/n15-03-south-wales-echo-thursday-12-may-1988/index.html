Interpretation and wider context

INTERPRETATION The search text runs two headlines together (“DRAMA SUCCESS” belongs to another story) and misreads “be killed” as “ibe”. The substance is that the Cardiff MP Alun Michael asked the Lord Chancellor to look at the case.

It gives a documentary source for a point that was previously SOURCE PENDING. The correspondence between Alun Michael and the Lord Chancellor’s Department is a record worth requesting (National Archives, LCO series).
