Its likely evidential value is to show a drainage arrangement and the land/location it served, subject to the actual wording and attached plan. A drainage agreement is not, merely by existing, proof of a freehold conveyance or of the boundary of Parcel A. Compare the WA231076 registration material and the parcel-title analysis.

Locality control — do not conflate the two Llandoughs

The Museum Wales catalogue for MS 3360/3 describes 1924 sale particulars for nine farms forming part of the Llandough Estate and gives the place as “Llandough, Llanfair (Glamorgan)”. The separate Gwyn & Gwyn archive-material list also records “Sale of Llandough estate 1924”, Box H, bundle 11.

Scope decision: material concerning Llandough/Cowbridge is outside this wiki’s subject. It is not attributed to Llandough-juxta-Penarth, Great House Farm or Parcel A/Parcel B. No GHF evidence ID has been assigned to MS 3360/3.

Do not connect the sale particulars to this 15 May 1924 Bute–Corbett drain agreement merely because both are dated 1924. The original agreement and plan remain unexamined; its relevance depends on their contents and geography.

INFERENCE · D-01The 15 May 1924 Bute–J. P. S. Corbett drain agreement is entered on the woodland title WA735527 as the only charge not limited to a plan colour, so it is registered against the whole title, including land that came through the 1975 conveyance; it no longer appears on the residual farm title WA231076 (record, register views). Glamorgan Archives DSA/12/4232 records a sale "Lord Bute to Miss O.S. Corbett" of a plot opposite O.S. 60, with correspondence of 6–22 Mar 1924, two months earlier. Working hypothesis: Bute was still dealing with this land as owner in 1924. That the May drain serves the March plot is an inference, not shown; O.S. 60 is a field on the south-east edge of the village, not the farmhouse plot (O.S. 36).

Rests on: EV-0015 · EV-0169 · EV-0170 · research log E119, E128; research cycle 35 (DSA/12/4232)

Would be confirmed by: The 1924 agreement and its plan (HM Land Registry WA231076 item 2; Glamorgan Archives DSA/12/4232) showing the drain's route across or beside the house parcel.

Would be refuted by: A plan placing the drain wholly away from the house parcel, or a Registry note that the entry was carried over without a plan for clerical reasons.

Status: Open lead

DISCARDED THEORY · D-02DISCARDED: the theory that the 1924 drain agreement burdens only land outside the 1975 farmhouse strand and so tells us nothing about Bute's title. Reason: on WA735527 the entry is unlimited by tint and so registered against the whole woodland title, including 1975 land.

Rests on: EV-0015 · EV-0169 · research log E128

Would be confirmed by: Would be revived by a filed plan limiting the entry.

Would be refuted by: Refuted by the register view as read.
