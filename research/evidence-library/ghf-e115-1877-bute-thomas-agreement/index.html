It becomes more evidence of an estate split only if the original terms, plan, reservations and later completion/vesting documents establish what land and interest passed. Do not treat the title of the catalogue entry as a conclusion. Read the Cedwyn Estate Working Theory.

Instrument identity and provenance boundary

This entry concerns the family-reported Bute–Thomas arrangement relating to the Great House Farm house/quarrying parcel. It must be kept distinct from the photographed 6 November 1877 Clyde Street, Roath building lease and the checked 2 May 1877 Pembroke Terrace, Cardiff lease. Neither Cardiff instrument is a substitute for the unlocated Bute–Thomas instrument or its plan.

The current evidential status remains limited: the family identifies this arrangement from recollection and related records; the original instrument and the complete associated plan have not been independently authenticated here. Keep “sale,” “quarry lease,” conditional transfer/reverter wording and parcel extent as questions until the operative document or reliable contemporary record resolves them. See EV-0008, EV-0213 and the Kissed Frogs register.
