Evidence status: PENDING VERIFICATION — the audiovisual item is a source; the claimed identity and role of the people shown are not established by the catalogue entry alone.

This page is a dedicated evidence target for an interpretation raised by the family: that persons seen in the Great House Farm eviction/possession-enforcement footage were members of the SAS acting undercover as BP bailiffs. It records the claim so it can be tested without converting the interpretation into a finding.

Source material already catalogued

  • GHF-E113 — ITV Cymru Wales report: Great House Farm, eviction / possession enforcement and injunction. The live catalogue page says the family’s interpretation is separated from what the footage itself establishes; its evidence object and provenance links require review.
  • GHF-E168 — Newspaper photograph captioned “Police wait outside the farmhouse”, circa April 1988. The caption/source context and identities require independent verification.
  • GHF-E172 — South Wales Echo, 3 December 1988. A contemporary report that may assist with chronology and statements attributed to participants; it does not by itself identify a person in later footage.
  • 1988: Possession and Demolition and BP Properties Ltd v Buckler for the legal/enforcement context.

What would be needed to substantiate the identification

  • The complete, original broadcast or highest-generation footage, with full audio, timecodes, captions and editorial context.
  • A frame-by-frame schedule describing visible features and actions without guessing identities.
  • Independent identification evidence for each individual identified in the allegation as SAS personnel, not merely appearance, clothing or a witness’s recollection.
  • Records linking the persons to BP, a bailiff firm, police, military service or a defined operational role: appointment/contract records, attendance logs, court/bailiff instructions, police records or contemporaneous correspondence.
  • Where the allegation is that a state/security actor acted undercover, evidence of role and authorisation—not just presence at the scene.

Current conclusion

The current published sources justify preserving and investigating the family’s interpretation, but they do not establish that the individuals were SAS, undercover personnel or BP bailiffs. The Wiki must describe this as an unconfirmed allegation until independent identification and role evidence is obtained. Any new frame, testimony or official record should be added here and assessed against alternative explanations.

Relevance to the 29 October 2026 working theory. The identity and role of people in the 1988 footage remain unverified. If authenticated, the footage may document the eviction operation and assist with the chronology of physical possession and demolition; it cannot by itself determine who owned the land or whether the 1939 signature was genuine. Keep identifications provisional until original footage, date, continuity and independent corroboration are established. See the hypothesis and its tests.
