DOCUMENTED. This index lists every newspaper cutting, press extract and related published record held by the Wiki. Each item has its own evidence page with a unique catalogue number. Each page gives the evidence image (or says where it is held), a verbatim transcript, a neutral description, provenance with archive links, and a separate interpretation that is open to challenge. The earlier single page of transcripts, Press Archive — Newspaper Transcripts, remains as a reading copy and cross-check against the family’s research file.

Rule for citing the press

Any page on this site that relies on a newspaper item links to that item’s page below, not to the transcripts page. A press report is evidence of what a newspaper said at the time. It is not proof that every statement in it is true.
