Identity control: these similar spellings are research leads, not proof that every place-name refers to the same holding or to Great House Farm at Llandough-juxta-Penarth. The link to this property must be tested by source provenance, parish, boundary, acreage, named parties, map/plan and continuity. In particular, “Ty Mawr” can mean “large/great house” in Welsh; that translation alone is not evidence of identity.

What the Cedwyn branch can and cannot support

At present, this branch is a place-name and manorial-history research line. It may help identify older names, holdings or boundaries worth comparing to the later farm; it does not yet prove that all the variants were used for the same land, that the family held the farm continuously from a medieval manor, or that any older lease determines the post-1877 title. Those conclusions require the original records and mapped extents.
