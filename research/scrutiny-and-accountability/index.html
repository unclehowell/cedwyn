Controlling Parcel-A case — 9 October 2026. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928, passing the full Parcel A to the Williams family: farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, forest north and ground south-east later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The 1939 agreement/plan is already held and depicts Parcel B only. Mary (Nancy) Williams’s c.1974 statement records the papers going missing in 1950–52 (Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony). [SOURCE PENDING] The 1949 permissive-occupancy attempt failed, and WGR’s 4 July 1955 enforcement of Parcel B also seized the Parcel-A north forest and south-east green under “whole farm” wording. Mary’s defence of the remaining occupied house group was not the limit of her title claim. Legal and registration issues are therefore being reassessed against this full scope, with fraud remaining contingent on specific evidence.

How to read this branch

  • Documented fact: supported by a primary record or reliable institutional record held in the Evidence Library.
  • Testimony: a named family member’s recorded statement, linked to their page under Testimony (https://greathousefarmwiki.wordpress.com/testimony/); privately held documents are cited by their evidence ID.
  • Source pending: no document or identified speaker yet.
  • Issue / inference: what follows from comparing the records, for the body responsible to answer.
  • Allegation: a suspected act of fraud or concealment, with the evidence for it and the evidence still being recovered.

Core distinction: an administrative registration, a court judgment, a possession order, a later amalgamation of titles and a subsequent transfer are different acts. The Wiki tracks each act on its own evidence and places them all on the Master Timeline. This is how the house parcel came to be swept into the whole farm: each step relied on the one before it, and none of them examined the house parcel’s own title.
