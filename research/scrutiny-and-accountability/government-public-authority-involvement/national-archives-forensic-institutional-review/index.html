Forensic Assessment — The National Archives

Scope: TNA as archival discovery/custody authority for transferred government and corporate records.

Role

TNA is relevant where government or historical corporate records may have been transferred from departments or deposited collections. It is not a title adjudicator.

Forensic control test

Identify the exact collection/classmark, transfer status, closure/restriction and catalogue provenance. Where a public-record route is exhausted, ask whether an earlier transfer or departmental retention record exists rather than treating catalogue absence as destruction.

Current finding

As at 10-10-2026, the material reviewed does not establish wrongdoing by The National Archives. This is not a finding that the archive had no involvement; its particular custody and search functions are addressed here.

Human action

Run targeted searches for Wales Office/Welsh Office, land-registry/leasehold and relevant corporate records, recording classmarks and transfer/disposal information. Where an expected record remains with a department, identify the department and preservation route.

Acceptance: each TNA search produces classmarks/copies or a documented negative-search outcome with custody explanation.
