Forensic Assessment — Llandough Community Council

Scope: local community-council records and representations.

Role

The Community Council is relevant principally to local representations, heritage/community knowledge and later public discussion. It did not adjudicate the private title dispute.

Paul Egan — documented role and casework assessment

Current public status (checked 18 October 2026). The Council’s own Contact Us page continues to name Paul Egan as Clerk to the Council. The Council’s 11 October 2026 Section 12 refusal is signed “Paul R. Egan … Clerk to Llandough Community Council” (GHF-E183, 11 October entry). A public procurement notice posted in January 2026 also names Paul Egan as the Council contact. These are evidence that he was publicly listed and signing in the Clerk role at the respective dates; they do not independently establish uninterrupted tenure or a formal start/end date.

Historical public-role record. Paul Egan / Paul R. Egan is identified as representing Llandough Community Council in Welsh Government’s 2018 local-government reform consultation response documents. The Council’s own 2018 newsletter and Winter 2023 newsletter also identify him as Clerk. These are dated snapshots, not proof of uninterrupted service between them. A family understanding in June 2026 that he might be retiring was explicitly unconfirmed and arose from a public social-media post; the present Council contact listing and 11 October signature show that he was still publicly listed/signing as Clerk at those later dates. The source reviewed here does not include a Council notice establishing the effective date of any retirement, reappointment, locum arrangement, handover or records-custody change.
