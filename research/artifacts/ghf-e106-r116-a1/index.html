Buses replaced trains on the Vale of Glamorgan Line next week due to

BUS STOP UPGRADES

The Vale Council was currently reviewing bus stop provision in the area.
A consultation was ongoing and if the Council had any requests for
upgrades throughout the village, they should let him know.
FLY-TIPPING

Recent incidents included a mattress being dumped on the Greenway

stronger deterrent and more investment in surveillance equipment.
ROYAL MAIL DISRUPTION

Several residents had reported problems with postal services. Some
had gone weeks at a time without receiving any mail at all. He had
written to Royal Mail's public affairs team in London to raise concerns
and would provide an update once they had responded.
