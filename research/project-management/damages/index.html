1. Loss of the land

Learn more
Learn more

Dependencies: title re-established (1987 judgment [EV-0080] set aside, or register rectified). LA 1980 s.15 (12 years to recover land) and s.17 (title extinguished), unless Limitation Act 1980 s.32 postpones. s.32(3): no recovery of property or its value from an innocent purchaser for value (current owners). Rectification against a proprietor in possession needs consent unless Sch 4 para 3(2)/6(2) applies (LRA 2002 Sch 4).

2. Mesne profits / loss of use

Learn more
Learn more

Legal basis: mesne profits are damages for trespass by a person in possession without right, measured at the ordinary letting value (Inverugie Investments v Hackett [1995] 1 WLR 713). Dependencies: title re-established; claim lies against whoever was in possession (BP 1988–93; Ideal Homes 1993–95; homeowners after); LA 1980 s.2 (6 years) unless s.32.

4. Deceit (CONDITIONAL on fraud)

Learn more

Measure: all loss directly flowing from the fraud, including consequential loss (Smith New Court v Scrimgeour Vickers [1997] AC 254; Doyle v Olby [1969] 2 QB 158). 4e cat. 2 is measured against the defendant’s profit: developed value £11,039,000 (1a) less land and build cost; build cost not held (TBC). Dependencies: fraud proven to the civil standard; 1987 judgment set aside (Takhar v Gracefield [2019] UKSC 13; RBS v Highland [2013] EWCA Civ 328); Limitation Act 1980 s.32(1)(a) runs from discovery or reasonable diligence; s.32(3) bars recovery of property or its value from innocent purchasers; no double recovery with Heads 1–3.
