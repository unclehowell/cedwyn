Controlling property-loss case — 9 October 2026. The family’s established title case is that the 1877 arrangement took effect in 1928, when the last rent to Thomas was paid (as Mary (Nancy) Williams recalled; quarrying had ended by November 1925, Glamorgan Archives DSA/12/4888), passing the full Parcel A to the Williams family. Parcel A includes the farmhouse/buildings, barn, stable and yard west of the house, garden, forest north of the house, and ground south-east later registered as VG41. Parcel B is the separate eastern agricultural fields. The 1939 WGR–John Williams agreement/plan is already obtained and depicts Parcel B only. [SOURCE PENDING] The 1949 attempt to make Mary’s occupation appear permissive failed. Mary (Nancy) Williams said the title papers went missing in 1950–52 (witness statement c.1974, Mary (Nancy) Williams testimony). [SOURCE PENDING] WGR’s 4 July 1955 enforcement of Parcel B also seized the Parcel-A north forest and south-east green under “whole farm” wording [record pending: EV-0034]. Mary’s defence of the remaining occupied house/buildings/garden group did not narrow the full Parcel-A claim. The primary title case and the alternative limitation case (twelve years from 1928 potentially maturing by 1940 if statutory conditions were met) are distinct. Current execution focuses on tracing the forest and green through subsequent titles and remedies; D696 is background only, and no 1939 retrieval is needed.
