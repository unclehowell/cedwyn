All AI agents and editors must follow the Site Agent Operating Rules. In particular: every substantive factual statement must be source-linked where possible; repeated statements must link to their canonical source; agents must distinguish documented fact, testimony of named family members, reconstruction, inference and unresolved material; and material edits must pass the site’s evidence, hyperlink and change-control checks before publication.

Source handling principles

  • Evidence records are intended to be traceable to an archive, repository, court record, register, media item or privately held family material where that source is available.
  • Catalogue identifiers beginning GHF-E identify evidence entries used by the Wiki’s evidence catalogue.

High-level Cedwyn Estate theory cross-reference: Agent rule — Cedwyn Estate working theory (18 October 2026): when editing pages materially touching estate identity, historic parcel boundaries, the 1949/1959 occupation proposals, the 1961 deed, VG41/1970, the 1982–83 registrations, the 1987 amalgamation, succession or the wider Welsh land question, link the Cedwyn Estate Working Theory. State exactly which proposition a source supports; use “more evidence” only where a source materially supports that proposition. Distinguish DOCUMENTED FACT, INFERENCE and SPECULATION / TEST REQUIRED. Record counterevidence and falsifiers; do not infer common title from name similarity, a twelve-year coincidence, institutional participation, or absence from a disclosure schedule. Do not state that the family currently owns all Llandough/Leckwith or that a Wales-wide annexation strategy has been proved without a parcel-specific chain and independent corroboration.
