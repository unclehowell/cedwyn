Reply 2, 2 Sep 2026: full text as published
Thank you for your email to the NPCC FOI Team at the National Police
Freedom of information and Data Protection Unit (NPFDU).
If your email constitutes a Freedom of Information request, you will
receive a separate formal acknowledgement confirming receipt and providing
a case reference number for use in all future correspondence.
All requests will be processed in accordance with the Freedom of
Information legislation.
Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act, you have a right to request
access to personal data related to you from an organisation. If this is
what you require you will need to contact a local force or ACRO Criminal
Records Office, rather than the NPCC.
Similary, if you require your data held on the Police National Computer
(the central record of conviction and non-conviction outcomes), [1]ACRO
Criminal Records Office manage these requests on behalf of the NPCC so you
can obtain this data from them. To make your request, you will need to use
the ACRO subject access online process via
[2]
https://www.acro.police.uk/s/acro-servic...
