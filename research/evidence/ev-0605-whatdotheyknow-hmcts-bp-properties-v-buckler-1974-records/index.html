Reply 3, 28 Jan 2026: full text as published
Morning,
We understand that the information you have requested relates to a court or tribunal case. Please contact the relevant court or tribunal directly to request access to these documents under the appropriate Procedure Rules. You can find contact details here:
https://www.gov.uk/find-court-tribunal
.
Please note that the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA) gives individuals the right to request recorded information held by public authorities. However, FOIA does not apply to information held by courts and tribunals for the purposes of judicial proceedings. This includes case files, court records, and correspondence relating to judicial decisions.
If you would still like us to consider your request under FOIA, please let us know. However, please be aware that the information you have requested is likely not to be held by the MOJ in the first place; this is because court and tribunal records are very often held by a court and tribunal for their own purposes only. It is likely, therefore, that any FOIA outcome from the MOJ will be that we Do Not Hold the data.
If such information is held by the MOJ it is likely to be exempt from disclosure under Section 32 of the FOIA, which exempts information from disclosure under the FOIA if it is held by the MOJ only by virtue of it being contained in a court or tribunal record.
In either situation, there exists another, more appropriate way to request access court or tribunal records, i.e. through the appropriate Procedure Rules.
Kind regards
Disclosure Team
Security and Information Directorate
Service Transformation Group
Ministry of Justice
