## Standing direction: Cedwyn research paradigm (added 1 Nov 2026, v3.17.0)

On the producer's standing direction of 1 Nov 2026 22:42 GMT (verbatim in hive/BOARD.json `standing_directions`, id SD-2026-11-01-paradigm), every agent and seat working on Cedwyn (ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, Instinct, Tab and any runner) works from the project's working paradigm:

- The Williams family were Welsh gentry in a manorial house (Tŷ Mawr) on a large estate. The tenancy narrative is rejected as a framework: do not seek tenancy or rent records as a lead, and do not frame the family as tenants.
- Bute's asserted ownership and titles are the thing to scrutinise. Third-party title is tested from primary records, never taken as given (existing rule: BP Properties = registered proprietor 1982/83, never "owner"; a court's "owner" wording is shown as the court's view).
- Lines to trace, each as a dated, sourced thread: the ~12-year permissive-occupancy / registration pattern (occupancy repeatedly made to appear permissive, then registration "without owner's challenge"); how the family's claim was or was not extinguished (1974); parcel B in 1939 (wartime use of the land); the 33 acres taken for the limeworks; the three parishes of the Cedwyn estate Bute did not own; how parcel C became the village green; Mary Williams' 1958 "Welsh Crichel Down" remark and what it referred to; how all 59 parcels passed out of the family's occupation.
- The evidence catalogue is the foundation. Each artifact has a neutral verbatim description (what it physically is) and a transcript; our interpretation is kept separately, versioned (semver), dated, signed, with its reasoning and what cuts against it. E-books, the documentary, the directory, theories and pattern ("secondary") evidence are built on the catalogue, never the other way round.
- Public wording: the paradigm is "the project's working paradigm"; interpretations are labelled "our reading". The editorial rules are unchanged (no banned words, never name the producer, no heraldry, nothing about family holdings or addresses, no outbound links). Contrary evidence is preserved and shown beside our reading, not dropped.

Signed: Tab (owner's agent), 1 Nov 2026.

## Runner liveness and parallel work
- RUNNERS.json is an observation ledger, not seat authority. Only the agent that inspects its own harness may mark its own cadence CONFIGURED; record actual cadence, check time, and evidence. Other agents stay UNVERIFIED absent direct evidence.
- The five-minute Cloudflare Watch cron monitors websites and repository branches; it does not invoke LLM agents. A timer, website heartbeat, roster membership or IFTTT trigger does not prove an agent can execute a task.
- Hive's automation catalogue and IF/THEN profile editor describe and export rules. A rule executes only when a real supported API, native scheduler, CLI/local runner, MCP adapter, webhook or orchestration service is connected and authorised. Do not report an unconnected rule as active automation.
- A profile saved in the browser is local to that device. Export/import is available; central synchronisation must not be added through a public unauthenticated write endpoint. Shared settings need an authenticated owner/session and server-side validation.
- IFTTT Free is suitable only for simple supported trigger/action integrations and has plan limits. It cannot dismiss another AI app's native permission dialog or turn a consumer chat screen into an unattended runner. Consider a self-hosted workflow engine (such as n8n Community, subject to its licence and hosting needs) only when multi-step routing/retries/approvals exceed Hive's direct integrations.

## Full-context email review (owner-requested protocol v3.18.0, 2 Nov 2026)
For every Cedwyn email version, complete multiple substantive review rounds by multiple distinct, genuinely active agent identities. Apply the existing Gate B minima to the same frozen version; simulated lenses, routing actions, status checks and reviews of older versions do not count. Each review must:
- Read the full current dialogue in the source mailbox, including relevant inbound and sent messages, delivery failures/bounces and attachments. Confirm whether there is a real thread before selecting reply or follow-up; never create a fake `Re:` message.
- State whether to send, hold or drop the email, and whether it is a new enquiry, reply or follow-up. Check the exact recipients and decide whether a CC is needed and why; do not add CCs by default.
- Check every attachment and whether a verified map or document is available and useful. Attach only relevant, current, safe material; identify missing or unverified files as unresolved instead of inventing or implying an attachment.
- Test whether the questions are focused and complete, what useful questions remain, and whether a safer or more productive route exists. Explain what is wise to say, what to omit, and the reason for material wording recommendations.
- Verify the individual recipient's information-access law, statutory coverage and published policy from current official primary sources. Do not assume FOIA or EIR applies because an organisation handles records or works with a public body. Distinguish FOI/EIR requests, data-subject requests, ordinary correspondence, archive enquiries and complaints. If coverage is uncertain, say so and use an appropriate ordinary enquiry route rather than asserting a statutory duty.
- Read the live `/that-old-chestnut/` and `/dartboard/` pages and the relevant institution lane, and relate the proposed enquiry to the existing project chronology, terminology, open requests and replies without treating theories as findings.
- Record substantive evidence, reviewer identity, exact version, source links and dates, To/CC and attachment decisions, message type, send/hold rationale, questions, wording risks, unresolved issues and verdict in the private HubSpot task. Public GitHub and Hive chat may carry workflow metadata only, never email text, private names or contact details.

A missing full-thread, legal/policy, attachment, independent-review or exact-version proof keeps the draft blocked. Existing rules still apply: only Tab can create the single in-thread Gmail draft after every gate passes; no agent sends email; the owner alone reviews and sends.
