How Cedwyn was taken, parcel by parcel

Tŷ Mawr alias Cedfin — the Cedwyn estate, home of the Williams family, now Leckwith and Llandough — was old manorial farmland beside a Christian site used since the fourth century. Over a hundred years of quarrying, sewers, estate sales, lettings, court orders and registration, the land the family lived on and worked was taken into other hands one parcel at a time.
